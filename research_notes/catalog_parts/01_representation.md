| REP-01 | Structure coded separately from content enables transfer | Entorhinal cortex carries a structure code that stays the same across sensory content, and the hippocampus binds it to content. This allows zero-shot transfer: human EC generalized across hexagonal graphs of different size and stimuli (N=28), and mice applied a learned task schema from the first trial. MEG rest replay of novel objects followed the sequence implied by a learned abstract rule rather than the order experienced, with a factorized position code leading the sensory code by about 50 ms (Liu 2019). The hippocampus encoded latent context and stimulus in an abstract, disentangled geometry only when patients could infer (17 patients, 2,694 neurons; shattering dimensionality 0.57 to 0.62, P=2.7e-3). This appeared on correct trials only, and verbal instruction produced the same geometry as trial-and-error. In ML, a shared latent workspace trained with translation and cycle-consistency aligned modalities with 4-7x less paired data and enabled zero-shot cross-modal policy transfer. | replicated | encode | Next to the surface statement, /encode writes structure.md. It holds (a) a content-free structure signature in a fixed schema: roles and slots in place of entity names, relation types, orderings and transition rules, conserved quantities, bottleneck; and (b) a latent-variable factorization table listing each latent variable (context, goal type, resource type) and its values, each stated independently so solutions can be recombined. Every idea in ideas.jsonl carries the same domain-neutral relational field next to its domain text. Embeddings and memory/semantic/ retrieval use the signature or relational field only, never the full text. /analogize matches sources by signature, using python graph comparison or a signature-text embedding, then binds content in a separate mapping step. Cycle check: a fresh subagent turns the relational field back into the target domain, and the result is compared with the original idea. /consolidate saves useful factorizations to memory/semantic/ as reusable instructions. Harness: shuffle the order of facts in the problem statement and confirm the shortlist stays stable; ablate the factorization table on transfer problems. |
| REP-02 | Prior knowledge imposes a default set | Existing concepts constrain imagination. When people imagine novel creatures, the results keep the defaults of familiar ones, such as bilateral symmetry and sense organs (Ward). Domain expertise can act as a mental set: on Remote-Associates-type items, experts were drawn to misleading domain-consistent answers (Wiley 1998). Strong priors also block unusual hypotheses: preschoolers (106) found an unusual conjunctive causal rule more often than adults (170), who kept the familiar rule as evidence accumulated. Flexibility fell with age, and the advantage comes from weaker priors, not better reasoning. | replicated | encode | /encode writes a defaults section to problem.md with two parts. 'Inherited defaults' lists properties any solution from the obvious category would carry without anyone choosing them (e.g. 'physical device', 'one user', 'runs continuously'). 'Expert default' gives the answer a domain specialist would give first and the conventions it relies on. Half of the /forage subagents must violate at least one named default, and the expert default is off-limits as a starting point, though domain knowledge may still be used for feasibility. Add a low-prior pass: a fresh subagent sees only the raw observations, constraints and resources, with no domain label, defaults section or expert framing, and lists every consistent hypothesis, including unfamiliar ones. Its output is tagged mode=low_prior. /evaluate reports embedding distance from the expert default and the naive default, plus the share of shortlisted ideas that kept every default, as a fixation metric. |
| REP-03 | Re-encode in a formal, executable notation | Language and reasoning doubly dissociate. The language network does not engage during logic, arithmetic, sudoku or theory-of-mind reasoning, and severely aphasic patients reason normally on logic, math and chess. Inductive reasoning uses the multiple-demand network, and deduction uses regions distinct from both, so language is a tool for communication rather than the medium of thought. New notations change which operations are thinkable (Menary). Computationally, LLMs can translate natural language into probabilistic programs, over which Bayesian inference, physics simulators and planners do the reasoning, as demonstrated across probabilistic, relational, physical and social domains (Wong et al. 2023). | replicated | encode | /encode writes problem.md in prose and also in a formal structure: a constraint table, a causal graph as JSON, a state machine, or, for quantitative or causal problems, problem_model.py, a samplable generative model with entities, uncertain parameters and rules. If no formal model fits, /encode records that and skips the step. /restructure adds a 'change notation' framing that re-encodes into a different formalism. A /forage subagent generates by manipulating that representation in the shell (graph edits, parameter sweeps) and translates the results back to prose. /analogize maps structure on the graph, and /evaluate and /insight-check test ideas by changing and running the model instead of arguing in prose. Prose remains the format for passing content between agents. Harness: formal plus prose versus prose-only encoding. |
| REP-04 | Externalise, then re-perceive the artifact | Externalising helps generation. Sketching helped people discover and solve more problems (Verstijnen 1997), diagrams improved inventive ideas (Mayer 1989), and complex tasks need externalisation because working memory is limited (Finke 1992). External representations create persistent referents that can be reinterpreted (Kirsh 2010). Deliberate external representation also raised output: encouraged iconic gestures predicted AUT fluency (b=0.24) and originality (b=0.19) in 80 adults, while spontaneous gestures predicted lower flexibility (beta=-0.30), as if fixating one image. An 83-sample meta-analysis found that producing gestures beats observing them. | replicated | forage | Each /forage generator writes its working representation to runs/<id>/scratch/<agent>.md before and during generation, and it must produce at least two different representations (an ASCII or mermaid diagram, a variable table, a python stub or simulation, a worked numeric example) so that no single image fixates it. Each idea cites the representation it came from. In a separate sketch step, a promising idea is turned into an artifact in runs/<id>/sketches/. A second fresh subagent gets only the artifact, not the idea text, and lists unintended readings, gaps and new affordances, which go to ideas.jsonl as new candidates. Harness: ablate the scratch and re-perception steps separately. |
| REP-05 | Value-guided construal simplifies the problem | Planners represent only the obstacles whose usefulness for the plan outweighs the cost of representing them. In pre-registered maze experiments (initial n=161), construal predicted awareness (chi-squared=2297), recall and hover-based process tracing, and perception and execution did not explain the effect. | replicated | encode | /encode lists every problem element in problem.md and tags it 'in construal' or 'dropped', with a one-line reason. On impasse, /restructure first re-adds dropped elements one at a time as candidate framings, since a relevant element left out of the representation is a source of fixation. The harness logs how often the final solution used a dropped element. |
| REP-06 | Flexible semantic network structure links remote concepts | More creative and higher-vocabulary people have less modular, more clustered and more interconnected semantic networks (N=506; N=161), and creative older adults keep youthful network flexibility. In 93 fMRI participants, more creative people rated remote concepts as more related and showed lower semantic-control activity on remote pairs. Semantic network structure mediated connectivity-based prediction of real-life creativity. Local network metrics are reliable across tasks; global ones (modularity, path length) are not. | replicated | encode | Design: /encode writes concepts.json, containing 10-20 problem concepts embedded together with candidates from a large vocabulary file, plus bridge concepts that sit close to two mutually distant problem concepts. Bridges seed /forage flexible mode and /analogize, so remote links are precomputed outside the context. Diagnostic: the harness collects fluency lists from the agent for problem-relevant categories, builds a co-occurrence network across runs with networkx, and compares local measures only (node degree, local clustering) between baseline and HTSA prompting. It also checks whether bridge-seeded ideas land in the target distance window more often than random-seeded ones. |
| REP-07 | Small focus of attention; chunk combinations into tokens | Working memory is activated long-term memory plus a focus of attention limited to about 3-5 chunks (about four when rehearsal and chunking are blocked, Cowan 2001; model fits suggest about three). Only one item is operated on at a time (Oberauer 2002). Chunking frees capacity and also improves recall of the items left unchunked. The limits reflect interference and fleeting attention. Rich combinations plausibly depend on chunking sub-combinations into single tokens. | replicated | forage | A script draws 2-4 elements (for example one constraint, one resource and one remote concept, as ids plus gists) from problem.md and ideas.jsonl and gives them to each combination subagent instead of the whole pool. Many small triads run in parallel. When a combination is useful, it goes into ideas.jsonl as a new named chunk with its own id, so later rounds treat it as one element and deep combinations build up level by level. Abstractions from memory/semantic/ enter as named one-line chunks, so a single slot carries a whole framing. Harness: small-set prompts versus full-pool prompts, scored on novelty and integration. |
| REP-08 | Cue order determines which hypotheses get considered | Hypotheses are retrieved from long-term memory into a small working-memory set (HyGene) whose size tracks capacity. Data order and presentation rate shift the balance between recency and primacy, the first hypothesis guides attention, and errors made during generation carry into judgement. | replicated | forage | A python script writes per-agent variants of problem.md with facts and constraints in different shuffled orders, so parallel /forage subagents form different first hypotheses. ideas.jsonl records which fact came first, so clusters can be checked for order dependence. Harness: cross-agent diversity with shuffled versus fixed order. |
| REP-09 | Labels make representations more categorical | Words modulate perception top-down, and verbal interference removes the effect (label feedback). Language makes perception more categorical. Hearing shared nonsense labels made people sort shapes more categorically and aligned their representations with each other. | replicated | restructure | Before reframing, /restructure replaces the problem's category labels and resource names with neutral IDs plus functional descriptions ('object R: rigid, 30cm, conducts heat' instead of 'ruler') to loosen category lock-in. In the other direction, /evaluate gives all evaluator instances a shared label glossary to raise agreement. Both changes can be ablated, for example on MacGyver-style functional-fixedness items. |
| REP-10 | Fixation hurts accuracy but not the Aha experience | Misleading fixation cues lowered accuracy (39.5% vs 53.8%, d=-0.80) and slowed responses, but left the Aha rate unchanged (59.8% vs 61.8%; N=349). This suggests that escaping fixation and experiencing insight are separable processes. | single-study | encode | /encode marks fixating cues in problem.md. /evaluate checks whether shortlisted ideas reuse them (embedding similarity to the cue text) and penalises reuse. Because a confident breakthrough can happen while the agent is still fixated, accuracy has to come from verification, never from insight or confidence flags. Harness: inject misleading cues into benchmark items and measure the accuracy drop per condition. |
| REP-11 | Counterfactual framing exposes more possibilities | In four experiments (n=731), counterfactual explanations of AI decisions were rated more helpful and improved people's own decision accuracy more than causal explanations did, consistent with counterfactuals conveying more possibilities. Judging alternatives recruits additional prefrontal-parietal activity (14 clusters). | single-study | restructure | /restructure runs counterfactual prompts over the default schema in a fresh subagent that sees only problem.md. Examples: 'what would have to be different for the obvious solution to work?' and 'if constraint X were removed, what solves it, and which part of that survives X?' Each answer becomes a candidate reframing that /think can write into problem.md. |
| REP-12 | Similarity walk over richly described concept embeddings | A softmax-over-cosine random walk on text-embedding-3-large reproduced human-like semantic foraging (IRT slope -18.0, p~7e-6) only when each concept carried generated descriptive sentences; bare names gave non-human-like results (+0.36). Metropolis-Hastings accept/reject sampling produced no human-like switch signature. | preprint | new | Add a python seed-walker. Expand each seed concept into 1-2 descriptive sentences in one cached LLM pass (memory/semantic/seeds.jsonl), embed the sentences, and run a temperature-controlled softmax walk from the problem's embedding to produce seed sequences for /forage and /wander. Choose the next seed by a fluid similarity walk, not by a generate-then-accept/reject gate. |
| REP-13 | Frames reweight salience and create blind spots | Frames and characterisations are not propositions. They are open-ended structures that set which features are prominent and central and how new information is taken in. Changing the frame (by metaphor or analogy) reconfigures everything at once, and a frame also hides known features that do not fit it (Camp). | theory | restructure | Each alternative framing from /restructure must include a salience table that ranks problem features as central, peripheral or ignored under that frame. Two framings count as distinct only if their top-3 central features differ. /evaluate checks every shortlisted idea against the features its frame marked as ignored, to catch the blind spots that frame creates. |
| REP-14 | Abstract concepts grounded in multiple systems | Depending on content, abstract concepts draw on sensorimotor, emotional/interoceptive, social and mentalizing systems, on language, and on amodal hubs. Right anterior insula rTMS (N=25) impaired heartbeat counting and changed emotional and social judgements of abstract concepts. | theory | restructure | /restructure re-encodes the problem along fixed grounding dimensions. Physical: what moves or touches what. Social: who negotiates, who benefits. Affective: what stakeholders fear or want. Informational: which terms and categories are in play. Each dimension yields one alternative framing for problem.md, giving the vocabulary-shift step a principled set of framings instead of ad hoc ones. These framings can carry REP-13 salience tables. |
| REP-15 | Analogy needs explicit structural mapping; fluent mapping is brittle | On letter-string analogies with permuted alphabets or symbols, humans stayed accurate while every GPT model declined sharply, and on story analogies the models were sensitive to answer order and paraphrase (Lewis & Mitchell). Webb et al. counter that GPT-4 reaches roughly human level when it can use code to convert letters to indices. In humans, space-time and space-number metaphor-consistent priming appeared only when primes were consciously seen; masked primes activated only language-encoded associations, which favours explicit structural mapping over automatic resonance. | contested | analogize | /analogize writes every mapping as an explicit JSON table of source role, target role and relation, stored in the ideas.jsonl mapping field, rather than relying on fluent prose or evocative imagery. A fresh subagent or a python check verifies that each relation holds in the target, and tool use such as index conversion is allowed. The mapping is rerun with source and target order swapped and with paraphrased inputs, and only mappings that survive both are kept. /evaluate scores any analogy idea without an explicit role mapping as 'unintegrated'. |
| REP-16 | Global workspace broadcasts a compressed, coarse summary | GNWT holds that conscious access routes one selected content to otherwise encapsulated specialist processors, which maintain, bind, transform and combine it. Two findings constrain what is broadcast. Human behavioral throughput is about 10 bits/s against about 10^9 bits/s of sensory input, handled one path at a time (Zheng & Meister 2024). In preregistered Cogitate (N=256), posterior cortex decoded content at over 95% for the whole stimulus duration, while PFC reached about 70% only 0.2-0.4 s after onset, coded category but not identity or orientation, and added nothing to the decoders. That result challenged key GNWT and IIT tenets. | contested | think | runs/<id>/workspace.md is the only broadcast channel. The orchestrator writes the admitted idea or framing there as pointers, not copies: idea id, category label, one-sentence gist, decision and reason, under a hard length cap (about 100 words) that a script enforces. Every specialist subagent spawned next (forage, analogize, restructure, evaluate) gets workspace.md and problem.md as its first inputs and fetches details from ideas.jsonl or evaluations.jsonl by id with a small python lookup. Specialists never read each other's raw output directly. Harness: broadcast-only versus full ideas.jsonl access, and pointer workspace versus full-copy workspace, compared on solve rate and token cost. |
| REP-17 | Placeholder symbol, then analogical filling | In Carey's Quinian bootstrapping, new primitive concepts arise when an uninterpreted placeholder system (such as the count list) is mapped by analogy onto existing representations and the mapping is then generalised. The result goes beyond anything previously representable. | contested | restructure | When /evaluate returns impasse and the shortlist shows a gap ('we need something that does X but nothing known does'), /restructure coins a named placeholder entity in problem.md, defined only by its relations to known elements. /analogize then fills it from source domains that have the same relational slot. The placeholder stays in problem.v2.md until it has a concrete realisation, and that realisation is checked by /insight-check. |
| REP-18 | ANTI: Phase-gated idea binding lacks causal support | Theta-gamma coupling decoded correct short-term retention in 12 of 16 patients, with gamma bursts locked to high-excitability theta phases. But no 2023-2026 study shows that manipulating phase changes which items get combined, and Buzsaki argues travelling waves merely reflect synaptic activity. | contested | new | This gives no design rule. Do not build oscillation-like schedulers, such as rhythmically cycling which memory items are 'in phase' for combination, on the premise that the brain gates combination by phase. If combination scheduling is wanted, choose pairs by embedding distance or the REP-07 small-set sampler, and justify the choice with a harness ablation on its own merits. |

### Sources and caveats

- **REP-01**. Sources: Whittington et al. 2020, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7707106/; Liu 2019, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6657653; Courellis et al. 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07799-x; Devillers, Maytie & VanRullen 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15711. Caveats: The neural evidence is a design pattern only. Human EC generalization was shown for hexagonal but not clustered structure in a small fMRI sample. Liu 2019 and Courellis 2024 are each single studies (the latter with one serial-reversal task in epilepsy patients). The GW results come from trained multimodal networks, so a text schema is only an analogy. Prompts cannot control an LLM's internal geometry; only the explicit text structure is under our control. LLMs may already abstract structure, so any benefit has to be shown by ablation. Merged from: Structure coded separately from content [computational_models_of_thinking]; Replay follows learned structure, not experience order [sleep_dreams_and_replay]; Shared latent space enables cross-module transfer [consciousness_and_global_workspace]; Abstract disentangled geometry enables inference [brain_dynamics_and_neuromodulation].
- **REP-02**. Sources: SEP Creativity (Ward; Karmiloff-Smith), https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/; Wiley 1998, https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03211392; Gopnik et al. 2017, https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC5544286; Wente et al. 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12943. Caveats: The imagination studies used drawing tasks and the notes give no effect sizes. The expertise finding is one foundational RAT-type study, and no 2023-2026 test of an expertise-originality inverted U was found. The prior-strength replications come from the same group (plus Peru and Head Start samples), and children do worse when the familiar prior is right. An LLM's priors live in its weights and cannot be removed by hiding context, so regression to the typical example may be stronger in LLMs. Merged from: Structured imagination defaults to near neighbours [philosophy_of_thought_and_creativity]; Domain expertise acts as a mental set [modulators_and_practical_levers]; Weak priors find unusual causal rules [development_language_and_culture].
- **REP-03**. Sources: Fedorenko, Piantadosi & Gibson 2024, https://news.mit.edu/2024/what-is-language-for-0703; Kean et al. 2026, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2520095123; Menary (PMC 2020), https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7437646/; Wong et al. 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12672v2. Caveats: The dissociation concerns human brain networks. An LLM's only native medium is tokens, so a formal encoding is still text, and any benefit has to come from the operations the notation and tools allow, which is untested. Some counter-evidence exists (Baldo: card-sorting deficits in aphasia). Wong et al. is an unreviewed preprint with demonstrations, not benchmarks, and it works only where the domain can be formalized. Merged from: Re-encode in a non-verbal formal notation [philosophy_of_thought_and_creativity]; Language network silent during reasoning [development_language_and_culture]; Language translated into runnable world models [computational_models_of_thinking].
- **REP-04**. Sources: SEP Creativity, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/; Kirsh 2010, https://philarchive.org/rec/KIRTWE; Hyusein & Goksun 2024, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11142943/; Dargue 2019, https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000202. Caveats: The human studies concern visual sketching and gesture under working-memory limits, and mapping gesture to scratch files is an analogy. The gesture effects depend on condition (on hard rotation problems gesture helped women and hurt men), and no adult study had a restriction condition. LLMs have a different bottleneck (context), so any benefit may come mostly from the fresh reader reinterpreting the artifact. Partly a generation mechanism. Merged from: Externalise, then re-perceive the artifact [philosophy_of_thought_and_creativity]; Deliberate external representations raise fluency, originality [emotion_body_and_curiosity].
- **REP-05**. Sources: Ho et al. 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06948. Caveats: Navigation mazes only, with several experiments in one paper. Extending construal to verbal or design problems is an extrapolation, and how LLMs construe problems has not been measured. Merged from: Problems simplified by value-guided construal [computational_models_of_thinking].
- **REP-06**. Sources: Herault et al. 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06493-y; Ovando-Tellez et al. 2022, https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abl4294; Li, Beaty, Luchini, Hu & Kenett 2024, https://cris.technion.ac.il/en/publications/the-role-of-semantic-memory-networks-in-crystallized-intelligence/; Behavior Research Methods 2023, https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-023-02271-6. Caveats: All evidence is correlational or cross-sectional, and no study changes network structure experimentally. Kounios & Oh 2023 argue structure is downstream of other causes, so the causal direction is disputed. Network flexibility is inferred from relatedness judgments. Embedding-space bridges and LLM-sampled networks are engineering proxies, not human memory. The diagnostic half is a measurement function. Merged from: Flexible semantic structure links remote concepts [brain_networks_of_creativity]; Flexible semantic networks accompany knowledge and creativity [memory_semantic_networks_and_combination].
- **REP-07**. Sources: Cowan 2001, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X01003922; Oberauer 2002, https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.28.3.411; Cowan et al. 2024, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12143923/; Thalmann et al. 2019, https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000578. Caveats: Foundational work, not specific to ideation, and no study measures how many ideas people hold while ideating. LLMs have no hard chunk limit; long contexts suffer attention dilution instead. Whether forced small sets help an LLM is purely an empirical question. Merged from: About four chunks held, one in focus [consciousness_and_global_workspace]; Focus of attention holds about three chunks [reasoning_metacognition_and_working_memory].
- **REP-08**. Sources: Thomas et al. 2008, https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295x.115.1.155; Lange et al. 2013, https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-012-0316-9; Buttaccio et al. 2015, https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-014-0463-5. Caveats: Foundational and mostly pre-2023, with no recent update found. LLMs show position effects, but their shape (long-context primacy and recency) differs from the human one. The design lever is arguably a generation/diversity mechanism. Merged from: Cue order determines which hypotheses get considered [reasoning_metacognition_and_working_memory].
- **REP-09**. Sources: Lupyan 2012, https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00054/full; Lupyan et al. 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2020.08.005; Suffill et al. 2024, https://doi.org/10.1162/opmi_a_00153. Caveats: Mostly perceptual and categorization tasks, with transient and modest effects. The link to creative restructuring is inferred. Any functional-fixedness benefit for LLMs has to be shown empirically. Merged from: Labels make representations more categorical [development_language_and_culture].
- **REP-10**. Sources: Chao 2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11947100. Caveats: A single study on a verbal RAT, humans only. The unchanged Aha rate is a non-significant difference, not a tested equivalence. Partly an evaluation mechanism (verification over feeling of insight). Merged from: Fixation cues hurt accuracy, not Aha rate [insight_and_aha_moments].
- **REP-11**. Sources: Celar & Byrne 2023, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10520145/; Bernhard et al. 2025, https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0001676; Byrne 2026, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13397060/. Caveats: The evidence concerns explaining decisions, not generating solutions, so the link to creative reframing is an inference. Merged from: Counterfactual framing exposes more possibilities [reasoning_metacognition_and_working_memory].
- **REP-12**. Sources: Moore 2025, https://arxiv.org/html/2511.12759v1. Caveats: A single unreviewed preprint, one category (550 animals), fluency data from 141 people; transfer to open-ended problem concepts is unknown. Only the 'rich descriptions beat bare names' part is representation. The walk itself is a generation/sampling mechanism and may belong under generation. Merged from: Similarity walk over rich embeddings [thought_dynamics_and_mind_wandering].
- **REP-13**. Sources: Camp 2019, https://philarchive.org/rec/CAMPAF-7; Camp 2009, https://philarchive.org/rec/CAMTVO. Caveats: Conceptual analysis taken from abstracts. The notes did not access Camp's detailed account of prominence and centrality, and there is no empirical test. Merged from: Frames reweight salience; double-edged blindness [philosophy_of_thought_and_creativity].
- **REP-14**. Sources: Kiefer 2026, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2026.07.004; Mancano & Papagno 2026, https://doi.org/10.1523/jneurosci.0238-25.2025; Del Maschio 2022, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8773921/. Caveats: These are claims about human semantic representation, and the one causal rTMS study is small (N=25). Whether reframing an LLM along these dimensions helps is untested. Merged from: Abstract concepts are grounded in multiple systems [emotion_body_and_curiosity].
- **REP-15**. Sources: Lewis & Mitchell 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14215; Webb, Holyoak & Lu 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13070; Saponaro 2026, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2026.104035. Caveats: The LLM brittleness is actively disputed, the tasks are toy analogies, and current models were not tested in these notes. Tool scaffolding helped GPT-4 in one study only. The metaphor-priming result is a single human study with no sample size reported, and LLMs have no conscious/unconscious distinction. Merged from: LLM analogy is brittle on counterfactual variants [memory_semantic_networks_and_combination]; Metaphor mappings need conscious, language-encoded structure [emotion_body_and_curiosity].
- **REP-16**. Sources: Mashour et al. 2020, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8770991/; Zheng & Meister 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10234; Cogitate Consortium 2025, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1; Naccache et al. 2025, https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2025/1/niaf037/8280147. Caveats: GNWT broadcast is a theory claim, the notes found no recent neural evidence that combining content across modules requires ignition, and Cogitate failed several GNWT predictions, though proponents dispute that reading. Cogitate used passive viewing of task-irrelevant stimuli, so extending it from percepts to thoughts is an inference. The 10 bits/s figure is an interpreted estimate, not an experiment on thinking, and a human bandwidth limit need not help an LLM. This is mainly an architecture/control mechanism (information routing), not problem encoding. Merged from: Global broadcast of one content to specialists [consciousness_and_global_workspace]; Behavioral throughput is about ten bits/second [consciousness_and_global_workspace]; Workspace carries brief coarse labels, not details [consciousness_and_global_workspace].
- **REP-17**. Sources: Rule & Piantadosi 2025, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.18277; Warwick notes on Carey vs Fodor, https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/philosophy/intranets/undergraduate/modules/philosophyofeducation/2015-16/notes_week_7.pdf. Caveats: Fodor and Rey call bootstrapping circular, and Rips et al. dispute its claimed incommensurability. The evidence is developmental (number concepts in children), so applying it to adult problem solving or to LLMs is an extrapolation. Merged from: Placeholder symbol, then analogical filling [philosophy_of_thought_and_creativity].
- **REP-18**. Sources: Borderie et al. 2024, https://lead.ube.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Borderie_et_al._2024.pdf; Quanta 2026, https://www.quantamagazine.org/surprisingly-complex-waves-reveal-the-brains-inner-workings-20260930/. Caveats: This is an absence of causal evidence, not evidence against the idea, and epilepsy-grid sampling may miss relevant patterns. The correlational coupling result is solid within its 16 patients. Merged from: ANTI: Phase-gated idea binding lacks causal support [brain_dynamics_and_neuromodulation].
