# Schema candidates from the thinking-sciences research

*Established findings that could change SCHEMA.md, checked against what it already says. Untested ideas are not here; they are in ../thinking_sciences/00_TEST_BACKLOG.md. Nothing is applied until the user approves it. Updated after each research batch.*

Version checked against: SCHEMA.md v0.2 (2026-10-20). Batch: field A01 (SYNTHESIS.md, 9 subfields) and A02 subfield 01 (problem space theory, DEEP.md section 7, claims with status established only).

**Read this first.**
- "Established" is the status in claims.jsonl. The stricter independence re-check (re-open sources, confirm independence, try to refute) has **not** been run on these claims; the A01 synthesis says so itself, and one known case counted one paper twice (A01.01-K06). Every row below is therefore a candidate that still needs that re-check before it enters SCHEMA.md.
- Evidence base. A01 claims come mostly from **human studies and 2023-24 LLMs** (log-probability scoring in places); A02.01 claims come from **human puzzle studies plus 2023-24 LLM search papers (GPT-3.5/4, Llama), mostly on math and puzzles with exact rules**. Almost nothing is from current Claude, GPT-5 or o-series models (A01 gap: no current-model data). Per SCHEMA section 8, new elements enter as **[S]** or **[D]** with an equal-token test; "Test" below names it.
- Source column: H = humans, OL = older LLMs (2023-24), P = LLM puzzle/math search papers.
