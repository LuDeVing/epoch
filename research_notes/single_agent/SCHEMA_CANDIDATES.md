*Established findings that could change SCHEMA.md, checked against what it already says. Untested ideas are not here; they are in ../thinking_sciences/00_TEST_BACKLOG.md. Nothing is applied until the user approves it. Updated after each research batch.*

Version checked against: SCHEMA.md v0.2 (2026-10-20). Batch: fields A01 (9 subfields), A02 (3 subfields: 01 problem space theory, 02 means-ends analysis, 04 strategies and cycle), A04 (2 subfields), A12 (2), C01 (2), C02 (2), C09 (2), E06 (2), E08 (2), E09 (2), E10 (2). All read from the field SYNTHESIS.md files, claims with status established only. SC01 to SC21 come from the earlier batch (A01 and A02.01); SC22 onward are new, and several earlier rows gained claim ids.

**Audit 2026-10-26 (code check):** 13 claims marked established had only one opened source and are now single-source. SC14, SC36 and SC43 have no established claim left and are on hold. SC04, SC05, SC24, SC25, SC26 and SC41 lost one or two claims but keep other support.

**Read this first.**
- Two results are counted once: the 42% to 56% counterexample result (A01.07-K12, E09.02-K12, E10.01-K10) and the 33.3% vs 52.6% Olausson result (E09.02-K10, E09.02-K22, E09.09-K05) are each one source, not three. All ten new synthesis files say their independence re-check is still pending.
- "Established" is the status in claims.jsonl. The stricter independence re-check (re-open sources, confirm independence, try to refute) has **not** been run on these claims; the A01 synthesis says so itself, and one known case counted one paper twice (A01.01-K06). Every row below is therefore a candidate that still needs that re-check before it enters SCHEMA.md.
- Evidence base. A01 claims come mostly from **human studies and 2023-24 LLMs** (log-probability scoring in places); A02 claims come from **human puzzle studies plus 2023-25 LLM search and strategy papers (GPT-3.5/4, Llama, some reasoning models), mostly on math and puzzles with exact rules**. A04, C01, C02, C09, E06, E08, E09 and E10 add mostly **2023-26 LLM papers on open or older models, simulated answerers with closed candidate lists (E08, C09), repo-scale coding agents (E09), single-lab trace labels (E06.04), and Soar design facts that are not LLM evidence (C02)**. A12 and parts of E06, E08, E10 are **human** studies (matchsticks, nine-dot, analysts, N often under 40). Almost nothing is from current Claude, GPT-5 or o-series models; where a result may flip for reasoning models the row says so. Per SCHEMA section 8, new elements enter as **[S]** or **[D]** with an equal-token test; "Test" below names it.
- Source column: H = humans, OL = older LLMs (2023-24), P = LLM puzzle/math search papers, RM = reasoning models, SIM = simulated answerer with closed candidate list, REPO = repo-scale coding agents, D = Soar design fact.
