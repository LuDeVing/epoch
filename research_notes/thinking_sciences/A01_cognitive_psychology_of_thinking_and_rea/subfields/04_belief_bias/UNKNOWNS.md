## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Item bank and grader: the bench has ARC and BRAINTEASER only. Unblock by building a syllogism generator with content-swapped twins, a 24-form code grader and a "does not follow" option.
- Believability labels: we have no fixed rating set. Unblock by collecting human plausibility ratings for the conclusions and comparing them with the model's own.
- Hard items at scale: easy items sit at a 99% ceiling and hide the effect. Unblock by generating three-term, multi-model and complex items and checking the ceiling first.
- Claude-family numbers: no d' or criterion analysis of any current Claude, GPT-5 or o-series model exists (K31). Only our own B0 can fill this.
- Prover cost: no equal-token comparison exists (K25). Needs a Z3 or prover wrapper and token counting on the same items.
- Human-only mechanism questions (selective scrutiny vs misinterpreted necessity, time pressure): human data are contested (K06, K09, K10, K30) and do not transfer to a model (DEEP section 9). Revisit only if U4 and U5 give a clear result.
- Primary papers still unread (Evans and Curtis-Holmes 2005, Newstead 1992, Evans, Handley and Harper 2001, Klauer 2000, Dube 2010): no open PDFs. Low value for the schema.
- Sizes of the 1990s-2000s human replications were never re-extracted. Low value for the schema.

## Order to run

1. U1 (baseline B0): tells us whether there is any effect to fix, and builds the bank every other test uses.
2. U5 (counterexample step by determinacy): cheap, and it decides whether S7 should be targeted at indeterminate items.
3. U2 (long CoT vs short attempts plus vote at equal tokens): the biggest open question for strong models, and it decides how we spend tokens.
