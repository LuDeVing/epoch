## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Does the high-probability conclusion effect hold in LLMs (K17)?** Human result is contested (Verschueren and Schaeken seen as abstract only). Unblock: read that paper, build items with stated P(p), P(q), exceptions.
- **Do human error maps predict current reasoning-model errors?** Needs per-item human data (Table 6 of Khemlani and Johnson-Laird 2012, author copy) in a usable file. Unblock: transcribe it, then compute Kendall tau.
- **Generalised quantifiers ("most", "few") for LLMs.** No data and no item set. Unblock: build items from the PHM scale.
- **Independent replication of the relevance effect and the 68% vs 17% result.** Needs outside human data. Cannot be run on a model bench.
- **Is Byrne's suppression size a response-format artifact?** Needs the 1989 original (not opened) and human participants.
- **Abduction (find the missing premise) is weak (K24, 42% vs 72%).** Single study, no item set. Unblock: build abduction items, test an enumerate-candidates step.
- **Frontier results come from binary tasks and templated data.** Unblock: 100 free-text syllogisms with a key and a stated import reading.
- **Which import reading each target benchmark uses.** Unblock: check each benchmark's key before scoring.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (reasoning-base NVC baseline).** It is cheap and it decides whether U2 and U3 are worth running at all.
2. **U4 (S0 existence line).** One line of prompt, large gap reported (about 62% vs 100% by reading), easy to score on 9 moods.
3. **U7 (requirement vs alternative classification).** Biggest reported failure (10-22% update accuracy), existing items (Belief-R), and the fix is a state we already have.
