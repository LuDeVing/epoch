# Content effects: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

## Test before adding

Common rules for every test: use the bench harness, fix answer format and scorer first, equal total tokens across arms (setup calls count), at least 3 seeds, free-text answers parsed by code. Run on one weak, one mid and one frontier model. Report the gap per model, with spread, never a pooled average. "Pts" means accuracy points.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Cheater detection vs plain question reading (K05, contested). Needs human-style data or new items with an independent cheating cue. Unblock: not worth it for the agent; the S1 slot in U1 covers the useful part.
- Effect sizes for relevance manipulations (Ragni 2018 Table 4) and the original Cheng and Holyoak 1985, Cosmides and Tooby 1992, Platt and Griggs 1993, Fiddick and Rutherford 2006 numbers. Unblock: full texts (paywalled or blocked). They would only sharpen priors; none gates a test.
- Does cognitive ability moderate content effects, and does it map to model size or reasoning mode (K17)? Needs the Stanovich and West 1998 full text. Unblock: read it, then reuse the model-tier results from U1 and U3.
- Why Qwen3-Next Thinking has +28.0 gap and its Instruct sibling -7.9 (K21). Needs those checkpoints and single-sample runs. Unblock: model access; partly answered by U3.
- Do results hold on messy real tasks, not classic syllogisms and Wason? Most studies use fixed formats. Unblock: a bench set of real tasks with a content-swapped twin of each item.
- Weights-level levers (steering, SFT, circuits; K13, K38). Cannot run through an API. Unblock: open-weight model access, and a decision that they are in scope.
- Whether the 2025-2026 frontier gap is truly near zero on our free-text prompts (critique: older models, log-probability scoring). Unblock: run U3 first; no extra data needed.

## Order to run

1. U1: violation-naming restatement vs "be logical" vs CoT. It is the only run the critique says would change the schema, and it decides whether A8 stays.
2. U2: content x "must" 2x2. It is cheap, needs only generated items, and tells us whether U9 and any norm-rewrite idea are worth anything.
3. U3: thinking-budget sweep on belief items with congruent controls. It reuses U1's syllogisms, and it tells us per model whether any debias step has headroom at all.
