## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Independent replication of the human diagnostic (K26). Needs human data from a lab outside the Markovits network. We can only treat effect sizes as upper bounds.
- Real size of the time-pressure switch (Markovits 2013, K06) and the instruction effect (2023, K07). Needs the paywalled full texts. Until then, run U7 as a fresh test.
- Whether the two-type split is real or a scale artifact (K15). Needs human free-text data. Our bench can only check it on the model (classify the model's own justifications).
- Whether the model's chain text shows the real process (G10, c13). Needs a way to read internal state. We have only outputs.
- Vendor "reasoning effort" settings are not equal tokens (d17). Needs token-level control on every model we test, or we only test token caps we set.
- Faithful translation into code for the search (K22). Needs a parser check set with known-good formalisations to measure translation error apart from reasoning error.
- Semantic-cue vs logical-intuition vs matching for fast answers (d13). Needs human reaction data. U10 only tests the model.
- Items for the "Other" group and non-abstract content. Needs a content bank beyond the invented-planet items (K14 says transfer to causal and social items is small).

## Order to run

1. A01.07-U1 (code search vs plain CoT): the biggest possible gain, and everything in S4 depends on it.
2. A01.07-U3 (the 13-item diagnostic on the agent): cheap, and it tells us whether the dual-strategy framing applies to our model at all.
3. A01.07-U2 (the "nothing follows" probe): reuses the U1 item sets, so the extra cost is small, and models almost never give this answer.
