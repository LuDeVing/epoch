## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Founding numbers (Desimone and Duncan 1995; Chelazzi 1993/1998; Downing 2000): full texts are paywalled. Unblock with library access. They would not change the schema much.
- Effect sizes of the 2026 capture papers (Hautekiet; Kim; Liu, Wang, Liu; Won and Zhang): only abstracts read. Unblock by reading the preprints.
- Mechanistic suppression of entrainment (head masking, 2-4% of heads) needs open-weight models and access to activations. Our bench is prompt-level. Unblock by running a small open model.
- Which of the four rival accounts fits an LLM agent: needs the full factorial from U1, U2 and U4 first.
- Whether "load" in an LLM means context length, number of held facts or reasoning depth: no accepted measure. Capacity studies are mixed (c13-c15). Unblock by defining load as step count and held-fact count in U1 and checking both.
- ENTRAP-VL and other listing-only 2026 entrainment papers: not read.
- Newer RLHF models on negation (U3): NeQA used older models.

## Order to run
1. U1 (load x match): the direct test nobody has run; it decides whether a load rule belongs in the schema.
2. U3 (positive vs negative lesson wording): cheapest, one wording change, and affects every lesson we write.
3. U5 (structure vs surface retrieval): established risk, fixes the memory design, and reuses the same items as U1.
