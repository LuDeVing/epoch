## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Contested human set-size dispute (K03).** Needs human eye-tracking and probe experiments. Unblock: not needed for the schema; read future replications.
- **Attention-layer mechanism.** Attention mass on distractors is seen only in small open models (NoisyBench Qwen3-4B). Unblock: open-weight access to our model class with attention logging.
- **Training-side fixes (K20).** Distractor-rich fine-tuning (77.95 vs 35.91 in-domain) cannot be tested in a scaffold-only design. Unblock: a fine-tuning budget, which the project does not have.
- **Reliability of human value-capture scores.** Needs new human data. No agent use now.
- **Dillitzer 2026 and Owusu and Feldman 2026.** Only abstract or not opened. Unblock: parse the PDFs, then re-score U6.
- **Frontier results with one seed.** Tennant 2026 used one seed per mode. Unblock: repeat on our bench in U8.
- **Direct contingent-capture test in an LLM.** Needs a goal-match manipulation with salience fixed and a ground truth for what the model "attended to". Partly covered by U5; full version needs token-level attribution on a hosted model.

## Order to run
1. **A07.04-U11 (size of the effect on our model).** Cheapest, plain arm only. It tells us whether the other tests are worth their tokens.

3. **A07.04-U2 (unreliability warning).** Nearly free to add and the best reported gain per token; run it on the same planted set as U1.
