## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Perceptual load proper. We have no visual input with controlled load. Unblock: a vision model plus a controlled display task (the Shin vision counting task is a start; d02).
- Brain-level mechanisms (alpha, contrast gain, V1). Need model internals. Unblock: an open-weight model with attention access; Attention Tracker style probes (c16).
- Whether the Snowden null is design or reality, and whether the human literature is conditional on blocked designs. Unblock: a preregistered human replication with blocked and interleaved arms and N above 150 (a29 urges this). Outside our control.
- Exact founding numbers (Lavie 1995, Lavie and Tsal 1994, Cartwright-Finch and Lavie 2007). Unblock: access to paywalled PDFs.
- Mind wandering analogue. Needs a free-running agent loop with probes. Unblock: a long-horizon agent run with periodic off-task probes.
- Whether model family or scale predicts omission (Shin). Unblock: a wider model panel with 65+ scenarios per model and non-LLM scoring.
- Whether S2A and Thread of Thought still win on 2026 models at equal tokens. Folded into U2; needs API budget for 3+ models.
- Interaction of cognitive and perceptual load in LLMs (K07). Needs a cleanly separated manipulation we do not yet have.

## Order to run

1. A07.02-U13 (noise baseline): cheapest, and every other test needs its spread to interpret a result.
2. A07.02-U1 (open-scope sentence or field): free if it works, a large expected effect (1.00 vs 0.00 in the source), and it directly targets the missed-critical-item risk.
3. A07.02-U2 (hard rewrite vs plain at equal tokens): the largest published gain (+10 to +17 points) and the one that decides whether S1 gets a new step.
