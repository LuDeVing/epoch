## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A large preregistered ACT replication: none exists. Unblock: wait for a multi-site study, or treat ACT only as a hypothesis source.
- Moran/Shi updating disagreement: needs Shi's moderator tables (paywalled). Unblock: access the full text or authors' data.
- Whether a Common EF factor exists in LLMs: needs a battery of inhibition, shifting and updating tasks on many models with latent-variable analysis. Unblock: a multi-model run with at least 10 models and 100 items per task.
- Why Claude 3.5 Sonnet holds longer than GPT-4o on incongruent Stroop lists: needs model-internal access or a controlled model family. Unblock: open-weight family sweep.
- Whether Stroop collapse is a counting or format limit, not control: needs a counting-only control. Unblock: add a list-counting control to U4.
- WCST and cued-switching results on 2025-2026 models: Hao 2025, Goto 2025 and the AGEA preprint need a full read for perseverative errors and prompting conditions.
- Frontier-model near-miss distractor numbers at matched tokens: only abstracts seen for GSM-Symbolic and Ghost Context. Unblock: read the full papers or run U3.
- Verification of Coda-Forno betas and Ben-Zion STAI numbers (not re-verified in critique): re-read the papers.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (fresh vs carry-on)**: highest expected gain and cheap; it decides the S6 reset design.
2. **U3 (code filter vs instruction)**: cheap, builds on an established harm, and sets the S1 hygiene rules.
3. **U2 (interference slope)**: a one-time measurement that fixes the forced-reset count and feeds U1 and U10.
