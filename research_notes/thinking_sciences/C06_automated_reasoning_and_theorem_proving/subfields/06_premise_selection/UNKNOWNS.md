## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Reasoning-retriever gain after charging tokens.** BRIGHT, ReasonIR, Rank1 and LeanSearch v2 never fix total tokens. U5 tests a cheap version. A full test needs a bench with a built, labelled library and token logs per step.
- **Independent reproduction of LeanSearch v2, LeanPremise and Lean Finder.** All are 2026 self-reports with small n (100 and 50). We cannot reproduce them without their Lean setup. Wait for third-party results.
- **Fine-tuned selector on our library.** Magnushammer needs about 4K labelled samples with pretraining. We have no such labelled set. Unblock: log usage labels (which stored subgoal a solve really used) for a few thousand solves.
- **Frontier-model distractor harm.** Studies use Llama2, GPT-3.5, GPT-3 era models. U10 gives one measurement; a full dose-response curve needs more items.
- **Natural-language items vs formal items.** Our stored subgoals may be rules or helper programs, not lemmas. No paper tests that format. Unblock: build the library first, then run U1 and U8.
- **How many examples calibrate a selector on a small library.** Only Magnushammer gives a figure, for a fine-tuned model. Unknown for prompt-only calibration.
- **Structure value on our library.** Dependency links gave gains in Lean GNN work and nothing clear in CSLib. We have no dependency graph of subgoals yet. Unblock: record which subgoal used which.

## Order to run

1. **C06.06-U3 (oracle gap).** Cheapest, and it tells whether any retrieval work is worth doing. If the gap is under 3 points, stop here.
2. **C06.06-U1 (retrieve vs none vs whole library).** The core claim: does library retrieval pay at equal tokens for a frozen LLM? Everything else depends on it.
3. **C06.06-U2 (k sweep).** Sets the default k and the cap, and the same runs give the data for U4 and U14 at little extra cost.
