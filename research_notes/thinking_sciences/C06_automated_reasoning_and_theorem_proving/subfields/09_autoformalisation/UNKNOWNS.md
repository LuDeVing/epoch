## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Wu's 25.3% on the same 150 MATH problems with modern models. Needs the exact 150 problem list and an Isabelle setup. Unblock: get the list from the authors or rebuild with a Lean 4 target.
- Cost per verified statement for an automated pipeline at equal faithfulness. Needs a labelled gold set and a human-minutes log. Unblock: log tokens and minutes in U1-U3.
- Full frontier failure taxonomy including silent weakening. Needs a larger labelled sample than one model and one benchmark. Unblock: tag failures in U1-U3 outputs.
- Whether Lean or Isabelle is available on the bench. If not, tests use Python assertions or SMT as the formal target, which changes what compile means.
- Whether the two-judge gate holds at larger N. Current evidence is 54 of 59 (CI 81.6-96.3). Unblock: run U2 at N of at least 200.
- Token-matched comparison for proofs (d14 compared queries). Needs a prover.
- Non-maths use of verify-by-formalisation (U10 is the first probe).
- TOMAP-style bottleneck allocation of test-time budget (abstract only, d18). Not evidence; read the full paper before designing a test.

## Order to run
1. **C06.09-U13 (hand audit of 50 checker-passed items).** Cheapest, and it sets how much every later metric can be trusted.
2. **C06.09-U2 (two-judge plus hard-check gate against compile-only and one judge).** Cheap with injected errors, and its result decides how every other test is graded.
3. **C06.09-U1 (meaning checklist at S2).** Single prompt change, large expected value, and it supplies failure tags for U3 and the open taxonomy question.
