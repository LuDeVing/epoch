## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Library or specification change repair (Pumpkin Pi task).** No LLM study; no bench item type. Unblock: a set of broken Coq, Lean or Isabelle projects after a version change, or skip, since the agent does not face this.
- **Contamination effect on repair gains.** All formal-proof evidence is on miniF2F, PISA, CoqGym and PutnamBench. Unblock: post-cutoff problems rerun with the same arms.
- **Bundy-era critics with counts.** Ireland and Bundy 1996 and Melis and Siekmann 1999 success counts vs nqthm were not read. Unblock: library access to the journal PDFs.
- **Reasoning-model round-by-round curves in formal proofs.** Only small or fine-tuned models were measured. Unblock: run a modern reasoning model against a Lean or Isabelle checker with 0 to 3 rounds.
- **Cobblestone vs PALM verdict.** Depends on the 15x token matching. Unblock: independent token-matched rerun with different matching rules.
- **Token-matched large systems.** Seed-Prover, Hilbert, Aristotle compare heavy pipelines with light baselines. Unblock: published equal-budget ablations.
- **PALM dataset size** (10,842 vs 10,826 vs more than 10K) unresolved. Low importance.
