## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Founding numbers of Chandler and Sweller (1992) and Ginns (2006) full text (K23, K02). Unblock: library access to the PDFs. Not needed for the bench.
- Bias-corrected human effect size (K28). Unblock: funnel plot or trim-and-fill from Schroeder and Cenkci 2018; sets how far to trust the human analogy only.
- Whether natural clutter is as harmful as adversarial clutter (Cats triggers, a few percent absolute). Unblock: a corpus of real long prompts from our own runs with a labelled irrelevant share.
- Mechanism on a closed model (attention to the gold passage, entrainment). Unblock: an open-weights proxy with attention probing.
- Whether human-style load meters add anything. The human data say no (K07); skip.
- Hard-prompt safety effects of cluttered prompts (up to 99.99% attack success, snippets only, K22). Unblock: open the paper; out of scope for accuracy tests.
- ToolLoad-Bench and Adapala results rest on a hand-built load score and a single-author preprint. Unblock: replicate with our own tool sets.

## Order to run
1. A08.02-U10 (format spread): cheap, and sets the noise floor every other layout test must clear.
2. A08.02-U1 with U2 (inline vs header vs tags): the only direct test of the classic contrast; no LLM study exists, and it decides whether the "integrate" half of the prompt rule is real.
3. A08.02-U4 with U5 (code filter vs rewrite, near-miss vs inert): largest expected gain, and its audit fills the gap no study has measured. Run U8 (question last / repeat) alongside because it is as cheap.
