## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Direct additivity test in humans (Park 2010).** The thesis page is blocked (403, proof-of-work check). Unblock: obtain the PDF through a library, or ask the author. Until then do not cite its numbers.
- **Zu et al. (2021): load-type separation depends on prior knowledge.** Not read; only secondhand through Krieglstein. Unblock: open the paper.
- **Human self-report results (K05 to K10).** The questionnaire findings cannot be tested on an agent. They are context only.
- **Frontier reasoning models at equal output tokens on CogniLoad.** Published runs used a 32K cap and default decoding, 10 puzzles per cell for proprietary models. We need our own token-matched runs (U1).
- **Search coverage of 2020-2026 LLM work is partial** (two APIs were rate-limited). A fresh search for "interaction" in LLM load studies is cheap and could close gap 1.
- **CLAI numbers look unverified** (single author, no seeds, Llama-3-8B MATH 50.2% looks high). Unblock: re-run the released prompt, or find an independent replication.
- **Expertise reversal by model strength.** No published test; needs U7 on two or more backbones.
- **Whether CoThinker's gains survive a plain token-budget baseline.** Needs the same matched-token run as U8 and a stronger baseline.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (interaction grid).** Decides whether any summed or single budget is wrong, and gives the capacity curves that U2, U5 and U6 reuse. One grid, many answers.
2. **U2 (context hygiene vs distractors, with filler arm).** Cheapest large effect (about 13 points in the published case), and code can do it. Result also covers U12.
3. **U3 (self-rating leak with distractor-only change).** Decides whether any difficulty gate in S0 is safe before we build one; cheap, reuses U2's items.
