## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A validated per-task element-interactivity score for LLMs. Missing: an agreed annotation protocol. Unblock: build one in U5 and check against human ratings.
- A human or model measure of "germane processing" that is not effort or engagement. Missing: any outcome-linked measure; Krieglstein r=.17-.22 only. Unblock: none likely; use outcome accuracy instead.
- Frontier-model results for adaptive gating beyond small models. Missing: published tests on frontier models; our own U2 and U3 are the route.
- Token-matched control for CoThinker. Missing: the paper's code and calls budget; unblock by re-implementing at equal calls (U7).
- Leppink 2014 numbers and 2023-2026 replications of the three-factor structure. Missing: those papers were not opened; low relevance to the agent.
- Human variability-by-format replications with cell means. Missing: the original 1994 data; not agent-relevant until U12.
- Bastani-style offloading effects in agent-human loops. Missing: a bench with human users; not testable on a pure agent bench.
- Mechanism link between attention entropy and load (CoThinker, Gong and Zhang). Missing: access to attention internals on our model.

## Order to run
1. U1 (integration vs length-matched elaboration): it is the one germane-related idea with human support and no LLM test; a clean result either adds a state or closes the question.
2. U2 (cascade vs always-on on frontier models): cheapest to run, and the gap in current evidence that most affects token budget (G7, G12).
3. U3 (scaffold on/off across a model ladder): reuses the U2 items, and tells us which stages to gate off for the actual model.
