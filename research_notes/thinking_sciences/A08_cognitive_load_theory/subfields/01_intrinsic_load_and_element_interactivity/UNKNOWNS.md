## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human interaction (extraneous-load aids help only at high interactivity).** The base is a handful of small studies from one school (K03). It needs a preregistered, multi-lab test with two independent raters counting elements. We cannot run it on a language-model bench. What would unblock it: a published replication, or a collaboration with a learning-science lab.
- **Tetzlaff et al. (2025) expertise-reversal meta-analysis.** The effect sizes we hold are second-hand (d02). What would unblock it: open the paper (Learning and Instruction 98:102142) and check the numbers and moderators.
- **Does the human recipe for counting elements (Chen 2016) transfer to model-specific chunks?** We need a measure of what counts as one chunk for a given model. What would unblock it: a per-model calibration set (items solved at near 100% by recall) so the rater knows what is one element.
- **White-box state steering (d07).** Works on open models with probes. We use API models. What would unblock it: access to open-weight models in the bench.
- **Mechanism for the CogniLoad distractor U-shape and the d plateau beyond 7.** The paper gives none. What would unblock it: the generator code and a reasoning-trace analysis.
- **Whether the self-estimate in CLAI comes before or after the answer in d14's prompts.** Unchecked. What would unblock it: read the d14 prompt appendix.
- **BAPO-style bandwidth as an item-level predictor.** It is task-class level (d09). What would unblock it: a method to compute it per item.
- **Frontier-model behaviour on the older findings.** GSM-IC, Faith and Fate, least-to-most, ADaPT and Du are 2022 to 2024 models. What would unblock it: re-run a subset on current models (partly covered by U1, U6, U7, U11).
- **Whether the schema's own steps change load.** We cannot yet separate a decompose operator's gain from added samples or added verification. What would unblock it: the token ledger in B5/U5 with every call logged.

## Order to run

1. **A08.01-U2 (element count vs length and steps).** Cheapest, no new generator, and it decides whether any count-based gate (U1, U3, U9) is worth building. If count adds nothing, drop the count and gate on pass rate instead.
2. **A08.01-U1 (load-gated vs always vs never at equal tokens).** The direct test of the S0 gate plus O3. It also gives the break-even load, which every other row needs.
3. **A08.01-U9 (scaffold off in the low-load bin).** Likely token savings with little risk, and it reuses the item bins from U1 and U2. It tests the one mechanism (scaffold cost) with three sources behind it, at equal tokens.
