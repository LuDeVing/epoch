## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Real, messy, accuracy-scored field data on ACH use. Only Martin 2025 (n=138, r=-.08, correlational). Needs a forecasting tournament with an ACH arm, or a partner with analyst reports.
- Original numbers from Lehner 2008, Cheikes 2004, Whitesmith 2020, Maegherman 2021, Chang 2018 and Coulthart 2017. They were seen only through reviews or snippets (HTTP 403). Unblock by institutional access, then re-read.
- Whether "ACH helps weak reasoners, hurts strong ones" holds for humans. A conjecture by Karvetski and Mandel; needs a human study split by skill.
- The ACH share of HypoArena's +88 to -60 spread. Needs the authors' per-skill logs or a rerun with a model-chosen vs forced-ACH arm.
- Heterogeneous panels (different models) need outside models. Our single-agent rule (G4) cannot test them without breaking its own scope.
- Transfer of the layout result (rows vs columns) from 161 young students to analysts. No analyst sample exists.
- Token cost of any published LLM scaffold. None of PRISM, AgentCDM, HypoArena or SIA report it, so equal-token comparison needs our own reruns.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (likelihood matrix vs plain vs C/I matrix).** It closes the biggest gap (K20) and decides whether any matrix stays in the schema. Items can be generated by program, so it is cheap.
2. **U6 (joint elicitation plus normalise).** The effect is large in the literature (incoherence to near zero), the cost is one prompt change, and it may reshape every multi-hypothesis step.
3. **U3 (disconfirm-first rule vs full ACH).** It tests the one LLM-established benefit (disconfirmation) at the cheapest cost, and checks the non-thinking-model risk.
