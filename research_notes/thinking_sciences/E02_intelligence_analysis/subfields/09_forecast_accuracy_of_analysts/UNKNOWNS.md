## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Equal-token scaffold vs plain comparison on forecasting.** None exists in the literature (K23, GAP 1). We can run it ourselves (U3, U4) but need a frozen, clean, post-cutoff question set with at least 300 resolved items.
- **Superforecaster parity in 2026.** The July 2026 claim is a non-rejection with human data from July 2024 and no readable 2026 Brier values (K22). It needs the fall 2026 human round, or our own paid human baseline.
- **Independent replication of Mandel and Barnes.** None found (K28). It needs a Dutch, UK or Australian forecast archive; we cannot create one.
- **Leakage-free large question set.** We have to build one from live forward-resolving questions (ForecastBench, Metaculus, Prophet Arena) and wait for resolution; this takes weeks to months.
- **Verbal probability forecasts scored against outcomes.** No LLM study exists (K29). We would need a paired set of word and number outputs on the same items; U8 covers the first step.
- **Long-horizon forecasts.** The bench resolves fast. Tetlock-style multi-year items cannot be scored soon, so claims about long horizons stay untested.
- **Unopened sources.** Tetlock 2005, Mellers 2015, Grove 2000, Satopaa 2014 and Baron 2014 (a27, a28, b13, b28-b30) hold the extremizing parameters and effect sizes. Reading them would unblock a better prior for U1.
- **AIA numbers.** All come from one vendor report with garbled tables (critique). An independent run of its stretch and sampling settings would unblock U1 and U2.
- **Real-money or consequence signal.** LLMs match the market Brier but lose money (K31); we have no way to test decision value on our bench.

## Order to run

1. **U3 (plain vs strategy prompts)** at equal tokens. It is cheap, uses no extra infrastructure, and sets the baseline every other arm must beat (G7).
2. **U2 (how many samples, which pooling rule).** Pooling is the best-supported lever (K05, K24) and the answer sets the token budget for every later test.
3. **U1 (fitted log-odds stretch on the pooled answer).** It is a few lines of code, uses outputs from U2, and has the largest measured gain (0.1140 to 0.1076). Run it after U2 so the stretch is fitted on pooled forecasts.
