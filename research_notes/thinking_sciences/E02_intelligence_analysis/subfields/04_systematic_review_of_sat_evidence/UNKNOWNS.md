## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Coulthart (2017) per-technique counts and effect sizes: the PDF is behind a bot wall. Unblock by getting the paper by another route; until then the headline stays a prior only.
- Schwenk 1990 and Mullen 1991 effect sizes: need the papers. Unblock with library access, then run U5.
- Real analyst tasks with ground truth: we have no intelligence-style items with known outcomes beyond synthetic or MuSR-like ones. Unblock by building a set with planted misleading, duplicate and correlated evidence (also needed for U1, U2, U6).
- Weighing vs seeking evidence in humans at proper power: the only data are 21 students. No unblocking from our side.
- Wynn 2025 flip rates: figures only. Unblock by reading the paper's data or rerunning its setup.
- Token-matched accounting for PRISM-style pipelines: the paper gives no token counts. Needs our own replication.
- Effects on frontier models: nearly all LLM debate and sycophancy numbers are from 2023-2025 small or older models. Needs our own runs on the current model (U4, U11, U12).
- Whether LLM-written dependency keys miss partial or causal overlap: needs items with hidden shared causes.
- Pre-mortem, key assumptions check and red-team prompts on analyst tasks: no measured baseline anywhere; U10 is the only cheap probe.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (code-scored matrix vs plain vs prompt-only ACH).** Biggest possible gain and it answers whether ACH has any role at all; the arms reuse our existing judge and SC code.
2. **U3 (disagreement trigger vs always-on).** It decides the cost of every structure we add and reuses U1's items, so it costs little extra.
3. **U2 (source-key dedupe with paraphrases).** Cheap, built on the same items, and the human harm it addresses is established; only the size is unknown.
