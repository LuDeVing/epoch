## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human ACH primaries (Folker 2000, Lehner 2008, Mitre 2004) and the exact means in Nemeth et al. 2001 are not opened; unblocking needs a library copy or author preprint. Until then K25 and the "non-analysts only" claim stay second-hand.
- No pooled effect size for ACH exists. Unblock by extracting per-study effect sizes from the Wilcox and Mandel PDF tables, or by emailing the authors (never done through this agent).
- Whether SAT benefits appear in calibration (Brier) rather than accuracy in humans: needs a human forecasting dataset with and without SAT training; we have none.
- Real intelligence-style tasks with keyed outcomes at scale. Our bench has diagnostic, puzzle and forecast items; analytic tasks with slow ground truth (months) are out of reach, so only proxies can be tested.
- Whether a model-played devil's advocate raises human analyst performance (human-AI group studies such as Chiang 2024 not opened; Lee 2025 is design only). Needs human participants.
- Whether findings from 2023-era models (GPT-3.5, Claude 1.x, Gemini 1.0) hold on current frontier models. U12 tests part of this; the debate and Multi-Persona comparisons need a rerun on the same models too.
- Wynn et al. Table 1 and figures were unreadable in text extraction, so per-dataset debate effects are not recorded; unblock by reading the PDF figures directly.
- Self-consistency baseline numbers (Wang 2022) were not extracted; use our own bench numbers for the equal-token baseline.

## Order to run
1. **U2 and U3 together (factored assumption check, "Wait" frame).** Cheapest, closest to a real SAT, and tied to the best-supported mechanism (blind factored checks), with the flip ratio as a clean metric.
2. **U4 (hold rule under challenge).** Almost free to implement and protects against the measured 15.8 vs 4.6 wrong-flip ratio and sycophancy; any later rival or critic arm needs it.
3. **U1 (rival list with normalised probabilities vs plain vs majority vote).** Fills the largest gap (no direct LLM ACH-style test) and decides whether any rival-listing step stays in the schema.
