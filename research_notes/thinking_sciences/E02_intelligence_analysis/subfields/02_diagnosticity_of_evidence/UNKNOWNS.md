## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Real analytic cases with ground truth.** Almost all human ACH evidence uses hypothetical, two-hypothesis tasks with stated numbers (critique weak spots). Our bench has puzzles with checks, not intelligence cases. Unblock: build a small set of historic cases with documented outcomes, or use the clinical-vignette format of Ling (d07, d09).
- **Frontier reasoning models on belief updating.** The belief-updating studies used open-weight models up to 70B (d11, d12) or small reasoning models. The newest frontier models are untested. Unblock: run U5 on the current backbone and publish the numbers in our own notes.
- **Whether the harness advantage survives strong reasoning modes.** d05 says a one-prompt recipe matches BED-LLM. Unblock: U3 on the current backbone in a reasoning mode.
- **Doherty 1979 percentages and Wolf 1988 effect size.** Paywalled. Unblock: institutional access, or an author request.
- **Nelson 2005 ranking of usefulness measures.** Not opened. Unblock: open the paper before U3 picks a score.
- **Rows versus columns hypothesis-table layout in LLMs.** No study. Unblock: U14.
- **Ling's case-level breakdown.** Category-level figures only exist as images. Unblock: authors' data, or our own replication on a small case set.
- **Real skill vs variance reduction in aggregation.** Needs the random-response control (U13).
- **Equal-token comparison on thinking models for TiO.** The source did not control tokens. Unblock: U2 with a token cap.
- **ACH matrix alone at equal tokens.** Only the trained AgentCDM exists. Unblock: the matrix arm in U1.
- **Failure of multi-agent claims.** AgentCDM's multi-agent gains conflict with G9. Do not test here; G9 stands until our own data say otherwise.
- **Search budget.** The gap-search pass ran into API limits (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, arXiv rate limits). Unblock: re-run searches for LLM ACH, hypothesis-table layout and likelihood-elicitation papers when limits lift.

## Order to run

1. **E02.02-U2 (negative-test line per mode).** Cheapest: one prompt line, existing rule-discovery items, and the answer tells us which modes it is safe in.
2. **E02.02-U1 (planted decisive item, rate-and-drop).** The direct test of S7 and the biggest open gap; no LLM test of the matrix idea at equal tokens exists, and the signal (Ling +38.8, WORTH 0 of 25) says the problem is real.
3. **E02.02-U3 (Split vs EIG vs plain).** Largest published effect sizes, but contested; Split is nearly free and decides whether any heavier machinery is worth building.

U4 (strip the stated hypothesis) is the fourth and costs almost nothing; run it alongside U1 if items are shared.
