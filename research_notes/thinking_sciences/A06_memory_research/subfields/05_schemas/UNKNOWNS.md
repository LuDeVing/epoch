## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Brewer and Treyens (1981) actual numbers** (N, intrusion and recognition rates). Unblock: get the paper through a library; until then no figure may be quoted.
- **Gauld and Stephenson (1967), Wynn and Logie (1998), Bergman and Roediger (1999) numbers.** Unblock: full texts; needed to size the instruction effect on distortion.
- **Newer congruency meta-analysis** (Stangor and McMillan 1992 not found). Unblock: search once the API quota resets.
- **Peer-review status of the 2026 preprints** (b14-b18, b20, d11, d15, a29, c11). Unblock: wait or check later.
- **Internal mechanisms** (schema circuits in transformers, clone-structured graphs, hierarchical active inference). Unblock: access to model internals; prompting cannot test them.
- **Long-delay effects (days to months).** Our agent has no clock delay; only rewrite counts. Unblock: persistent multi-session runs.
- **Whether "abstraction hurts" in Wang et al. is due to seeded errors.** Unblock: their code or our own U4 run.

## Order to run

1. **U4 (wrong rule vs wrong raw case).** The sources conflict directly, and the answer decides whether our lesson store keeps rules or cases.
2. **U1 with U2 (source-first, forced derivation check).** Large reported effects from single sources; cheap to build; same task stream as U4.
3. **U3 (assumed-defaults line).** One extra line in S1 if it works; also gives the counterfactual probe we need for U5 and U6.
