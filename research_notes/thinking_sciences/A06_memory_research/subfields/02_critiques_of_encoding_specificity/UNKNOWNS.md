## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Founding numbers (Tulving and Thomson 1973; Watkins and Tulving 1975; Thomson and Tulving 1970).** Not retrieved (K24). Unblock: a legal copy through a library. Needed only to cite the human anchor, not for the schema.
- **Full text of Nairne 2002 and Goh and Lu 2012.** Abstract or partial text only. Unblock: library access. Effect sizes would let us set tighter thresholds.
- **A preregistered, out-of-lab replication of Poirier 2012 in accuracy.** None found (K23). Unblock: a new human study; outside our scope.
- **Transformer key/value interventions (Dinh 2026).** Need weights and hooks; our agent is black-box (K14). Unblock: an open-weight model run.
- **Why random documents helped RAG in 2024 (attention entropy hypothesis).** Needs attention access on open models. Unblock: an open-weight run with attention logging.
- **Human outshining with accuracy and preregistration.** Isarida (N=80, 40) is recognition only. Unblock: new human study; outside our scope.
- **Guo 2026 human fan data and code.** None linked; arXiv id and date oddity unexplained (K17). Unblock: author release or independent replication.
- **Terranova 2025 and Pink 2026** (arXiv 2510.23730, 2607.22575). Only skimmed. Unblock: read in full and check for direct tests of temporal-context reinstatement.
- **Order effects in recognition-recall independence (K36).** Needs a bench that randomises probe order; low cost, but only informative once U12 runs.

## Order to run

1. **A06.02-U1 (filler control).** One test answers whether any write-time line beats extra text; three vendor and preprint results depend on it and none has the arm.
2. **A06.02-U2 (outshining 2x2).** Same notes as U1 with a degraded-key arm; tells us where to spend the write-time cost, and a null here shrinks the whole idea.
3. **A06.02-U6 (rival-count rekey).** Cue overload is the best-established loss (K07); a cheap rule that flattens it is the likeliest direct gain.

After these: U4 (ratio score) and U7 (likely-query line) reuse the same store; U3 needs a multi-session bench and runs once such a set exists.
