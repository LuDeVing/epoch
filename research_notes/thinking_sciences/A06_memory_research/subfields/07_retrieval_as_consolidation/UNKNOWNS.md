## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Weight-level effects.** PIT, Active Reading, SEAL and the two-speed adapters (c01-c08, d09) need training. Unblock: a small open model we can fine-tune, or accept that these are out of scope for a prompt scaffold.
- **Neural predictions** (hippocampus down, mPFC up, schema speed-up). Not applicable to an LLM. They only matter for deciding how much weight to put on the consolidation story, which is already low (K03, K10).
- **Reconsolidation as an update mechanism.** Contested in humans and needs a mechanism a prompt cannot provide. Only the hazard version (U10) is testable.
- **Tse 2007 and Rowland 2014 effect sizes.** Not read in full. Unblock: an open copy or library access.
- **The 2024 Chinese Advances in Psychological Science review.** Not found. Unblock: a Chinese-language search with a larger budget.
- **Sleep-time compute with changing context.** No data (c25, d16). Needs a benchmark where stored context is edited between query rounds; we would have to build it.
- **Link-on-write drift in real stores.** Needs a long-run store with ground-truth detail probes. Build as part of U4.
- **Any "no LLM study found" claim.** Search was rate limited (d19). Re-run gaps 1, 2 and 4 on arXiv when the limit lifts before assuming no prior work.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (recall-first vs reread):** the direct test of the one robust human effect (d about 0.8 to 1.2) on our own agent, cheap to build, no LLM study exists.
2. **U3 (verbatim + date beside gist):** problem is established by three 2026 papers, fix is cheap in tokens, and it protects the schema's existing S1 rule.
3. **U2 (question-first self-study):** the prompt-level cousin of PIT and Active Reading, which gave 15 to 40 point gains in weight training; reuses the U1 fact set.
