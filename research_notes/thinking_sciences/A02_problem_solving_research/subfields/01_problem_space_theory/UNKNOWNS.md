## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Primary human data.** The 1972 book, Kotovsky, Hayes and Simon 1985, Jeffries 1977 and Kaplan and Simon 1990 were never opened. The 2:1 and 16:1 ratios and move-versus-change timings are second-hand. Unblock: get the full texts.
- **Kotovsky operator-compatibility transfer in LLMs.** No isomorph design exists for our items. Unblock: build paired isomorph item sets (same space, different operator rules).
- **Building a problem space for a new problem.** Flagged in 1966 and 1976, still unsolved. Our S1/S2 is hand-designed. Unblock: a set of new-problem items and a scoring rule for "space is right".
- **Real collapse on River Crossing.** Needs valid instances and a clean count table across sizes for the same model on both designs. Unblock: a generator with a solvability check.
- **Ill-defined problems with no goal test.** No LLM search result exists; our checker-based arms cannot score them. Unblock: a judged item set with human or rubric scoring.
- **Subgoal library helping or hurting (chunking stand-in).** LT's order effect (45 vs 15 minutes) suggests stored results can slow search. Needs a long run of related tasks. Unblock: a sequence of 50+ related items.
- **Which strategy a model used on Hanoi-style tasks.** Needs a strategy classifier on traces. Unblock: hand-label 50 traces, then check an automatic labeller.
- **Goal-stack loss in context windows.** Needs long runs with logged context. Unblock: harness logging (partly covered by U8).
- **Training-based gains (AlphaLLM, Stream of Search).** Out of scope without training; only informs trace formats.
