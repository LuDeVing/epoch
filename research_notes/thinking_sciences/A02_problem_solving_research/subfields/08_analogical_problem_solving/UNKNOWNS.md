## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Real far analogies from a large memory.** We have no bank of solved problems with verified deep analogues. Unblock: build a small bank (50 cases) with planted true analogues and look-alikes, scored by a program.
- **Human systematicity effect sizes** (Clement and Gentner; Markman and Gentner) were not found. Unblock: open those papers; then run the same materials on the model.
- **Why the pre-hint rate differs** (30% vs 1.3-8%). Human-only; no bench test. Matters only for how much weight to give the 1980s numbers.
- **Whether frontier or reasoning models share the Puranam failure.** No published data. Unblock: run T1 on our current model (this is U1 and U12).
- **Whether analogy gains persist when baselines are high.** Published gains come from GPT-3.5 and PaLM-2 era models. Unblock: U1 and U2 on the model we actually use.
- **Training-data leakage in letter-string and story items.** Cannot be ruled out (Hodel and West). Unblock: freshly written items not on the web.
- **Mechanism inside the model** (Opiełka: no stable abstract successor vector). No scaffold test exists; note only.
- **ConceptARC link.** Humans 91%, GPT-4 25-33%. Linking to the ARC portfolio control needs a mapping step on ARC items; defer until U1 and U4 pass.

## Order to run

1. **A02.08-U1** (forced retrieval step vs best-of-N at equal tokens): it is the central question, nobody has measured it, and it decides whether the rest matters.
2. **A02.08-U4** (code check on the mapping after a hint): the one known LLM failure after the hint is over-application (precision about .5), and a code check fits the schema's rule that code selects.
3. **A02.08-U5** (index type for the case store): cheap, offline, and it sets the retrieval quality that U1 depends on.
