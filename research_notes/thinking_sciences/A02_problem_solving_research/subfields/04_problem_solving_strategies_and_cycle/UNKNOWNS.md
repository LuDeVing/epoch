## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Well- versus ill-structured classifier.** No validated test exists. Simon (1973) was not obtained; Jonassen and Funke came as summaries. Unblock: read both, write 5 yes/no features (one correct answer, known rule set, checker exists, information missing, conflicting goals), and check them against sample disagreement as ground truth.
- **Luchins water-jug rigidity in LLMs.** No clean test. Unblock: build a 10-item Luchins set, run long method runs then critical items, compare to unprimed controls.
- **Mapping LLM long-run failures to Dorner's list.** Unblock: label traces from our own sim with the seven Dorner failures and compare against the monitors in U12.
- **Productive failure effect size by subgroup.** Only the abstract was read. Unblock: full text of Sinha and Kapur (2021), in particular the fidelity and age moderators.
- **Strategy choice on stakes-bearing open tasks.** Almost all human evidence is arithmetic, Building Sticks and MicroDYN. Unblock: an open-ended task set with a checker, which our bench does not have.
- **Set-shifting for problem-solving strategies.** The only test is on tool choice (d05). The WCST-style LLM paper was not identified (d23). Unblock: find it and build a strategy-flip item stream (this is U4).
- **Duncker and Luchins originals.** Not opened. Unblock: obtain the monographs and record the protocol and counts.
- **Equal-token comparisons in published routing papers.** None fix tokens. Unblock: only our own bench can do this.
- **Reasoning-model results for routing.** Routing papers use 4B to 8B models or GPT-5 with mixed paradigms (tools confound the paradigm). Unblock: run U1-U3 on our own thinking model.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (oracle gap with repeats).** Cheapest, and it decides whether U2 and any router are worth building. The published gaps are inflated by single lucky runs.
2. **U2 and U3 together (disagreement escalation and cheap-first).** Same item set and same samples; the best-supported route to a gain or a token saving. Reuse the U1 runs.
3. **U4 (success-changed check).** Small stream test, tests the one weakness that appears in both people and agents, and tells us whether the monitor must be structural.
