## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Ohlsson 1992/2011 and Ollinger 2006/2013/2014 and Jones 2003 numbers.** Only abstracts seen. Unblock: get full texts (library access or authors) before citing any number from them (K25).
- **Chunk theory on LLMs.** No benchmark has Roman-numeral tightness labels. Unblock: adapt the MathSticks generator (public) to Roman numerals and tag Types A to D.
- **Whether "active constraint" can be measured independent of difficulty.** The authors stipulate it. Unblock: trace-level labelling by two raters with agreement stats, once U6 traces exist.
- **2025 reasoning-model BRAINTEASER consistency.** Not found. Unblock: run it ourselves in U2.
- **Contamination of classic insight items.** Unblock: use only NazoNazo-style blind items or newly written ones.
- **Frontier models in d'Aliberti-style tests.** Main results use Qwen2.5-1.5B-family GRPO checkpoints. Unblock: logprob access for entropy gating on our models.
- **Human memory of "type naming" (Nasvytis).** The classifier reached only .64; whether naming causes transfer is untested.

## Order to run

1. **U2 (harvest-and-commit):** cheapest, uses existing logs and benchmarks, and the verification-failure ceiling (up to 37% to 62%) is the largest measured gap.
2. **U3 (cue vs plain resample):** one missing control arm decides whether the largest LLM gain (+8.41 pp on math) is real or just a second sample.
3. **U1 with U9 (list-and-relax and the Knoblich order):** the core O4 claim has no LLM test at all, and the same item set also gives U4 and U6 for free.
