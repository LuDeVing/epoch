## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A design-matched human lambda (symmetric, incentivised, same scale). Needs a new experiment with people, or access to the Brown and Yechiam and Zeif data splits.
- Whether the 1992 gains and losses weighting medians (0.61 and 0.69) are correct. Needs the original paper's full text.
- Per-model decisiveness numbers for the description-history gap. Needs the authors' data or a rerun.
- Whether the territorial replication passes peer review. Needs the journal record, if any.
- Independent replication of Tarabeih's meta-analysis and of the prompt contamination result. Needs a different lab.
- A paid, incentivised test of what "right" means for value-laden decisions. Needs an expert-rated item set.
- LLM replication of the 1979 and 2020 batteries. A literature search could not be run because the search budget ran out.

## Order to run
1. **E18.04-U1** (re-frame step, accuracy and flips at equal tokens): the only item that could add an operator step, and the main gap.
