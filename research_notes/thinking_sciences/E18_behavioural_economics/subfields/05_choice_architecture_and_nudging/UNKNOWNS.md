## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **True human default effect size.** The Maier and Jachimowicz corrections point opposite ways (0.04-0.11 vs 0.68 to 0.80 after trim-and-fill). Needs a preregistered large replication; no value for our bench, so we do not wait on it.
- **Scaffolded agents.** No published study tests layout effects on a program-plus-model agent. Our own bench is the only source.
- **Full text of Cherep PNAS 2026.** Only the abstract and the preprint are read. Unblock by reading the PNAS full text.
- **Many Labs primary PDF.** Blocked (HTTP 403). Human framing numbers are second-hand. Unblock with another mirror.
- **Unread: Hagendorff 2023, Horton 2023, Szaszi letter, Johnson and Goldstein original, Save More Tomorrow original, PMC12478545 (decision time).** Read them before citing numbers.
- **Number of order swaps before returns vanish.** Existing designs use one swap; a sweep over 1, 2, 4, n orders on our own items would unblock this.
- **Self-preference combined with order.** Partly measured in d04; needs candidates produced by our own model.
- **Meaning preservation of rewrites.** No external audit exists; U1 builds one.

## Order to run

1. **E18.05-U2 (permute-and-vote vs fixed vs instruction)** because it decides whether the biggest established guard pays at equal tokens on our model, and it is cheap to run.
2. **E18.05-U10 (matched battery on one model)** because it tells which of the other guards matter at all on current models, and it settles the human-versus-LLM sign question (K16).
3. **E18.05-U1 (neutral restatement with equivalence check)** because S1 is already in the schema and its framing remedy has no meaning audit; the test either confirms it or removes a risk.
