## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Primary readings of Tversky and Kahneman 1981 and 1983 and Gigerenzer 1991 (Linda, Tom W., Asian disease). Needs the papers; the numbers we hold are second-hand.
- Whether reasoning-mode effects come from tokens or from training for DeepSeek and Qwen. Needs matched-weight thinking toggles, which only some vendors offer.
- Fresh-item bias rates for the models in our agent. Needs an item generator that avoids the public benchmark and training data.
- Whether bias scores correlate across biases in models (one summary score or many). Needs a wider item set than our bench holds now.
- Self-preference when a model judges its own output. Needs a blind-authorship setup.
- Long multi-turn contexts with irrelevant numbers planted early. Needs a long-context harness; RealICU is the nearest study but not our domain.
- Two representativeness-in-LLM papers (Ryu 2024 and one other, d18) could not be opened.
- Abstract-only items (Yao, Hantel, Mayer and Rebholz, Nawaz): need the full text before any number is used.

## Order to run
1. **E18.03-U1** (code quarantine vs CoT vs "ignore" for anchoring): it tests the S1 rule directly, costs no extra model calls for the code arm, and has the cleanest metric.
2. **E18.03-U3** (thinking on vs off): evidence conflicts (Huang vs Kraishan vs Yao) and the answer changes how G11 treats judgement items; one toggle on the same model is cheap.
3. **E18.03-U2** (self-rewrite at token-matched cost): it is the best-supported fix in the literature and the only real alternative to U1, so the pair decides which S1 design to build.
