## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- True size of the structure-nudge effect in human field data after bias correction. Missing: preregistered large trials. Unblock: search for new megastudies and registered reports (gaps 6 and 9 were not searched because of rate limits).
- Why LLMs over-follow cues (sycophancy, training data, position priors). Missing: a causal test with base vs tuned versions of one model. Unblock: access to a base and instruct pair.
- Whether Halimeh's and Montero-Porras's 2026 results are reproduced by others. Missing: independent groups. Unblock: wait for replications; re-search arXiv.
- Effects of guidance on real task success in deployed agents. Missing: public accuracy data from Wink-style systems. Unblock: our own agent-loop items (U7).
- Social or authority cue pull under prompt-level reflection. Missing: any study. Unblock: U4.
- Persistence over repeated interaction (the LLM analogue of the gym 8% result). Missing: multi-turn designs. Unblock: a multi-turn bench with a planted cue early in the thread.
- Cross-cultural or language transfer of scaffold effects (Maksimenko decoys failed outside the US). Missing: non-English items on our bench.

## Order to run
1. U3 (susceptibility probe): cheapest, only needs planted-cue items, and sets the baseline for U4 and U12.
2. U2 (explore/fresh shrink protocol): costs nothing but a split, and it protects every other test from the winner's curse.
3. U1 (structure vs advice at equal tokens): the largest expected gain, and nobody has run it for LLMs.
