## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Risk versus uncertainty (K20).** Artinger 2022 is abstract-only and no LLM task was found. Unblocked by a task family where payoffs are known (risk) versus unknown (uncertainty), plus library access to the conditions list.
- **Stop-signal calibration under correlated samples (gap 9).** Needs many same-model samples with known correctness and a calibration (ECE) measure. Partly testable in U13; the full version needs logprobs we may not get from a hosted model.
- **Hidden-state probes and DeepConf-style confidence.** Need open weights or token probabilities. Unblock with an open-weight model arm.
- **Simon 1955/56 and Todd and Miller 1999 exact numbers.** Never opened (403, paywall). Needs library access. Does not block any test above.
- **Choice-overload size (Chernev 2015).** No numeric d obtained. Unblock with full text; does not block U4.
- **Position bias of 2025-26 frontier models.** No opened source. U5 measures our own model directly.
- **Planning tasks.** No stop-rule data beyond math, science QA and code. Needs a planning bench item type.
- **Satisficing alignment with human judges.** Needs human raters and a best-of-N arm; we have neither.
- **Maximizer traits and agent policy.** The scales do not map to an agent policy. Nothing to test.
- **Whether GPT-4-class rationality (CCEI .997-.999) holds for our model.** Cheap to check with a budget-allocation probe; low value for the schema.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (first-pass stop vs fixed passes at equal tokens).** It decides whether the biggest published saving survives an equal-token comparison; every other stop test builds on its harness.
2. **U2 (verifier before stop on hard bin).** Shares U1's items and costs one extra arm; it measures the cost of a wrong stop, the main risk of U1.
3. **U3 (pilot-agreement S0 gate vs prompt-only gate).** The S0 gate is the entry point for all effort allocation; both arms reuse U1's items. U5 (order bias) can run alongside because it needs no new items.
