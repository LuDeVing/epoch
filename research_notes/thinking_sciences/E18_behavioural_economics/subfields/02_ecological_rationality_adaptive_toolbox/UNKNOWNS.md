## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Whether a prompt can actually make a model follow take-the-best order. We lack a measure of compliance (did it stop at the first decisive check?). Unblock: log which checks were written and read before the answer.
- Recognition inside the model. Popularity and known-entity probes exist (d09, d10), but they need weights or token probabilities we may not have for our frontier model. Unblock: use popularity proxies and the model's own "do you know this" answer, and validate against accuracy.
- Frontier-price cascade savings. We need 2026 price lists and our own model tiers. Unblock: price log from our harness.
- Dependent-cue environments (Martignon and Hoffrage 2002, unopened). Unblock: open the paper and build a test with correlated cues.
- DeMiguel 1/N and Dana and Dawes 2004 are abstract-level. Unblock: open full texts.
- Whether results on paired-comparison items carry to our ARC and BrainTeaser items. Unblock: pick which bench items have a cue-based form.
- The toolbox selection mechanism is only partly specified (a11). Unblock: our own class-record data on which mode wins per class.

## Order to run

1. E18.02-U1 (TTB prompt vs weighted-sum vs plain): the one direct gap in the literature, cheap to run, and it decides whether direct mode should be an ordered stop-early routine.
2. E18.02-U3 (cheap class router): reuses logs we already have, and the break-even is unknown; every other saving depends on it.
3. E18.02-U2 (record size crossover): tells us when the class record is worth trusting, and costs only replays of existing runs.
