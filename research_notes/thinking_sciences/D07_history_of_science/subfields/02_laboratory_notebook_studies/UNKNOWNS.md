## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Step counts and operator proportions in Krebs's notebooks. Needs Holmes 1980, 1991, 1993 and Grasshoff and May 2003 (paywalled). Unblock: library or interlibrary access, then extract the step sequence.
- KEKADA match rate without user help. None reported. Unblock: recount the scenario traces in Kulkarni and Simon (1988, full text already held) against Holmes's segments.
- Whether Dunbar's "about half unexpected" is true, and how labs responded. Unblock: obtain Dunbar 1997 and Dunbar and Fugelsang 2005.
- Gruber's per-topic Darwin counts and the critical readings (Kohn, Schweber, Ospovat). Unblock: obtain Darwin on Man and the critics.
- Any LLM replay of a historical notebook scored against the record. Unblock: build one ourselves from Kulkarni and Simon's dated segments (Krebs is small enough), or complete a proper literature search once search quotas return.
- Inter-rater agreement between reconstructions of one notebook. Unblock: Nickelsen and Grasshoff 2009 text, or our own U7.
- Whether the Safdar saturation and anchoring pattern repeats across agents and runs. Unblock: a long-horizon run with at least 3 seeds per arm; too costly for the nano bench now.
- Whether the human Krebs pattern (3.5 months routine, one large miss, 5 months of explanation) says anything about agent time profiles. Unblock: log timing in U1 and compare the shape of steps-to-anomaly vs steps-to-explanation.
