## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A true-and-nontrivial check for conjectures. Today we only know that a claim was "not killed". Need an independent oracle (small exact solver, Lean on a few classes, or expert labels). Unblock: pick 2 domains with brute-force truth on small cases.
- Primary Schoenfeld 1985 effect sizes. Need the book or a full-text review of his teaching experiments.
- Lakatos 1976 and Polya primary texts were not opened. Need the books for exact move definitions.
- Pease/Colton HRL, Lenat AM, Graffiti benchmark numbers. Need the original theses and papers.
- Georgiev et al. matched / improved / failed counts for the 67 problems.
- 2026 frontier-model results on novel proofs with and without a scaffold at equal tokens. QED and First Proof Sprint have no no-scaffold arm; running them ourselves is too expensive (USD 100-600 per case).
- Skeptic-gain replication in a second domain and with an LLM.
- Multiplayer effects (Polymath style). Needs the multi-user build.

## Order to run
1. **U3 (hold-out checker):** cheapest, and checker-gaming is already seen in two independent cases. Affects every code-checked loop.
2. **U2 (funnel at equal tokens):** the funnel is the core of conjecture work and the stage has clear metrics.
3. **U1 (typed vs free counterexample reaction):** the only direct Lakatos test, no prior study; run on the same seeded items as U6 to share setup.
