## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Operator sequences from real discovery.** We have one first-hand sequence (KEKADA, built from Holmes) and none from Faraday or Darwin. Unblock: open Holmes (1980/1991), Gruber (1974) and Nickles (1980), and code 3 to 5 dated traces by hand.
- **Rate of anomaly detection by LLMs.** Only one anecdotal trace. Unblock: build a planted-anomaly set (this also feeds U1) and measure the rate.
- **Measured gap between a rational reconstruction and the real trace** (Hanson against Lugg). Nothing is measured. Unblock: two coders code the same notebook and a published reconstruction, then compare step lists.
- **LLM-SRBench Figure 4 gap in points.** The values are in the plot. Unblock: the authors' data or an image read.
- **Independent replication of Co-Scientist's cf-PICI result and a leak check.** Search was not available. Unblock: rerun the literature search when quota returns.
- **Human against LLM grader like for like on DiscoveryWorld knowledge.** Needs the same rubric on both. Unblock: grade a sample of agent notes by hand on the 0 / 0.5 / 1 scale.
- **Biology and wet-lab discovery.** Our bench is equation and data tasks. Unblock: a simulated lab with delayed and noisy results, or a partner with real data.
- **Whether mined operators survive rediscovery of non-textbook cases.** Needs post-cutoff or counterfactual cases at scale. Unblock: a set of 2026 papers with known findings.
- **The Faraday 16,041 count and the Krebs chronology from primary text.** Unblock: open the Tweney volume and Holmes.

## Order to run

1. **U13 (memory share and blind arm).** Cheapest, and every later result depends on it: without it any gain on historical cases may be recall.
2. **U4 (independent runs against one long run, equal tokens).** The most contested claim with the largest possible payoff; it decides how we spend the budget in S3 and tests the one preprint's result under a token match.
3. **U1 (expectation range and surprise trigger).** The only place the case studies give a concrete, codable mechanism, and it fills the missing anomaly-detection rate; run U3 on the same tasks at low extra cost.
