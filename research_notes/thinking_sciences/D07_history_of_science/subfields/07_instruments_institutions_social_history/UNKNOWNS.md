## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **What Shapin and Schaffer and Collins really claim about replication failure.** Needs the books or a library copy. Without it K01, K02, K03 stay single-source. Unblock: obtain the books, read the Huygens and TEA laser chapters.
- **Whether LLM discussion collapses diversity as AgentReview reports (-27.2%).** The only number is from GPT-4 agent simulation. Unblock: U2 gives our own number.
- **Human reviewer behaviour.** We have no human panel. Review noise numbers (25% disagreement, r=0.55) come from one 2014 conference. Unblock: a small human rating panel on our items.
- **Whether novelty judgement correlates with later usefulness.** Si 2025 has N=43 and one field. Unblock: a long-run study of executed open-mode ideas.
- **Whether the disruption decline is real.** Index is contested and the Holst and Park papers were not opened in full. Low value for the schema; leave alone.
- **Independent replication of the AI Scientist-v2 workshop result.** None found. Low value for the schema.
- **Effect of the Thakkar feedback agent on correctness.** The RCT measured engagement and length. No ground truth exists.
- **Real autonomous lab results (Adam, Eve, Coscientist).** No numbers verified, and no instruments in our loop.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (executed test vs judge vs vote).** Biggest expected gain and uses data we already produce; it decides whether the pump-versus-committee analogy holds for our agent.
2. **U3 (judge samples needed for 70% agreement).** Cheap, label-only, and sets the rule for every later judge-based test.
3. **U4 (prestige and source blinding on our judge).** Cheap paired test; the answer tells us whether the blind step in G5 is doing real work.
