## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Real historical cases as items.** We have no vetted set of historical episodes with expert answers on what each shows. Unblock: have two historians or philosophers build and score about 50 items (also needed for U11).
- **Rediscovery from period-only text.** No time-locked model is strong enough (TypewriterLM ARC-E 43.3 vs Qwen3-4B 77.4; GPT-1900 mostly failed). Unblock: a stronger period model with a scored rediscovery task.
- **VPI effect sizes.** Laudan et al. 1986 and Donovan, Laudan, Laudan 1988 were never opened. Unblock: read them and extract effect sizes and coder agreement.
- **Planck replications outside biomedicine.** None found. Unblock: a targeted literature search in physics and social science.
- **Pitt, Hull 1978 and Sauer and Scholl 2016 in full.** Only secondary quotes. Unblock: obtain the texts.
- **Token-matched case-memory studies in the literature.** None found; only our own test (U5) can answer.
- **Pre-cutoff vs post-cutoff effects.** FIRE-Bench gave noisy, small-n results. Unblock: a larger, difficulty-stratified split.

## Order to run
1. **D07.08-U1 (tag-and-flag).** Cheapest, highest unknown, no study exists, and it is the core HPS rule.
2. **D07.08-U5 (case bank vs repeat-tries).** Memory is costly to add; the equal-attempt baseline decides whether it earns a place under G7.
3. **D07.08-U2 (triggered refute step).** Best direct evidence of a failure mode (confirmation bias) and it needs a model-type gate, so measure before adding.
