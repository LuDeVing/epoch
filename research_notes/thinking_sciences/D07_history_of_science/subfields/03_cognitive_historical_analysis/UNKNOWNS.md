## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Which operator precedes real breakthroughs, and when (start or impasse).** This is the SUBFIELDS question for S6. It needs counts over many notebook-documented discoveries; we have 3-5 hand-picked cases. Unblock: code a set of 20+ cases from public notebook archives with two coders and kappa.
- **Whether Maxwell and Kepler readings are historically right.** No independent replication or historian critique was found (search was rate limited). Unblock: read Siegel, Darrigol, Chalmers on Maxwell and Lugg on Hanson/Kepler.
- **Dunbar's primary in-vivo numbers.** Only second-hand. Unblock: obtain the original papers (lab site is gone).
- **Pre-1900 or pre-event training tests of true rediscovery.** Needs training a model on date-filtered text (c05 gave only glimpses). Not possible for a scaffold.
- **Human-judged novelty and feasibility of analogy-sourced ideas.** Needs blind expert reviewers and execution time (d12 used 43 experts at about 103 hours each).
- **Token break-even of simulation and of staged operators.** No source measured it; our own logging will answer once U1 and U7 run.
- **Reasoning-model results on ARN far cells and counterfactual letter strings.** Not in anything we opened; first rows of U2 and U3 will fill this.
- **Human sample sizes in Lewis and Mitchell (46 vs 136) and ARN table rows.** Minor; resolve by re-reading the appendix.
