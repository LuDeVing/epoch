## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Founding numbers of Davidson and Sternberg 1984 and Davidson 1995 (N, effect sizes, training gains). Needs the paywalled full text or a library copy.
- Chuderski and Jastrzebski 2018, Weisberg 2015, Gilhooly and Murphy 2005 full results. Needs full text; settles special-process versus business-as-usual, which has little bearing on the schema.
- Outside replications of Macchi 2025 (verbalisation) and Grimmer 2022 (false insight). Needs other labs; no bench substitute.
- Whether insight in LLMs depends on a distinct process at all. Needs interpretability access to activations, not available on API models.
- An uncontaminated lateral set with human baselines. Needs us to write and pilot items after the model cutoff, plus a human sample.
- Whether the three-process account itself (as opposed to its proxies) predicts any LLM behaviour. No study exists; U1 is the first direct test.

## Order to run
1. A12.07-U12 (fresh lateral set). Cheap, and every other test depends on a bench that is not contaminated.
2. A12.07-U2 (candidate retention). Targets a measured failure (up to 39% of wrong answers contained the correct candidate) and is cheap in code.
3. A12.07-U1 (three-step framing against direct and plain CoT). The only direct test of the account; existing proxies say long step lists can hurt, so it must be short and paired with U3 on the same items if budget allows.
