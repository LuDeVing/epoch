## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Whether real insight is often gradual. No number exists; the Klein and Jarosz 2011 full text and Weisberg's case work were not read. Unblock by reading them and coding the share of gradual cases.
- Human item-level insight rates (Aha dimensions, accuracy, RT) across CRA, matchsticks and riddles. Needs a small human panel (about 30 people) or a published norm set.
- Whether NazoNazo results replicate in other languages or labs, and whether the reasoning-model gap is length or verification. Needs an independent replication or our own run in a second language.
- Model self-reports of Aha and their link to speed or correctness. No study found; unblock with U9 and U10.
- Whether Aha markers carry signal in closed models once entropy is controlled. Needs token-level logprobs, which closed APIs may not give.
- The full Ash, Cushen and Wiley (2009) method obstacles. PDF blocked; get it by another route or by request.
- Hidden-state probes as the non-report marker of restructuring. Needs open-weight models and access to activations.

## Order to run
1. **U12 (contamination screen):** cheapest, and every other test depends on items that are not already in training data.
2. **U1 (gated vs always vs never vs cue-free resample):** biggest possible schema change at S6/S5, and the only source of the claimed +8.41 pp has no control.
3. **U2 (harvest every candidate in the trace and test it):** cheap, code-only, and aimed at the largest measured model failure on insight riddles (5% to 39% verification failure).
