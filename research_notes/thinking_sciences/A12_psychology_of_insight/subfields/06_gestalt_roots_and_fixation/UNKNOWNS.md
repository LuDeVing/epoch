## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Primary human numbers (Duncker 1945, Glucksberg 1962, Adamson 1952, Frank and Ramscar 2003, Luchins 1942, Weisberg and Alba 1981).** Missing: access to the papers (paywalled; DTIC returned 403). Unblock: library access or open-access scans. Effect: only decides how large human effects are, so does not change the bench plan.
- **A human control on our own fresh isomorphs.** Missing: a human sample. Unblock: a small Prolific batch, or use published solve rates for the classic items only.
- **Physical grounding in MacGyver** (is the gap fixation or missing world knowledge?). Missing: a tool-use sim or image input in the harness. Unblock: add a grounded subset with checked feasibility.
- **Reasoning-effort sweeps on closed models.** Missing: equal-token control when thinking tokens are hidden. Unblock: count billed tokens; use effort level as the matched variable.
- **MedEinst numbers.** The paper contradicts itself (text vs Table 1). Unblock: authors' released data or a corrected version. Use trap rates only until then.
- **Contamination.** Classic candle, nine-dot, water-jar items are in training data. Unblock: fresh isomorphs and a rename control (DEEP.md section 8).
- **Vision fixation (CREATE).** Missing: image input path and checked model names. Unblock: test only if the agent uses images.
- **Large-model replication of "aha" shifts** (Illusion of Insight beyond small Qwen/Llama). Unblock: access to traces of frontier reasoning models.

## Order to run
1. **U5 with U12 (decoy-present vs decoy-clean pairs, effort sweep).** Cheapest, answers whether fixation-type failure still exists in current models; if the gap is gone, most of this subfield's candidates (U1, U2, U6) drop in priority.
2. **U1 with U2 (name-only vs list-only vs list-then-try, with generic reconsider as control).** This is the largest hole in the evidence (K33) and tests the S1/O6 claim directly at equal tokens.
3. **U3 (gated vs always-on O6).** Reuses the items and logs from 1 and 2, and decides whether O6 can be added without breaking G1 and G7.
