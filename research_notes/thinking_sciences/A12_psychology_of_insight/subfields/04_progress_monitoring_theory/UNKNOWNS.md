## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human replication of Chronicle 2004 move statistics (N=19).** No independent lab found (d07). Unblock: find or run a replication; until then do not calibrate to human rates (K25).
- **PRODIGI itself.** Only a chapter by the same group was read; the manuscript is unpublished (d01, d02). Unblock: obtain the manuscript or wait for publication.
- **MacGregor et al. 2001 full text.** Never opened; lookahead mix is second-hand (a01, b22). Unblock: library access to the full paper.
- **Hidden-state probes (AUC 0.84 at 4 tokens) and logit penalties.** Need open weights or log-probs (d09, d10, c14). Unblock: an open-weight run of the scaffold.
- **Open-ended tasks without a distance measure** (research, writing). CSP cannot be computed; Companion-type monitors gave no usable result (d12). Unblock: a task family with external progress markers (d17).
- **Frontier-model behaviour for spontaneous revision.** Studies cover small and open models on math (c11, d11). Unblock: run B12-style test on frontier models with token logs.
- **Whether the matchstick result transfers to prose tasks.** Jones is spatial, Ollinger 2006 is symbolic (d03, d04). Unblock: a verbal puzzle set with a known representation obstacle and a distance variable.
- **Goal inference from abstract goals in LLMs, and lookahead budgets against greedy traps.** No studies exist (d18). Unblock: build the item sets in U4 and U8.
- **Standard Rush Hour is too easy.** Frontier models solve 6x6 at about 92% (d13). Unblock: harder boards with solver-confirmed traps.
- **Stuck-detector isolation.** Published gains are bundled with planning, memory or reflection (c19, c20). Unblock: ablation runs on our own agent in U1 and U7.
- **Culture, expertise and task-type moderators.** None found in human studies (b30). Unblock: not testable on our bench; skip.

## Order to run
1. **A12.04-U5 (computed vs reported progress).** Cheapest: needs only logging and AUROC on traces we already produce, and it decides whether any progress variable is worth building.
2. **A12.04-U4 (do models take the greedy trap).** Builds the trap item sets that U1, U2, U3 and U11 all need, and tells us whether the whole CSP frame applies to LLMs.
3. **A12.04-U1 (criterion trigger vs counter vs entropy).** The main design decision for S6; run it on the U4 items right after they exist, with false-positive logging, then run U2 and U3 on the same set.
