## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Frontier edge error rate for text graphs.** Needs items with a known ground-truth graph and an edge-by-edge scorer. Unblock by building a graph-from-text generator with an answer key.
- **Reasoning-model rerun of CogEval.** The original prompts and graphs must be rebuilt from the paper; the 2023 code or data may not be usable. Unblock by reconstructing the six graph families and the task list.
- **Image-built maps.** Our bench is text. Unblock only if image tasks are added (Zhu 2026 shows frontier success under 6%).
- **Human grid-like code in abstract space.** Brain imaging; not testable by us. Do not wait for it.
- **Latent learning in an agent.** Needs a multi-episode task with free exploration steps; our bench is mostly one-shot.
- **Is a gain from GAVEL-style verification due to the map or the verifier?** Needs a symbolic world with action definitions; our items would need formal pre/post conditions.
- **Slow consolidation of structure.** Needs cross-session memory that persists a map; unblock when memory is built.

## Order to run
1. **U2 (move legality check).** Cheapest and the strongest prior (38.6 to 99.4 on a frontier model); also reveals the invalid-move rate used in U1, U3.
2. **U1 (written map vs plain CoT at equal tokens).** The central open gap; decides whether S2 map-writing exists at all for a strong model.
3. **U3 (edge check).** Cheap, reuses U1 items, and tells whether the written map can be trusted before U4 and U5 build on it.
