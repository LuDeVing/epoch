## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human-versus-agent comparison of pruning on the original Huys maze.** We have no human data of our own. Unblock: run the text maze only on the agent (U1) and compare to published numbers (78% vs 37%, d=2.06; 52% of errors are pruning).
- **Which account explains pruning: Pavlovian reflex, value-of-information, or pessimism about later choices?** Needs new human data with modified trees (Zorowitz). Unblock: a human lab; not in our scope. Our agent can only show whether the guard in U1 helps.
- **Independent-lab, different-task replication of aversive pruning.** None exists. Unblock: someone else's study; we can only read new papers.
- **Journal versions of Callaway 2022 and van Opheusden 2023, and the supplement on fixed-beam variants.** Unblock: institutional access or the full supplement.
- **Large closed models and non-math tasks for SSDP/UAA-style merges.** Claims rest on four open models up to 8B. Unblock: we run U9 and U14 ourselves.
- **Per-path loss rate of model-judged pruning by task and judge size.** Needs our own seeded items with known true paths (part of U2 and U3).
- **Exact numbers in Feng, Stechly, ProcessBench and CoT2-Meta tables.** Extraction was garbled. Unblock: re-read the PDF tables before quoting them outside this folder.
- **Whether CoT2-Meta's 77.1 vs 74.6 matched-token ablation holds.** Single preprint with very high headline numbers. Unblock: an independent replication or our own U5.
- **Whether the human medium-difficulty peak carries to ARC-style items.** Needs difficulty labels for our bench items (plain pass rate per class).
- **Sub-plan caching in LLMs and the depth where internal search collapses once format and token limits are fixed.** Partly in U6 and U12. Full answer needs matched answer formats across models.
- **Whether LLM written lookahead is real search or decoration (Chen).** Needs causal ablation on more than one game and without a judge model for tree extraction.
- **What leaf values suit a new problem class with no history.** Cached values need prior runs. Unblock: class records accumulate over bench batches (memory section 5).

## Order to run

1. **U2 (code prune vs judge prune, with loss rate) plus U3 (revisit list).** Cheapest, most established direction, and it also measures the unknown judge loss rate that U1, U9 and U14 depend on.
2. **U5 (checker vs width, matched tokens).** Sets the default width and tells us how much of S6 is worth its tokens (G7). Missing from the literature.
3. **U1 (affective-pruning guard on seeded early-failure items).** Large human effect, a cheap guard, and no LLM study exists. A positive or a clean null both settle the question.
