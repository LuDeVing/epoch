## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Independent replication of human findings.** No outside lab confirmed the reward > transition asymmetry (n=58, n=88) or the fMRI replay-replanning correlation (n=24); the one outside study (Zhang 2026, n=119+119) found no rest benefit (p=.13). Unblock: wait for or commission larger preregistered human studies. Cannot be run on a bench.
- **Is replay for planning or for memory maintenance?** Gillespie 2021 vs planning accounts. Needs rodent or human recording; does not bear on a text agent.
- **Surprise-driven replay for LLM memory.** SuRe (d19) works for fine-tuning, not read. Needs a training-free version and a bench with repeated families large enough to measure it.
- **Independent replication of sleep-time, AWM, Dyna-Think, DreamGym.** None found. Unblock: rerun on our own benchmarks (U1, U6); until then treat as single-source.
- **Explicit stale-subgoal detection in published LLM work.** Nothing found beyond indirect train-test-gap results (d11). Our U2 is the first test.
- **Training-based variants** (Dyna-GRPO, DreamGym). Not testable without weight updates. Unblock: only if the project ever adds training.
- **Sutton 1990/1991 primary text.** Image-only PDF; needs OCR to verify the early episode counts. Low decision value.
- **Hidden-state replay.** SR-Dyna cannot handle hidden state (b03). Our items have partial information. Unblock: a theory paper, not a bench test.
- **Search coverage.** The gap pass hit rate limits; absence of a study means "not found", not "does not exist". Rerun the search for matched-compute replay studies before trusting the U1 gap claim.

## Order to run

1. **B05.03-U1 (replay pass vs fresh attempt, equal total tokens).** It decides whether any offline pass belongs in the schema at all; no study has tested it, and every other replay idea depends on it.
2. **B05.03-U10 (write gate on multi-step items).** Cheapest test, and it checks the one default we are already adding (K22) on our own item type before more is built on it.
3. **B05.03-U5 (break-even simulator hit rate for lookahead).** It sets when self-simulation is safe to use, and the answer feeds the gate for S2 and U9; the fixed-model sweep is cheap with a noisy oracle.
