# Hypotheses in debugging: what we don't know and should test
*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: both arms at equal total tokens (setup and ledger overhead counted), at least 2 seeds, results split by item difficulty (plain pass rate bands). "Gold ceiling" means injecting the true cause after S3 to see the maximum a hypothesis method can add.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human replication of Alaboudi.** Need professionals, real bugs, N above 60 per arm, and a wrong-only arm. We cannot run human studies. What would unblock it: a collaborator, or relying on the LLM replay (U1) only.
- **Founding classics.** Gould, Vessey, Katz and Anderson, Gilmore were read at abstract level only. Vessey's PDF has no text layer. Unblock: OCR a scan, or a library copy. Matters because the "novices hold early hypotheses" claim rests on it.
- **Frontier-model anchoring.** Verma, DDI used 0.5B to 16B models. Unblock: run U6 on our main model and a small one side by side.
- **Real SWE-bench-style tasks.** Our bench is ARC and BrainTeaser type items. No repo, no debugger. Unblock: a small seeded-bug suite with known causes (also needed for U1 to U4).
- **Self-written versus user-supplied wrong hypotheses.** XYEval used user hints. Unblock: record the agent's own wrong first hypothesis in ledger runs and compare stickiness with a planted one.
- **Why Verma and Can disagree** on whether feedback content matters. Unblock: U6 with the facts arm on both easy and "dead" tasks.
- **Whether a model recognises a true cause when shown it.** Humans clicked the faulty line and did not stop. Unblock: show the true cause as one of 5 candidates and see if the agent selects it by probe.
- **Whyline replication.** Needs a trace tool. Unblock: build one for U13.
- **Cost accounting for loops.** AutoSD about 5x time, SWE-Doctor 1.9x cost. Unblock: log tokens per ledger step from the first run.
- **Whether the hypothesis idea carries to non-runnable puzzles.** For BrainTeaser items there is no probe except the stated clues. Unblock: define the probe as "does this reading satisfy every clue" and test it as the ledger check.

## Order to run

1. **U1 plus U2 (one item set, supplied hints).** They share items, answer the central gap (no LLM replay exists), and tell us whether to build a hypothesis-supply operator and a test-the-hint guardrail at all. Cheapest and highest information.
2. **U3 (loop vs best-of-N at equal tokens), with the gold-ceiling arm from U4.** If the loop does not win on the mid band, most of the rest of the ledger work is not worth the tokens. The ceiling shows whether diagnosis is even our bottleneck.
3. **U6 (restart with rejected list vs full history) together with U14 (k failures trigger).** Both use the same "first two attempts failed" items and decide the S6 behaviour: what to carry forward, and when to change method.
