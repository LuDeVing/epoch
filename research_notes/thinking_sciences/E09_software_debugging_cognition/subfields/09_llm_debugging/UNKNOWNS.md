*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: same base model, equal total tokens per arm (count feedback, test generation and setup), held-out hidden check, report broke-vs-fixed counts for any revising arm. With about 100 items, gaps under 5 points are noise.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Repo-scale bugs.** Most of the SWE-agent findings (failed edits, 25-round cliff, branch point) come from multi-file repositories. Our bench items are puzzle-sized. Unblock: build or import a small set of repo-style items with hidden tests, post-cutoff.
- **Matched-token tests of loop designs on SWE-bench-like tasks.** No published test exists. Unblock: our own harness, once repo-style items exist (U1, U6, U14).
- **Whether self-debug gain depends on base-model strength.** Needs the same loop on at least two model sizes and a reasoning model. Unblock: access to a second model tier in the harness.
- **Stronger monitor than actor.** The monitor result needs a second, stronger model. Unblock: a budget line for a second model.
- **Wrong design vs slip labels.** Labels in the literature are LLM-assigned (87% agreement with authors, a26). Unblock: a human-labelled sample of 100 failures from our own logs.
- **Human-feedback ceiling (Olausson 52.6% vs 33.3%).** We have no human in the loop. Unblock: use a stronger-model diagnosis as a stand-in, and treat it as a ceiling only.
- **Test-suite quality for hidden checks.** Weak tests inflate scores (31.08%, 29.6%). Unblock: build adversarially strengthened tests (differential testing against a reference).

## Order to run

1. **U8 keep-best checkpoint.** It costs zero tokens and only needs logs we already plan to keep; if the last draft is worse than an earlier one in 5%+ of runs, adopt it at once.
2. **U1 repair vs fresh draw at equal tokens.** It decides how much of the budget S5 deserves against S3, and the literature (Chen vs Olausson) cannot answer it for a reasoning model.
3. **U4 selector comparison.** It settles whether code selects with differential real-output tests or with votes, and it tests the strongest single gain found (Agentless +19 patches) while guarding against the Reflexion false-pass failure.
