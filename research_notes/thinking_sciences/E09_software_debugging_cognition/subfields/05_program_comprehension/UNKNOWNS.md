## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Soloway and Ehrlich (1984) and Pennington (1987) primary figures: papers not opened. Unblock by obtaining the PDFs; until then no classic number is used.
- ICSE 2025 student-attention papers (n=27): only a snippet. Unblock by reading the journal-first paper.
- Large-repo, long-horizon comprehension (above SWE-bench): our bench has no such repo set. Unblock by building a 500k-line item set with injected bugs.
- Human think-aloud comparison with agent traces: needs human participants or a public think-aloud dataset.
- Whether attention-training gains (EyeLayer, EyeMulator) have a prompt-only counterpart: unknown; needs a prompt that lists the lines a human reader would fixate.
- Whether frontier near-ceiling scores on LiveCodeBench reflect contamination (d01, d02): needs fresh, post-cutoff items.
## Order to run
1. E09.05-U3 (attribution and refutation in verify): cheap, uses existing patches, and the blind-spot evidence is the strongest in this folder.
2. E09.05-U2 (hypothesis-first vs read-first): the central open question of the subfield and no direct test exists; result sets the whole reading strategy.
3. E09.05-U1 (name-blind restatement): cheap to build from rename mutations; the loss is established and the fix is untested.
