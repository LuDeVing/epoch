## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Clinical medical items.** Our bench is ARC and BRAINTEASER style. Whether findings hold on real diagnostic items (MedR-Bench, SDBench) needs those sets and an LLM-patient simulator. Unblock: obtain SDBench or MedR-Bench access, fix the simulator and turn cap.
- **Calibrated outcome models for EIG.** LLM-simulated outcomes are uncalibrated. Unblock: a benchmark with ground-truth outcome tables for each test.
- **Original numbers** from Eddy 1982, Gigerenzer and Hoffrage 1995, Fagan 1975, Kassirer 1989, and the numeric thresholds in Pauker-Kassirer and Sox. Unblock: library access to full texts. They do not block any test above.
- **Spectrum effect on decisions.** Only a TB model exists. Unblock: a controlled set of items with stratified vs pooled LRs.
- **Human-AI combination.** Rodman suggests collective intelligence; K18 shows no gain for physicians given GPT-4. Out of scope for a single agent.
- **Real-world prevalence for non-medical tasks.** Priors for puzzle tasks come from the S0 pass rate or the count of live rules. No evidence on whether those priors are calibrated. Unblock: log per-class pass rates (S9) and compare to realised accuracy.
- **Whether LR dispersion in d06 is memorisation** of TheNNT. Unblock: test on LRs not in public sources.

## Order to run

1. **U1 elicit-then-compute vs one-shot posterior.** Cheapest, closest to G5, and the largest open gap; the result decides whether A1 becomes a hard rule.
2. **U3 EIG vs halving vs model-chosen test with run outcomes.** Decides the S7 test chooser and whether the extra machinery pays for itself.
3. **U2 threshold gate on S7.** Tests the central claim of the subfield (do not test when the result cannot change the action) and cuts tokens directly (G7).
