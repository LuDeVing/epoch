*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

Common test rules for every row: arms at equal total tokens; a CoT-SC arm gets the freed tokens as extra first passes; at least 3 seeds; paired items; report fixes, breaks, churn and tokens; "Add if" always also requires the break-to-fix ratio below 1 to 3 and a gain above two standard errors across seeds.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Real practice or patient outcomes**: no trial exists (K30); our bench has no clinic. Unblock: only a partner site; until then the schema carries the limit statement.
- **Real diagnosis with gathered evidence**: our bench has vignettes and puzzles, not interactive patient data. Unblock: build a staged-reveal set (VivaBench-style) from a licensed case source.
- **Items not seen in training**: most medical vignettes are likely in model training data (b34, c14). Unblock: new items written after the model cutoff, or items perturbed by code.
- **Independent replication of the delayed-learning effect** (K10, K28): needs a human lab outside the Rotterdam/Brazil network; not something we can run.
- **Mechanism (knowledge activation vs reprocessing)**: no direct test even in humans (a26). For the agent, an ablation that removes the retrieved contrast knowledge would separate them; it needs a retrieval store first.
- **Hidden-state gates** (d15, AUC 0.7 to above 0.9): need model weights; unblock by an open-weight model run.
- **Multi-agent critic gains in medicine versus matched single agent** (K17, K25): G9 already forbids debate; test only if the schema ever considers a mixed-model critic, which needs a second model family on the bench.
- **Human-AI interface findings** (K20): not testable on a single agent.
- **Whether the 2026 abstract-only results hold** (d17, d18, d26, c07 effect sizes at n=244 to 1273): need full-text reads or reruns.

## Order to run

1. **U12 plus U3 (flip rule and wording)**: cheapest, uses the logs we already keep, and the harm side is the best-established fact (544 vs 198 flips; 84.11 to 66.34 by wording). A positive result fixes S8 for every mode.
2. **U2 plus U4 (agreement gate and trigger source)**: decides when S5/S8 may fire at all, which feeds G1 and S0. It reuses the same first-pass samples as U1 and U14, so it costs almost nothing extra.
3. **U1 (structured second look vs plain re-answer vs CoT-SC)**: the top open gap (Gap 1). Nobody has run it for an LLM, and its answer tells us whether the DR template earns any place at all or whether sampling more first passes is the whole story.
