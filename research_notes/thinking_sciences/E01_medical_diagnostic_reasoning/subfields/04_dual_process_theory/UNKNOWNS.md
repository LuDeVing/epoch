## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Exact Bago and De Neys percentages for correct first answers. Full text was behind a bot check. Unblock: obtain the paper or its OSF data.
- Whether a conversational plus reasoning model pipeline beats a single reasoning model at matched tokens (Zuo 2026 does not match tokens). Unblock: run T4 arms on a reasoning model alone and on the pipeline at equal tokens.
- Whether CRT-style items are contaminated for current models. Unblock: build fresh, never-published variants and compare with the originals.
- Whether a case-retrieval rare-disease agent (PMC12999473) helps through retrieval or through model strength. Not read. Unblock: read the paper, then run U11.
- Whether the human reflection effect exists beyond one research group. Unblock: no human trial is possible for us; only the LLM analogue (U9) is testable.
- The per-model, per-bias table for BiasMedQA with a reasoning model on. Unblock: rerun with a current reasoning model.
- Whether verbal confidence from newer models varies enough to route on. Griot 2025 covered 12 earlier models. Unblock: sample confidence on our bench (cheap, folds into U2).

## Order to run
1. U1 and U2 together (router and routing signal): the central S0 question and no published study answers it; one item set covers both.
2. U3 (task-type gate): cheapest test, general evidence is already established, and a win saves tokens everywhere.
3. U4 with U5 (challenge step and draft hiding): the human and LLM evidence shows ranking, not recall, is the weak step; both use the same open-ended cases.
