## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Real-patient or outpatient common-disease cases with a pending test: we have written vignettes only. Unblock by building the staged set in U4 and having a clinician check the closure points.
- Premature-closure share with interrater agreement: no post-2015 audit found. Needs human chart reviewers; out of scope for the bench.
- Which MAI-DxO role carries the gain: needs the SDBench gatekeeper and the 304 cases. Unblock by getting the SDBench release or building a similar loop.
- The Instant Answer accuracy for SDBench is figure-only. Unblock by re-running that arm.
- Sheppert 2026 (LLM anchoring vs physicians): model list and prompts unread. Unblock by reading the full text.
- Goh UK replication and the Ke 2025 letter: not located. Unblock by a targeted search.
- Everett 2026 (52% vs 24% overlap when AI sees the physician first): secondary summary only.
- Whether the b17 early-distractor effect exists in experienced physicians: needs a human study.
- BiasMedQA follow-up (2025) on reasoning models: not opened.

## Order to run

1. U4 (common-disease staged set with an Instant Answer arm): builds the item set and baseline that U1, U3, U5, U8 and U10 all reuse, and fills the biggest evidence hole.
2. U3 (challenger flip rate on first-correct answers): decides whether any challenger step is safe; the human and LLM evidence both warn of harm.
3. U1 plus U8 (closure gate and stop-signal AUC): U8 is nearly free once U4 logs exist and sets the trigger for every other gated step.
