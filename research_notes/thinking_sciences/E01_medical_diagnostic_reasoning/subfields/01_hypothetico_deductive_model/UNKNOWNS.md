## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Exact figures from Elstein, Shulman, Sprafka (1978), Barrows (1978) and Neufeld (1981). Missing: the books and full texts (abstracts only). Unblock: library copy or interlibrary loan. Until then, treat all founding numbers as unverified.
- Sprafka (1973) result on instructions to generate many or few hypotheses (N=30, null, secondhand). Missing: repository access. Unblock: request the dissertation; run U7 on LLMs as the substitute.
- Which MAI-DxO role carries the gain. Missing: the orchestrator code and an SDBench licence-free copy, plus the gatekeeper. Unblock: rebuild a five-role loop on our harness with code-held facts and drop one role at a time (extension of U3).
- Whether results on NEJM cases hold on unseen cases. Missing: unpublished cases with held-out answers. Unblock: write rewritten or synthetic cases with code-held facts; check with a before/after cutoff test.
- Real-encounter validity (healthy patients, false-positive rate, noisy history). Missing: any such data. Unblock: not on our bench; note as out of scope.
- A true sequential-evidence item type on our bench. Missing: the ARC and BrainTeaser items give all input at once. Unblock: build a "reveal on request" wrapper (hidden examples, hidden constraints) with code-held facts. This gates U1, U2, U3, U5, U9, U10.
- Human replication of "more hypotheses with training" (Arocha and Patel, N=13). Missing: human subjects. Unblock: none needed for the agent; keep K04 contested.
- Bass 2013 gain without a priming-only control. Missing: full paper and a new control. Unblock: U8 gives the LLM version.

## Order to run

1. E01.01-U2 (info-gain tests vs model-picked vs random): cheapest to code (code does the scoring, G5), the clearest prior for a large gain, and it includes the random baseline that undercut earlier claims.
2. E01.01-U1 (hypothesis-first vs plain CoT at equal tokens): tests the founding claim directly, which no study has isolated; reuses the same item type as U2.
3. E01.01-U3 (challenger vs extra sampling at equal tokens): settles whether MAI-DxO's challenger is more than sampling, which decides whether G9 needs an exception.
