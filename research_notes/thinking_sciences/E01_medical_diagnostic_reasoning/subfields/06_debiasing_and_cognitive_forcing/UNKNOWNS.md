## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Real-practice effect of any debiasing step: needs patient outcomes; our bench has only items.
- Share of agent errors that are self-generated premature closure versus injected bias: needs error-labelled traces from our own runs; an LLM labeller cannot type the bias (K30), so human labelling of a sample is needed.
- Whether reasoning-mode models remove the need for any debiasing prompt: needs a matched-token arm per model and the numbers from the BiasMedQA reasoning preprint (d13), which was blocked.
- Outcome tests of Croskerry's specific named pitfall rules beyond Sherbino 2014: needs cases built to trigger each pitfall; we have none.
- Whether 2026 preprints on premature closure (c09-c13) replicate: needs reruns or independent reports.
- Expert-level effects of CFS: human trials used novices; no way to test on our bench.

## Order to run
1. U1 (signal-triggered check vs scheduled): cheapest, builds on logging we already have, and tests G1 directly.
2. U2 (input hygiene strip vs bias warning): the injected-bias damage is established, and a code strip costs no model tokens.
3. U3 (commit before advice): gives a direct answer on a gap with only indirect LLM evidence, and affects how any critic or tool output is read.
