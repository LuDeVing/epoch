## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Athlete reflection: no source found beyond a decision-tree paper (b27). Unblock: a literature search for reflection or debriefing studies in sport that report numbers.
- Independent replication of the chess skill gradient (GM 100% to CM 0%): none found among 59 citing records (d20). Unblock: a registered replication, or a search by task name in other journals. Our bench cannot test chess experts.
- Process-level evidence for human fixation beyond gaze (think-aloud): Blech et al. 2020 seen only as a snippet (b31). Unblock: open the paper.
- Benchmark validity: the MedEinst table was read from garbled text, and Qwen3-32B and several cells need the PDF image (critique, d02, d03). Unblock: read the table image or contact the authors; until then, quote K18 as contested.
- Whether M-ARC and MedEinst measure fixation or plain difficulty: no control separates them (a26). Unblock: add matched non-trap controls with equal difficulty from LLM-free pilots.
- Whether M-ARC items leaked into training (b20). Unblock: write fresh items in the same style.
- Haq 2025 and Li 2025 WCST numbers were not re-found by the critique (b22, c06-c08). Unblock: retrieve both papers and re-read.
- Hatano and Inagaki 1986, Luchins 1942 original, Binz and Schulz journal version: unopened. Unblock: open or cite only the reconstruction (d11).
- Adaptive-expertise training for agents (invention before instruction): no LLM analogue defined. Unblock: design a task where the agent invents a measure before being shown the standard one.
- Resource-rational stop rule: no validated rule for open-ended search (d13). Unblock: fit the cost parameter to our own token budget curves.

## Order to run

1. **A10.02-U1 (informed prompt on paired trap and control items).** Cheapest (one prompt line), human effect is large, and it tests the over-switching cost that G1 depends on.
2. **A10.02-U2 (second route and compare versus best-of-N at equal tokens).** The central question for S6 and S7, and the only prior LLM scaffold result (ECR-Agent) is confounded by tokens and structure.
3. **A10.02-U3 (mixed exemplars versus same-method exemplars).** Biggest human effect (83% to 16%), costs nothing to apply in memory, and no LLM test exists.
