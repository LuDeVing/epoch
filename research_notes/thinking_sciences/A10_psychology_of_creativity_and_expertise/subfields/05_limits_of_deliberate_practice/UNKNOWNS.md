## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human-rated predictability for LLM tasks. Only coder ratings exist for human domains (b03). Unblock: build a small rated item set with two raters.
- A neutral re-run of the 88-study set and the 14-study Ericsson-Harwell subset (d16). Needs raw data from the authors or a third group. It would settle the 14% vs 29% dispute but would not change the schema.
- Per-moderator corrigendum values for Macnamara 2014 (d17) and the Burgoyne 2016 corrigendum (d18). Both pages returned 403. Unblock: library access or author copy.
- Range-restriction-corrected elite estimate (critique.md). Needs the sports study data.
- Direct scaffold-level far-transfer tests with an active control. Needs a held-out family set built for it (U7 is the first step).
- Whether weight-training results (SCoRe, STaR, RLVR, d05, d10, d11) carry to prompt-only scaffolds. Our project does not train weights, so this stays out of reach.
- Human practice effects with objective logs. All human hours are self-reported or diaries; no fix on our side.

## Order to run
1. U1 (dose-response and knee, T1): cheapest, decides loop caps for everything else, and the budget-matched arm is also needed for U2.
2. U2 (external signal vs own critique, T2): reuses the T1 items; confirms or rejects the strongest established finding on our own scaffold.
3. U3 (difficulty routing, T3): largest expected token saving (about 4x in d08); the bins also feed U5 and U4.
