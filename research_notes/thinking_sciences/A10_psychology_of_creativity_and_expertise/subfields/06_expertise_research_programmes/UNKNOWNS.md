## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Whether SPIRAL's game-to-reasoning gain is game-specific or format exposure: needs training runs with a non-game synthetic control. Unblock: access to a small RL setup or published ablations.
- Whether prompt scaffolds drift like SFT or RL on unrelated tasks beyond misalignment: needs a representation-level probe on an open model. Unblock: run the scaffold on an open-weight model and compare logit or KL shift on unrelated prompts.
- Exact sample sizes and effect sizes of Jerrim 2016, Unsworth 2015 and Melby-Lervag 2016: full text blocked (403). Unblock: institutional access or author copies.
- Whether the human far-transfer null holds in preregistered large-N work outside the Sala and Gobet group: no such study found. Unblock: new literature search once the API limits lift.
- Whether counterfactual-twin gaps persist in current frontier models: Wu et al. used GPT-4 and GPT-3.5. Unblock: run U3 on current models (this is the test).
- Expert gaze as a training signal (EyeMulator, +6.8 to +13.17%): needs fine-tuning, outside our scaffold-only scope.

## Order to run

1. A10.06-U1 (placebo scaffold on held-out types): decides whether any scaffold gain is real and general; cheap, uses existing items; also answers part of U9.
2. A10.06-U3 then U4 (counterfactual twins, then rule-restating): a measured weakness (100 to 21 in one paper) with a cheap candidate fix; twin items double as a permanent bench check.
3. A10.06-U9 (filler-token placebo): finds which scaffold parts are only extra computation; cheap and could remove setup cost, which G7 counts.
