## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Original numbers for Chi 1981, Hardiman 1989, Gick-Holyoak 1980/83, Ross 1984 and Schoenfeld-Herrmann 1982. Unblock: open the originals (library or author copies); until then use only the direction of each effect.
- Human baseline for our own item sets. Unblock: run the same crossed items with a small human sample, or use published faculty data for physics items.
- Whether Wolf's "sorting style" effect has an LLM analogue. Unblock: U8 data across runs.
- Natural (non-adversarial) distractor rates in real agent inputs. Unblock: sample our logs for irrelevant clauses and measure their share.
- Effect of S2 on models we cannot afford to run at scale (the strongest reasoning models). Unblock: budget for a small, paired sample.
- Search coverage: the web search budget was used up and scholarly APIs returned 429 during the gap pass. Unblock: re-run searches for 2025-2026 LLM card-sort replications and for any Step-Back or typing test on reasoning models.
- Whether our bench has enough items with a principle-by-surface split. Unblock: build or buy the item set; U1, U3, U8 all need it.

## Order to run

1. **A10.07-U1 (S2 vs plain at equal tokens).** It decides whether the S2 typing step earns a place at all, and its answer changes the value of every other test here.
2. **A10.07-U3 with U4 (restate-and-strip and code-checked quantity use).** Distractor harm is the best-measured effect and the fix is cheap and mostly code, so it is the likeliest real gain.
3. **A10.07-U2 (forced labels).** If the label is not causal, U5, U6 and U10 lose their value, so run it before any retrieval work.
