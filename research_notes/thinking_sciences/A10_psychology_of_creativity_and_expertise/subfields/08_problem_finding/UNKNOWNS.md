## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **The primary 1976 and 1971 numbers.** We need the book or paper to know the true r values, N per cell, blinding and scoring. Unblock: get a legitimate copy; until then, cite only "moderate positive, about .4 to .65".
- **A true Getzels paradigm for LLMs.** No benchmark has an open task with exploration steps and blind expert originality ratings. Unblock: build one from U3, with logs of frames tried and human raters; needs rater budget.
- **Long-term outcomes.** The career follow-up cannot be mapped to an agent. No test planned.
- **Human harm baseline from reframing.** Needs a labelled set of benign tasks with a program check for violations (U5 builds a small one).
- **Age and domain moderators.** Not relevant to an agent; leave.
- **Interactive user data.** Clarification gains assume a user or simulator replies. Our bench has no live user; needs a scripted user simulator, whose own quality is unknown.
- **Whether LLM idea novelty (Si et al.) means better problems.** Rated novelty and downstream success are not linked in any study found. Unblock: score ideas by a downstream task on a subset.
- **Different-family judge reliability on reframes.** d13 tested stories, not reframes. Needs the U10 rating set.
- **Primary text of some LLM sources.** Snippet-only items (d16, d18, c19, c22) could change numbers. Unblock: open them.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (missing-information detector, separate call).** Cheapest to build, strongest signal (+14.6 points on code, +8 over a single prompt), and it also tells us the false-alarm cost before S1 grows.
2. **U10 (reframe-quality proxy).** Every other test (U3, U4, U5, U8) needs a metric that is not a same-model judge, and judges are near chance. Build the rating set once, reuse it.
3. **U4 (gate O8 on a trigger).** O8 is still [S] with a large null and a known breakage rate; this decides whether the operator stays in the schema and under which rule. Run U5 alongside, since it reuses the same arms.
