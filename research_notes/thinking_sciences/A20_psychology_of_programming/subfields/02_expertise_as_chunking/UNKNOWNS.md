## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- An independent replication of Vessey (1985) with a larger N, a second language and hard novel bugs. Needs human programmers and a protocol coder; we have neither. Unblock: a small crowd study, or accept the hypothesis status.
- The primary effect sizes of Shneiderman (1976), McKeithen (1981), Wiedenbeck (1986) and Adelson (1984). Needs the full texts (paywalled). Unblock: library access or author copies.
- A human expert group on the misleading-names task large enough to trust the 55.56% cell. Unblock: recruit experts (at least 30).
- Human or execution-based grading of the Glasz advantage of LLM chunks. Unblock: expert graders for legacy code, or a modern language with tests.
- Whether a Chase-Simon template account (chunks with slots) beats plain chunk summaries in memory. Needs a memory design and a task set with repeated plans; revisit after the subgoal library exists.
- Whether results from 0.5B-8B models and 2025 chat models carry to our agent's backbone. Unblock: run U12 and U13 on that backbone.
- Trace metrics on runs from other scaffolds. We only have our own and a few published ones (AgentLens is OpenHands only).
