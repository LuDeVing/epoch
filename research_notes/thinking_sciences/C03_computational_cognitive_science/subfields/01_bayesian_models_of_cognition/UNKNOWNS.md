## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Independent human replication of the 2006 prediction study with new participants and a fit statistic. We have no human subjects; would need a published replication.
- Correlation among samples of one model (not across models). Needs logged repeated samples with error overlap on a verifiable set; partly doable once the bench logs per-sample answers.
- A pre-registered Bayesian-versus-rival comparison on LLM behaviour. Needs a fixed prior and rival models before data; a design project in itself.
- Token-probability beliefs for the Martingale Score on current reasoning models. Needs logprob access, which many hosted models hide.
- Full text of the critiques (Jones & Love, Marcus & Davis, Tauber et al., Sanborn & Chater, the 2025 myth paper) and perceptual suboptimality reviews. Needs reading, not a bench run.
- Original numbers of Xu & Tenenbaum 2007 word learning. Needs the paper.
- Whether the flight-task results hold for real people with inconsistent choices. Needs a human study; the bench uses simulated users.

## Order to run

1. C03.01-U10 (sample count with verifier): cheapest, needs only logged samples, and sets the budget for every other test.
2. C03.01-U3 (disconfirming test): cheap prompt-level change with the largest measured effect so far (42 to 56% success), and it fits S7.
3. C03.01-U1 (code-held posterior beyond flights): highest expected gain and the main open claim in this subfield, but needs a hypothesis-set builder, so run it after the two cheap tests.
