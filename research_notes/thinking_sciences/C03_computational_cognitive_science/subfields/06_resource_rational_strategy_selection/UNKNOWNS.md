## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Independent replication of the 2017 human results.** All four experiments are one lab; the search was blocked (K22 on hold). Unblock: retry the search when quota returns; open Rieskamp and Otto 2006, Payne 1988 and the BBS commentaries first-hand.
- **Backbone-stable feature set.** Size predicts strategy (pseudo R2 0.160) and the sign of entropy flips. Unblock: log candidate features on two or more backbones and compare in U3 and U7.
- **Number of logged runs per class before the table beats one global mode.** Only asymptotic orders exist (d13). Unblock: log replay with subsampled histories (a by-product of U1 and U4).
- **Safe online discovery of new modes.** Humans reach under 30% in 120 trials. Unblock: needs a candidate-mode generator and U13 to pass first.
- **LLM through the human paradigms.** No study runs an LLM through Mouselab, SSL tasks or sorting selection against human data. Unblock: build a small paradigm harness; low priority for the schema.
- **Pure Lieder-Griffiths form on an LLM** (per-mode reward and time regression, Thompson sampling) with code-only rewards. Covered by U1 and U3; not published anywhere.
- **Does process feedback at token level work at all for an LLM?** The human result is on click tasks with a delay penalty; there is no token-level analogue. Unblock: U11 is the cheapest proxy.
- **Class keys for open tasks.** The class table needs a stable class label. How to assign a class to a new item cheaply is unsolved here (S0 slot in SCHEMA section 7). Unblock: test hash-by-source, nearest-neighbour on item text, and size bins in U1.
- **Per-run noise.** Single runs mix selection value with sampling noise (K13, d05). Unblock: three or more runs per item on the bench; costs tokens.
- **Several LLM routing papers rest on small test sets** (Airline about 50 tasks) or LLM-judged rewards. Unblock: replicate on our suites with code checks before citing any ARES-style number as a target.

## Order to run

1. **U4 (forgetting, log replay).** Needs no model calls, only existing logs, and it fixes the shape of the class table every other test depends on.
2. **U5 (short vs long per class at three caps).** The missing per-class table (critique gap 7); its output feeds U1, U9 and U10 and tells us whether any gate has room to gain.
3. **U1 (class table vs fixed modes at equal tokens), with U2 as an extra arm.** The headline test: it decides whether S0 gets a learned gate at all, and whether the model must stay out of mode choice.
