## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Whether prompted confidence matches trained confidence: needs fine-tuning or RL access (K05). Unblock: a small open-weight model with LoRA.
- Token-probability gates (CGES, CAS): needs logprobs from the backbone. Unblock: an API or model that returns them.
- Human metacognition measures (M-ratio, meta-d') at scale: need two-choice tasks and 400+ trials per condition (Rahnev). Unblock: a binary-choice benchmark subset with ordinal ratings.
- The Maniscalco and Lau (2012) and Koriat (1997) primary results: could not be opened. Unblock: a library copy or another host.
- Replication of the 2010 brain-structure link with a large sample: outside our bench; no action.
- The AAAI-25 memory-driven choice-supportive bias paper: snippet only (d11). Unblock: open it and read the accuracy results.
- A general LLM metacognition factor: needs many tasks and models, not three of each (d14).
- Frontier reasoning models other than gpt-oss-20b, Qwen3-30B and DeepSeek-R1-8B: results may differ (d01, d02).

## Order to run
1. C03.09-U1 (hybrid confidence plus one extra sample): highest expected gain, uses runs we already make for S3, and directly tells us how to rank without a vote.
2. C03.09-U2 (confidence-gated second attempt): the one test that could change spending, and the only place stated confidence might override schema 4b.
3. C03.09-U5 (masking the earlier answer in revise): cheap, one prompt change, and the bias is large (32.5% vs 13.1%) with an unmeasured accuracy effect.
