## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Independent replication of DreamCoder-style gains.** All large effects come from one group. Unblock: a public DreamCoder or Stitch run on a domain we choose; no LLM needed. We have no such domain loaded.
- **Whether newer reasoning models can compress well.** The GPT-4 failure (19.01% valid) is from 2023. Unblock: re-run the LILO appendix C.2 prompt on our current model, 5,000+ abstractions, count valid ones.
- **Whether a wrong stored helper actually spreads.** Counted only for episodic memory. Unblock: a poison test where we plant a wrong helper and count downstream failures; needs a long stream with a clean check.
- **Soft reuse versus the model just solving a similar problem.** Needs a matched random-example control at scale; U2 covers a small version.
- **Stitch on natural-language or code traces.** Needs a conversion from traces to a typed tree. Unblock: restrict to tasks with a function-call DSL.
- **Learning from scratch.** DreamCoder-style bottom-up growth needs millions of tokens (about 5 days on 64 CPUs). Not affordable. Skip unless token budgets grow by orders of magnitude.
- **ARC transfer at 100+ tasks with a no-memory arm at equal tokens.** Needs a held-out ARC set, 3 seeds, and an agreed token cap. This is U1 on ARC; blocked on bench size.
- **LILO library margin over a strong control.** Under 1 point on two of three domains, with seed sd up to 14. Needs many more seeds than the original; not worth running before U1.
- **Self-reported ARC results (77.1% / 26.0%).** No ablation exists. Unblock: author release of a no-library arm; or our own U1.

## Order to run

1. **C03.03-U1** (store vs verified resampling at equal tokens). It decides whether the planned subgoal library exists at all; two independent re-tests already erased the published gains.
2. **C03.03-U9** (token break-even) run on the same stream as U1. Costs nothing extra, and it says when a store is worth building (G7).
3. **C03.03-U2** (callable vs example vs random example vs none). Reuses U1's stream. It separates the three explanations for any gain, and tells us whether retrieval quality matters before we spend on U7.
