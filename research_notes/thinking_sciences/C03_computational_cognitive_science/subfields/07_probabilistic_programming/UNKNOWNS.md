## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human graded-judgment data for our own items. The strongest PPL results are fits to human ratings (r 0.92-0.93). We have no rater pool. Unblock: a small rater study, or published item sets with released human means.
- Token-level SMC steering needs logits, which our API models do not expose (c06). Unblock: an open-weights model in the harness, or block-level resampling as in U5.
- Many-labs or cross-language replications of Frank and Goodman 2012 and Lake 2015 (K24). Unblock: read Qing and Franke 2015 and later replications. This is reading, not a bench test.
- Systematic re-fit of Bayesian models with flexible vs fixed priors (K17). Unblock: a published multi-study re-analysis, or our own re-fit of three open datasets.
- Frontier-model results for Battleship and Stan tasks beyond o3 (K22). Unblock: rerun Loose LIPS and a fixed PosteriorDB set with the models we use.
- Exact LM-only R2 in Wong 2025 and the 2014 tug-of-war originals (K25). Unblock: author data release or figure digitising.
- Whether Ellis-style Bayesian reweighting helps beyond a pass-all-pairs filter on ARC (K23). Unblock: implement reweighting with a measured prior on a held-out ARC split.
- Which data-science Full SMC cell in Loula is right (0.346, 0.407, 0.285 vs 0.225) (K08). Unblock: authors' code or a table check.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (solver gate on many-variable items).** Cheapest to build, clearest expected effect (CoT 25 percent at 5 variables vs code 96-100 percent in 2024), and it tests whether the gap survives with current models.
2. **U3 (matched-token model-building vs long CoT).** It fills the one hole in the literature (K21) and decides whether any PPL-style operator belongs in the schema at all.
3. **U2 (propose-and-check rules).** Reuses ARC harness pieces, overlaps with G3, and has the best single-source gain (17 to 30 percent).
