## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human difficulty data for our own items.** To check whether rule length tracks difficulty, we need human accuracy on our items. Unblock: borrow the ARC human data (Johnson 2021, LeGris 2024) or run a small human study.
- **Feldman 2000 and Lafond 2007 numbers.** The first has only its abstract read; the second is unverified. Unblock: get the full text (library or author copy), then check whether shortest-formula length alone predicts difficulty.
- **Token counts of published 2025-2026 systems.** Leaderboards give dollars, not tokens, and do not say whether a model induces programs internally. Unblock: run our own arms with token logs (U1).
- **Whether reasoning models already do program induction inside their thinking.** We cannot see their hidden reasoning. Unblock: compare accuracy with and without an external propose-and-execute loop (U1), and read any visible summaries.
- **Why refinement fails on MiniARC.** The paper's reason (pattern matching, poor grid reading) is a guess. Unblock: per-item analysis from U2 and U7 on perceptual items.
- **Primitives of LLM-written rules.** Whether models prefer iff or XOR like people needs a rule-labelled item set and rule parsing. Unblock: a generated Boolean-concept bench with verbal-rule capture (extension of U11).
- **Benchmark contamination.** ARC items may be in training data. Unblock: fresh generated items with the same structure.
- **Cost of verification on large K.** Published K sweeps charge hypotheses, not tokens. Unblock: our token-logged sweep (K sweep in DEEP.md T3).

## Order to run

1. **C03.02-U1** (propose-and-execute vs plain extended reasoning, equal tokens): it decides whether this whole subfield changes S2/S3/S4 on a current model, and every other test builds on its harness.
2. **C03.02-U3** (held-out demo or vote against false accepts): cheap, reuses U1 outputs, and it protects G3, the guardrail with the most field support.
3. **C03.02-U2** (program-first with direct fallback): free router, reuses U1 outputs, and it answers whether the program route should be the default or a mode.
