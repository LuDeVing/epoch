## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Primary founding numbers.** Rao and Ballard 1999 and Murray 2002 methods and effect sizes are unread. Unblock: institutional PDF access or author preprints. Not needed for the schema.
- **Brain alignment: forecast vs transfer.** No test separates Caucheteux 2023 from Antonello and Huth 2024. Unblock: a new neuroimaging analysis; not our bench.
- **Three-level language prediction (syntax, semantics, discourse).** No study found; search coverage was poor. Unblock: a fresh literature search when APIs are not rate-limited.
- **PC-trained language models past 100M parameters.** No result; irrelevant to a prompt scaffold.
- **Pre-registered replications of expectation suppression after 2021.** Yan 2023 may be pre-registered (unconfirmed). Unblock: check OSF.
- **Kok 2012 sharpening replication.** None found; neuroscience only.
- **Surprise signals on open-ended tasks.** Needs a non-verifiable benchmark with a trusted grader, which we lack (G8: model judges are unreliable).
- **Equal-cost foresight on large models.** The 70B agent runs used very large FLOPs. Needs our own token-matched run (U2).

## Order to run

1. **U1 (residual-only passing).** No study exists, it is cheap (a prompt change at S5), and it can lower tokens without losing accuracy.
2. **U2 (foresight vs best-of-N and self-consistency).** The only measured PC-adjacent lever with real gains (+7.2 GSM8K, +25.3 AlfWorld); the open question is the edge at equal tokens, and the test reuses our existing best-of-N arm.
3. **U9 (do self-correction nulls hold on current models).** Cheap, reuses arms from U1 and U2, and it sets the baseline that every other loop test depends on.
