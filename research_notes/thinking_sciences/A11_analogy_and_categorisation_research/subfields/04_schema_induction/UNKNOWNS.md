## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Per-moderator effects in Alfieri et al. 2013** (principle after vs before, find similarities vs differences, lag). Needs the full paper. Unblock: a legal copy through a library, or a second meta-analysis.
- **Size of the Boyce et al. 2023 replication** and whether the Kurtz and Loewenstein replication exists. Unblock: the project data or authors' report.
- **Whether humans' delayed-transfer rescue (third case plus matched wording) maps to anything in a model.** There is no delay inside a context. Unblock: tests with an external store and long task streams (see U10).
- **Altered-alphabet and counterfactual tests on current reasoning models.** None published. Unblock: build a small renamed-symbol suite and run it ourselves.
- **Forced causal-structure check on source choice** (GPT-4 chose the right source 0 of 15 unprompted). Unblock: a distractor-case set with a causal-check arm; needs the analogical access results from the sibling subfield.
- **Leakage between template stores and test items** in BoT, AWM and ReasoningBank. Unblock: held-out task classes built by us.
- **Behaviour of reasoning-trained models with supplied cases.** DeepSeek reports harm with no numbers. Unblock: run zero-shot vs few-shot arms on our own model in U1.
- **Recent literature (2025 to 2026) coverage.** Search tools hit quota; absence of a paper means not found. Unblock: a later search pass when budgets reset.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (pair comparison with forced alignment):** it decides whether S2 needs an induction step at all, and no paper has tested it on a model.
2. **U2 (abstraction step vs terse plain answer):** it is cheap, uses existing items, and decides whether step-back and abstract-plan prompts are worth any tokens on a current model.
3. **U11 (raw cases vs code-built routines vs model-written templates):** it decides the form of the subgoal library, the largest published gains (BoT, Dynamic Cheatsheet, ReasoningBank) are in this area, and leakage can be checked with a held-out class.

U3 (the gate) can reuse the logs from U1 and U2 at no extra cost.
