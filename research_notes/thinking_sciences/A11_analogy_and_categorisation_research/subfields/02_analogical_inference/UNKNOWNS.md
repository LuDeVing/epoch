## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- ARN and StoryAnalogy have not been re-run on 2025-26 reasoning models. Unblock: obtain the item sets and run a reasoning model at matched tokens (our bench would need the datasets, not only our own items).
- Lee and Holyoak 2008, Day and Gentner 2007, Lassaline 1996 and Holyoak, Lee, Lu 2010 cell values are unread (paywalled or scanned). Unblock: library access or OCR.
- Whether human systematicity evidence holds in independent labs with larger, non-student samples. Unblock: only new human studies; not testable here.
- Whether one causal-model process (Lee and Holyoak) or two steps (Colhoun and Gentner) is right in people. Not testable on our bench; we design with two steps and ablate the second (U1, U12).
- Thought Propagation gains depend on model self-evaluation in most variants. Unblock: rerun with execution-based aggregation (needs tasks that have a simulator).
- Reasoning-model behaviour on letter strings (Hellwig et al. give figures only). Unblock: extract per-model numbers from figures or rerun.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (executable check on conjectures):** the highest-gain, cheapest test; it decides whether O7's check step is code or nothing, and every other row builds on its items.
2. **U3 (counting vs structure with code):** resolves the live dispute in K10 and tells us whether the mutated-variant G3 check should be routed to code.
3. **U2 (decoy source with relation-only check):** surface-similar distractors are the main failure in every study; this tests the cheapest fix and reuses U1's item format.
