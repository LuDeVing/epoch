## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Independent replication of Clement and Gentner 1991. We have no human participants. Unblock by finding a lab replication or running a small online study.
- Gentner 1983 core experiments. The PDF is a scan and there is no OCR on this machine. Unblock with an OCR tool or a text copy.
- Human reference for far-analogy items at our difficulty. Unblock with a small human panel, or reuse published human data (ARCANA, StoryAnalogy).
- Alfieri moderator results (objective, principle, lag). The published paper was not opened. Unblock by reading the full 2013 PDF.
- Whether the LISA working-memory limit has an LLM analogue (context length, attention). Needs a controlled case-size sweep and model internals, which an API does not give.
- Whether systematicity should be a hard rule (SMTB says no). Needs an independent benchmark that is not SME's own corpus.
- Why relation information is present but not applied to new entities (probing and patching). Needs model internals.

## Order to run
1. A11.01-U12 (permuted-symbol baseline on our agent): cheap, and it tells us whether the weakness still exists before we build anything.
2. A11.01-U1 (code-scored vs free-text vs both mapping at equal tokens): the central question for O7. The only direct evidence so far had the code score losing.
3. A11.01-U3 (extraction fidelity): every mapping gain depends on the representation step, and nothing measures it.
