## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Founding-study numbers (Rouder and Ratcliff 2004/2006, Goodman 2008, Smith and Minda 2000, Nosofsky 2000, Feldman 2000): need full PDFs from someone with library access. Until then all founding claims stay qualitative.
- Human working-memory effect sizes (Lewandowsky 2011, Hoffmann 2014): need the papers. Without them U11 has no human reference.
- Internal-representation claims (Xiong 2026 prototype learner, function vectors): need open-weight models with internals; our agent is a closed-model scaffold.
- Whether the human prototype-to-exemplar drift exists at all (Nosofsky vs Smith and Minda, counts unknown): needs the 30-data-set reanalysis numbers.
- Frontier-model rule-vs-example gap at matched tokens: Fu 2026 used minimum-coverage examples; a token-matched run on open models would give an effect size to plan around.
- Agent-memory results use benchmarks (WebArena, Mind2Web, AIME) that are not our problem classes; a class-structured agent bench with repeated classes is needed for U2, U7, U8.

## Order to run

1. A11.07-U2 (summary vs cases and the case-count crossover): cheapest, directly sets the memory design, and the established evidence points one way, so a null is informative.
2. A11.07-U1 (rule-first vs few-shot, rule vs rotated classes): highest expected gain and also tests the harm case on non-rule classes.
3. A11.07-U3 (Shepard bank on our model): the only frontier-model measurement of human-style structure effects; its output also feeds U4 and U10.
