## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Independent replication of analogical encoding and of alignable-difference results.** Needs a literature pass that is not rate-limited, and ideally a run with human raters. Unblock by reading Alfieri, Nokes-Malach and Schunn (2013) and a citation search for replications.
- **Effect size of case comparison overall.** The meta-analysis was never opened (b19, d16). No figure may be used. Unblock: open the paper (DOI 10.1080/00461520.2013.775712).
- **Estes and Hasson (2004), Hansen and Richland (2020), Kok et al. (2013).** Only seen second-hand. Unblock: open and extract numbers.
- **Reasoning-model behaviour on few-shot and contrastive prompts.** The R1 statement has no numbers. Unblock: our own T2 run on a reasoning model.
- **Whether the human similarity/difference asymmetry is alignability or complexity (Ichien 2024), in LLMs.** Needs the triad items in U3 plus relational versus featural difference conditions.
- **Retrieval versus mapping on ARN itself.** AnaloBench says retrieval; ARN was not decomposed. Needs a split of ARN into given-pair and bank conditions.
- **Contamination.** Gentner stories and letter-string items are likely in training data. Needs fresh items built after the model's cutoff.
- **Current-model behaviour on alphabet drop and position bias.** Published numbers are 2023-2025 models. Needs our own runs on the model we deploy.

## Order to run
1. **U1 correspondence table vs free-form comparison.** It is the cheapest change to S2 and the only item where human evidence (explicit listing carries the effect) and a close LLM result (structure forcing) both point the same way, so it tests the core of the subfield.
2. **U7 structure-summary retrieval.** The strongest LLM evidence says retrieval, not mapping, is the bottleneck, and the fix is a storage-time change that costs one short call per case.
3. **U2 contrastive demos on reasoning vs non-reasoning models.** The 2023 gain was large (+9.8 to +16.0) and the 2025 contrary signal has no numbers, so one run decides whether it belongs in the prompts for each mode.
