## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Outcome check beyond code tests.** All human evidence uses citations. Our bench has verified answers on ARC and BRAINTEASER only; open-ended idea quality has no outcome check. Needed: a held-out downstream use of an idea (an execution test), as Si 2025 did with 43 experts. Unblocks: any open-mode usefulness claim.
- **Real part tables.** Part familiarity needs counts from a corpus (Fleming used prior patent use). We have no corpus of "parts with success counts" for ARC primitives. Unblock: log which operators and primitives succeeded in earlier runs.
- **Human replication of key LLM pattern.** Distance threshold, structured analogy and the cost-of-far finding come from product design, recipes and NLP ideas, not grid or logic items. Needs a task-matched pilot before sizes can be trusted.
- **Open-weight models.** Liu 2026 gave no per-open-weight threshold; the gain was in the top two models only. Needs tests per model tier we actually run.
- **Unread sources.** Singh-Fleming 2010 (collaboration tails), Fleming-Sorenson 2001, Katila-Ahuja 2002, Kaplan-Vakili 2015, Schilling-Green 2011, Kneeland et al. 2020, and Uzzi's supplement. Unblock: full-text access; they could change U7 and U14.
- **Within-run exhaustion in LLMs.** Needs run logs with repeated-pair counts across many runs; none exist.
- **Direction vs distance of far LLM blends.** Whether far blends differ in kind. Needs an ONS-style metric validated against verified outcomes; the metric is new and not validated.

## Order to run
1. **E14.01-U1** (explicit recombination vs best-of-N): the biggest open gap, nobody has run it at equal tokens, and it decides whether any of O7's recombination machinery pays for itself (G7).
2. **E14.01-U8** (Fleming mean and spread under an outcome check): cheap, uses the same runs as U1 (log per-item mean and SD), and tells us whether a pre-execution screen of far blends is worth adding.
3. **E14.01-U2** (far-move share sweep): reuses the U1 harness with one parameter, and gives the number the schema currently lacks for how much budget far moves deserve.
