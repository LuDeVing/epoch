## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Why the 2022 and 2025 papers disagree on the sign of the atypicality-disruption link. Needs both measures re-run on one corpus (MAG or OpenAlex) with the same D window. We have no citation corpus on the bench. Unblock: ask the authors for data, or build a small OpenAlex slice.
- The size of the A-D effect in 2506.15959. No coefficient, window or code is published. Unblock: author data or a re-computation.
- Whether pairwise 1D replicates on patents or by a third group. Needs a patent citation set.
- Whether distant-analogy and in-topic attempts differ in a real LLM agent. No published study; only our own test U1.
- Whether novelty survives execution in math and code beyond ML recipes. Needs a verifiable-task benchmark with a novelty rating. AlphaResearch (8 problems) is the only data.
- Whether Uzzi's conventional-core pattern holds with LLM-based novelty measures.
- Whether an LLM theory-vs-method label matches humans (no accuracy reported).
- Whether the decline of disruption is real in null-network comparisons (about 85% of the decline survives randomization per Huang and Xie, against the artefact papers).
- Long-horizon payoff: disruptive credit takes about 10 years in papers. Our bench has no delayed outcome. Unblock: cross-session memory with later re-checks (S9).

## Order to run

1. **U3 (embedding distance vs win, offline).** Costs no extra tokens; uses logged attempts; tells us at once whether a cheap atypicality score means anything.
2. **U1 (displace vs distant-mix attempt types).** The core question of this subfield; it decides what open mode should favour.
3. **U2 (one distant element on a standard method).** Cheap on ARC impasse items; the only claim here with a clear human effect size (9.11 vs 5.82 vs 5.33 per 100) to carry over.
