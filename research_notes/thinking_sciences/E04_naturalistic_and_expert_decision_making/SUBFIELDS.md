# Naturalistic and expert decision making: subfields

Naturalistic decision making (NDM) studies expert choices in real settings: firefighters, nurses, pilots, commanders, athletes. It grew from chess studies (de Groot, Chase and Simon) and stands against the heuristics and biases tradition. It divides into the recognition-primed decision model, mental simulation, sensemaking, expertise elicitation, the pre-mortem, and the debate on when intuition is valid. Opened facts: the RPD model has three levels (simple match, diagnosis, evaluation by mental simulation); in a sport review, 60 to 81 percent of decisions were simple matches, 13 to 28 percent diagnosis, 3 to 24 percent evaluation. Klein's evidence is from interviews and observation, not experiments, and a critic of that evidence finds the support for mental simulation weak.

## Dig into next

**1. Valid-intuition conditions as the S0 gate.**
- Can regularity and feedback volume be estimated per problem class from our logs?
- Should direct mode be allowed only where they hold?
- Evidence: Kahneman and Klein 2009 (I could not open it: the PubMed page returned no abstract), our class pass rates, an equal-token test.

**2. Mental simulation as a test without a code check.**
- Does a stepwise run of the plan, compared by code to the goal, find faults the author missed?
- How does this differ from banned unstructured reflection (G2)?
- Evidence: Klein's studies are interview-based, so we need our own controlled test with flip counts.

**3. Pre-mortem as an operator.**
- Reports of a 30 percent gain are secondary only; the original 1989 paper was not opened.
- Does it add distinct causes in an LLM, or only text?
- Evidence: the 1989 study, an LLM test measured by distinct failure causes and answer change.

## Does not transfer

Time pressure, stress, fear and command structure are about people. Interview methods need a human expert. Years of experience cannot be replaced by a prompt; the agent can only keep a record of solved cases. Klein's findings are case-based, so effect sizes are scarce.
