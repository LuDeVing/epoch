## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Real incident-style decisions.** We have no expert-rated item set for fireground or command decisions (K24, c26). Unblock by building a small set of scenarios with expert-ranked options, or by borrowing from sport or chess data with engine ratings.
- **Primary chess numbers.** Calderwood, Klein and Crandall (1988) and Klein et al. (1995) were not retrieved (d16). Unblock with library access. They are the best human tests of first-option quality.
- **Human premortem outcomes.** No controlled study of decision quality was found (d13, d14). Unblock by reading Mitchell et al. (1989) in full and searching for later replications.
- **Direct human test of the simulation step.** None found (b29). Unblock by a targeted search of cognitive-psychology work on mental simulation and plan checking, or accept that this stays an LLM-only question.
- **Newer models.** Many cited numbers use 2023-era models (b17, b23, c12). Unblock by rerunning the world-simulator and plan-verification probes on the model we use.
- **Long-horizon open-ended tasks.** Retrieval grounding was shown on computer-use tasks with tutorials (d10). We have no reference store of that kind. Unblock by building a small reference store for one class.
- **Reasoning-model internals.** Whether a reasoning model "recognises" or retrieves cases cannot be seen from written traces (G10). Unblock by behavioural probes only (perturb the problem, watch which first candidate changes).
- **Fairness of the RPD prompt result.** a26's OR values and CIs were not re-checked (critique). Unblock by reading the full table before any comparison with our own numbers.

## Order to run
1. **E04.01-U13 (first sample vs mean of 8 samples).** It costs almost nothing, reuses existing logs, and tells us per class whether any recognise-first path can work.
2. **E04.01-U1 with U2 (one candidate plus sound check vs best-of-N, gated).** It is the central equal-token question of the subfield and the one every other item depends on.
3. **E04.01-U4 (rollout depth per class, imagined vs executed).** It sets the rollout cap that the schema currently holds as a placeholder, and the literature conflicts on it.
