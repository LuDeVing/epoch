## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Primary source and sample behind the "80% first option" figure (Johnson and Raab 2003 is paywalled). Unblock: obtain the full text through a library or the authors.
- Studies and Ns behind Macquet 2020's 60-81% range. Unblock: the CRC Press chapter (doi 10.1201/9781351060073-12).
- Match-first vs analysis on real sport performance. No causal sport study exists; needs field data with outcomes.
- Controlled outcome trial of similar-case retrieval in sport (TacticAI evidence is five raters). Needs match results.
- Replication of Kopka and Feufel outside the ChatGPT family and on emergency cases. Needs a vignette set with many emergency cases.
- Any real LLM test of RPD on athlete decisions. Needs an expert-coded sport decision set; none was found.
- Perceptual expertise (eye movements, anticipation): no channel in a text agent. Would need a multimodal bench.
- Unopened items: Ivanova et al. 2025 chapter (finance), Musculus N=169, Roca d values second-hand.

## Order to run
1. U1 (RPD vs length-matched neutral, four arms): the only LLM result in this area has no length control, and it decides whether any RPD framing is worth a state.
2. U2 (direct-then-check vs reasoning-first): cheap, uses existing items, and the sign of the effect could change the mode rule in S0.
3. U3 (case retrieval vs none): the closest analogue to RPD's match step, and it tells us whether the memory library earns its setup cost.
