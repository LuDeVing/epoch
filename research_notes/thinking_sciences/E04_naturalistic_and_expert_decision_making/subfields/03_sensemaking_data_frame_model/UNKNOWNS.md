## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **What the founding papers actually say** (seven activities, the three-frame limit, anchor rule). Missing: a legal full text of Klein, Moon, Hoffman 2006 and Klein et al. 2007. Would unblock: rewriting every "Klein says" line from the primary.
- **Human effect sizes** for Bruner and Potter 1964, Tolcott 1989, Lord 1984, Lehner 2008. Missing: full texts. Would unblock: sizing how large early-frame stickiness is in people, to compare with LLM anchoring.
- **Whether the human three-frame limit exists at all.** Missing: a lab study with story tasks and enough participants. Pontis had 11 people and structure tasks.
- **Plausibility transition model** predictions (individual differences, abrupt gap closure, gap detection). Missing: any experiment; the model is case-based with one coder.
- **Thinking-mode interaction.** Missing: current reasoning-model data on sequential fixation outside medicine. Would unblock: whether the ledger still pays when thinking is on. Needs our own run (U2).
- **Mechanism of residue and anchoring.** Activation patching works on open models only (b23, b18). Missing: access to internals for the models we use.
- **Why Pro and Flash react in opposite directions to more hypotheses** (d02). Missing: a larger, token-matched study.
- **Agent-trace residue after reframe.** Missing: logs where a reframe is marked. Our harness logs doubt steps and could add a reframe marker; then analysed offline.
- **Comparison of triggers for reframing in one study.** Missing anywhere in the literature; only our own bench can supply it.
- **Learned rolling memory** (d18) as an upper bound for the ledger. Needs RL training, which is off the table.

## Order to run

1. **U2 (code cue ledger vs recap on sequential items)**: best evidence, cheapest to build, and the answer decides whether S2 and S3 need a ledger at all.
2. **U3 (O8 trigger comparison)**: the flip counts are already logged, and the result fixes the one rule the schema still lacks (when to reframe).
3. **U1 (2 scored rival frames vs 1)**: the only open item with large expected gain and a clear negative-result prior, so it needs a fair equal-token run before anyone re-proposes it.
