## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Reflexion as a bias amplifier.** Only one paper (SACD tables). Needs a Reflexion arm built; our schema does not use one, so low priority.
- **Large human replications of availability, representativeness, base-rate neglect, Linda rates.** No data found. Needs a literature pull of preregistered replications; this affects how much weight the original human effect sizes deserve.
- **Kahneman-Klein analogue for LLMs** (regular domain plus feedback means a trustworthy first answer). Needs a set of domains graded by regularity and a measure of first-answer accuracy per domain. Our class record in memory could supply this over time.
- **Accuracy against ground truth in planning, forecasting and code review.** Published work mostly counts decision flips. We need our own tasks with known outcomes.
- **Frontier models with contamination control.** Needs freshly generated items and a model release not seen in training; we can build the items but cannot prove non-exposure.
- **Activation steering and probes.** Bias directions are linearly readable (d13), but need open weights and access to activations. Not usable in a prompt-only scaffold.
- **Truncated d15 record** (seek-disconfirming instruction side effects). Re-open arXiv 2611.05720 before quoting any number.
- **Corrected a26 numbers.** The human-like rates in a26 are unreliable (critique item 10). Use c13 only.

## Order to run

1. **E04.09-U11 and E04.09-U14 together (bias pilot with stability check).** Cheap, and they tell us which biases are live in our model at all and whether our probes can be trusted, so every other test is sized correctly.
2. **E04.09-U2 (input hygiene vs wrong hint).** The harm is established in four papers and the schema already contains the remedy as [S]; a pass promotes it to [E] and protects every later state.
3. **E04.09-U1 (counter-anchor vs CoT vs ignore-instruction) with U3 (reasoning cap) run on the same items.** Both use the same anchor and cue item set, so the cost is shared, and they decide whether a new operator and a G11 amendment are needed.
