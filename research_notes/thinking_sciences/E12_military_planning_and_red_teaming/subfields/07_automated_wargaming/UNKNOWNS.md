## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human expert baseline for plan verdicts: only Lamparth has one, and it is a Taiwan Strait game. Need human ratings of our seeded-flaw plans.
- Cranfield/AIAA studies (Yuksek 2023, Schwartz 2020): full text blocked (403). Unblock with institutional access or authors' preprints.
- 2026 frontier replications of Rivera and Lamparth protocols: none found; search budget and API rate limits blocked the gap search. Rerun searches later.
- Real combat simulators (DAVINCI, StarCraft-like): we have none on the bench. Unblock with a small executable scenario simulator.
- Validity of LLM adjudication in open-ended games: needs human adjudicator panels.
- RAND 1950s and RSAS evidence base: needs primary archives (Pauly, Emery sources).
- Payne's escalation ladder validity and the independence of agents within a run (bootstrap width).

## Order to run
1. **U1 (propose-run-revise vs propose-once vs self-critique):** cheapest, strongest prior (K21), directly decides whether S4/S5 default to checker-driven revision for plans.
2. **U2 (k verdicts, median vs single):** one run gives the cost-quality curve and the plateau; sets the budget rule for every simulated test.
3. **U3 (adversary diversity at equal calls):** the only unsettled piece of the G4/G9 evidence, and the one that decides how red-teaming is built.
