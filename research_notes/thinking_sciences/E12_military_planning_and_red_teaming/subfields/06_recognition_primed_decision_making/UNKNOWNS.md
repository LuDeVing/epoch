## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Expert-labelled fireground-style cases with workable options. Missing: a labelled case set. Unblock: have experts write and label 50 to 100 incident cases, or adapt an existing military or incident dataset.
- Controlled human outcome data on keep-first versus comparison (none exists; a14 shows the planned test was dropped). Unblock: an expert panel as proxy rater, or a rerun of the literature search once quota allows.
- Whether a model's first option matches expert first options. Unblock: the same labelled case set.
- Gaps 7, 8 and 9 were blocked by search quota and 429 errors, so literature may exist. Unblock: rerun the search.
- Head-to-head of draft-agreement gating with Thinkless and Dualformer. Missing: trained gate checkpoints and a shared eval. Unblock: open checkpoints.
- Adversary behaviour in real red-team settings. Missing: an adversary simulator with known best replies. Unblock: build a seeded wargame set.

## Order to run
1. U3 with U1 (measure first-option rate, then keep-first vs compare-N at equal tokens): decides whether keep-first mode is worth having at all, and where.
2. U2 (draft-agreement gate on free-text items): the cheapest training-free typicality gate, and it feeds U1.
3. U4 (written expectancies with code-side violation check): the human mechanism that fits G1 and G2 best, and it is cheap to build.
