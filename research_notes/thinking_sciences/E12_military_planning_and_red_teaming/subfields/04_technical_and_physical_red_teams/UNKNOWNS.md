## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Whether red-team findings reduce later failure in organisations: no retest data exists. A bench proxy is U7.
- Human purple-team and white-team roles: no study; our bench has no second human.
- Live-network comparison with humans: needs a controlled range and professionals; ARTEMIS is the only data (n=10).
- Physical-security counts beyond TSA and GAO: public data is classified or blocked; would need GAO and NRC pages or FOIA records.
- Same-model, equal-token comparison of a tuned plain agent against a scaffold on a common benchmark with CIs: needs our own runs.
## Order to run
1. E12.04-U1 (red role vs vote): decides whether any adversary role earns a place; cheap, uses existing ground-truth items.
2. E12.04-U2 (stop rule): loops are the cited top failure and the change is code-only.
3. E12.04-U4 (decoy and injection marking): large effect reported, tiny code change, safety value for tool tasks.
