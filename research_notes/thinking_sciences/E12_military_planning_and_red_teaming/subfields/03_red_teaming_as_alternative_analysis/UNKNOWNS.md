# Red teaming as alternative analysis: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: equal total tokens in every arm, same items, three seeds, paired bootstrap 95% interval. Always include a plain self-consistency arm and a noise-placebo arm (irrelevant revision prompt of equal length). Net flips = wrong-to-right minus right-to-wrong.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Pooled human effects of devil's advocacy and dialectical inquiry (Schwenk 1990) and authentic vs assigned dissent (Nemeth 2001). Both returned HTTP 403. Unblock: obtain the PDFs by library access, then extract numbers. Until then no human effect size is used.
- What the UFMCS Red Team Handbook lists as tools, and any outcome data for Ipcha Mistabra, ADSO or the DSB 2003 report. Seven URLs failed. Unblock: a copy of the handbook from a mirror or archive.
- Team A / Team B versus a single devil's advocate on forecasting. No LLM study found. Unblock: a forecasting item set with resolved outcomes, then run the arms.
- A frontier-scale, different-family challenger at equal tokens. Published tests use 7-9B open models. Unblock: our own runs in U4.
- Whether reasoning-trained models make explicit challenge steps redundant (Kim 2026 shows internal conflict and reconciliation; abstract only). Unblock: run U1 and U2 on a reasoning model and a non-reasoning model of the same family.
- Whether a pre-mortem helps in real plans with long horizons. Only WebArena and safety results exist. Unblock: a multi-day agent benchmark with graded outcomes.
- Setting challenge intensity from a cheap signal (pool diversity, verifiability). Nothing published. Unblock: collect pool diversity during U1 and U10 and fit.

## Order to run

1. E12.03-U1 (verifiability gate): it decides whether any challenge step is worth its tokens, and every other test depends on its strata.
2. E12.03-U3 (code-located failing step with a random-location and resample control): the cleanest version of "attack tied to a check", and it settles the confound in the strongest single-source result.
3. E12.03-U2 (other-agent framing plus Wait): nearly free to run, and the effect on non-reasoning models in the evidence is large.
