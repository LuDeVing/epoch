## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Martin and Schwartz 2005 full text (Ns, effect sizes, exact manipulation). Unblock: obtain the PDF through a library; then design U4 as a faithful analogue.
- Hutchins's calculator episode and any error rate for the navigation team. Unblock: book text; not needed for the schema, framing only.
- Original effect sizes for Ward 2017 and Sparrow 2011 memory experiments, so original versus replication pairs can be closed. Unblock: read the originals and the Acta Psychologica 2022 replication.
- Any measure of load in an LLM (not accuracy). Unblock: a proxy such as length-controlled error rate on a fixed ladder of difficulty, with a method agreed first.
- Which SWE-agent features matter, with variance, on current models. Unblock: a public feature ablation by another group, or a run on our harness (U5).
- Whether the social setting reduces load for LLMs with different models or roles. Unblock: an equal-compute study of heterogeneous models; present evidence uses copies of one model.
- Human offloading-harm family (phone, Google). Not a schema item; skip unless a design needs it.
- Why CoThinker loses on instruction following (0.95 vs 1.03). Unblock: rerun with a gate and an equal-token single-chain control.

## Order to run

1. U1 (program-held log vs transcript): cheapest to build, highest chance of a real gain, and it touches every multi-step task.
2. U2 (validator vs prompted rules): cheap code, a clean binary measure (violation rate), and the human evidence is strong.
3. U3 (probe actions with an information-gain picker): tests the one idea in this subfield that is new to the schema, and its gain or loss is easy to count at equal tokens.
