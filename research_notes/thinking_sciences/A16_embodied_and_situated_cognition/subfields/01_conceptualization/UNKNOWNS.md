## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Causal mediation for perceptual concepts.** We need open weights and activation access. Unblock: run a probe and edit study on an open model for colour or size concepts.
- **Vision versus scale in GPT-4 over GPT-3.5.** Needs a matched pair of models that differ only in image training. Unblock: use open matched pairs (Llama text vs Llama Vision, as in Roy et al.) and test on our concept items.
- **Many Smiles pooled effects and the Coles bias-corrected effect.** Needs the full papers or the psymetadata R dataset. Not relevant for the agent, only for judging the human literature.
- **Petilli 2024 blind vs sighted data and abstract concepts in blind groups.** Paper not located. Unblock: fetch the University of Milano-Bicocca repository PDF.
- **Frontier-model performance on PIQA, VEC and affordance tasks (2025-2026).** Not measured here. Unblock: run our model on these public sets.
- **Equal-token comparison of imagery prompts.** No published study. Unblock: U1 and U3 above.
- **Human-coded replication of the grounding-gap result.** The source study used an LLM judge. Unblock: code a 100-item sample by hand.
- **Embodied agents (robotics, real sensors).** Out of scope for a text-only scaffold. Unblock: only if the agent gets tool or sensor access.

## Order to run

1. **A16.01-U1 with U11:** the state-table test and the code-checked state variant. They have the best prior evidence (GPT-4 gains of 5 to 10 points) and cost almost nothing, and they split the effect into trace versus correctness.
2. **A16.01-U2:** code or simulator step against an imagery prompt. The only intervention with a large reported gain (+27.9 points), and our bench has code execution already.
3. **A16.01-U7:** probe of our agent on motor and sensory norms, with a tool-check arm. It is a cheap diagnostic that tells us whether the "text carries part, not all" finding holds for our model and decides if a routing rule is worth adding.
