## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Original human data.** Primary text of Chambers and Reisberg 1985 and the original gesture-capacity and tic-tac-toe studies (K20, K35) were never opened. Unblock: get the papers; low value for the schema.
- **Independent replication of typed state.** Needs a second lab or our own run with another model family. Our bench covers this via U2, but only on our models.
- **Small model plus exact tool vs larger model alone at equal cost.** Needs the cost model and small current models on the bench. Mind's Eye is 2022 zero-shot; ReTool and SMART are trained.
- **Trained-regime tool results (ReTool, SMART, MeCo hidden-state probes).** We do not train and may use closed APIs. Unblock: an open model with hidden-state access, or skip.
- **Physical-interactivity analogue.** No agent test of "acting on the structure gives no gain over thinking about it." Nearest leads: code-only 80.3 vs question-only 80.6 (GPT-4o), tool offers -4.0 on Mistral-7B MATH. Unblock: add a no-gain control item type to E3 (task answerable by thinking alone with a tool offered).
- **Tetris signature in LLMs.** Needs a rotate-to-see style task where an agent can probe and undo. Unblock: build a small reversible-probe environment (e.g. grid puzzle with undo) and log probes.
- **Chambers-Reisberg style reinterpretation in LLMs.** No test of whether a rewritten (externalised) representation lets the model reframe where its first reading sticks. Unblock: pairs of ambiguous problem statements, with and without a forced re-represent step.
- **Position-paper benchmark (acquire / transform / probe, told vs not told which).** Not published as data. Unblock: build a small version on our bench.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (scratchpad on top of native thinking, with filler control).** It decides whether S2 and S4 notes survive at all on our base model, and it is cheap: one extra arm on the existing bench.
2. **U6 (real code run vs thinking only vs predicted result).** The best-supported lever (PAL ablation 23.2 vs 72.0) and the cheapest to implement; tells us if execution adds beyond hidden thinking.
3. **U5 (router for when notes hurt) with U4 (gate) logged in the same run.** Both are about not acting when acting costs (-23 on implicit items, -3 to -14 from unneeded calls); a positive result saves tokens everywhere.
