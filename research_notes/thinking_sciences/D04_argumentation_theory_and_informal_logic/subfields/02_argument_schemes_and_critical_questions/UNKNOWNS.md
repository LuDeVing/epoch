## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Exact Walton 2008 wording** for analogy and cause (and the full 96-scheme list). The book was never opened. Unblock: get the book text (library or purchase) and code the lists; check the 39.44% hand-edit rate on our slots.
- **2026 frontier and reasoning models on CQ writing.** The public leaderboard has no retrievable scores. Unblock: run the public validation set ourselves, or wait for a new shared task.
- **Real human accuracy gains from CQs.** All human studies are about criteria people use, not whether a CQ improves judgement. Unblock: none soon; do not infer it.
- **Mizrahi versus Walton on expert opinion.** The underlying expertise studies were not read. Unblock: read Mizrahi and the cited expertise studies.
- **Model-size effect on judging CQs.** Evidence is mixed (CriticBench near chance; proof checking within 10%). Unblock: run U3 on 2-3 model sizes we can afford.
- **Long-form and multi-turn arguments.** All results are on short debate interventions. Unblock: a corpus of multi-step reasoning traces from our own agent.
- **Whether 2026 sycophancy numbers are real.** SycoBench, omission blindness and proof-verification results are single-source with model names we cannot cross-check. Unblock: re-run a small version of each on models we use.
- **Overlap between schemes' CQ lists.** Nobody measured it. Unblock: code the lists and count shared question types (cheap, but needs the book).

## Order to run

1. **D04.02-U13 (count schemes in our traces).** Costs nothing and tells us which lists are worth building; it can kill U5 and U11 early.
2. **D04.02-U2 (CQ step versus best-of-N at equal tokens).** The single biggest unknown; if the CQ step cannot beat voting on hard items, the rest of the subfield is a measurement tool rather than a schema element.
3. **D04.02-U1 together with U11 on the seeded set.** One build of the seeded set serves both and answers whether scheme-specific lists are worth any more than a generic checklist; U3 reuses the same set next.
