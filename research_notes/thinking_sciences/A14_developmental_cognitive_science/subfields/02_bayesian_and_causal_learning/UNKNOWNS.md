## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Independent reproduction of the 2025-26 blicket LLM findings: all located papers share a research circle (K30). Unblock by running U1 and U2 ourselves on our model.
- Exploration versus evidence reading in frontier models (K04): needs oracle test results fed to the model next to self-chosen tests. Unblock by building an oracle-feed mode into the blicket harness.
- Original pass rates of Gopnik 2001, Kushnir and Gopnik 2005 and Schulz 2007: need the primary papers; human data only, so no bench effect.
- Whether Samiei's active/passive effect comes from running the test or from planning (K07): needs a proposer-only arm with hidden outcomes.
- Child-like flat priors in an LLM: no known prompt produces one. Needs a measure first (rate of choosing the unusual rule on ambiguous evidence).
- Bayesian teaching by fine-tuning: out of scope (no training).
- Open-ended hypothesis spaces: blicket has 32 hypotheses; real tasks do not. Needs a task with a checkable but unbounded hypothesis space.

## Order to run
1. U1 (form-listing A/B): cheapest, one prompt line, and it decides whether the sampling gain (U2) is real or just listing.
2. U3 (code-ranked tests vs model-chosen): fits G5, mostly code, and the class has two sources behind it.
3. U4 (forced falsifying test): known token cost, so it must be tested at equal tokens before anything else touches S4.
