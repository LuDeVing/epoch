## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **A real replication of the Cook contrast.** We have no preschoolers. OSF, Many Labs and CogSci proceedings are unchecked. Unblock: search the registries, and ask whether anyone with developmental access wants a registered replication. [K25]
- **Which human reading is right (A: prior knowledge of isolation, B: trial and error, C: learning in the session).** Needs children with isolation trained first, or the splitting act given for free. Our agent cannot answer this. [K31]
- **Figure values in GX-Chen (Q&A gain from hypothesis sampling) and Jhaveri correlations.** Unblock: tables from authors or reruns of their code. [critique]
- **Pan 2025 on DeepSeek-R1 vs humans.** The paper contradicts itself. Unblock: rerun R1 on Little Alchemy 2 or ask the authors. [d05]
- **Alderete model names and p-values** were not re-checked. Unblock: read the full tables. [critique]
- **Opened-only-by-abstract items** (Lapidow and Walker, van Schijndel, Koksal, Legare, Ruggeri, Bramley, Sobel). Do not cite for numbers. Unblock: open the PDFs. [d02, d14-d19]
- **Culture, expertise, time-pressure moderators.** None found. Unblock: new literature search.
- **A design that keeps ambiguity but removes expected gain.** The Cook confound. No such human study exists. [b26]
- **Contamination.** The blicket paradigm is in training data (LaMDA cited the authors). Unblock: new machine names and new rule families; check with a leak probe. [critique]
- **Whether an LLM's vignette answer predicts live behaviour.** Needs matched vignette and live items. [K20]

## Order to run

1. **A14.03-U1 with U2 (gate vs always vs never at several tie rates).** This is the direct LLM version of the Cook contrast, nobody has run it, and one toy harness answers both. It tells us whether S7's trigger is worth building.
2. **A14.03-U3 (code-chosen vs model-chosen test at equal tokens).** The best-backed gain (Zendo +1.8 of 8) has never been measured at equal tokens with a current model. If it holds, it is a safe default.
3. **A14.03-U4 (enumerate-and-eliminate on AND rules).** Cheap to build in code, targets the best-documented LLM failure (the OR bias), and shares the harness with U1 to U3.
