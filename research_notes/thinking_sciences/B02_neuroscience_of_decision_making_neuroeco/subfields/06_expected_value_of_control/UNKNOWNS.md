## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Brain-level claims (dACC computes EVC): not testable on an agent and not needed.
- Human trait links (Need for Cognition, self-control) to the agent's behaviour: no trait analogue; unblocking would need many agent variants, which is not a goal.
- Generalisation beyond maths and the current bench to agentic, open-ended and long-horizon tasks: needs a bench with graded difficulty and checks for those tasks.
- Pre-generation activation probes for difficulty (Bulut suggests they may beat text features): needs model internals; unblock by testing on an open model.
- Underthinking numbers (225% more tokens in wrong answers): needs the paper text opened (d21 is summary only).
- Hagger full text and last third of Vohs: only needed if we decide a felt-effort route (fatigue moderator) matters; low priority.
- Recursive value (spending on S1/S2 to save cost later): needs per-state cost accounting and logs of downstream savings.
- Whether the reward x efficacy interaction is real in humans: needs an independent lab; not ours to run.

## Order to run
1. B02.06-U1 with U2 as a fourth arm: one cheap prompt test, answers the largest gap (does a price or efficacy line move allocation beyond a random-length control).
2. B02.06-U3 and U5 together: one budget sweep gives the knee and the first-pass difficulty signal, and both feed every other rule.
3. B02.06-U6, after U3/U5: tests routed allocation against uniform and random at realised equal cost, which is the decision that matters for S0; run U4 on the same items to price a wrong call.
