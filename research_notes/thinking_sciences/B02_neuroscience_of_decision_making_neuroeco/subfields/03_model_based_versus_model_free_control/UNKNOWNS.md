## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human cost-benefit arbitration with story instructions and a payoff gap.** No sample combines them (K22). Unblock: a human study; we cannot run it. Until then treat the human incentive effect as supported only with extensive instructions (d02).
- **A direct test of the Keramati VPI rule on humans.** Only simulations and one n = 15 per group study exist (d05). Unblock: a preregistered time-cost manipulation. No bench action.
- **Larger replication of Keramati 2016 and Otto 2013.** None found in 137 and 252 citing records (d05, d06, d07).
- **A trained gate for our own model.** AdaptThink, Thinkless, Paglieri and He and Feng all need training or logits. Unblock: open-weight fine-tuning or logit access; our bench uses a closed API.
- **Calibrated posterior over a cached answer.** The VPI step needs it. An LLM has none. Unblock: a probe or ensemble that gives a usable posterior; sample disagreement is the cheap stand-in tested in U2.
- **Whether LLM two-step scores are skill or recall.** Contested (c18). Unblock: unseen-structure probes with fresh names and rules.
- **Predicting the Goldilocks frequency.** No rule exists (d14, d15). Unblock: a pilot across several tasks and models to find a predictor.
- **Any study the last search pass missed.** APIs returned 429 and search was out of quota, so gaps 1, 2 and 5 may have studies we did not see (critique). Unblock: re-run the search when quota returns.

## Order to run

1. **U1 with U12 (gain-minus-cost gate and its ledger).** This is the open experiment for S0 (K24) and the one most likely to change the schema; it also gives the gate-cost number for free.
2. **U2 (signal head-to-head).** It decides which signal U1 should use, costs only extra scoring on the same items, and is the cheapest way to confirm or kill the sample-disagreement hint.
3. **U5 (changed-conditions diff).** The stale-cache failure is established for LLMs (d18-d20) and our lessons store makes it live; a pass here protects memory before it grows.
