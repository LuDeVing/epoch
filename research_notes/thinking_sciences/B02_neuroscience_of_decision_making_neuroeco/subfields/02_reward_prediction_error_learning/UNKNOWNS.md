## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Original 1990s numbers (Schultz 1993; Mirenowicz and Schultz 1994: neuron counts, monkeys, effect sizes). Needs full-text reading, no bench test.
- Body text of Gershman et al. 2024 (paywalled): which findings it says generalised prediction error cannot explain.
- Steering or causal tests of LLM TD-like features (needs hidden-state access and an open model with an SAE; Zou 2026 and Demircan 2024 only lesion or probe).
- Reasoning-trained versus base LLMs on the two-step task at equal tokens (no published comparison; needs a two-step task harness on our bench).
- Reward-loop versus best-of-N at equal tokens with an external hard verifier (no public result; this is U3, runnable once the bench has matched-token accounting for a one-context loop).
- Human pharmacology replications (Deserno 2021 and other levodopa studies); not relevant to the bench, kept for completeness.
- Whether current frontier models (not Claude v1.3, Qwen 7B to 9B) show the same optimism asymmetry; needs a current-model bandit harness (U4, U5).

## Order to run
1. B02.02-U6 (code-computed summaries in multi-attempt items): the best-supported mechanism (established in bandits), cheap to build, and it directly tests whether the code-selects principle (G5) extends past bandits.
2. B02.02-U2 (one-line failure note vs raw failures): reuses the S5 retry loop already in the schema, established in two sources, and the result decides how failed attempts are shown everywhere.
3. B02.02-U1 and U3 together (label vs no label, loop vs best-of-N): same item set and harness as U2; they decide whether reward annotation and one-context history belong in the schema at all, and whether they conflict with G4.
