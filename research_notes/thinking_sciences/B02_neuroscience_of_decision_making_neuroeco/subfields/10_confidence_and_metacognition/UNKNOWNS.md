## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Token logprobs.** DeepConf and CGES need them (c23, d07). The API we use may not return them. Unblock: check provider support; or run on an open model for a side test.
- **Probe-based confidence (AUROC 0.95, d05).** Needs hidden states. Unblock: an open-weight model run, only to learn the ceiling.
- **Human data on BA10 at large N.** Cannot be tested here and does not change the schema. Unblock: none needed.
- **Frontier reasoning models at scale.** Our bench cannot cover 17 tasks and many models. Unblock: reuse Del's task list on 2 or 3 models we can call.
- **Equal-token pricing of signals.** Needs a single token accounting across sampling, verifier calls and judge calls. Unblock: the harness must log tokens per component.
- **Semantic clustering of long free-form answers.** Needs a cheap entailment judge. Unblock: a small NLI model or a rule-based normaliser per task type.
- **Hedging after 2025 post-training (Yona's metric).** Needs an LLM-judge pipeline for the cMFG metric. Unblock: reimplement with a fixed judge.
- **Introspection (K16).** Self-prediction works for short behaviours (+17 points), fails for long outputs. We cannot test injection (needs weights). Unblock: test the short-behaviour self-prediction only, if wanted.

## Order to run

1. **B02.10-U1 (signal ranking).** It decides whether the 4b wording changes and which cheap trigger every other test uses; also builds the metrics layer (AUROC, ECE, selective curve) that all later tests need.
2. **B02.10-U2 (weighted vote and adaptive stop).** Largest expected saving in tokens (20 to 40% or more) and uses the same samples collected in U1.
3. **B02.10-U3 (hide own answer in verify).** Cheap to build (one prompt change), a large effect in the only study (13.1% vs 32.5% change of mind), and the gap is still open in the literature.
