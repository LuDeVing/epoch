## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Original chess numbers.** Klein et al. (1995), Calderwood et al. (1988) and Johnson and Raab (2003) were blocked or unread. Unblock: library access or author copies. Until then the "chess players use RPD" claim has no first-hand number.
- **Independent replication of the 80% figure.** None found. Unblock: a sample-based count in aviation, ICU or military with neutral coding.
- **Decay with distance from stored cases.** DiLu has three settings. Unblock: a similarity-graded item set on our bench, which needs a distance measure for our item classes.
- **Human novice-expert split mapped to LLM item classes.** Referee data show the split for people. For the agent we need classes with known plain pass rates across several models; unblock: a multi-model pass-rate table.
- **Recognition vs evidence accumulation.** Speed-quality links in chess fit drift-diffusion as well as recognition (Sunde). Not separable on a text bench.
- **Reconciliation of source figures.** Schultz +68 vs +300 Elo row pairing and the Monroe 93.5% vs Ruoss 95.4% puzzle gap. Unblock: read the paper figures. Does not change the schema.
- **Unverified Vidulich and Tsang group means** (tables stripped from the text). Unblock: PDF tables. Does not change the schema.
- **Frontier-model behaviour.** Most LLM evidence here is older models or small chess-trained transformers. Unblock: rerun Kopka-style and Hagendorff-style items on the current model.
- **Whether a chess-style "expert pattern library" exists inside an LLM at all.** Probes show board state and skill are stored in small trained models (Karvonen). No evidence for a chat model on general tasks. Unblock: probing needs open weights.

## Order to run

1. **U1 and U2 together (agreement gate and switch-signal comparison).** Same items, same logging. This sets the S0 mode rule and is the best-supported signal, so the cost is small and the gain is the largest.
2. **U7 (cheap first attempt plus code check vs full graph, by difficulty bin).** It finds the break-even pass rate that S0 needs and tests G11/G12 on our own data.
3. **U5 (similarity threshold for stored cases).** It is the operational form of "recognition fails in unusual situations" and guards memory before the subgoal library grows.
