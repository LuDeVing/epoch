## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human mechanism (certainty vs ambivalence vs basis).** Needs human data or a preregistered multi-site study. We can only test agent analogues (U4). Unblock: an outside-lab replication.
- **Outside-lab, preregistered replication of Bizer and Petty 2005.** Not found (search was limited). Unblock: a full literature search with working APIs.
- **Token cost of restatement in published work.** No paper reports it. Our own logs (U1, U2) will give it.
- **Hidden reasoning traces.** Whether a stable final answer still carries framing in the trace needs trace access.
- **Long-context analogue of memory-load nullification.** Needs a long-context bench with matched framed items.
- **Models we cannot call.** Frontier closed models may not be in our bench. Unblock: an API budget for U9.
- **Real-stakes agent settings.** Human effects need personal relevance; no agent analogue of relevance is defined.

## Order to run
1. **U1** (restate vs instruction vs thinking at equal tokens): it decides whether S1 restatement stays and measures the main cost. Cheap: paired items already exist in the literature.
2. **U2** (constraint-preservation check): restatement is only safe if it does not distort. Cheap, code-only check.
3. **U3** (restating the challenge in multi-turn pushback): the biggest measured failure (GPT-4o 55.85% to 27.32%) and the closest to the support/oppose resistance question; also tests the cost of blocking valid corrections.
