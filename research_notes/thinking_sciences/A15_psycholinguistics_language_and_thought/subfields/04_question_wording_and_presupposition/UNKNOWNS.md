## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **The true human effect size.** The multi-lab Loftus-Palmer replication (75+ labs) has no result and no N published; the Goldschmied 2017 sample size was not seen. Unblocks when the result is posted. It sets how much weight to give any human number here.
- **Frontier-model sizes of the multi-turn contamination effect.** Evidence is open models plus three 2026 models, with big provider differences (0.0% to 37.9%). Unblocks with API access to the exact model we ship and a long-run bench item set with planted premises.
- **Whether LLM judges used in the false-premise studies bias results.** Cancer-Myth and others use LLM or expert labels. We need our own human-checked labels for a 100-item sample.
- **Warning timing in LLMs.** No study found. Needs only U7, but the post-answer arm needs a second call and is tied to the "no unstructured self-reflection" exclusion in SCHEMA 4b; it must be a fixed one-line check, not a free reflection.
- **Which of presupposition strength, authority and repetition drives the dose-response.** No single study varies all three. Needs a factorial item set (about 5 x 3 x 3 cells). Unblocks after U10 and U11.
- **Whether Loftus-Palmer effects exist for estimate items.** Our bench is mostly exact-answer items. Needs a small estimation item set (speed, size, count) with a known truth.
- **The Loftus, Schooler and Wagenaar reply and the Blank and Launay 2014 meta-analysis** were seen only as snippets or secondary summaries. Read them before relying on the warning moderators.
- **Order effects in self-check lists** (Michael and Garry, K23). Contested and human-only. Needs a randomise-order test on a calibration metric.

## Order to run

1. **U12 (foil design), then U1 (verb ladder).** U12 is the scoring rule every other test depends on, and U1 is the direct test nobody has published. Both are cheap (no new infrastructure) and decide whether wording is a real problem for our models.
2. **U2 (neutral rewrite).** Highest expected gain per token: harm is established (K16), the remedy has the best positive evidence (d02), and one rewrite call is cheap.
3. **U8 (rebuild at checkpoints).** Contamination persistence is the largest effect in the newest evidence (retraction restored 2 of 14, rebuild 12 of 14) and it affects every long run; the fix is code-only.
