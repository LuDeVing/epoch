## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Prompt-reachable version of activation patching (Lee 2025: 30-38% of failures recovered on 7-14B open models). Needs open-weights models and a probe harness; unblock by running our bench on a Gemma or Qwen model with hidden-state access.
- Whether the human surface bias exists in our embedding retriever. Needs a retrieval log with relational vs same-domain ground truth. Unblock with the U1 case library, which doubles as the diagnostic.
- Replication of Jamrozik 2020 and Lupyan 2007 with sample sizes. Needs the primary papers' full text for the failed replication (b10, abstract only) and the 2026 Lupyan reviews (d11 abstract only).
- Whether labels help learners that lack the relation concept (infant nulls, a13). Models lack no vocabulary, so this has no analogue unless we use invented relations; test with made-up relation names defined in-context.
- Perception and timing effects (Lupyan label feedback, 1600 ms window). No text analogue; park.
- Gerson & Woodward 2014 (labels help action goals) not opened; resolves the infant contradiction but not relevant to our agent.
- Reasoning models on relation naming: all LLM naming evidence is 2023-2024 models except ConceptARC. Unblock by running U4 and U9 on current models first.

## Order to run
1. U1 (relation name vs domain tag vs none in retrieval): cheapest, closest to the Jamrozik effect, and the case library doubles as the diagnostic for surface bias.
2. U9 (name step on far analogies with surface distractors): targets the best-established LLM failure (ARN far/near 29.1%), so a win has the biggest upside.
3. U3 (compare-then-name 2x2): tests the most replicated human structure (alignment plus label, additive), and its result decides whether O7 gets a naming step.
