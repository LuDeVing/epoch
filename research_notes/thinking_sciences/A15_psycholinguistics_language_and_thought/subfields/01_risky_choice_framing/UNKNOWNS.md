## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Exact human numbers: Kuhberger 1998, Steiger and Kuhberger 2018, Zhou 2021, McNeil 1982 and Many Labs 2 primary text were not read in full. Unblock: open the papers through an institutional or open-access route and replace the second-hand figures.
- Peer review: nearly all LLM framing results are 2025-2026 preprints. Unblock: wait for or check published versions; rerun the key number (FrameBench 34.20% to 6.68%) on our own models, which U1 does.
- Training-data contamination of the classic Asian disease text. Unblock: write novel isomorphs and compare with the classic text on the same model.
- Model-specific repeat noise and per-domain results for FrameBench. Unblock: the authors' released data, or our own run (U4).
- Full-agent-loop framing (tools, memory, multi-turn). Only Szeider (tool descriptions) and Yun (multi-turn QA) touch it. Unblock: a loop bench with seeded framed premises (U8).
- Why direction differs between models (GPT-4 versus GPT-3.5 on admissions). Unblock: run several model versions on one item set; needs access to the same models.
- Whether the fuzzy-trace gist account or the prospect-theory reference-point account fits LLM behaviour better. Unblock: U7 plus a numbers-deleted version of the item.
- Weight-level fixes (VALIGN, activation steering): need model weights, not usable on a hosted model.
- Time-pressure effect in humans (Svenson and Benson vs Guo, Diederich): unopened. Unblock: read the papers before using it to motivate U11.

## Order to run

1. U1 (canonical forms against instruction, self-rewrite and plain): it decides whether an S1 rewrite step exists at all and which form to build, and the same item set serves U2, U4 and U12.
2. U4 (flip versus repeat noise on our model, story and explicit formats): cheap, and it tells us whether a strong model needs the step. A null here ends the line before more is built.
3. U3 (both wordings in one context): cheap to run on the same items, tests the human two-thirds reduction in a model, and checks whether the model just copies the first wording.
