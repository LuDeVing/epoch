## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Classic Moses word swaps on 2026 models.** No published rate; we need our own item set with close and distant swaps. Unblock: build 50 items from the Hannon and Daneman style (primary text not obtained).
- **Primary rates for passives (Ferreira 2003) and the original Moses rate (Erickson and Mattson 1981).** Not opened. Unblock: pull the PDFs or use our own human-free baseline only.
- **Which mechanism causes the lingering misreading** (partial reanalysis, memory trace, noisy channel). Needs human or interpretability work; not needed for the behavioural tests above.
- **Human comparison on equal terms.** Human garden-path data were timed (400 ms per word, 5 s); models saw the full text. Unblock: no untimed human data; use item-order agreement only.
- **Frontier 2026 models on every trap.** Most LLM results are 2022-2025. Unblock: run the item sets above on our current model before trusting any published number.
- **Scaffold versus prompt tweak at equal tokens.** No published study. Our bench is the first test.
- **Real users instead of simulated users for clarifying questions.** LHAW, INTENT-SIM and EIG use simulated answerers. Unblock: a human-in-the-loop sample, or accept simulation limits.
- **NPI vs meaning illusion profile** (Zhang vs Paape, second source unread). Low value for the agent.

## Order to run

1. **A15.10-U1 (presupposition extractor with clean twins).** Cheapest, biggest known gap (flag rates 26-42% missed, Cancer-Myth false-alarm cost), and it fixes the scoring rule for U4 and U12 at the same time.
2. **A15.10-U3 (ask-when-critical vs assume vs always-ask).** The largest measured gain in the evidence (47% to 78%), but never measured per token; this decides whether S1 gets a clarify event.
3. **A15.10-U2 (role listing for passives and garden paths).** Closes the main open gap (thinking helps these items half as much) and shares its item set with U13.
