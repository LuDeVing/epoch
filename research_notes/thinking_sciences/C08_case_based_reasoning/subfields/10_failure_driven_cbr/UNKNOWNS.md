## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Independent modern replication of CHEF-style retrieval.** No ICCBR or LLM-agent paper measures it (d18 snippets only). Unblock: open the ICCBR 2020-2026 proceedings and CBR-LLM workshop papers; or run U1 ourselves.
- **Confabulated-lesson noise.** Judge-noise tests flip labels at random; Dixit shows real noise is a wrong but plausible cause. Unblock: build a bench of seeded wrong lessons written by a model.
- **Natural errors in frontier reasoning models.** Tsui and CorrectBench use injected errors or marginal gains; no natural-error study. Unblock: log our own flips per state (SCHEMA section 6) and analyse.
- **Quote-the-trace outside ALFWorld and HumanEval.** No study. Unblock: U2.
- **Learned reflection writers.** Retroformer and Reflect-Retry-Reward need training; we do not train. Unblock: a decision to train a small writer.
- **Classical sources not opened.** Schank 1982, Hammond's thesis and 1990 paper, Kolodner 1993, Schank and Leake 1989 full text. Unblock: library access.
- **Fang et al. 2026 and Yin and Leake 2026 not re-verified.** Unblock: re-open before relying on them.
- **Token-matched numbers for published reflection gains.** Most papers do not report cost. Unblock: re-run key conditions under our token ledger.

## Order to run

1. **U3 (reflect-retry vs equal-token resample):** cheapest, uses existing runs, and decides whether any lesson loop is worth its tokens (G7).
2. **U1 (CHEF-style entry vs plain note):** the central unmeasured claim of the whole subfield; a win would justify a new lesson format.
3. **U5 (judge-accuracy break-even):** sets the write-gate threshold for every other memory test, so it must be known before U7 and U8.
