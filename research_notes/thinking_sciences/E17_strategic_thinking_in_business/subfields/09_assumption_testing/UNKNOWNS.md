## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Real founder outcomes for LLM-proposed experiments (gap 7). Needs a field study or a log of experiments founders actually ran with later results.
- The 759-firm replication's revenue effect and panel fragility. Needs the full SMJ text or data; only the abstract was read.
- Whether the Otis low-performer harm reproduces with other advisers. Needs a second RCT or access to its data.
- Mitchell et al. (1989) premortem figure. Needs the original paper; do not cite until read.
- Wason 1960, Klayman and Ha 1987, McGrath and MacMillan 1995 were not opened. Needs the originals to check the second-hand numbers.
- Whether bias metrics depend on the LLM judge labelling guesses and tests (oracle accuracy 85% to 95%). Needs human-labelled samples.
- Open-ended business assumptions have no oracle. Needs a simulator with hidden ground truth, which we would have to build (U5).

3. U5 (assumption map vs plain): highest gain if it works, no LLM evidence exists, and U6 comes free from its arms.
