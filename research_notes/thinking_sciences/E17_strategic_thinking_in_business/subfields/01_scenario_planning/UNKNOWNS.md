## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Effect size of scenario planning on firm outcomes: needs full texts of Phelps 2001, Meissner and Wulf 2013, Cordova-Pozo and Rouwette 2023, and the 2020 black-box paper. Unblock: retry when API limits clear or get PDFs by hand.
- What Shell did in 1972-73 and which steps are original: needs Wack 1985 full text, Wilkinson and Kupers 2013 body, Chermack 2017. Unblock: library or purchase. Matters for whether the funnel can carry Wack's name.
- Human confidence conflict (Schoemaker 1993 vs Phadnis 2015): sample sizes and effects unseen. Unblock: full texts.
- Long-horizon strategic quality of scenarios: no ground truth on a bench with checkable answers. Unblock: a retrospective set with dated outcomes, such as the Polyscene boards.
- Whether Csaszar's ranking skill is real: N = 30 and 26, p = 0.063. Unblock: a larger prospective tournament, not ours to run.
- Social function of workshops (changing mental models): no analogue for a single agent. Unblock: multi-agent setting, but G9 bars debate.
- Time-R1 gain of 0.09 points and author attribution: unresolved. Unblock: variance data from the authors.
- Findings on Claude-class models for scenario tasks: none measured. Unblock: our own runs (U1-U12).

## Order to run

1. U3 (diversity forcing): cheapest, measured fully in code, and its result feeds every funnel test.
2. U2 (cut rule): reuses U3 drafts, tests the one step that makes the funnel a funnel, and is judged in code.
3. U1 (funnel vs best-of-N at an impasse): the gate. If it fails at equal tokens, the rest of the funnel work (U4, U6, U8, U11) stops, and only the code-side items (U3 forcing, U7 check) may stay.
