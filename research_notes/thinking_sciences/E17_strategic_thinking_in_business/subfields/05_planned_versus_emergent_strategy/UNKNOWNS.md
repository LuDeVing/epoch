## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Whether the older firm-level meta-analyses (Miller and Cardinal 1994, Brinckmann 2010) show planning helps less in dynamic settings. Needs the papers (publisher blocked).
- How Mintzberg and Waters (1985) measured deliberateness. Needs the full text or a faithful summary. Our own ledger (U1) does not depend on it.
- Numeric effects in Camuffo (2020) and Koning (2022). Needs the full papers.
- Original HBR trendslop figures. Only a search summary and a Zenodo pilot were seen.
- Whether current reasoning models still lose with fixed up-front plans. The ADaPT and ReAct numbers use GPT-3.5 and PaLM-540B. Unblock by rerunning a small ADaPT-style set on our own models (this is U2/U7).
- Business-strategy tasks without a hard verifier. We have none in the bench; a rubric-judged set with human-checked grading would be needed.
- Long-horizon business simulations (Vending-Bench, CEO-Bench, EcoGym). Running them needs the environments and long token budgets.
- Whether promoting an unplanned success helps in business settings. No LLM study found, and our bench has verifier-backed items only.

## Order to run
1. **U2 (replan timing).** Cheapest, uses the same items as U1, and the answer decides whether a gated trigger can be written into G1 now.
2. **U1 (plan-versus-trace diff).** The core Mintzberg gap and the largest possible gain; it reuses the logging from U2.
3. **U8 (verified-only lessons).** Backed by the strongest evidence of the three, cheap to run on existing S9 logs, and it fixes what S9 stores.
