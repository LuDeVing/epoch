## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Frontier closed models under unanimous wrong groups (GPT-5-class, o-series, large Claude) with a thinking-budget sweep. Unblock: API access to at least one such model and a budget parameter, or the owner of the bench running U3 on our own backbone.
- Direct solve-first versus read-first at equal tokens. No published test. Our U1 would be the first.
- Bond and Smith (1996) numbers and Asch 1951/1955 partner and group-size numbers. Unblock: library access to the papers. They matter little for the agent.
- Independent replication of Bose's naming gate (preprints.org 202608.0971 returned 403). Unblock: open that page or reproduce it ourselves (U11).
- Conformity as help in repeated agent loops (no LLM study). Unblock: a repeated-feedback bench task.
- Why model size and instruction tuning point opposite ways across studies. Unblock: one shared protocol across Weng and Hussain model pools.
- Whether peer-conformity and user-sycophancy share one mechanism. Only R^2 = 0.31 and activation patching on 7-9B models; needs open-weight probing we may not have.

## Order to run
1. **A17.06-U3** (baseline flip rate with no-peer control and two thinking budgets): cheap, tells us how large the problem is on our backbone, and decides whether U1 and U2 matter.
2. **A17.06-U1** (solve-first versus read-first at equal tokens): the most decision-relevant open question and the first direct test in the literature.
3. **A17.06-U2** (attack-aware derive step versus generic verify): largest measured effect (12 to 64-67 of 100) and the only mitigation named at the failure point.
