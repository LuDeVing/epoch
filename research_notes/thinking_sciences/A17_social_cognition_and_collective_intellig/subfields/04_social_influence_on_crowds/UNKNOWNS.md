# Social influence on crowds: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: both arms at equal total tokens; post-cutoff or private items; report accuracy, correct-to-incorrect and incorrect-to-correct flip rates, and log-variance of attempts. "Isolated + median/vote" means N attempts in fresh contexts (S3), pooled by code (G5).

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Direct replication of Lorenz 2011 in a large non-student sample.** Needs human participants. Unblock: read Da and Huang 2020 in full for effect sizes (d20 is abstract only); look for a many-labs-style replication.
- **Real incentives for LLM agents.** A frozen model has no stake. Unblock: a harness where the selection rule really is the incentive (U7), or RL training (out of scope; MAPoRL only for small models).
- **Reasoning (thinking-on) models under exposure.** Almost all LLM evidence is non-reasoning models of 7-8B or older. Unblock: run U1 and U2 on the reasoning mode.
- **Long-run collapse.** Semantic collapse appears at 200-1,000 rounds (c13). Our scaffold runs 1-2 rounds. Unblock: a long-run harness with logged embedding distance, only if we later add loops.
- **Non-numeric items.** Code, proofs, and puzzle-like items. Human studies are all estimation. Unblock: items with exact checks (U3).
- **Interaction of exposure with the schema's impasse detector (S6).** Does a stuck state change the value of seeing others? Unblock: log S6 events, then split U1 by stuck vs not stuck.
- **Training-cutoff contamination in forecasting crowds** (35.8% to 8.9% gap, c23). Unblock: fresh post-cutoff items for every forecast test.
- **Peer reasoning vs peer answer in frontier models.** Only open-model data (d10). Covered by U4.
- **Network structure with LLM agents (star vs symmetric).** Hub effect rho=0.92 in humans (c05); LLM version only with injected hubs (c15). Unblock: a star-topology run, only if we ever build networked attempts.

## Order to run

1. **A17.04-U1** (isolated + median vs one exposure round, frontier model, equal tokens). It is the only direct test of the central guardrail on our own model, and every other item depends on its answer.
2. **A17.04-U3** (exposure accepted only through an exact check). If any exposure helps, this is the form that is most likely to; it reuses U1's attempts.
3. **A17.04-U2** (own earlier samples as peers). It decides whether S5 revision should see earlier attempts; it also reuses U1's attempts and the same flip-rate logging.
