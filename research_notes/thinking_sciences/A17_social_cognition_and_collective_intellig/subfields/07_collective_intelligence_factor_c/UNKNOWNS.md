## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human c itself (social sensitivity, gender share, turn-taking from badges): needs human groups. Not applicable to our agent.
- A preregistered large replication of c versus member IQ: needs hundreds of human groups and matched tasks. Cannot be run here.
- Multi-family LLM group factor: needs access to several disjoint model families at scale; we have limited model choice.
- Why Graf and Barlow and Dennis found no c: Barlow and Dennis is paywalled and per-sample numbers of Credé and Howardson are unread. Unblock by opening the full texts.
- Token cost of the structured exchange: not reported in the source; our own measurement in U1 supplies it.
- Riedl 2021 SI and 2022 correction: needed to settle the 44% vs 27% question.
- Temperature as a driver of group success (x1.50 per unit): only in a preliminary grid; needs a controlled sweep.

## Order to run
1. U2 (crossover of single vs N agents): cheapest, reuses existing arms, and decides whether any multi-agent element is worth building.
2. U1 (structured shared-facts step): largest published effect (+.55 to +.76) and the most likely candidate to enter the schema; also measures its token cost.
3. U3 (scaffold factor): needs only runs we already do across task types; tells us whether to route by problem class (S0) or rely on one general setting.
