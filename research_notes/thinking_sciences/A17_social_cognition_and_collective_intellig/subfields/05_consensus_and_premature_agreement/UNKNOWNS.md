# Consensus and premature agreement: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

Common rules for every test below: same model, both arms at equal total tokens (within 5%), same items and seeds, items with plain pass rate 30-85%, at least 200 items per cell, paired bootstrap 95% interval, log every flip with direction. "Pass" means the interval excludes zero and the point estimate clears the stated threshold on at least two item types.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Closed frontier flip tables.** All measured flip rates come from open 7-70B models (K08, K29). We can measure our own agent (U2), but cannot compare against other frontier models without access to them.
- **Real hidden-profile items.** HiddenBench is LLM-generated (28.5% pass rate). We have no human-validated hidden-profile set for text reasoning. Unblock: build 30 tasks by hand, with the 6/3 vs 3/6 attribute layout, and check that single fragments give near-zero accuracy.
- **Authentic vs role-played dissent in LLMs.** No design exists that makes a dissenter "authentic" for a model. Unblock: use a dissent that is grounded in a different sampled solution that actually differs, versus an objection written on request (partly U7).
- **Why voting vs consensus splits by task.** Mansuri offers a partial mechanism (grounds for disagreement differ by benchmark) but no detector. Unblock: label our items by derivability and test U11.
- **Base-vs-instruct and prompt-vs-training split of "yield".** We cannot run base models. Unblock: access to base checkpoints.
- **Human anchors.** Stasser and Titus 1985 exact numbers, Lu et al. 2012 effect sizes, Mullen 1994 and Nemeth 1986 not opened. Unblock: institutional access or open copies. They do not change the agent design; they only fix the citation numbers.
- **Non-author replication of Sentinel, Consilience, DCI.** Unblock: wait for replications, or re-implement on our bench (U9, U10).
- **Withholding threshold for one model with internal roles.** The mean-field model assumes separate agents. Unblock: a design that logs private and stated answers in a role-based single-model run (part of U3).

## Order to run

1. **U2 (flip probe with hygiene).** Cheapest, needs no new operator, measures our own baseline and decides whether anything about peer pressure matters for this agent at all. It also gives the flip and fix rates reused by U6, U8 and U12.
2. **U1 (dissent step vs vote at equal tokens).** The largest open gap. If the vote wins as the literature predicts, we close the question and keep G9 as written. If B wins, a new operator is justified.
3. **U4 (unused-evidence step).** Cheap, uses existing multi-clue items, and is the one idea with a large measured effect (0.037 to 0.926 in panels) that could carry over to a single agent.
