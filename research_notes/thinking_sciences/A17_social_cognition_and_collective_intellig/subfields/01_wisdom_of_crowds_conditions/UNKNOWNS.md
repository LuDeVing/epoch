## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **A different-build verifier.** U2 needs a second model that we do not control the hosting of. Unblock: fix a verifier model and budget line, or use code execution as the verifier where possible.
- **Open-ended reasoning traces.** Independence is unmeasured outside multiple choice, exact math and short numerics. Unblock: an agreed answer-equivalence grader for free-form items.
- **Post-cutoff items.** Contamination hides the true effect (K16). Unblock: generated or freshly dated item sets for each test.
- **Relation of n_eff to realised gain across tasks (critique gap 3).** Only indirect evidence. Unblock: run U1 on at least three task types.
- **Quality-gap threshold for mixing.** No number exists. Unblock: U8 with graded gaps.
- **Retrieval as a use of extra tokens without a verifier.** No study compares it with verification and critique at equal tokens. Unblock: a retrieval source for our classes.
- **Probability aggregation (extremising, geometric mean) under acquiescence.** Needs a forecast-style item set with outcomes. Unblock: a resolved-question set after the cutoff.
- **Large-model and reasoning-model behaviour on peer-visible revision.** d05 covered 7-8B models only.
- **Surprisingly Popular on LLMs.** Searches found nothing; the search budget ran out, so absence is unproven. Unblock: re-run the literature search with a full quota.
- **Several 2026 preprints.** Single studies; replicate key numbers (d04, d08, d05, c16) on our items before any rule depends on them.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (correlation audit).** Cheapest, needs no new model, and every other test uses its numbers to choose items (high-rho items for U3, split items for U2).
2. **U2 (verifier on split votes).** Largest expected gain in the evidence (+24.2 pp on pivotal items) and a direct replacement for wasteful extra voting.
3. **U5 (median of 5 for numerics).** Very cheap, large effect size in the evidence, and settles an aggregation rule used in S7 at once.
