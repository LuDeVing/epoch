## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- True false-finding rate in a field: OSC, Camerer and Many Labs 2 were read as abstracts only. Unblock: open the full papers and supplements.
- OSC dispute (Gilbert et al. vs Anderson et al.): needs full texts and data to split sampling error from real failure.
- Begley and Ellis (6 of 53), Pier 2018, Gundersen and Kjensmo: not opened, so numbers cannot be cited. Unblock: open the primary pages.
- Human intra-rater consistency: no figure to compare with LLM self-disagreement. Unblock: find a study with repeat ratings by the same reviewers.
- LLM-ensemble forecasting of replication: no published study; needs item lists of replication projects with outcomes.
- Net effect of LLM review (help vs inflation vs manipulation): needs a trial with ground-truth outcomes; not on our bench.
- Author-reviewer same-family inflation on scientific papers: needs a paired generation and review study.
- 2026 models on PaperBench and CORE-Bench: needs a rerun with equal scaffolds to separate scaffold from model.
- Whether reasoning models change the self-correction result (Huang et al. used 2023 models).

## Order to run
1. U1 (held-out recheck vs extra samples at equal tokens): the central G3 question, no study answers it, and the bench already has held-out parts.
2. U6 (judge self-disagreement, 5 runs on 100 items): cheapest test, decides whether any judge may select at all.
3. U2 (status tag in memory): small code change, tests the least explored link between citation persistence and agent memory.
