## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Full text and sample sizes of Horner and Tung 2011 (abstract only). Unblock: get the paper, read Exp 1 and Exp 2 numbers.
- Kuhn 1962 chapters VII-VIII and Kuhn 1977. Unblock: open primary text.
- Gawali 2026 restart cutoffs (title only). Unblock: open the abstract and data; may give a best cutoff N.
- Chinn and Brewer numbers (.97 vs .72; 6 of 10 vs 4 of 32; r=-.46) and Dunbar's 23 vs 176 were not re-verified in the critique pass. Unblock: re-open the PDFs.
- Rios-Garcia 68%/53%/26% are from a press article. Unblock: read the arXiv paper.
- LongChang model names are unchecked. Unblock: cross-check the paper's model list.
- Real-task paradigm shifts: our bench items are small tasks; no way to test real frame replacement over many sessions. Unblock: long multi-session tasks with seeded wrong assumptions.
- Whether a trigger helps closed frontier models that already reason at length. Unblock: run U14 per model class.
- Independence of failures: same-model samples are not independent (G4), so "independent failures" must be defined by different inputs or tests. Unblock: a measure of failure overlap on our items.
- Sources are mostly 2026 preprints from single authors without replication. Unblock: wait for replication or rerun key results (Alderete, EvoSCM, Smith) ourselves.
