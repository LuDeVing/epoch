## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Do scientists, analysts or other humans shift acceptance thresholds with stated error cost? Unblock: a small human study or a literature search of behavioural decision research (only one null, N=118, abstract only: d14). Without it we cannot judge LLMs against a human baseline.
- Contested social values and legitimacy (whose values set the cost?). Unblock: a task where stakeholders disagree and a rating of legitimacy by people. No LLM test exists and our bench has no such items.
- Open-ended scientific claims (Rudner's actual setting). Unblock: a set of claims with expert-labelled evidence strength and a stated cost. Current evidence is multiple-choice QA with verbalised confidence.
- Probe-based asymmetric classifiers (c25). Unblock: white-box access. Not possible with an API-only agent.
- Whether prompted ratio equals realised threshold for Claude (Yamin: recovered ratios differ). Unblock: the sweep in U13 on reviewed cases.
- Kalai's penalty-to-target convention. Our records give t=0.75 with penalty 2, but t/(1-t) is 3. Unblock: re-open the paper's scoring section.
- Replication of any 2026 LLM threshold study (all single-source, preprints, two with ids 2608.* and 2609.*). Unblock: wait for follow-ups, or run U1 and U3 ourselves.
- Real, non-stated costs. Unblock: an environment where wrong answers actually cost the agent something over many rounds.
- Philosophy of acceptance (credences versus acceptance). Unblock: a downstream test comparing probability-only outputs to probability plus decision rule on user loss (K03).

## Order to run
1. **U1 (code gate vs prompt).** Cheapest and highest expected gain: one script, one penalty grid, and it decides whether the commit bar lives in code (the single strongest recommendation in this subfield).
2. **U3 (confidence precondition).** U1 only works if our agent's confidence has signal. This takes 100 items and tells us whether any gate is safe or whether to fall back to sample agreement.
3. **U2 (collapse point with and without reasoning).** Reuses the U1 data, so almost free, and tells us which penalty range the model can self-handle and where the gate must take over.
