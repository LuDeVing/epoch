## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Second model.** The best-supported cross-model gains (+0.027 matched quality; +2.7 to +8.2 heterogeneous debate; 91 vs 82 on GSM8K) need a second model of similar quality. Unblock: allow a two-model arm as a one-off measurement, not a schema feature, to size the ceiling for one model.
- **Weisberg-Muldoon, Alexander et al. and Lazer-Friedman full texts** were blocked. Unblock: obtain PDFs through an institutional route; read the update rules before any maverick or social-learning operator is designed.
- **Human meta-analysis tables** were read only through the Wallrich summary. Unblock: open Horwitz and Horwitz 2007 and Bell et al. 2011 if the human prior ever drives a decision.
- **Frontier-model behaviour.** Most numbers are from 7-8B models, GPT-3.5 or GPT-4. Unblock: repeat key tests (U1, U2, U3) on the model we ship.
- **Reasoning-model internals** (c15) cannot be steered by a scaffold. Unblock: an open reasoning model run locally, if the question is ever worth it.
- **Independent replication of Self-MoA** with the original protocol on reasoning models. Unblock: replicate on our own pool (U10).
- **Online landscape detection.** No method exists. Unblock: build a labelled set from U7.
- **Sharing in a single-thread agent.** Hidden-profile and diversity-collapse studies use many agents. Unblock: define what "sharing" means between our isolated attempts (U5).

## Order to run

1. **F03.06-U3 (selector quality crossover).** It gates everything else: if our selector is weak, diversity and resampling cannot pay, and the answer decides how U1 and U4 are run. Cheap: reuses existing attempts.
2. **F03.06-U2 (error correlation by operator).** Cheap logging on a dev set; tells us whether any operator is real diversity before we spend tokens on U1.
3. **F03.06-U1 (varied approaches vs same prompt vs best approach).** The one question no paper answers at equal tokens, and the main place diversity could add to S3. Run after U2 so the arms use operators that moved correlation.
