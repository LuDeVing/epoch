## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Several model families as independent voters (PoLL, deliberating mixed groups): needs a second and third model on the bench. Unblock by adding two other model APIs for a small pilot.
- Human-independence results (Kurvers, Lorenz, Becker): cannot be run on samples; only analogies. Unblock: none needed; treat as background.
- Reopen unread sources: Genest-Zidek 1986, Dietrich-List 2016, Prelec 2017 (the 21.3% figure), Becker 2017 and Satopaa 2015 beyond recorded numbers, Karger 2025. Unblock: library or institutional access.
- Whether long-chain reasoning models show the same error correlation as the small or non-reasoning models measured so far. Unblock: run U2 on the strongest reasoning model we can afford.
- Per-item competence below 1/2: needs many samples on many items to label hard items. Unblock: reuse U2/U3 data.
- Training-data leakage in forecasting sets: needs questions resolved after the model's cutoff. Unblock: a rolling post-cutoff set.
- Extremizing for correlated LLM samples: no theory for the shared-information case beyond "a at or below 1". Unblock: U7 data plus a simulation.
