## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Parallel agents with shared state.** Our bench is one agent. The Coral Discovery result is for four parallel agents. To test, we need a run harness with parallel attempts that can read a shared direction board.
- **Human field counts of researchers per approach** (peptic ulcer, funding portfolios). We found only model-based inference (Rzhetsky et al. 2015). To unblock, find bibliometric counts or funder databases; these decide how large real credit effects are.
- **Full texts of Alexander et al. 2015, Rosenstock et al. 2017, Grim et al. 2019, Fernandez Pinto 2018, Thompson 2014** and the Kitcher and Strevens primaries. All were paywalled or blocked. Interlibrary access or author preprints would let us record numbers instead of abstract claims.
- **Equal-token versions of published LLM results.** Most papers match iterations or evals, not tokens. We would need to rerun them or ask the authors for token logs.
- **Variance of the Coral Discovery gain.** Best-of-3 on 9 tasks, third-decimal margins. We need their per-run results or a rerun with 10 seeds.
- **Long-horizon allocation.** Our items run tens of attempts. Real research runs hundreds of evals; the explore-share schedule may differ. Needs a long bench with cheap graders.
- **Whether direction labels are reliable.** Coral Discovery labels directions with an LLM reading diffs and has no inter-rater check. Our log needs a code-computable direction label (embedding cluster or operator tag) and a small human check.
- **A priority or credit rule inside one agent.** No test exists. The only handle we have is per-direction yield; whether anything beyond it matters is unknown.

## Order to run

1. **F03.05-U2 (rho probe).** Cheap, needs no new arms, and it tells us whether U1, U3 and U4 can win at all (toy says code spread helps only when rho is low).
2. **F03.05-U1 (quota vs cap vs free).** The core S3 question that no paper has answered at equal tokens; a clear result either confirms or removes the main open-mode allocation rule.
3. **F03.05-U7 (distinct-direction yield: seeds, verbalized sampling, similarity filter).** The allocation rule is useless if the model cannot produce different directions; this fixes the input that every other test depends on.
