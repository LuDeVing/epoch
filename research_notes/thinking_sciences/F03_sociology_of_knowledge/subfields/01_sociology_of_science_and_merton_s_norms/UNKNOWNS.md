## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Mitroff 1974 per-norm data.** We have never seen the paper. Unblock: obtain the full American Sociological Review paper, or a faithful secondary with tables.
- **Real human decisions behind AgentReview.** Simulation validity is checked against theory only. Unblock: a dataset with real reviewer discussions and outcomes.
- **Huber replication outside finance.** One paper, one field. Unblock: a second field experiment; we cannot run one.
- **Kitcher and Strevens models.** Not opened. Unblock: read the papers, then build the toy model with LLM agents of fixed priors.
- **Post-2025 replications** of the 15.8% AI-assisted review rate and the 12% top-30% displacement. Unblock: working search access and a check of 2025-2026 venue data.
- **Shrinkage for LLM-generated claims.** Needs a large set of the agent's confirmed claims with fresh data; depends on the bench size.
- **Newer models.** Most judge numbers come from 2023 models; need our own probes before any number is trusted.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (swap-test our judge)**, with U3 (both orders) on the same items: cheapest, one judge, tells us whether stripping and order checks are worth their tokens.
2. **U2 (outside source for doubt)**: the largest expected gain and the closest to G3; settles whether doubt may ever use the same model.
3. **U4 (sanitise injections)**: cheap code and the harm is large (rating 5.34 to 7.99), but the fix has never been measured.
