## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Founding numbers of Guilford 1950/1967 and Runco 2008.** Paywalled or books. Needs library access or a text copy. Not blocking the schema.
- **Acar et al. 2020 explicit-instruction effect (g about .24-.27) and DT vs evaluative skill (r = .13).** Snippet only. Needs the full papers.
- **Whether AUT/DAT items are contaminated.** Needs a set of objects and word tasks that are provably new. We can build 10 invented objects, but we cannot prove the model never saw similar ones.
- **Serial-order effect inside LLM chains.** No study. A bench test (U8) gives a first look but not a theory.
- **2026 frontier DAT/AUT table.** None found. Would need a run on current models with a fixed prompt (U14).
- **Human ratings of usefulness for agent ideas at scale.** Needs a rater budget. LLM raters score ideas near chance on novelty (G8), so human ratings are needed for U13 and U7.
- **Weight-level steering (CreativityNeuro).** Cannot be tested without open weights.
- **Whether the Hivemind effect is real.** Only one preprint re-analysis. Needs the original-paper authors' response or an independent replication.

## Order to run

1. **A09.08-U13 and A09.08-U14 together (scorer validity and noise floor).** Every other test depends on a valid scorer and a known drift. Cheap.
2. **A09.08-U1 versus U2 versus U3 on one AUT/DAT/NoveltyBench set.** These three diverge-step designs share items and arms, so one run answers which structured method is best at equal tokens.
3. **A09.08-U7 (do AUT gains predict a checked outcome?).** Decides whether DT metrics should be the open-mode metric at all, per Runco. Reuse the arms of the second run.
