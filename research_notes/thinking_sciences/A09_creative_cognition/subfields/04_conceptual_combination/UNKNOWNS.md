## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **A large-sample human replication of Hampton (N over 200).** Missing: no such study found. Unblock: run a web survey outside the bench, or accept the human numbers as direction only.
- **Costello and Keane (2000) human-match numbers.** Missing: the paper was paywalled. Unblock: obtain the full text, then redo U8 with the paper's own constraint definitions.
- **Does blend quality predict task success?** Missing: no outcome that links the two. Unblock: U4 gives the first data (blend scores plus downstream pass rate on the same items).
- **Human design work on seed distance.** Design Studies 2015 and 2016 titles were found but not opened. Unblock: open them and extract numbers before settling U4.
- **Quantum-cognition claims and Bell tests on LLMs.** Not testable on a bench for pass rates; wait for an independent replication.
- **Per-model KOMBINE ranking.** The model names look speculative. Unblock: rerun KOMBINE's items on our own models.
- **Search coverage.** Web search was exhausted during the gap search (Crossref, a few arXiv pages only). Unblock: repeat the gap search with a working search tool for gaps 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (generic-space step).** Highest gain: it targets the measured bottleneck (43.4% valid, 97.1% once found) and costs two calls.
2. **U3 (code-randomised seeds plus structure).** Cheap, uses established findings (decoding fails, inputs plus structure work), and it feeds every other open-mode test.
3. **U2 (scenario step for emergence).** The weakest LLM skill (56.4% vs 81% on property type) and the one with a clear human mechanism; U4 can follow on the same item set.
