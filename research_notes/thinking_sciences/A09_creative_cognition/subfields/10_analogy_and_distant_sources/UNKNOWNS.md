## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Reasoning-model reruns of ARN, Stevenson and Lewis and Mitchell are not published. Without them we cannot tell whether the far-analogy gap still exists. Unblock: run T2 and T3 ourselves on our current models (U2, U8).
- Dunbar's original in vivo counts (which analogies were distant and which led to discoveries) are known only second-hand (98% local or regional). Unblock: open the 1997 chapter or the 2001 paper. This changes only how much we trust the human distance story.
- Gentner, Rattermann and Forbus 1993 and Gick and Holyoak 1980 numbers were not opened. Unblock: open the PDFs. This would not change the schema, since 1983 numbers are already read.
- No meta-analysis of far-source ideation effects exists. Chan 2011 vs Song 2017 may be unresolvable from the literature. Unblock: our own distance sweep (U4).
- A code check that a mapped relation is the right one (not only one-to-one) is not designed. Unblock: define a relation-role check on a few task families and test whether it catches wrong mappings.
- A trustworthy novelty score that does not rely on a model judge (G8) is needed for U4, U7, U10. Unblock: use code-run diversity (Vendi on outputs, duplicate rate) and held-out solve rate instead of judge ratings. See the Novelty measurement subfield in this batch.
- Whether analogy hurts or helps on near-solved items (the near cost in ARN) is only seen on narrative benchmarks. Unblock: include near controls in U2.

## Order to run
1. **A09.10-U1** (analogy vs extra sampling at equal tokens). It decides whether O7's mapping step earns its place at all (G7), and it is cheap with existing items.
2. **A09.10-U3** (constraint audit after analogy). Infeasible analogies are the best-established failure, and the audit is one extra call, so the expected gain per token is high.
3. **A09.10-U5** (purpose/mechanism retrieval from own memory). Retrieval quality is the one finding with three sources, and the agent's memory is where far analogues would come from. It has no direct test yet.
