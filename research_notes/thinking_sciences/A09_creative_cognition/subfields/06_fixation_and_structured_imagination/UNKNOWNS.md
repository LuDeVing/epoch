## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Sio, Kotovsky and Cagan 2015 meta-analysis.** Publisher blocked; only a machine summary. Unblock: the PDF from the user, or an open copy. It decides whether "examples cause harm" is the right frame at all (K11).
- **Primary papers on abstraction and avoid instructions** (Atilola 2016, Ezzat 2020, Agogue 2014, Chrysikou and Weisberg 2005, Jansson and Smith 1991, Viswanathan). Needed for effect sizes behind U8 and the human side of U2. Unblock: PDFs, or search quota.
- **Generic parts technique replication.** None found. Unblock: a later study, or our own small human pilot (not planned).
- **Group vs individual and discipline as moderators of avoid instructions.** Human design needed. Not our concern unless the product puts people in the loop.
- **Judge-free creativity score for open ideas with no runnable check.** Our bench lacks one. Unblock: the evaluation-of-creativity subfield, or restrict open tests to keyed or runnable items.
- **Fixation versus usage knowledge in models.** Do more knowledgeable models fixate more, as older children do? Needs a model series with matched scale and a usage-knowledge probe.
- **Within-run fixation in a second model and with a second coder.** Deng used one GPT-4o for both generation and coding. Unblock: rerun the category slope with another model and a human second coder.
- **Multiplayer effects** (AI examples fixating people, group homogenization). Out of scope for the single-agent schema; relevant to the multiplayer plan.
- **Interaction of delay after examples with a break after impasse.** No human or LLM design exists.

## Order to run

1. **A09.06-U1 (equal-token defixation shootout).** Cheapest to set up from existing NoveltyBench and MacGyver items, and it decides the open-mode default that every other test builds on.
2. **A09.06-U7 (knowledge-driven trap: thinking vs sampling vs harvest).** Uses keyed pairs with no judge noise. It tells S0 whether to spend budget on longer thinking or on held-out checks, and doubles as the standing fixation probe.
3. **A09.06-U4 (retry context: drafts vs no-good list vs window).** One-key tasks with checks, so scoring is exact, and the effect is already established on other tasks. If it holds it is a direct token saver. Run U2 and U3 next, since they share the harness.
