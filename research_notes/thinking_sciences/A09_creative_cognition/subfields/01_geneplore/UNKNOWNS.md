# Geneplore model: what we don't know and should test
*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests run both arms at equal total tokens, setup cost counted (G7). Thresholds are our proposals, to be fixed before the run. "Pass" means pass on a held-out or mutated check (G3), never on the given examples.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human parts-to-object numbers on an independent lab.** Finke's 38% to 44% rate comes from one lab. We cannot run people. It needs a published independent replication.
- **Ward 1994 original counts.** Not opened. Unblock: obtain the paper and check the numbers behind the 99%, 97%, 91% figures against the 1994 source.
- **McKnight 2006 numbers.** Not re-opened after two failed downloads. Unblock: a clean copy.
- **Judging originality without human raters.** Our bench has code checks only. Any test of "originality" needs a human or retrieval-grounded rater. Heuresis used an LLM with web search as the rater, which is only as good as that rater. Unblock: a small human-rated sample to calibrate any model rater.
- **Whether results from non-checkable domains (stories, research proposals) carry to checkable tasks.** Unblock: the results of U1 to U5 on our own items. Until then treat every open-domain result as a hint.
- **Reasoning and larger models.** Hivemind shows larger models in a family give fewer distinct answers; one study found negative brain alignment with creative responses for a reasoning-distilled model, not replicated. Unblock: run U2 and U11 on at least two model sizes.
- **Whether a text structure behaves like a mental image.** The human preinventive structure is a held image. No data on the text analogue. Unblock: U1 results.
- **Independent replication of 2026 preprints** (Verma, Bilal, Heuresis, Grammenos and Lubart). None reproduced. Unblock: our own U3 and U2 runs act as the replication.
- **Multi-person data and findings.** The exposure result (beta=0.31 for group diversity) belongs to the planned multi-person layer, not to the single agent. Unblock: build that layer.

## Order to run

1. **A09.01-U4 (stop rule by coverage slope).** Cheapest: it needs only the sample logs we already produce, and it decides how much budget every other test spends on S3.
2. **A09.01-U3 (revise vs resample vs bandit).** Decides the S5 route, which touches every checked-mode run, and answers the contested K18 directly.
3. **A09.01-U2 (instruction wording after earlier attempts are shown).** Settles the conflict between conformity and diversity findings (K03 vs K15, K30), and guards S3 and the memory rules; then run U1 (the only direct test of the Geneplore claim) with the same item machinery.
