## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **No frontier-model insight benchmark with fresh items.** Old public items (Orru, Speed) may be contaminated. Unblock: write a set of post-cutoff riddles and compound RAT items, with a held-back split.
- **No human baseline for our own items.** Needed to judge a gap. Unblock: small paid or volunteer sample, or use published human rates only for published items.
- **Brain-level claims (right aSTG, gamma burst, representational change).** No agent counterpart. Unblock: only an interpretability study of the model, outside this project.
- **Human boundary conditions on break length, sleep, visual vs verbal, individual differences.** Not representable in an LLM. Unblock: none; drop them.
- **Whether impasse is necessary in humans (Gestalt vs Weisberg).** Matters only as design intuition. Unblock: Weisberg 2015 and the later N=42 study are unread.
- **Knoblich 1999 statistics not re-verified.** Unblock: obtain readable PDF text, or find a replication.
- **Frontier-model GRPO findings.** Illusion of Insight used small Qwen and Llama models. Unblock: run the shift-vs-accuracy count on our own traces (cheap check).
- **Search tools were rate-limited in the gap search.** "None found" for LLM incubation and Alderete replication is weak. Unblock: repeat the search when Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex budgets reset.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (gated second pass vs best-of-2).** It decides whether S6 should be a trigger plus a second pass, and it is the missing control behind the only positive LLM restructuring result.
2. **U3 (independent verifier plus harvest).** Verification failure is the largest measured insight-stage loss in LLMs, and the remedy is cheap and already backed on other tasks.
3. **U4 (stall detector precision and recall).** U1, U2 and U6 all depend on a trigger that fires on real impasses; this test is cheap and reuses logged traces.
