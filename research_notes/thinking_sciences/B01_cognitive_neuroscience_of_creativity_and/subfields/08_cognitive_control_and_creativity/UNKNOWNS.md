## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Equal-token human-vs-LLM mapping of "control".** We have no accepted measure of an LLM's control level. Unblock: define control as a set of knobs (prompt strictness, checks, sampling, context length) and test each (U1, U3, U11).
- **Weight-level steering (CreativityNeuro, activation steering).** Needs open weights and compute. Out of scope for a scaffold-only agent. Unblock: only if we later run open models locally.
- **Brain-alignment findings (Ismayilzada).** Cannot be acted on. No knob.
- **Chen 2024 and Chan 2025 effect sizes with bias tests.** Human-only, only background value. Unblock: full-text access.
- **Preregistered replication of Chrysikou 2011/2013 and Rosen 2016.** Human lab work. We can only wait for the literature.
- **Stroop collapse in the full PNAS Nexus paper (press page only).** Unblock: read the paper and rerun on our model set.
- **Creativity scoring of ideas without an LLM judge.** The bench needs a code-run or human novelty metric (G8). Unblock: a held-out human-rated set.

## Order to run

1. **B01.08-U1 (two-step generate then select vs single call).** Highest expected gain and cheap: prompt-only change, and it answers gaps 1 and 7 directly.
2. **B01.08-U3 (relax a constraint on open vs closed items).** Tests the core matched-filter claim behind O4 and decides whether O4 gets a type gate.
3. **B01.08-U5 (rule restatement vs perseveration).** Cheap, large reported effect in older models, and code-side trigger fits S6.
