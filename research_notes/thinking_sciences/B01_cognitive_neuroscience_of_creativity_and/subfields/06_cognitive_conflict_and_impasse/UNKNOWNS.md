## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Whether conflict is a separate step from restructuring in people. Needs an independent replication or a timed design (pupillometry, eye tracking); two candidate papers were not opened (Wiley and Danek 2023; a 2026 pupil-dilation paper).
- Whether incomplete encoding (Kaplan and Simon) or misleading encoding (Ohlsson) describes LLM failures. Needs human-labelled failure types and both original papers opened.
- How often a confident new frame is wrong (false insight) in our own traces. Needs a labelled set of reframes with outcomes.
- Neural claims (ACC vs posterior regions). Not testable and not needed; behavioural triggers only.
- Whether a neutral review prompt helps current frontier reasoning models. Needs access to the model the agent will use and a flip-rate log.
- Cai (2009) REM benefit vs Schonauer (2018) null. No LLM analogue other than a context reset; low value.

## Order to run

1. B01.06-U1 (gated cue vs second plain sample): the largest open gap, directly decides whether S6 should add any cue at all.
2. B01.06-U2 (trigger comparison): cheap, uses logs we already collect, and decides what fires S6.
3. B01.06-U4 (adopt only if check improves): cheapest test with a measured, sizeable cost in the literature (right-to-wrong flips).
