## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Per-idea outcome data from Si 2025.** Needed to test which cheap checks predict execution outcome. Unblock: ask the authors, or find the released data; otherwise build our own small outcome set.
- **Regression to the mean in the ideation-execution gap.** Si 2025 does not analyse selection of top-scored ideas. Needs the idea-level data above or a new execution study.
- **Execution-grounded replication outside NLP prompting.** No such study. Needs domain experts and many hours. Not testable on our bench.
- **Effect sizes for premature evaluation and example fixation.** Jansson and Smith 1991 and Sio 2015 are paywalled. Unblock: library copy.
- **Ellamil full text and a forced-phase, pre-registered replication.** Full text unreachable. This does not change the schema, since we do not encode brain regions.
- **Human baseline for NoveltyBench larger than eight authors.** Unblock: collect a human sample for 20 prompts, or use a published human response set.
- **Quality ratings that are not model-based.** Needs a human rater panel for the held-out score used in U1, U3, U4, U5.
- **Different-model judging of ideas, in published work.** None found. Our own U9 would be the first data.
- **Generation phase versus evaluation phase in LLMs.** Whether hidden-state signatures differ between modes is not studied here. Unblock: open-weight model access; low priority.

## Order to run

1. **U13 (check-picked versus judge-picked).** Highest expected gain and cheapest: it uses the harness we already have, and it tests the strongest claim behind G8 on our own items.
2. **U1 (split versus single call).** The central unknown; the whole human literature only motivates it. If it fails, S3/S7 can be simplified.
3. **U3 (judge pick versus random pick before revision).** Cheap, and if random wins we can drop a call from the open-mode path; U4 (prior ideas shown) can run in the same batch as it needs the same items.
