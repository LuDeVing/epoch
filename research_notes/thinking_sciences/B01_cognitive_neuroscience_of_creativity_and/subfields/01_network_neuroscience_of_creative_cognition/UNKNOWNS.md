## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human-rated outcomes.** We lack blind human raters for novelty. Unblock: a small paid or volunteer rating panel; or a prior-art retrieval check as a proxy.
- **Cross-model homogeneity under scaffolds (gap 8).** Needs several model families run under the same scaffolds. Unblock: a second and third model on the bench.
- **Creativity post-training and internal representations (gap 7).** Needs model internals and the Beaty 2018 data. Unblock: open-weight model access; the 2026 study's code.
- **Which human network result survives a large pre-registered study (coupling vs separation; salience-first).** Human neuroscience question; not testable here. Unblock: wait for new large replications.
- **A layer-wise generate/control analogue inside an LLM.** No study exists. Unblock: interpretability tools plus a published protocol.
- **Insight tasks other than CresOWLve.** Needs a benchmark with non-canonical answers. Unblock: build a small set with human-rated originality.
- **Token accounting for Gacha, VS and Nova.** Papers do not match tokens. Unblock: our own runs (U3, U6).

## Order to run
1. **B01.01-U1** (control pass vs code selection): cheapest, directly tests the two-phase claim at equal tokens, and every other row depends on it.
2. **B01.01-U2** (discard the obvious vs VS vs resample): cheap, targets the saturation problem, and tests the one behavioural result from the brain work that could transfer.
3. **B01.01-U5** (forced linking vs scoring on insight items): tests whether "control" means retrieval control, and the answer decides what the critic prompt should be.
