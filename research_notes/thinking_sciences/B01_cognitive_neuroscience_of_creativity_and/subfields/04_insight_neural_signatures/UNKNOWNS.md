## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Independent replication of the right-temporal gamma burst (other lab, N over 30, corrected statistics). Needs an EEG lab; we cannot do it. Not needed for the agent.
- Whether sudden confidence jumps exist in frontier or closed models. Needs token-level probabilities or hidden states; unblocked by an open model with logprobs.
- Whether the Aha label in humans is difficulty, style or both. Needs human data with item difficulty; unblock by reusing public datasets.
- Whether 2601.00514 transfers to frontier models. Needs the authors' detector code or a rewrite of it.
- Whether tDCS nulls are low power. Human lab work only.
- The Sheth critique (post-solution gamma stronger) and the Kounios and Beeman 2009 review were not retrieved. Unblock by fetching the full texts.

## Order to run
1. **B01.04-U1 (decoy step, gated):** cheapest, uses existing item sets, and fixation is the one established effect here.
2. **B01.04-U11 (literal-first rule):** same items, adds the harm control, and protects U1 from over-inventive errors.
3. **B01.04-U2 (entropy-gated reconsideration):** largest reported gain (+8.41 pp) but from small models on math; needs logprobs, and tells us whether it carries to insight items.
