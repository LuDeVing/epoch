## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Direct test of LLM warmth.** No one has had an LLM give warmth ratings mid-trace on insight problems and checked them against outcomes (gap 1, still open). Unblock: copy the 15-second rating schedule as token intervals and use the Metcalfe and Wiebe problems. We have no human-matched protocol yet. The guardrail stands by analogy until then.
- **A large preregistered warmth replication in humans (N > 200).** None found in a limited search (search quota ran out). Not ours to run. Unblock: an outside lab, or crowd workers.
- **Do results hold in frontier models?** The entropy-gate result is Qwen2.5-1.5B. The confidence-validity papers use non-reasoning or 0.5B-7.6B models. Frontier APIs often give no token probabilities. Unblock: an open-weight frontier-class model or API logprob access.
- **English and other languages.** NazoNazo is Japanese, and the thought-log language moved Grok 4 accuracy by 19 points. Unblock: an English riddle set of the same kind.
- **Unopened primary papers.** Metcalfe 1986 (two papers), MacGregor 2001, Ohlsson 1992, Weisberg 1992 were read second-hand. Unblock: library access.
- **A reframe flag for our own traces.** We have only lexical cues. Unblock: a validated reframe detector (judge plus lexical prefilter, kappa 0.79 reported on MATH-500 in c05).
- **Weights-based steering and activation reports** (c15, a32) need open weights; our agent is a prompt scaffold.
- **Entropy AUROC protocol.** A snippet (d19, unopened) says within-problem evaluation drops entropy AUROC from 0.80+ to 0.60-0.75. Unblock: open the paper before using entropy thresholds from any source.
- **Unverified numbers.** Mizumoto d = .946 (a25) and Salvi press-release numbers (b09) are unverified. Do not cite them.
