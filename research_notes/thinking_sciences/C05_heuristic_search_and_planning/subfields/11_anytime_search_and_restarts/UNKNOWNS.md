## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Logprobs.** DeepConf-style windowed confidence needs token log-probabilities. If the harness does not expose them, only code-only features can be tested (U5). Unblock: add logprob logging to the harness, or accept the code-only version.
- **Independent seeds.** We do not know how independent LLM attempts are given the same prompt and temperature. Unblock: measure the correlation of outcomes across attempts within an item (part of U1).
- **No-verifier tasks.** Open reasoning without a check has almost no restart evidence. Unblock: construct items with a hidden checkable answer to compare a weak proxy stop signal against the true check.
- **Reasoning-model refinement with tool feedback beyond unit tests.** Only 2023-era models and unit tests were studied. Unblock: add a tool-result arm to U4 on a current model.
- **Persistence of the steel-loop result.** One domain, one model, one campaign. Unblock: replicate U2 on a second domain with 40 attempts and bootstrap the best split.
- **Difficulty router from a cheap pilot.** Needs many labelled items. Unblock: reuse the 30+ attempts per item data from U1 as labels.
- **Peer review of 2026 anytime preprints** (Zhang, Conformal Thinking, steel paper). Unblock: wait or replicate the key numbers on our tasks.
- **Wall-clock parallelism.** Our accounting is tokens only. Parallel speed-up (Truchet) is outside the bench.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (hazard measurement).** One cheap batch of 30+ attempts per item. It tells us whether restart can help at all and sets the unit and cutoff for every other test.
2. **U2 (one long vs k short vs Luby at equal tokens).** The main decision for S3/S6; reuses the U1 attempts.
3. **U3 (what crosses a restart).** Restart without the right memory can cost; this fixes the note format before anything is added to memory.
