## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Human overlap-replay theory itself (iOtA). Needs direct recording of overlapping-set replay in humans. No bench can test it; treat as inspiration only.
- Peer-reviewed large replication of Durrant 2011 and Matorina's REM result. Needs a new sleep lab study. Until then do not cite sleep results as support (K34).
- Whether merged rules cause confident unsupported generation, the LLM analogue of false memory. Needs a labelled set of implied-but-unsupported claims; U8 only partly covers it.
- Best overlap finder: entity vs embedding vs symbolic fragments on free-text rules. Needs rules written in a structured form first. Unblock by testing on ARC rules written as code.
- Count of bad rules admitted by each guard. Needs a ground-truth label for every merged rule. Unblock by logging every merge and checking each on a large held-out set.
- Learned router between rules and raw cases. Needs a stable pair of task types and enough items to train on; run U11 first.
- Unopened leads: MOOM (LLM merging beat embedding and rule-based merging under a cap, snippet only) and strong-to-weak skill transfer snippets (SKILL-DISCO, ASDA). Open the papers before using them.
- Long-horizon (year-scale) human gist effects have no agent analogue; ignore.

## Order to run

1. B04.09-U3 (library vs raw-case retrieval vs none vs plain + vote at equal tokens). Decides whether any consolidation pass is worth building; math libraries failed this exact test.
2. B04.09-U1 (overlap vs random merge groups). Decides whether the "overlap" in the design does anything; no published test exists.
3. B04.09-U2 (regression gate). Cheapest safeguard with the largest measured effect elsewhere (GRASP); results shape how every other test is run.
