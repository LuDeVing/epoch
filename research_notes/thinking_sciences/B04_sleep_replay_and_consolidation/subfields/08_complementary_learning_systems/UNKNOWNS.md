## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Weight-level replay and LR re-warming.** Our agent is frozen. Needs a fine-tunable model and training budget to test dose-response (0.25%, 1%, 5%, 25%).
- **Independent reproduction of CLS-ER, FearNet, DualNet.** Needs those codebases and a tuned ER baseline (Buzzega 2020b: tuning alone gave +51.2 and +26.9 points). Not our task.
- **Whether graded retrograde amnesia in animals is robust** (flat gradients in some labs). Biology; no bench test. Matters only as a design prior.
- **Tse 2007 schema effect and McCloskey and Cohen 1989.** Primary papers unopened; needs reading, not a bench run.
- **Does the real effect of prioritised replay show up for rare, hard lessons?** Needs a lesson store with enough rare classes and many seeds to detect a small effect.
- **Does practice at using retrieved context matter (12% and 6% oracle retrieval)?** Needs a trainable model with and without ICL-supporting sequences.
- **Scale of lesson stores.** All agent-level data are tens of items (a 19-problem slice, 97 tasks). Behaviour at thousands of lessons is unknown.
- **Search limits.** Gap search used one WebSearch and rate-limited APIs. A newer agent-level interleaved-replay paper may exist; repeat the search before spending on U1.

## Order to run
1. **U1 with U2 (regression gate and k sweep).** It is the most direct use of CLS, nobody has run it, and the damage it targets is large (58.5 to 38.5).
2. **U3 (raw episodes vs rules vs both).** Cheap, uses the same sequences as U1, and two 2026 papers already point the same way.
3. **U7 (re-cue before demoting).** Cheap, reuses U1 failures, and can stop good lessons being thrown out when forgetting is partly spurious.
