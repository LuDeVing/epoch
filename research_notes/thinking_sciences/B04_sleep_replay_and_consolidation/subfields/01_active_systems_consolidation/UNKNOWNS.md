## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Exact effect sizes for Rasch 2007 and Marshall 2006 (not open access in this run). Unblock: get the PDFs; pull cell sizes and means.
- Henin follow-ups and any 2025-26 large closed-loop replication. Unblock: search again with API access restored.
- Large direct replication of Wagner 2004. None found; unblock: search PsyArXiv and OSF.
- Human stage dependence (N1 vs N2 vs SWS) for rule discovery. Cannot be tested on an LLM; it only decides whether to cite the biology.
- Weight-level sleep (SRC, SleepGate, Sleep, Auto-Dreamer GRPO). Needs a trainable model; our agent is scaffold-only. Unblock: a small open model and a training budget.
- Whether the offline step helps frontier models, since most results come from small or Qwen-class models. Unblock: run U1 on our own frontier model.
- Seed variance of every published memory-agent result. Unblock: re-run the top 2 papers' baselines with 3 seeds.
- Whether Anthropic "Dreams" beats plain dedup. Unblock: product numbers or access.
- Absence claims (no LLM test of schema fit, hub replay, false rules) are weak because search quota ran out. Unblock: repeat the search.

## Order to run
1. U1 (offline pass vs same tokens at query time): it decides whether any offline consolidation job is worth building at all.
2. U3 (preserve-detail line and source links): cheapest test, one prompt line, and the loss it targets is the best-documented failure.
3. U4 (false and harmful rule rate): sets the trust gate; the schema already holds that rules need two helps, so this tells us how strict to be.
