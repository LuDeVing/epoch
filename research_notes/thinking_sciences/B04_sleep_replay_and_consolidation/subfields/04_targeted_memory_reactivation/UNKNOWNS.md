## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Updated human effect size after 2020.** There is no meta-analysis newer than Hu 2020. We cannot run this; it needs a pooled update with 2022-2026 nulls and multi-lab studies. It would reset how large a gain we should expect (0.13 to 0.29).
- **Conditioning and the mouse substage flip.** No LLM analogue and not testable here. Would be unblocked only by a human replication.
- **Does Shin 2025 replicate independently?** Needs another lab, not our bench.
- **Item-specific vs context-wide benefit in humans.** The 2026 odour study points to spread; needs a preregistered human study.
- **A cue-tied external token test on LLMs in the literature.** No paper exists. Our bench is the only place it can be built; the open part is the right control for "extra attempts".
- **Sleep-time compute across predictability and budget in one sweep.** Needs a benchmark with controlled predictability; ours would have to be built. Sleep-time Compute used stateful variants the authors built.
- **Weight-level sleep methods against strong rehearsal baselines with variance.** Needs weight access and seeds; out of scope for a scaffold-only agent.
- **Do replay passes work across models?** Zou 2026 used one model and 3 histories. Needs at least two model families.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (cue-tied isolated replay vs extra attempts at equal tokens).** It is the core unknown: no study has tested it, and every other row depends on it.
2. **U3 (isolated vs in-task replay).** Cheap to run with the same items as U1, and it decides whether the state gating guardrail applies to the agent at all.
3. **U2 (weak-only vs all).** Reuses the U1 items with failed-item logs, and the weak-item rule is the best-supported human moderator, so it has the best chance of making U1 pay.
