## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- A large replication of Peng and Nisbett 1999 (N of 300 or more per culture, non-student samples). It needs human participants and cannot be run here. It only matters for how much to trust the human story; it does not change what we build.
- Jin et al. (2023) rerun with 30-60 items and two languages on current models. We could run a cheap version (item sets and models), but the result would not change the schema. Run only to settle the A3 note.
- Whether a held-open stance under pushback is more often correct. Needs items with a known right answer plus scripted pushback; partly covered by U4, but SYCON-Bench does not score correctness.
- Whether strong frontier models gain from antithesis at equal tokens. Needs access to the frontier models and an equal-token harness; U6 is the first step.
- Debate versus self-consistency on partly true tasks. No benchmark exists. Needs a new item set (see U10).
- Measurement invariance of the Dialectical Self Scale across the 28 meta-analysis countries. Needs raw data.
- A human or model baseline for what counts as a "good" discriminating input on open tasks (no oracle). Needs an annotated set.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (hold-two with code-run case vs CoT and SC).** It is the core question, nobody has tested it, and its nearest result (28.0 and 29.0 vs 59.0) is large enough to pay for the run.
2. **U3 (defer commitment on staged evidence).** Cheap to build and has the biggest single gap in the literature (30.9 vs 64.4); it also decides how U1 is routed in S0.
3. **U4 (pushback rule and reviewer wording).** The hold-two loop is useless if one casual "are you sure" kills it; this can reuse the U1 items with scripted rebuttal turns.
