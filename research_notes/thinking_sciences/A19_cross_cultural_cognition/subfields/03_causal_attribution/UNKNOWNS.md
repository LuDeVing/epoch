## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Original N and effect sizes for Choi et al. 1999, Morris and Peng 1994 and Miyamoto and Kitayama 2002. Unblock: obtain the full papers, or run our own item set.
- Large preregistered replication of the East-West correspondence-bias gap. Unblock: read Castagnetti and Potters 2025 and Thierman 2011 (leads in DEEP.md), and finish the search once rate limits lift.
- Per-model effect sizes in Raj et al. 2026. Unblock: released code or figure images.
- Human baseline on the same prompts as Raj, Malberg or Rosenblum. Unblock: a small human panel; not planned.
- Whether authorship effects (Tsui, Khullar) hold for reasoning models in long agent runs. Unblock: run U1 and U2 on our actual model.
- Whether Malle 2006 effect sizes by moderator predict LLM behaviour. Unblock: read the full paper.
- Whether a fine-tuning route (76.0% reduction in the blind spot, Tsui) is relevant. Our schema works without training, so out of scope.

## Order to run
1. **U3 (assigned-or-chosen check on Jones-Harris items).** The only direct test of the founding effect on our model. It decides whether a new guard exists, and the items are cheap to generate.
2. **U1 and U2 together (fresh-turn check and Wait).** Both use the same known-bad outputs and could change S8 for every task, not only attribution.
3. **U4 (strip-the-label pass).** Largest measured LLM effect in this subfield (17.5 points) and a simple pass to test; it also covers U12 items cheaply.
