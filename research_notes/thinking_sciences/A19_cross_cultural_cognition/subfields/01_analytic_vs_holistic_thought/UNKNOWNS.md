## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Eye-movement and perception effects: the bench has no gaze. Unblock only with a vision-model bench; low value for a text agent.
- Priming of style by a prompt (human priming analogue): needs the multi-wording replication in U6 first. Human priming evidence is contested (n=84, n=135 nulls vs 104-experiment meta-analysis; no bias-corrected re-analysis found).
- Cause of LLM style (training data vs RLHF vs prompt): needs base vs chat model pairs and training-data ablations. We have neither.
- Exact LLM effect sizes of Lu et al., Sun and Wang, Sabir: only summaries or carried-over numbers. Unblock by re-reading the papers.
- Large preregistered replication of the Chua and Masuda-Nisbett effects: human research gap. Unblock only with outside data.
- Link between dialectical thinking and reasoning accuracy in humans: no study. Our U3 would be the first LLM test.
- Whether results hold for reasoning models with long chain-of-thought: all LLM style studies used chat models; frame effects may differ.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (forced second framing vs second sample):** the central question for O6/O8; no study tests it, and the answer decides whether the rest of this subfield matters.
2. **U10 (abstraction-first vs sampling at equal tokens):** the only framing switch with a human-free positive result; cheap and likely to produce a real element or a clean null.
3. **U2 (task-fit by class):** one run yields the framing-by-class table that U3, U4, U5 and U9 depend on, and tests the main human finding that goal and task beat culture.
