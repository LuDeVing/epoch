## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Independent replication of the person-level null: none found; the 2023 "independent manifests" paper was seen as a snippet. Unblock: open that paper, extract N and r.
- Whether a seven-dimension model (Vignoles 2016) gives stronger person-level links: full text blocked. Unblock: access to the article or authors' data.
- Original Lu, Song and Zhang items and prompts: withheld. Unblock: request data from the authors, or accept the rebuilt-items route.
- Human reference data for our own probe items: we have none, so style shifts have no human anchor. Unblock: a small human pilot or published norms.
- Base-versus-tuned model pairs of one family: depends on available open weights.
- Human test-retest for the exact scales given to 2025-26 models: not found. Unblock: published retest values for AHS, SCS, DSS.

## Order to run

1. A19.08-U8 (wording and format stability): cheapest, no new items needed, and it sets the noise floor that every other A19 test depends on.
2. A19.08-U2 (cultural persona on real reasoning): directly tests the one thing the research does not cover, and a null confirms the guardrail cheaply.
3. A19.08-U3 (per-user note versus group label at equal tokens): the only path to an actual add (memory), backed by the strongest human-grounded result (Park et al.).
