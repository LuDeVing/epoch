## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Human reference data for Nisbett-style tasks (triads, change blindness, contradiction) on the same platform as an LLM run. Atari 2023 triad scores are in figures only. Unblock: get the data from the authors or run a human panel.
- Exact N and effect sizes for Mercier 2015 and Hakim 2017, and original-author replies. Unblock: full-text access.
- A base-vs-RLHF ablation for cultural style. Unblock: access to paired base and tuned weights.
- Non-Western human data for belief bias and content effects. Unblock: new data collection or an existing multi-site dataset.
- An independent causal replication of the rice-wheat effect. Unblock: external lab or a second natural experiment.
- Whether reasoning accuracy (not values) changes with a cultural persona on logic, math or ARC. Needs the T3 arms in section 8 of DEEP.md with cultural personas added.
- Whether format spread (Sclar) persists under chain of thought and at equal tokens. Unblock: run U1 first.
- Whether version drift (Tao: no trend, P=0.036) affects any of the tests; re-run each test on each model version.

## Order to run

1. **A19.09-U1 (paraphrase restatement).** Cheapest, and wording variance is the largest effect in this subfield (eta2 0.54, spread 7.5 to 76 points); it also gives the noise floor every other test needs.
2. **A19.09-U7 (injected-context check).** Models accept false context (c05); a code check is cheap and the gain, if it exists, is large and certain to matter for any scaffold that adds context.
3. **A19.09-U2 (persona vs CoT).** Closes the contested persona question against 4b and uses the same item set, so it costs little after U1.
