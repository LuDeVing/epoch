## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Item set for holistic versus analytic reasoning with a keyed answer. Most human tests (spatial memory, attribution) are not scored on accuracy. We would need to build 150 relational and 150 analytic items with unambiguous keys. This blocks U1, U2, U4.
- Lu et al. (Nature Human Behaviour 2025) items and effect sizes. Full text was paywalled. Asking the authors or finding a data release would let us run U9 on their items.
- Reasoning-mode models with a controllable thinking budget, to test whether a prefix survives long chains of thought (U11).
- Founding human papers (Gardner 1999, Kuhnen and Oyserman 2002, Hong 2000, Trafimow 1991) not opened. Opening them would let us copy the exact prime texts and check original numbers. Low cost, needed before U1 prompts are final.
- A second independent replication of the compliance gradient (d01-d04 is one lab, cut-off not preregistered). Cannot be done on our bench; only read-through of new work.
- A search for LLM studies using human priming tasks as prefixes used Crossref only (other APIs rate-limited). A repeat on Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex would harden the K16 absence claim.

## Order to run
1. **U5 (check-and-revert, pick between cued and neutral).** Cheapest and highest expected gain; any positive result is a general guardrail and does not rely on a cultural theory.
2. **U1 (SDFF and pronoun prefix on reasoning items).** The direct, never-run test of the subfield's headline idea; a null closes the question for the schema.
3. **U4 (explicit framing instruction versus identity cue, with mismatched items).** Strongest observed LLM lever (d about 2); the mismatched items separate real help from demand and feed U8.
