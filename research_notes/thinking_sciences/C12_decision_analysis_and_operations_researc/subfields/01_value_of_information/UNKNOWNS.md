## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Howard 1966 and Raiffa-Schlaifer 1961 proofs** (nonadditivity, risk-averse case): the primary sources were not opened. Unblock: get the papers or a textbook chapter (for example Raiffa and Schlaifer, or Howard and Abbas, *Foundations of Decision Analysis*). Low value for a risk-neutral agent.
- **Real tools or users as answerers:** every LLM result here uses a simulated answerer. Unblock: a bench item type with a real executable check or a human-in-the-loop sample.
- **2026 frontier reasoning models on QuestBench-type tasks:** no scored model after o1-preview exists. Unblock: run QuestBench ourselves on our shipping model (needs access to the benchmark and compute).
- **True EVPI with an elicited payoff table in free-form tasks (coding, ARC-style puzzles):** no ground truth for the payoff table. Unblock: tasks with known per-answer payoffs (graded partial credit).
- **Risk-averse stakes:** not testable on accuracy-only metrics. Unblock: a bench with asymmetric costs of wrong answers.
- **A direct EVPI-versus-EIG comparison from outside:** no paper does it. Our U1 would be the first; results will be single-lab.
- **Whether the explicit hypothesis list is created cheaply enough in open tasks** (the cost of sampling and filtering hypotheses vs. the tests they save): needs token accounting inside the scaffold, which depends on the harness being built.
- **Nonadditivity in practice:** how often does batch scoring differ from one-at-a-time on our tasks? Needs logged runs from U2 and U10.
- **Information gain vs probability gain in LLMs:** needs items where they disagree; we do not yet know how common these are in our task mix (U8 measures this).

## Order to run
1. **C12.01-U2 (code-side ranking vs model-chosen tests on our models):** the best-supported idea (7 sources) and the base for every other row; if it fails on our model, most of U1, U5, U9, U10 and U13 lose their point.
2. **C12.01-U6 (does uncertainty predict item-level gain from another test):** it decides whether the schema's G1 trigger can lean on uncertainty at all; the evidence is contested and the test only needs logs we are already making.
3. **C12.01-U1 (payoff gate vs EIG ranker vs tuned threshold):** the largest gap in the literature, and the one that decides whether S7 gets a net-value stop rule; run after U2 so the ranker is validated first.
