## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human anytime curves.** No expert-versus-novice data on accuracy against allowed time, and no test of Simon's aspiration adjustment against anytime stopping. Unblock: open Simon 1955/56 and find speed-accuracy studies with expertise as a factor; a human study is outside the bench.
- **Simon, Russell and Wefald, Horvitz and Hansen and Zilberstein were not opened.** Unblock: fetch and read them; check the Hansen and Zilberstein DOI (the two files give different DOIs).
- **Closed APIs cannot be cut mid-reasoning.** Interruption tests used open-weight models. Unblock: run the cut tests on an open model we host, or use API reasoning-effort settings as a proxy and say so.
- **Trained anytime models (AnytimeReasoner, L1, E1, SelfBudgeter, MRT).** Gains need RL training we do not do. Unblock: only if a training track opens. The scaffold analogue is U4.
- **Direct replication of s1 budget forcing and AnytimeReasoner.** None found. Unblock: a multi-seed run with 100+ items on an open model; low value for our scaffold.
- **Illusion of Thinking versus its rebuttal.** Unresolved. Unblock: rerun with compact answer forms and solvability checks; do not use the collapse claim until then.
- **Agent-task evidence is thin.** GAIA (165 tasks, single runs), SWE-bench Verified (500 items), LiveCodeBench. Unblock: build bench items with multi-step execution and a real check, 100+ per cell.
- **Doubling schedule and non-myopic monitoring have no LLM literature.** Unblock: U2 and U11 produce the first numbers.
- **Cost of difficulty prediction.** Oracle bins give larger gains than predicted bins. Unblock: U7 counts probe cost inside the budget.

## Order to run
1. **C12.06-U3 (curve shape audit).** Cheapest (measurement only) and it decides whether stop rules built on a concave profile are allowed at all. Its data also feeds U2, U4 and U9.
2. **C12.06-U1 (agreement or check stop vs self-confidence stop).** Biggest expected token saving, widest use across schema states, and it closes the only open head-to-head (K24).
3. **C12.06-U4 (reserved answer segment) together with U2 (doubling schedule).** Same harness (random cuts). Together they test whether G6 "never abstain" holds when time runs out, which is the core anytime promise.
