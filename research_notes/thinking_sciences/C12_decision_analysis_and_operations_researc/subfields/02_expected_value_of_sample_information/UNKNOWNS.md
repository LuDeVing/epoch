## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Raiffa and Schlaifer (1961) and Howard (1966) were never opened. Unblock: get the books or a full-text secondary source and extract the exact results (conjugate tables, bounds).
- Real noisy answerers (real users, real retrieval, real tests): all LLM evidence uses simulated answerers. Unblock: a small human or tool-backed pilot with measured answer accuracy.
- Large-N or preregistered replication of Bastardi and Shafir (1998) and its cross-cultural claim. Unblock: a literature search (search quota ran out) or running the scenarios ourselves on humans.
- Interacting tests (non-myopic value): no benchmark exists. Unblock: build synthetic items where two tests pay off only together.
- Raw model-strength effect: needs at least three models of different strength on the same bench.
- Stakes-weighted (utility) value versus entropy-based value on open reasoning tasks: no study. Unblock: items with asymmetric loss.
- Whether value gating generalises beyond question asking to extra attempts and revision rounds (the schema's S3, S5): no study.

## Order to run

1. C12.02-U3 (price a noisy test with exact answer key): cheapest, no tools needed, tells us whether the model can supply likelihoods at all; everything else depends on it.
2. C12.02-U1 with U2 (EVSI gate versus tuned threshold versus budget-left line at equal tokens): the central question; the budget line is the cheap rival that may make the gate unnecessary.
3. C12.02-U4 (necessity check on useless tool calls): cheap prompt-level test, directly addresses over-calling, and also measures the Bastardi-Shafir analogue.
