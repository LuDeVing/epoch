## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **What the 1991 Russell-Wefald text measured.** The journal paper returned 403; the unit of the 60:1 figure is unread. Unblock: obtain the PDF through a library, or find a citing paper that reproduces the tables.
- **Gate behaviour on reasoning-model APIs.** All prompt-only evidence is on non-reasoning models (GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet). Unblock: a reasoning model with a thinking-budget parameter on our bench.
- **Log-probability signals.** DeepConf and DEER need logprobs; our API may not return them. Unblock: an open-weight model hosted locally, or an API that exposes logprobs.
- **Hidden-state probes.** AUC 0.608 on one 7B model (d14). Unblock: access to hidden states and a few hundred labelled runs.
- **Whether human Mouselab results generalise.** Only 51 participants in one conference paper; the 2022 journal version is unread. Unblock: read Callaway et al. 2022 and run U8.
- **Real-agent base rates.** No survey of negative VOC in long-horizon agent workloads. Unblock: log thinking length and outcome across bench runs (U6).
- **Training-time methods.** RaM, Arora-Zanette, AdaptThink and MRT need fine-tuning; we cannot test them on a fixed scaffold. Unblock: only if the project later allows training.
- **True VOC with a value-of-information estimate.** No LLM work computes it at runtime. Unblock: an answer-distribution estimator that is cheap and calibrated (needs U5 first).
- **Independent replication of any stop-signal claim.** Every comparison is by the proposing group. Unblock: our own head-to-head (U3).

## Order to run
1. **C12.05-U1 (budget gate).** Cheapest, highest expected gain, and it settles whether the S0 gate pays for itself net of the estimator call (G7).
2. **C12.05-U5 (same-model sample correlation and stopping threshold).** Fixes the biggest hidden risk in the best-supported S6 saving; the stop rule is useless if G4 makes it overconfident.
3. **C12.05-U2 (multi-step vs one-step stop).** The one place where theory predicts a clear failure of the simplest rule, and no LLM test exists. Run U3 and U6 on the same logs at no extra cost.
