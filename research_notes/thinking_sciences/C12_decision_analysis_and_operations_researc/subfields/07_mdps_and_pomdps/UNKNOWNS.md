## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Learned belief compaction (PABU, Bayesian teaching, DPT). Needs fine-tuning. Unblock: a training run, outside the scaffold scope. No prompt-only arm exists in the literature.
- Sound verifiers for open-ended tasks. Needs a domain checker like VAL. Unblock: build one per task class; measure what a weak one costs (U10).
- Exact optimal-policy comparison for realistic tasks. Only toy POMDPs (Tiger, RockSample) have it. Unblock: use those as calibration, then trust relative gains elsewhere.
- Model-elicited likelihoods. No source measures their accuracy on our tasks. Unblock: score model likelihood estimates against known test outcomes.
- Complexity classes from primary sources. Puterman and the 1987 paper were never opened. Unblock: open them; low priority since it does not change a design choice.
- Whether LLM-internal belief geometry (Shai et al.) exists in prompted chat models. Needs open weights and probes.

## Order to run

1. U12 (seed and sample-size audit): every other result is unreadable without it, and it is cheap.
2. U1 with U2 (code posterior vs free text, then commit rule): the central claim of this subfield, and the one contested result; shares one Tiger/RockSample harness.
3. U10 (verifier false-negative cutoff): cheap, uses existing tasks with a code check, and protects against a known harm.
