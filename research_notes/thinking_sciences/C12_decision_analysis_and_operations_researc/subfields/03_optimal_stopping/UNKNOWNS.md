## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Exact founding human numbers (Seale and Rapoport 1997; Gilbert and Mosteller 1966). Missing: full texts. Unblock: library access or author copies. Until then do not cite a percentage for human early stopping (K25).
- Hidden-state probe stops (LYNX, OS-Pruner, abort probes). Missing: open-weight hidden states on our hosted model. Unblock: run the bench on an open-weight model.
- Learned stop heads (OS-Pruner, StreamStop, CaRT). Missing: training data and a fine-tuning path. Unblock: a fine-tuning arm outside the no-training scope.
- Independent replication of OS-Pruner, OptPO and reliability-aware voting. Missing: released code. Unblock: authors' code or reimplementation.
- A regret-versus-miscalibration curve for a stop rule. Missing: a controlled bench with injected miscalibration at several levels. Unblock: build the distortion harness used in U7.
- Number of episodes needed for feedback learning of a stop threshold. Missing: long episode runs per condition. Unblock: U9 extended to 100 episodes.
- Joint stop rules across retrieval and tool stages. Missing: multi-stage tasks with real tool costs. Unblock: a retrieval bench with priced calls.
- Whether coverage gaps hide an LLM secretary-problem study outside arXiv (d01, c27). Unblock: a search of Google Scholar and ACL Anthology when quota allows.

## Order to run
1. C12.03-U1 (harness stop rule): cheapest, two labs already agree, and it fixes the largest observed LLM failure (not stopping on a failing source).
2. C12.03-U2 (sequential vote stop): well established elsewhere and low risk, so it gives a quick token saving and the baseline harness for the other stop tests.
3. C12.03-U3 (cardinal secretary probe): fills the one gap with no data at all and tells us which direction the agent's stop error runs, which sets how every other stop rule should be tuned.
