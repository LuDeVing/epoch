## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Per-plan success priors before any attempt. We have no calibrated prior per plan; stated confidence is overconfident (d13). Unblock: log outcomes of many plans by class (the class record), then check whether a hidden-state or agreement-based prior predicts them.
- Hidden-state probes for early pruning (Damani, Zhang et al., ST-BoN). We call models through an API. Unblock: access to an open-weights reasoning model on the bench.
- Adversarial or reactive options (the world reacts to the agent's choice). Our items are fixed. Unblock: a simulator where payoffs depend on past choices.
- Interaction between allocation and the weak-verifier problem (coverage is not accuracy). Unblock: items with a graded, noisy verifier, including a measured verifier error rate.
- Costs of bookkeeping at long horizons. Our runs are short. Unblock: a long-horizon task (T of 500 or more).
- Whether the human-derived hybrid exploration model describes any LLM mechanism. Unblock: logprob or sampling access on a controlled choice task.
- Original Gittins, Lai-Robbins, Robbins, Thompson and Weber papers were not opened. Unblock: read them to confirm index conditions and any extensions to Beta-Bernoulli arms.
- Sample sizes for Phelps 2025 and effect sizes for Lee 2023, Laskin 2022 and Schubert 2024 are missing. Low impact for our scaffold.

## Order to run

1. C12.08-U9 (agreement-based difficulty router versus flat budget): cheapest, uses existing pass-rate data, and tests the one claim G12 rests on with a black-box signal.
2. C12.08-U1 (successive halving versus uniform best-of-N): the biggest possible gain for candidate selection, and it decides whether the schema needs a prune operator.
3. C12.08-U3 (code tally versus raw history on a current model), run together with U8: it tells us whether the best-known LLM bandit fix still matters for reasoning models, and costs only a small synthetic task.
