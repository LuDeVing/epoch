## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Exact 1986 counts and coding rules behind 80-90 percent RPD. Needs the original HFES proceedings (DTIC copy damaged). Does not change the schema; it changes how we cite.
- R-CAST-style multi-agent RPD with modern LLM agents. Needs a multi-task switching benchmark we do not have.
- LLMs as expert stand-ins. Needs expert decision data beyond one crisis game; tasks on our bench have checkable answers instead.
- Time-pressure analogue. No human-style stress exists in a model; only token caps (U12). Do not claim stress effects.
- Field outcomes of ShadowBox-type training. Only expert-agreement scores exist; no performance outcome in any source.
- Whether the GPT-5.2 null in Kopka and Feufel is model change, date drift, or ceiling. Needs re-running on current models.
- Re-run searches for gaps 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 when search quota returns (critique d15).

## Order to run
1. E13.06-U4 (direct vs deliberate on pattern items): cheap, no new components, and settles whether S0 should have a direct mode for pattern classes.
2. E13.06-U2 with U3 (RPD prompt vs equal-length CoT on balanced sets): the only direct LLM evidence for RPD is single-source and may be a bias shift.
3. E13.06-U1 (serial first-then-next vs parallel candidates): decides the shape of S3/S4 and costs one extra arm on the existing checkable bench.
