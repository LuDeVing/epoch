## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Real rates of the three Woods patterns (decompensation, cross-purposes, outdated behaviour) and the scoring rule for fixation. Unblock: open NUREG/CR-6208, Woods 1994, De Keyser and Woods 1990 (PDFs were blocked).
- Fixation under real time pressure or live side effects. Our bench has static scripted tools. Unblock: a live fault-injection environment (SREGym or AIOpsLab) with a reasoning model.
- AIOpsLab and ITBench rerun on 2025-2026 reasoning models with step logs. Unblock: run them ourselves, or find a paper that did.
- Independent replication of AgentDebug, and a test of the log-length explanation. Unblock: our own long trajectories (U7 gives part of this).
- Whether the 63% human fixation figure replicates under one scoring rule. Unblock: a simulation-study group that publishes its coding rule.
- Whether a learned monitor beats a code counter for our schema. Unblock: enough logged runs to train one; not worth it before U1.
- Whether ITBench diagnosis with traces is 13.81% or 18.10%. Unblock: ask the authors or rerun.
- Whether detector thresholds transfer from SWE-bench and OSWorld to ARC and puzzle items. Unblock: calibrate on our own runs (U6).

## Order to run
1. **E13.02-U1** (code counter vs step cap vs model flag vs none): the central unmeasured claim; cheap to code; gates U2, U3 and U6.
2. **E13.02-U3** (what crosses a restart): the largest measured effect size in this area (+0.2 versus +5.2 and 27 to 36% of good runs lost) and it changes how S6 rebuilds context.
3. **E13.02-U2** (two-part fixation detector): the only mechanism aimed at anchoring in strong models, where the loop counter is expected to be silent.
