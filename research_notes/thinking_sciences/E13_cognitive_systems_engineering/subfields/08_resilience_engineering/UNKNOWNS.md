## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Primary texts of the field.** Woods 2018, Woods and Branlat 2011 and Hollnagel's Resilience Assessment Grid are closed or blocked. Unblock: obtain the PDFs by library access, then check whether they state timing predictions for decompensation. (K25)
- **Hidden-state loop detection.** Needs open weights and a probe. Unblock: run a small open model on the nano loop; calibrate on 50 or more non-loop cases per model as LoopBench did. (K13)
- **Multi-agent cross purposes.** Our agent is one agent. Unblock: only if we add role-bound calls; then count MAST inter-agent modes (reasoning-action mismatch, task derailment) in our traces.
- **MAST split by version.** Different totals per release and counting unit. Unblock: pin the dataset release and the counting unit before citing any share. (K11)
- **Drift and buffer loss in LLM agents.** No source tests it. Unblock: U10 on a long logged run.
- **Strong-model behaviour of failure-layer methods.** Sentry, Anchor and EvoCUE start from weak bases (WebShop 0.17). Unblock: rerun a minimal version on our strongest model with held-out items.
- **Independent replication of AIR and Liu et al.** Single author groups with self-defined metrics. Unblock: a re-implementation of the rule-specified vs self-invented remedy contrast on our tasks (U5, U9).
- **Real stress.** Tool-fault tests are at ceiling. Unblock: perturbations that are cognitive (misleading feedback, changed rules mid-task), not random faults.

## Order to run

1. **E13.08-U1** (trigger audit on existing traces): costs nothing in new runs and tells us whether S6 fires early enough to matter at all.
2. **E13.08-U2** (pass^k at equal tokens): the one metric the literature shows plain agents fail and no scaffold study has tested; it also gives us the logs for U6.
3. **E13.08-U3** (located revise with exit vs blind review): the largest harm in the evidence (-49.3% without an exit) and a cheap flip-count on items we already run.
