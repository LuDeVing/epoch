## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Reasoning-class effects of the self-correction blind spot.** Tsui tested only open models; closed models were excluded (prefix injection). Unblock: a prefix-injection or own-draft-in-prior-turn harness for the model we use.
- **Natural (not injected) errors.** On natural errors only 4.3-10.8% of misses are caught even when shown externally. Our bench seeds errors; natural error corpora for our tasks need collecting from real failed runs.
- **Reason's and Rasmussen's primary numbers and CREAM intervals.** Books not opened. Unblock: library scans of Reason (1990) ch. 2 and 7 and Hollnagel (1998) ch. 9, or leave them unused (they are not needed for the schema).
- **Doireau 1997 (90% self-detected vs 30% third-person recovery).** Primary not opened; direction only. Unblock: the French paper, or a fresh human-subject study (not our scope).
- **Shared wrong belief across samples.** Agreement signals cannot catch it by construction; a test needs items where the model has a systematic wrong belief and a trusted answer key. Unblock: a curated "confident wrong" set from our failed runs.
- **Training-based fixes (SCoRe, correction fine-tuning, probes, steering).** Need weights. Our agent is scaffold-only. Unblock: open-weight proxy runs, used only as ceilings.
- **Token-controlled comparisons from the literature.** Almost none of the measured results controlled tokens (exceptions: Huang vote vs debate, Olausson, Snell). We must run all arms ourselves.
- **Human checklist null (Urbach) mechanism.** Is it poor adoption or no effect? Unblock: not testable here; use only as a warning that benefits may not survive scale.

## Order to run

1. **E13.07-U2 (own-draft approval, false-pass rate) and E13.07-U3 (joint miss rate of same-family reviewers).** They are cheap measurements with no scaffold change, and they size every other review idea. If self-approval is high and reviewers are correlated, all model-review ideas die and the code-only line in G2 is confirmed.
2. **E13.07-U1 (code-located fix vs resample-and-vote at equal tokens).** This is the one comparison that decides whether S5 earns its place under G7. Established in direction, never measured at equal tokens.
3. **E13.07-U5 (revise-trigger precision gate with keep-best).** Cheap once U1 data exist (same drafts), and it sets the numeric gate that protects against the 7-38% flip harm.
