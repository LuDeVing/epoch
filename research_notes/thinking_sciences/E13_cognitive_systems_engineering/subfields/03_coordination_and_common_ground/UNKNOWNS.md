## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Real users. Most clarification results use simulated users. We need a few real answers to see whether question costs and false trust (N=180 study) change the result.
- Interactive deployment. Collaborative Gym style effects (interleaved action, notifications) need a live user loop we do not have.
- Klein et al. Basic Compact (predictability, directability) on LLM teammates. No measured task or scale exists. A benchmark with human-AI common-ground measures (d14) is abstract-level only.
- Partner-specific conventions over many rounds. Needs a fixed partner and many rounds, not our single-item bench.
- Replication record of conceptual pacts (Metzing and Brennan 2003 not opened). Needs a reading pass, not an experiment.
- Why the MAST +9.4% worked (role specification, CEO final say, or a submission gate). Needs a re-run of ChatDev, outside our single-agent scope.
- Preference tuning's effect on grounding in current models. Needs access to training checkpoints or paired base and tuned models.
- The spatial-coding rival to egocentric bias for LLMs. Needs a director-task style referential benchmark.
- Li 2023 round counts (28.3 vs 12.3 vs 11.0). Needs the table from the paper or the released code.

## Order to run
1. **E13.03-U2 (detector accuracy alone).** Cheapest, and it decides whether any gate (U1, U6) is worth building: Ambig-SWE shows some models near chance.
2. **E13.03-U1 (gated ask vs never-ask vs always-ask).** The only candidate where the evidence is a measured gain (69.4 vs 54.8), but not at equal tokens. Run after U2 passes.
3. **E13.03-U3 (recap or slot-filled rule on sharded items).** Large reported loss (-39%) and a partial fix (recap recovers about 52%) with no equal-token test.
