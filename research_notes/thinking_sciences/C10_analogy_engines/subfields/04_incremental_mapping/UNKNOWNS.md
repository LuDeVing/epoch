## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human order-effect data.** Keane et al. (1994) was never opened; N, errors and stats are unknown. Unblock: get the paper (publisher page returned 403) and read the method and tables.
- **I-SME successors.** The 2016 SME journal paper is an image-only scan. Unblock: OCR it, or find another copy, then look for accuracy or speed numbers on incremental sessions (Companion, Bridge).
- **Replications of the order effect.** None found, but search was tool-limited (HTTP 429 on Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, arXiv; WebSearch spent). Unblock: forward-citation search on Keane et al. 1994 when limits reset.
- **Do thinking-budget caps change surface vs relational matching in LLMs (the time-course effect)?** Needs a set of cross-mapped items with surface lures and a controlled thinking cap. Unblock: build the item set; may fit a sibling run.
- **Tests on strong frontier models.** Most evidence is on 2023-24 models or open-weight 3B-70B. Unblock: run the bench suite on our current model before trusting any size estimate.
- **Weights-level methods.** Bayesian teaching and activation patching need fine-tuning or open weights. Unblock: only if the no-training rule changes.
- **Garbled tables.** Hu 2023, Yasunaga 2024, BeliefShift, Kim and Khashabi cells are approximate. Unblock: re-read from source PDFs if any number is to be quoted.
- **Check two evidence ids.** BayesBench and Ping et al. arXiv ids as recorded look unusual (2606.30850, 2608.01017). Unblock: verify before citing outside this folder.

## Order to run
1. **C10.04-U1 (extend vs re-map with a code-held table).** It is the core open question (no LLM test exists) and the suite it needs is reused by every other test.
2. **C10.04-U3 (evidence gate on new facts).** Five sources already show flip-flopping; the gate is cheap code and likely to pay off at once.
3. **C10.04-U10 (code RECAP vs raw history).** Multi-turn loss is large (39%) and the fix is cheap; it tells us whether rounds are worth using at all compared with all-at-once.
