## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Independent replication of Clement and Gentner 1991 with effect sizes and expert samples. Needs human participants or a fuller literature search (rate limits blocked it). Unblock: search with OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar access, or a small human pilot.
- Token cost of a mapping-score step against best-of-N. No study exists. U1 gives the first number from our harness.
- Whether systematicity helps on code and math "systems", not stories. Needs a bench of code-analogy items with gold mappings; none exists yet.
- Misleading causal chains: when a connected system points the wrong way. Needs items built with a wrong but connected mapping.
- Extraction precision against gold relations. Needs a gold-annotated relation set; neither Sultan and Shahaf nor YARN reports one.
- Time pressure (700-1000 ms in people) mapped to small token budgets. Only a secondary report exists; the original was not opened.
- Internal-state methods (patching, probes). Not usable by a prompt-only agent.

## Order to run

1. **C10.02-U1** (extract-abstract-map-score vs plain judge on far-near items): the main decision question; nothing else here can replace it.
2. **C10.02-U2** (thinking off vs on on far-near): cheap, reuses the U1 items, and tells whether U1's gap is a budget problem.
3. **C10.02-U5** (retrieval key: raw vs purpose/mechanism line): cheap on our own case bank, and the retrieval gap (surface .78 vs analogy .44; embeddings near random) is the best-supported memory weakness.
