## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- 2025-2026 frontier model scores on SAT, U2, U4, BATS, SemEval-2012 Task 2 with intervals. Missing: published runs. Unblock by running the public sets ourselves (cheap) and reading the CI.
- Contamination of public analogy sets. Missing: training-data access. Unblock by perturbation tests (U5); a clean answer may stay out of reach.
- Whether 2024-2026 embeddings keep the asymmetry and SIMILAR/CONTRAST failures of Chen et al. Missing: a human-rated relation set with order effects. Unblock by rerunning their 5,000+ SemEval-2012 ratings against new embeddings.
- Whether gains hold when no gold relation exists (open reasoning). Missing: an open-mode bench with a relation judged by running code. Unblock by building twin-problem items with a known shared method.
- Table values in Lewis and Mitchell (model mapping), Ushio and Freestone were read from garbled extractions. Unblock by re-reading the tables.

## Order to run
1. U6: control arms (candidate-only, ONLY-B) on our analogy items. Cheapest, and it decides whether any later analogy number means anything.
2. U5: perturbed copies of the benchmark. Cheap and tells us whether public sets can be used as evidence at all.
3. U1: relation-description vs topic-embedding retrieval for O7. Highest expected gain; it is the one decision our agent depends on.
