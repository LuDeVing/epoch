## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Independent replication of the 1996 goal reversal with people (N over 200, online). Needs human participants. Would unblock: knowing whether the human result is solid before we copy it. Until then treat it as one lab.
- Reasoning-model results on ARN far-vs-near items. Needs access to the ARN data and a run of our base and reasoning-mode model. Unblocked by simply running T2 on the full set.
- A mapping benchmark with a goal-dependent answer key. None exists for LLMs. We must build it (U1), with at least 100 items and two human raters for the key.
- Whether the SME-line "structure first" account or the multiconstraint account describes LLM mapping better. Needs the load manipulation (U9) plus a graded-similarity lure set.
- Contamination: ARN, StoryAnalogy, and the Gentner stories are public. Needs freshly written items, or items whose cover stories are changed, before any result is trusted.
- Gentner-camp tests of the goal effect. None found. Needs a search of the structure-mapping literature for pragmatic-goal manipulations.
- Mechanism: whether "not applying the relation to the new entity" (d15) can be cured by a prompt. Needs a two-step relation prompt on open models (U3 variant).

## Order to run
1. **C10.06-U1 (goal reversal).** Cheapest, and it settles whether the purpose line means anything for our model. If the model ignores the goal, the goal line in U2 is empty and U2 should be cut to two lines.
2. **C10.06-U2 (three-line scoring vs holistic choice).** The core claim of this subfield and the largest expected gain, on the one failure (far analogy with a near lure) with an established baseline.
3. **C10.06-U4 (order bias check).** Cheap, and it removes a confound from every later analogue-choice test. Run it first inside U2's item set.
