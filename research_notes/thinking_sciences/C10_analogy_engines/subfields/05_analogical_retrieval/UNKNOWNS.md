## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human replication of the 32-story design with an LLM agent.** Missing: the original story set and a store of similar size and delay. What would unblock it: obtain the Rattermann and Gentner materials or rebuild them; use a long-lived store with filler.
- **Outside replication of Gentner 2009.** No other lab found. Missing: independent human data. Not testable by us; track the literature.
- **An LLM as the encoder for content vectors (MAC stage on predicates).** No work exists. Needs a stable relational encoding of text. Unblock: a small pilot that extracts predicates with an LLM and checks consistency across paraphrases.
- **Fine-tuned retrievers on cross-topic pairs.** Large gains (0.12 to 0.50) need training and data. Our scaffold does not train. Unblock: only if we decide to allow a trained component.
- **Real natural base rate of analogues in a long-running agent.** Needs weeks of logged runs on a varied suite. The planted-twin set measures retrieval, not the base rate.
- **Peer review of the 2026 arXiv numbers** (HippoSpark, RA-RFT, ADR, CPR). Single preprints. Unblock: re-run the key arms ourselves; do not cite as established.
- **Expert versus novice relational retrieval and encoding depth** (Novick, Faries and Reiser, Blanchette and Dunbar). Cited second-hand and never opened. Unblock: open the originals; then design the "richer encoding at store time" arm.
- **Retrieval timing on analogue tasks with the same key.** The papers differ in benchmark and key. Needs U4 results first.

## Order to run

1. **C10.05-U1 (two-stage vs one-stage vs stuffing).** It is the central claim and decides whether any other retrieval work matters; the planted-twin set it needs also serves U2, U3, U5, U8.
2. **C10.05-U3 (mapping check against surface decoys).** The biggest known LLM weakness is false positives (precision about 0.5); a cheap verifier is useful even if retrieval stays naive.
3. **C10.05-U4 (retrieve at start vs stuck point, same key).** Contested in the literature, cheap to run on existing bench items, and it fixes where retrieval sits in the state graph.
