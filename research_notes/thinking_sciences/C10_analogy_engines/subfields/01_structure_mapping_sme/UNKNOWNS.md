## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Current frontier reasoning models on ARN, StoryAnalogy and the Gentner stories.** All numbers are 2023-2024 models. We need the benchmark data (ARN, StoryAnalogy, the 18-story set) and a run with our model. Unblocked by downloading those sets; no new method is needed, but the sets must not be in the model's training data (leakage risk, FAME).
- **Independent, preregistered replications of the classic human findings** (Clement and Gentner 1991; Gentner and Toupin 1986; Markman and Gentner 1993). Not testable on our bench. The Alfieri et al. 2013 meta-analysis and Mason and Tornatora were found but not opened; opening them would unblock a view on analogical encoding.
- **Token cost of a mapping step versus side-by-side comparison.** Needs U1 and U4 run first, then a cost table.
- **Higher-order structure in model-written lists.** We do not know whether models write nested CAUSE/IMPLIES structure or flat lists (flat lists remove SME's advantage, K31). Needs a manual audit of 30 extractions.
- **Predicate vocabulary.** Fixed vs free predicates; unknown which is better. Part of U5/U6.
- **Interpretability results (hidden-state patching, concept vectors).** Need weights; a scaffold cannot use them (K27).
- **Time-pressure effects (500-1,600 ms).** No analogue in the agent (K11).
- **Why Yasunaga and Qin disagree** (relevant vs random examples). Needs a like-for-like baseline; part of U4.
- **Search was rate-limited at the end of the run**, so "no study found" for U1, U4, U7 may be a search gap. Re-run a literature search for 2025-2026 papers before spending bench time.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (score vs benefit).** It is the central unknown: if the score does not predict benefit, most other rows lose their reason. It also produces the transfers that U7, U10 and U11 reuse.
2. **U2 (code mapper vs model-only on far analogies with distractors).** Cheap, uses public item styles, and tests the one place where LLMs are known to be weak (39.6% far, 33.9% far-vs-near-distractor in 2023).
3. **U4 (two-case compare with random-correct-case control).** Cheapest change (a prompt format) and the best-supported human effect (47% vs 6%); the control decides whether the gain is really relational.
