## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human effect sizes for Ferguson and Sanford 2008, Ferguson et al. 2010 and Nieuwland and Martin 2012. Needs full-text access to the papers; the repository pages gave abstracts only.
- The Steele companion paper on belief (c09). Needs the paper itself; it would show whether belief-not-special (K25) is better supported.
- Independent replication of Steele et al. (binding subspaces, report versus use). Needs open-weights models and probing code; our bench is prompt-only.
- Leakage versus the number of spaces and context length in frontier models. Needs a purpose-built synthetic task with a controlled number of spaces; no public benchmark found.
- Any measured gain from blending as a prompt method (K31). Needs a creative or analogical benchmark with an agreed scoring rule; novelty judged by running (G8) is hard for this.
- Equal-token comparison against SimToM and SymbolicToM with reasoning models. Needs their code and prompts re-run; partly covered by U2.
- Whether RL-trained belief tracking (d09) transfers to scaffolded use. Training is outside the project.

## Order to run
1. U1 (scoped blocks vs flat list): the central S2 claim has no direct test and the bench already has the items, so it is the highest expected gain.
2. U9 (over-separation controls): cheap, reuses U1 items with true-belief controls, and protects against a scaffold that hurts shared-knowledge cases.
3. U3 (restate before query, filler sweep): cheap on synthetic items, tests the retrieval-shaped binding idea and gives a dose-response curve.
