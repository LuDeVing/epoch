## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A human-graded hard slice for the KOMBINE judge. Only one author checked 60 items. Unblock: two blind human raters on 60 blends from our own runs (this is U4).
- Whether the 43.4% valid-generic-space bottleneck reproduces on a second benchmark. Unblock: a second item set built from our own entity pairs, graded by humans.
- Monk riddle solve rates for people. We have no human panel. Unblock: a small human pilot, or use model solve rates only.
- Whether blending beats relevance theory on noun-noun compounds (Hilpert 2010, Schmid, Sabzevari 2011). A linguistics question; no agent test. Unblock: read the papers; see the conceptual combination sibling subfield.
- Whether VisiBlends' 96% vs 21% survives reading. Unblock: open the CHI 2019 paper.
- Effect sizes in Keon et al. 2025, and Scideator ablation scores. Unblock: open the tables.
- Whether classical systems (Divago, COINVENT) report any measured result. Unblock: open the papers.
- Whether the N400 cost for far mappings has an agent analogue (compute needed for far blends). Unblock: measure tokens-to-valid-blend against input distance in U1.
- Training-time blend losses (Gomez-Ramirez and Turner 2026). Outside the no-training rule.
- Image-side questions (midpoint salience, method by pair type). Outside a text agent.

## Order to run
1. F01.02-U1 (generic-space blend vs plain CoT at equal tokens). It is the main claim; nobody has tested it; a null here settles most other rows.
2. F01.02-U2 (propose-then-check generic space on KOMBINE-style pairs). It targets the one measured failure point (43.4%) and is cheap with a small human slice.
3. F01.02-U3 (several blends plus code selection). Cheap, reuses the U1 items, and tests the strongest numerical pattern (56% single, 90-97% any-of-five).
Run U4 alongside any open-mode grading, since it decides whether judged scores can be trusted at all.
