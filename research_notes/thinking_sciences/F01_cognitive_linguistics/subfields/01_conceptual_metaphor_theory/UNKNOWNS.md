## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Whether the beast/virus framing effect is real in people. Needs an independent preregistered replication. We cannot run human studies. Unblocked by: a published replication.
- Full-text numbers for Boroditsky 2000, Brugman 2019 and Lynott 2014. Unblocked by: access to the papers (Wiley and Stanford returned 403 and 404).
- Cross-language mapping choice (Aymara, Mandarin, Spanish). Needs multilingual item sets and a use case; the agent is monolingual.
- Whether Lakoff and Johnson 1980 and Lakoff 1999 contain behavioural tests. Unblocked by: reading them.
- A cognitive explanation for why models over-label metaphor (surface cues vs concept). Needs internal probing on current models; probes so far use BERT-class models.
- Which source domains beyond space-time give benefit. Needs a library of tasks with known natural source domains.
- The 62.12% error figure in Wachowiak and Gromann. Unblocked by: reading the paper table.

## Order to run

1. **F01.01-U1**: the main question. Nothing else in this folder matters if O6 does not beat RaR and step-back at equal tokens. It also yields the baseline arms for the others.
2. **F01.01-U3**: the invariance check is the cheapest guard and addresses the best-documented harm (imported wrong inferences, 15-25% irrelevant readings, up to 30 points in code models).
3. **F01.01-U4**: the frame-invariance check reuses the same items in two frames, costs little, and gives a flip rate we can log on every run.
