## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Real stakes.** Every LLM study here is hypothetical. What would unblock it: a task where the agent loses real budget or score across steps. Our bench has token budgets but no losses framed as stakes.
- **Per-model description-history numbers for 20 vs 100 draws.** The appendix of Ge et al. was not read. Unblock: read the appendix, or run U9.
- **Human effect sizes for small-stake designs** (Gal and Rucker 2018, Yechiam 2019, Wulff 2018). Only abstracts read. Unblock: open the full papers.
- **1992 CPT weighting parameters** (0.61, 0.69). Unblock: obtain the primary text or the Brown appendix.
- **Why lambda falls across model releases.** Training data, tuning or safety objectives cannot be separated without matched checkpoints. Unblock: open base and tuned checkpoints from one family.
- **Process claims about the editing phase.** Our bench measures outputs, not the model's internal editing. G10 says reasoning text is not evidence. Unblock: only behavioural tests like U1.
- **Reasoning versus narrative format.** Ge (numbers) and Payne (stories) seem to conflict, but the tasks differ. Unblock: one study running both formats on the same models. U1 plus U2 is a start.

## Order to run
1. **A03.03-U2** (verbatim 1979 items on our bench model). Cheap, and it decides whether any bias guard is worth building.
2. **A03.03-U1** (final-outcome restatement vs plain chain of thought at equal tokens). The most direct candidate for a schema addition from this subfield.
3. **A03.03-U4** (priming guard). Cheap, ties directly to input hygiene in S1, and the human-side evidence for the effect is the strongest among the LLM results here.
