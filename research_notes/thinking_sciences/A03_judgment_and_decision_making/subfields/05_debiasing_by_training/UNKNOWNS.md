## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Independent replication of Morewedge 2015 and Sellier 2019: needs human subjects outside the original group. The Sellier corrigendum text is unread; getting it would settle the 29%/19% question.
- Weight-level feedback and practice (EIT, steering, fine-tuning): needs open weights and training; out of scope for the scaffold.
- Long-run retention in a deployed agent (days, many sessions): needs a persistent memory deployment, not a bench run.
- Whether human-style delay effects (2-3 month retention) have any LLM analogue: needs a cross-session memory design.
- The effect on frontier closed reasoning models of any prompt debiaser: needs enough baseline bias to measure; many frontier baselines are already low.
- Real-world transfer (unannounced traps inside long tasks): needs a task bank with hidden confirmation traps that the agent is not tuned on.

## Order to run
1. U14 (baseline bias audit with rewordings): cheap, and tells us which later tests are worth running at all.
2. U1 (within-run error log with feedback): the central human mechanism, untested in LLMs, cheap to build from the S9 lesson line.
3. U2 (SACD vs matched-token self-help): the strongest LLM result, single group, and the token-parity check decides whether it earns S1 space.
