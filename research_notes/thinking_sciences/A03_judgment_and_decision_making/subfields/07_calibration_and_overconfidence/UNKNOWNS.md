## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Original human baselines.** Fischhoff, Slovic, Lichtenstein 1977 (original N, per-bin hit rates), Koriat 1980 table values, Gigerenzer 1991, Juslin 1994, Brenner 1996 were never opened. Unblock: obtain the PDFs through a library route and read the tables, or cite only the second-hand figures with a flag.
- **Frontier closed models on agreement vs verbalized.** Del 2026 used three open mid-size reasoning models. Unblock: run U2 on our own frontier arms; no outside source needed.
- **Agreement inside an agent loop with tools.** No study tests agreement-based confidence with tool use. Unblock: our harness logs, then U3 and U4 on agent items.
- **Representative sampling for LLMs.** Needs a random draw of a natural task mix with ground truth. Unblock: a labelled log of the agent's real tasks (U9).
- **Number of ordinal levels.** No study sweeps the number of levels. Unblock: a small sweep (2, 4, 7, 11 levels) after U7 shows the hedge format is worth keeping.
- **Reader-side effects of calibrated wording.** Needs human readers. Unblock: a small reader panel, or skip.
- **Persona and prompt-variant correlation on single-answer problems.** Only a garbled table exists; the schema (G4) already bans persona voters. Unblock: U8 as a measurement-only study.
- **Test results versus reflection.** Not isolated in any study. Unblock: U4 with three readouts.
- **Whether temperature-like maps transfer for verbalized confidence.** Asserted in one 2026 abstract, not measured. Unblock: U5.

## Order to run

1. **A03.07-U3** (shared-error rate of unanimous attempts): cheapest (logs plus one outside check), and it sets how far any agreement-based gate can be trusted in each class.
2. **A03.07-U2** (hybrid vs agreement at equal tokens): decides what the S7 confidence score is, and it reuses the same attempts as U3.
3. **A03.07-U1** (counter-case vs pro/con vs none): the one human-derived operator with a real chance of a gain, and the one most likely to break G1 if added without a test.
