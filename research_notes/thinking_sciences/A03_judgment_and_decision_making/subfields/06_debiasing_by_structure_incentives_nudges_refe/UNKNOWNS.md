## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Lerner and Tetlock (1999) and Camerer and Hogarth (1999) effect sizes.** Full texts unopened. Unblock: get the papers and extract effect sizes before U10 and U11.
- **Failed replication of RCF outside infrastructure.** Lovallo, Clarke and Camerer (2012) not opened. Unblock: read it; build a software or planning class record from our own logs.
- **Preregistered outside-view prompt test (planning fallacy).** Only secondary figures. Unblock: find Buehler et al. 2010 or later preregistered work.
- **Default effect alone after bias correction.** Nobody computed it. Unblock: rerun Maier's RoBMA on the defaults subset (needs the Mertens data on OSF).
- **Current-model behaviour (2025-2026).** Newest bias tests found use 2024 models. Unblock: our own bench runs (U3, U7, U9).
- **Does showing a base rate reduce over-reliance on a model's advice?** No study. Unblock: a human-AI experiment; not on our bench.
- **Class record size.** We may not have enough logged tasks per class for stable quantiles. Unblock: log class outcomes now (schema section 6) and count per class.
- **Order-bias result in Sumita et al.** Internal conflict. Unblock: read their tables.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (retrieved class record vs base-rate prompt).** It is the central S0/S7 question, no study exists, and base-rate items have the most headroom (10-15% correct).
2. **U3 (anchor placement and re-ask vs warning line).** Cheap, anchoring is established in LLMs, and the answer decides a guardrail wording.
3. **U5 (blend weight) on the same items as U1.** It reuses U1 data, costs almost nothing, and has two sources behind the idea.
