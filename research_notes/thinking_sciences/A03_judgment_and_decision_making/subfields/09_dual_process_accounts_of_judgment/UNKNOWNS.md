## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Founding human numbers (Evans 1983 belief bias, Frederick 2005 per-site, Hagger 2016, Bago and De Neys 2017) are unverified. Unblock: open the primary PDFs.
- Moral dual-process results rest on one secondary review. Unblock: read the primary papers; low value for the agent.
- Trained routers, steering vectors and distillation need weights or activations. Unblock: an open-weight model setup.
- Human data on LLM-type items (same items, same format, human timing) are missing for U8 and U12.
- RL-trained reasoning models: switching reasoning off may be impossible (o1 could not be switched off), so U2 and U4 may only be partly testable on them.
- Why Claude 3.5 Sonnet resisted CoT harm while GPT-4o and Claude 3 Opus did not. Unblock: run U2 on several model generations.

## Order to run
1. **A03.09-U1** (equal-token routing): it decides whether any fast/slow router is worth building and no study has done it.
2. **A03.09-U4** (verify-flip rate): cheap, settles whether S8 and any "check your first answer" step help or harm on modern models.
3. **A03.09-U3** (trigger comparison): reuses the U1 items and tells us what signal opens the slow path.
