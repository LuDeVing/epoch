## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Does the LLM elicit better weights than unit weights?** No LLM study exists (critique gap 3). Needs a labelled set large enough to fit weights, which our bench does not yet have per class.
- **Boundaries where the formula loses (small N, regime change, adversarial gaming).** Undocumented for LLM agents (critique gap 6). Needs a shifted-distribution item set built from our own classes.
- **Leak-free replication of the 2024 to 2025 forecasting gains.** Needs live or post-cutoff questions with resolution dates; our bench has exact-answer items only (d01, d02).
- **Reliability and validity of LLM feature ratings in general.** Only pairwise-judge data exist (d06). Needs a rating set with human or ground-truth labels.
- **Primary texts.** Tetlock 2005, Mellers 2014, Grove 2000 full text, Kahneman and Klein 2009 not read. Unblock by reading them when search quota returns.
- **Whether superforecaster-like selection of agent configurations by past score persists.** Needs repeated runs of several configurations over disjoint item sets.
- **Why the Pros-versus-bots gap closed between Q2 2025 and spring 2026.** Not testable by us; re-check the next Metaculus quarter (c11, c13).
