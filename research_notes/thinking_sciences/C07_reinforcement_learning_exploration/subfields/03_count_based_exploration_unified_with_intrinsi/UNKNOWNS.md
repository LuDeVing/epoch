## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A proper notion of "state" for reasoning. We use approach labels, but there is no ground truth for whether two labels are the same. Unblock: human-labelled pairs of attempts for a small item set to measure label agreement.
- A reliable measure of distinct correct solutions on non-code items. Unblock: a solution-equivalence checker per item type.
- Original proofs (Kolter and Ng 2009, Strehl and Littman 2008) are unopened. Unblock: open both and check whether any carries to a discrete label space with deterministic checks.
- Whether results hold on newer frontier models. Most cited bandit evidence is GPT-4 era. Unblock: re-run U3 on the current model family.
- Whether the Montezuma gains came from the bonus or return type (MMC) cannot be settled from the papers. Unblock: none for us; only relevant as a caution.
- Independent replication of MERCI per benchmark. Unblock: another group, or our own U10.

## Order to run
1. U2 (key granularity): cheap, decides what every other table test uses.
2. U1 (table vs independent resampling): the main claim; no paper has tested it, and the sampling baseline is strong.
3. U5 (S0 gating): the cheapest guard against losing on easy items, and it decides whether the table is on by default (G12, G7).
