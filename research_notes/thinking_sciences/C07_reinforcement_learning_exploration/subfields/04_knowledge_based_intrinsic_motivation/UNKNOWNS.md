## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Training-time curiosity (CDE, IMAGINE, MAGELLAN). We do not train. Unblocked only if we fine-tune. CDE itself has one base model (d10).
- Independent replication of the infant studies (Kidd 2012, Poli 2020). No paper found. Needs a human lab or a literature search with a larger budget.
- Verified founding numbers for Kaplan and Oudeyer 2004 and Schmidhuber 1991. Needs the original papers; the 2010 review has no numbers.
- Third-party reproduction of ECO and a text version of it. Needs a DMLab-style task or a clean table from d12.
- Reward hacking of LLM-judged interest at scale. Needs a large item pool and a hard audit set.
- Token cost accounting for curiosity operators in other labs (d15 snippet only). Our own logs will answer it for us.
- Forward-citation coverage of ECO, MAGELLAN and OMNI. Search budgets were exhausted.

## Order to run
1. T1 (U1, U10, U11): LP vs error vs random with unanswerable items. It answers the main question and tests the control in one run.
2. T7 (U2): no-progress stop on unanswerable items. Cheapest, likely the largest token saving, and directly tests the LLM noisy-TV failure.
3. T3 (U3): stated prediction calibration. It is a pure measurement; its result decides whether any model-based surprise signal is possible at all.
