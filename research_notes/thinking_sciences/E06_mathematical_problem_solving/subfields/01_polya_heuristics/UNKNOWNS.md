## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Hembree effect sizes by grade and method.** Only the abstract was read. Unblock: get the full JRME paper through a library. Needed only to calibrate human effects, not to run the bench.
- **Larger replication of Schoenfeld 1979 and Rott and Gawlick 2014.** Human study; nothing for us to run. Unblock: a search of 2015-2026 literature in German and English education journals.
- **Schoenfeld's 60% to under 20% control result.** No N, no test. Unblock: original data from Schoenfeld 1985 (book chapters 4 to 7), not opened.
- **Equal-token numbers for all published LLM Polya prompts.** The papers did not log tokens. Unblock: our own runs (U1 to U14) are the only source.
- **Selection versus execution split.** Needs gold decompositions or gold plans per item to separate "chose the wrong operator" from "executed it badly". Unblock: hand-write plans for 50 items and run executor only.
- **Whether reasoning models already use Polya moves unprompted.** Needs coded traces. Unblock: run the SHAPE taxonomy annotator (F1 70 to 77) over our logs; link to the Schoenfeld episodes subfield.
- **Live self-monitoring by a reasoning model.** ProcessBench judges finished solutions by others. Unblock: U3 with a mid-attempt interrupt, which needs harness support for pausing and resuming a thinking trace.
- **"Draw a figure" for a text model.** No definition of a figure for an LLM. Unblock: define it as a table, a coordinate list, or an ASCII grid and test in U5.
- **Training-time effects (SHAPE heuristic RL, Llama-Polya, RevThink).** Out of scope; we do not train.

## Order to run
1. **E06.01-U1** (menu vs bare vs diversity at equal tokens on a reasoning model): decides whether a Polya menu is worth anything at all; every other operator test depends on it.
2. **E06.01-U3** (concrete control step vs generic review vs none): the Schoenfeld control claim is the strongest idea in the field and has no model test; guards G1 and G2 gain or lose weight.
3. **E06.01-U4** (operator record vs label): cheap, and it fixes the most-cited weakness (descriptive not prescriptive); its result shapes how U1 and U5 operators are written.
