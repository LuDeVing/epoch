## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **No natural-language conjecture benchmark with known counterexamples.** Almost all LLM evidence is Lean, Isabelle or GAP. Unblock: build 60 to 100 natural-language claims with a known counterexample or proof, labelled by code.
- **No code sandbox in some arms.** REFUTE and MathConstruct need an interpreter. Unblock: add a restricted Python tool to the harness, and keep a no-sandbox arm.
- **No second-target replication of CPL.** A fair test needs several lemma-rich target theorems. Unblock: collect 5 to 10 problem families where intermediate results are reusable.
- **Human posing evidence cannot be tested on the agent.** The g 0.72-1.31 gains are on human posing skill. Unblock: none; use only as a prior.
- **No test of the primary texts.** Brown and Walter and Lakatos Part II and the 1976 book were not opened. Unblock: read the books and extract any operator list not covered.
- **Unit of the BrokenMath validate-first gain is unknown.** Unblock: reread the paper table.
- **Larsen and Zandieh guided reinvention and WIN geometry studies (EJ1186756, EJ1301417) not read.** Unblock: open the full texts.
- **Independent checker quality.** LLM judges decide validity in MathDuels, BrokenMath and ConjectureBench. Unblock: a human-labelled sample of our own variants to measure checker recall.
- **Contamination.** ConjectureBench and DAP show signs. Unblock: held-out items written after the model cutoff.

## Order to run

1. **U2 with U3 (code counterexample search with sanity gate, T2).** Cheapest, highest expected gain, and the gate rule can be tested in the same run.
2. **U1 (What-If-Not vs restate-and-check, T1).** It is our only operator with no LLM test at all, and O4 is already in the schema as [S].
3. **U5 (explicit-redefinition rule, T4).** Needs only a few seeded tasks, and the baseline rescue rate is itself unknown; it decides whether Lakatos moves belong in S5.
