## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human baseline.** What share of novice attempts pick a path and never monitor it? Needs the 1985 book or the 1992 chapter read directly. Our bench has no human protocols. Until then no human percentage goes into the schema (K28).
- **Independent replication of the Schoenfeld-label result.** Both labelling papers are from one lab. A third-party run of the correctness analysis with held-out scoring is missing. Our own B1 logging (U1) is the closest we can do.
- **Precision and recall against human-labelled stuck points.** We have no human stuck labels. Needs two annotators to mark where a trace becomes stuck, on 100 traces, before any detector claim.
- **Hidden-state probes and decoding penalties.** Need open weights and logit access. Our scaffold is black-box. Unblock by running one open model locally as a side study, only to see whether text counts track the probes.
- **Forward citations from ThinkARM.** Search quota ran out. Replications or rebuttals since 2025 may exist. Unblock by a new search pass when quota returns.
- **Frontier models with hidden reasoning.** Published traces are open o1-like models. Whether the shapes hold for the model we call through an API is unknown. Unblock by logging visible reasoning where the API gives it.
- **Why difficulty flips the signal (AUC 0.76 easy vs 0.48 hard).** Needs larger per-difficulty samples than we have.
- **Whether visible traces reflect the computation.** Schema G10 says no. Any episode label inherits that limit. No test planned.

## Order to run

1. **E06.04-U1 (within-problem logging).** Cheapest: pure logging of runs we already make, no new arms. It also tells us whether U4, U5 and U6 have a usable trigger, so run it first.
2. **E06.04-U3 (dead-end note vs delete vs keep).** Best causal evidence in the subfield (+8 to +14 points) and a direct fit for S5. Only the note variant is new, and it is cheap.
3. **E06.04-U2 (three-arm check placement).** Resolves the one real contradiction (Verify helps vs review hurts) and decides how S8 and G1 should be worded.
