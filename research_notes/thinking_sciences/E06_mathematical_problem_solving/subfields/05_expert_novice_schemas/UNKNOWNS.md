## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human expert and novice sorts of the same items. Needs a human panel; Chi's original cards are lost and the Mason-Singh set was not obtained. Unblock: write our own crossed items and use published human rates only as rough anchors.
- Original Chi 1981, Veldhuis and Hardiman journal versions were not opened; numbers rest on replications. Unblock: obtain the PDFs.
- Whether the sorting gap exists in non-extreme groups in maths. Unblock: a human study with crossed maths items.
- Contamination of our items in current models. Unblock: fresh, hand-written items made after the training cutoffs.
- Mechanism (pattern matching vs a failing schema pipeline). Needs open weights and probing; not in the bench.
- Why 2011 students sorted less by surface than 1981 novices. Needs historical human data.
- No published equal-token comparison exists in this literature; every reported gain needs our own re-run.

## Order to run
1. E06.05-U1 (principle-first vs CoT at equal tokens): cheapest, one prompt line, and it decides whether any "name the principle" step stays in the schema.
2. E06.05-U5 (class-keyed vs surface-keyed memory): memory is the largest planned component and the evidence is small and token-unmatched.
3. E06.05-U4 (restate/filter vs distractors): targets the one surface-pull failure with solid measured evidence, and its token cost decides whether S1 hygiene needs a model step.
