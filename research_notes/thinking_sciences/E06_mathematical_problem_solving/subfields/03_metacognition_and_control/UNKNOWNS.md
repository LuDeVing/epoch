## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Schoenfeld's original coded rates.** The 1985 book and 1992 chapter were not opened. The 7 of 12 wild-goose-chase figure comes from the 1983 paper only. Unblock: obtain the book or chapter and read the coded tables. This affects only how strongly we cite the human case, not the tests above.
- **Maths-only human training effect.** Dignath and Buettner's maths subgroup d and Donker 2014 were not found. Unblock: full text of both. Low value for the schema.
- **Hidden-state probes (K11) and logit-based tools (TIP, DeepConf).** Need open weights or logprobs. Unblock: an open-weight model on the bench, or an API that exposes logprobs.
- **Current frontier models (2026).** Almost all overthinking and underthinking evidence is from QwQ-Preview, R1 and distills. Unblock: run U1 to U3 on the current agent model first and treat the published numbers as direction only.
- **Stable wrong answers.** The main hole in answer-stability stops. Unblock: measure on our own traces how often a repeated wrong answer ends a run (counts needed from U1).
- **Cost per call of monitors.** No paper compares judge, disagreement and tool-check monitors on cost. Unblock: log tokens in U8.
- **Control rules in proof-style or open maths.** Partial states are hard to judge there, so ToT-type search and progress markers may not apply. Unblock: a bench set with graded proofs or step labels.
- **Effects of trace faithfulness on monitoring.** If the trace hides the real cause, a monitor reading it may mislead. Unblock: plant a hint and test whether a stall monitor reading the trace sees it (K12 test).

## Order to run
1. **U1 (stall rule vs no trigger vs DEER-style probe).** Cheapest to build, the central S6 question, and no paper answers it for maths. Its logs also give the stable-wrong-answer rate.
2. **U7 (Wait recheck vs parallel samples at equal tokens).** Sources disagree most here and the answer sets how S6 spends tokens; it reuses U1's items.
3. **U8 (error-location monitor comparison).** Location is the proven bottleneck, so the monitor choice limits every revision rule; it reuses wrong traces from U1 and U7.

U2 and U3 follow directly on the U1 harness. U9 is nearly free to add as a flip counter in all runs.
