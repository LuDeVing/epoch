## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Founding texts unread.** Brier 1950, Murphy 1973, Ferro and Fricker 2012 returned HTTP 403. Unblock: a full-text copy from a library or author page, then check the exact partition conditions and the Ferro-Fricker estimator terms.
- **2026 frontier forecasting Brier without prices.** We have no resolved live forecasting set of our own. Unblock: submit our agent to a live board such as ForecastBench, with prices hidden, and wait for resolution.
- **Superforecaster and crowd benchmarks.** No human panel. Unblock: a human baseline on a subset of our items, or a second-model "crowd".
- **Hedging cause.** Halawi's safety-training explanation is untested. Unblock: a model pair (base vs chat) on the same items with identical prompts.
- **Rare-event classes.** We do not know whether Jewson's catastrophic case ever bites on real records. Unblock: a bench class with base rate under .05 and 1,000+ items.
- **Training-based calibration.** Brier-reward RL works (Damani, Turtel) but we do not train. Unblock: only if the project later fine-tunes.
- **Long-term stability of class maps.** All studies are one-shot. Unblock: a rolling log over weeks with model version tags.
- **Frontier model reasoning vs calibration.** KalshiBench shows an extended-reasoning model far worse; the other models are unnamed in our notes. Unblock: replicate on our own items at several reasoning caps.

## Order to run
1. **U12 with U1 (map and break-even size).** Cheap, uses data we already log, and tells us whether the whole S9 calibration idea pays at our class sizes.
2. **U2 (elicitation form).** One prompt change; the answer decides how every later probability is collected, so run it before U3, U5 and U7.
3. **U4 (REL and RES by scaffold arm).** It tells us whether our scaffolds help honesty of confidence or only accuracy, which is the main gap in the literature and feeds G7 directly.
