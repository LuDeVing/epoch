# LLM forecasting benchmarks: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: equal tokens per question across arms, resolved binary questions in a frozen corpus (cutoff-filtered models) with a live ForecastBench check, paired bootstrap CIs. "Brier" means mean Brier on the same questions. Test numbers T1-T12 refer to DEEP.md section 8.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **How the parity-reaching systems work and what they cost.** Cassi, xAI, DeepMind and Torchcast methods are unpublished; ForecastBench records no tokens. Unblock: a published method card or our own re-implementation at known cost.
- **Whether parity holds against fresh superforecasters.** The human baseline is July 2024. Unblock: a new FRI human round (none found as of 2026-11-01) or our own panel.
- **Component effects at matched compute.** No public paper isolates them. We can only test this on our own harness (U2, U5, U6, U8).
- **How much leakage inflates 2025-26 backtest results.** Rates of leaking search results are measured (1.5-1.65%); effect on forecasts is not. Unblock: re-run a backtest with and without leaked results removed.
- **Horizon decay against markets for 2026 models.** Only Prophet Arena (2025 models, figure only). Unblock: lead-time scoring on our own logged forecasts (U11).
- **Real-money or expert-grade outcomes.** We cannot hire experts. Metaculus experts' median Brier was 0.0225 against mean 0.157; we cannot reproduce that.
- **Training effects.** RL on resolved markets lowered Brier by 0.012 vs o1; not testable without training.
- **The Metaculus "nine months" report and "Pros Beat Bots".** Not found. Unblock: the original reports.
- **Equal-token comparison of diversity vs samples at larger N.** The only study has 113 questions and no CIs. Unblock: 600+ question frozen set with multi-family access.

## Order to run

1. **U12 (frozen vs live check).** Cheap, and every other result depends on it being a trustworthy bench.
2. **U9 (price baseline and blend).** Largest known effect on market questions, cheapest to run, and it sets the baseline every later result must beat.
3. **U2 then U4 (mean of N runs, then held-out Platt).** Established-direction ingredients with the clearest size estimates (0.004 each); they decide whether the aggregate and calibrate steps pay for themselves under G7.
