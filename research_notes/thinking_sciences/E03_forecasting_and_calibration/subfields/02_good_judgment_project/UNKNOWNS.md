## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Live resolution.** Real forecasting needs questions that resolve after the run. Without a live feed we can only use archival questions, with leakage risk (at least 3.8% to 10% trivial items in published sets). Unblock: a post-cutoff question feed, such as ForecastBench dataset questions or Metaculus AI Benchmark items, with retrieval dates logged.
- **Enough questions.** A 0.01 Brier gap needs about 650 to 1,500 questions (0.02 needs 200 to 450). Unblock: batch runs of 400+ items, or 5-10 samples per item to cut per-item noise.
- **Human comparison.** We cannot rerun superforecasters, persistence or training effects on our agent. Only ForecastBench-style published superforecaster scores on the same questions give a reference.
- **Primary source of .65 and 70%.** Not found in Mellers 2015, 2017, or the 2020 reality check. Unblock: reading Superforecasting p.104 and its footnote, or asking the authors' data papers.
- **Hauenstein et al. 2024 effect sizes.** Only the abstract was read. Unblock: full text to see which method variables erase the teaming and training effects.
- **Tetlock 2005 primary numbers** (calibration, discrimination, fox vs hedgehog). Book not opened.
- **Lu 2025 expert comparison** redone with mean Brier on identical questions. No follow-up found.
- **Equal-token fairness of published scaffolds.** Bot-maker survey and tournament data are not controlled; only our own runs can answer this.
- **Cross-question memory at scale.** EvoForesight reports skill distillation gains (rating, abstract only). Unblock: full paper and an ablation that isolates the outcome record.
- **Non-binary and long-horizon questions.** All sources are mostly binary and short to medium horizon. Unknown whether any finding holds for numeric or multi-year questions.

## Order to run

1. **E03.02-U1 (retest of variant ranking):** cheapest and it sets the question count every other test needs; without it no scaffold comparison on forecasting is trustworthy.
2. **E03.02-U2 (5 short samples vs one long chain at equal tokens):** the largest open gap and the one most likely to change a default (G11); if it passes it also licenses U3 and U6.
3. **E03.02-U4 (retrieval ablation):** the biggest measured gain in the literature (Halawi, bots) and the one with the clearest cost question for S0 gating; run U5 (base-rate-first) in the same pass since it is one extra prompt arm.
