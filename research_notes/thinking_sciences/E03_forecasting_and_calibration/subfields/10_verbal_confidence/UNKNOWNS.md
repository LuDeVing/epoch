## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Human reliance on our output.** We have no human readers. Unblock: a small reader study, or accept model readers as a proxy and label results as such.
- **Primary human calibration papers.** Lichtenstein 1982 (DTIC ADA101986) and Wallsten 1986 (ADA196944) were blocked. Unblock: an alternate copy of the PDFs.
- **Mechanism (cached confidence, inflation circuit).** Needs open weights and probes. Unblock: an open model on the bench.
- **Training-based calibration (RLCR, PPO-M).** Needs RL. Use as upper bound only. Unblock: a training budget.
- **Frontier stated-confidence AUROC on large real forecasting sets.** The literature has none; we need dated questions with resolution over weeks. Unblock: a resolving question set and time.
- **Token-probability channel.** Needs an API with log-probs. Unblock: an endpoint that returns them.
- **Commitment effect (Kumaran).** Single author, open models. Unblock: replication on our model.

## Order to run

1. **E03.10-U1 (agreement vs single stated confidence at equal tokens).** Cheapest, closes the biggest gap, and the result sets what every other test uses as the confidence signal.
2. **E03.10-U3 (AUROC on resolving questions).** Decides whether any stated confidence is usable on forecasting at all; no paper has measured it.
3. **E03.10-U2 (abstain threshold under priced errors).** The only test that turns confidence into an action; needs U1's winning signal and checks the G6 interplay.
