## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Superforecaster use of decomposition.** No opened source isolates it. Unblock: the Tetlock book text or Mellers/Tetlock raw training data with a decomposition arm. Until then, do not cite it.
- **Event-probability decomposition in humans scored by Brier.** All human data are quantities. Unblock: a new human study, or a published one we have not found; not cheap.
- **Whether the Bosse gain is a calibration fix for a YES-biased model.** Needs the paired per-question data. Unblock: reproduce U1 and read the Brier split by arm.
- **2025-2026 frontier results on Kalyan's set.** None found because search quota ran out. Unblock: rerun the literature search, or just run U3.
- **Human extremeness rule for models.** Does the model's own knowledge boundary mark the "extreme" zone? Needs U2 results by item type, plus a per-item knowledge probe.
- **Part-level correlated error in LLMs.** Needs the injected-bias design in U5; no existing data.
- **Reasoning models with long traces.** All decomposition prompting studies use non-reasoning models. Whether a long trace already decomposes implicitly is open. Unblock: add a thinking-mode arm (SCHEMA section 7 "thinking-mode baseline") to U1 to U3.

## Order to run
1. **E03.05-U2 (gate on sample disagreement).** Cheap, uses existing Fermi data, and decides whether O3 may fire at all; wrong gating is the main way O3 loses at equal tokens (G7).
2. **E03.05-U1 (three-arm forecast test).** The decisive and currently contested question; it needs the retrieval budget but settles b13 vs c01.
3. **E03.05-U11 (conformal wrapper).** Established problem, a known fix, and cheap to check on our agent; high expected gain in calibration whatever U1 shows.
