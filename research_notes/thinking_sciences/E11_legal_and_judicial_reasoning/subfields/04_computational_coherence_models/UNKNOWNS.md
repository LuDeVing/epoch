## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human effect sizes for coherence shift. The founding papers give F and p only. A meta-analysis or a data request would unblock this. It matters only for sizing U1.
- Independent replications of ECHO on human confidence (Schank and Ranney; Read and Marcus-Newhall). Not opened. Needs full texts.
- Probabilistic coherence (Bovens and Hartmann; Olsson) versus ECHO on multiple testimony. Needs the books or papers and a bench with testimony items.
- Whether real legal text can be graph-built at all by an LLM. We have no blind-coded legal cases with outcomes. We would need a small set of decided cases with rival-hypothesis structure.
- Lehr and Cummins sample sizes and the new three-condition result. Needs authors' data on OSF.
- Whether predecisional distortion (Russo) and network coherence shift are separable on LLMs. No design found.
- Frontier-model versions of BeliefBank, ConCoRD and REFLEX. None published; we must run our own.

## Order to run
1. U1 (fresh-context re-rating): cheapest, no solver needed, and it decides a guardrail rule that applies to every rating step.
2. U2 with U3 together (solver v majority vote, with flip-to-wrong count): decides whether any solver belongs in S7 at all, and gives the missing error rate in the same run.
3. U4 (repeat-build stability): if graph builds disagree, U2 results cannot be trusted, and U5 and U6 become moot.
