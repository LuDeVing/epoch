## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Original numbers of Thompson and Schumann (1987) and Koehler (1996).** Paywalled; we have only abstracts or citations. Unblock: access to the papers. Until then do not quote their percentages (d05, d06).
- **Real prosecutor's-fallacy transcripts or case files with expert-labelled ground truth.** All legal-domain LLM evidence is vignettes or BN arithmetic. Unblock: a small labelled corpus (court judgments with expert critique, for example appeal decisions that name the fallacy).
- **Human legal professionals in 2020-2026 on the Hoffrage DNA problem.** Needs a human study; we can only compare LLMs with the 2000 figures (13% vs 68%).
- **LLM readers' sensitivity to forensic conclusion format.** The only near-match (Pompedda 2025) was read through press releases (d13). Unblock: the full paper.
- **Claude-thinking and o3-class results.** The published legal-order and conservatism studies use GPT-4o and GPT-5.2. Our bench can run Claude models, so U3, U4 and U7 cover this once built.
- **Whether LLM order effects come from recency or from the story change when order is reversed.** Needs items where reversing order does not alter the narrative (d09 notes the confound).
- **Ground truth for real cases.** Priors and LRs for real evidence are disputed (Fenton vs critics, K08, K23). We can only test against constructed items with known LRs.
- **Explaining-away in LLM answers.** Keppens' error cause (4 of 100 runs wrong) could not be reproduced (c03). Needs more networks.
- **Does Bayesian fine-tuning apply?** Out of scope for a scaffold (K19); would need a training budget.

## Order to run

1. **E11.09-U7 with U3 (order invariance on reasoning models).** Cheapest, code-scored, and it decides whether the order check and the conservatism fix apply to the models we actually use.
2. **E11.09-U1 (convert-to-counts versus plain versus code).** The most direct candidate operator; the human evidence is established and the LLM test is missing.
3. **E11.09-U2 (prosecutor's fallacy detection and commission rates).** Nothing is measured yet; the result decides whether any fallacy check is worth its tokens for evidence tasks.
