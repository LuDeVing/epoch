## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Human expert baseline on exception-chain and burden-of-proof items. Needs legal practitioners or a gold-labelled set.
- Natural-language statute items with a trusted gold label for burden and standard of proof. Unblock by building a small matched-pair set with law-trained annotators.
- Edge extraction from natural-language text into an attack graph (U3 depends on it). Needs a corpus with gold edges.
- Independent replication of the CoT penalty on 2026 models by another team.
- Whether human semantics (CF2, preferred, void precedence) should guide the schema. Human data are small-N and contested; no bench test applies.
- Full text of the sources read only at abstract level (rule-grounding, NormBench, LLM jurors) and the unopened ones (Ford 2005, Hahn and Oaksford 2007, Rahwan 2010). Unblock by opening them.
- Whether vendor-style rule engines with expert rules are worth building at all; needs an expert rule base we do not have.

## Order to run

1. **U12 (repeat rule and drift check).** Cheapest, and every other result depends on knowing how noisy our model is.
2. **U2 (CoT vs direct on defaults) together with U1 (exception slot).** Same item family; tells us whether to add a structure or to remove thinking, and both are single-source today.
3. **U3 (code labelling table) and U5 (separate label-then-verdict loop).** The largest measured failures (recall 38%, GPT-5-mini 20.3% revision) and both fixes are mostly code, so the token cost is low.
