## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Exact N and effect size of Braman and Nelson (2007). Needs the paper or book chapter; the page was blocked (403).
- Whether Spamann-Klohn-style results hold for LLM judges with exact cell counts. Needs the Posner and Saran paper and the Katz and Zamir and SAGE items, all unopened or press-only.
- Independent replication of Zhang 2026 and of SILVER on common-law data. Needs another group; none found.
- Full text of Levi (1949) and Sunstein (1993). Needs a library copy.
- Whether the flat or hierarchical result model predicts real court behaviour. No empirical design exists.
- Whether legal training reduces motivated analogical perception. Only a page summary.
- Prompt stability of abstention (needs prompt variants; the source papers used one).
- Counterfactual analogy on 2025-2026 reasoning models. Not in the literature found; our own run (U7) is the only route.

## Order to run

1. **U2 (code-side hierarchy lookup).** Largest expected gap (11-34% baseline), exact ground truth from a symbolic solver, cheap to build synthetic items, and it tests G5 directly.
2. **U1 (rule-naming step).** Directly tests the founding Levi claim as an operator on exact-label items; the nearest prior result has no rule-naming arm and no token match.
3. **U3 (typed NO_ANALOGUE fallback).** Cheap, scores a behaviour that moves headline accuracy (131 of 236 "unknown" cut Gemini-Pro to 42.37%), and resolves the G6 conflict one way or the other.
