## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Founding numbers.** Test statistics and cell sizes for the 1988 and 1992 order experiments are missing; the original papers were never opened. Unblock: obtain the JPSP 1992 and JEP:LMC 1988 articles.
- **Real-trial scale.** The order effect has only been shown on short, written or taped mock trials with students. Our bench has no trial-length evidence set with gold labels. Unblock: build or find long-evidence items with known answers.
- **Post-2000 replications and failures.** None found for the order effect; a student-vs-community reversal (d16) is unconfirmed. Unblock: a proper literature search with the paywalled sources.
- **Human baselines on our items.** Humans' best-case story processing cannot be run on our bench. Unblock: a small human panel, if the question matters.
- **Combination rule for the five certainty principles.** No weights exist in any source. Unblock: a labelled set of stories with human certainty ratings to fit weights.
- **Multi-agent story deliberation.** G9 bars debate, and 12 LLM jurors hung in 17 of 18 runs, so group story-model effects are not worth testing now.
- **Prompt-time token cost of the coverage audit.** Not measured anywhere (critique gap 6). Our own run logs will be the first data.
- **Reasoning models.** Most LLM numbers come from GPT-4o-era models. Whether reasoning models remove order and early-commitment effects is unmeasured except for GPT-5.2 on guilt ratings. Unblock: run U1, U2 and U6 on the current models.
- **Stage-two reinterpretation in LLMs.** Whether an LLM reinterprets answer criteria through its story (as jurors do with "exhaust all means to avoid combat") has no test. Unblock: items with abstract criteria and a typical-case trap.

## Order to run

1. **E11.01-U1 (story order of facts).** Cheapest (no extra tokens), largest expected gain, and the only test that copies the founding manipulation on a model.
2. **E11.01-U2 (two-order check).** The order bias is the best-measured LLM effect (F=424.76), and the test also tells us whether disagreement predicts errors.
3. **E11.01-U4 (code-counted coverage audit).** The cheapest form of the model's central idea, coverage, and the only one that code can check without a judge. Run U5 on the same data afterwards to learn which score to trust.
