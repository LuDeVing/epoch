## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Founding story-model numbers** (verdict shares by evidence order, confidence by uniqueness). Missing: access to Pennington and Hastie 1986, 1988 and 1992 full text. Unblock: a library copy or open-access preprint; then verify the "78% vs 31%" figure before anyone quotes it (K20).
- **Duration of the coherence shift** (the "transient" claim, Simon et al. 2008). Missing: the paper. Unblock: obtain it; for the agent, the question may not transfer since context is rebuilt each call.
- **Whether coverage and coherence matter separately.** Missing: any opened experiment that varies them independently. Unblock: our own U9 and U13.
- **IBE-Eval on frontier models and open-ended tasks.** Missing: any follow-up study (d17). Unblock: U2, which is the replication.
- **LLM story-model coding (precision 72.7%) and the GPT-4o/Claude 3.5 mock-jury replication.** Missing: the full texts (c21, c22 seen as snippets only). Unblock: open them; they may report directly on LLM story-model behaviour.
- **Internal hidden-state coherence signals** (probe accuracy 0.80 to 0.96). Missing: white-box access to the model we run. Unblock: only with an open-weight model; useful only as a research probe, not as a schema element.
- **Real-world generalisation of a code coverage score.** Missing: a benchmark with a ground-truth evidence list and several coherent rival explanations of similar quality. Unblock: build planted-story items (U9) and extend to a public causal-explanation set.
- **Gaming of coherence scores by adversarial stories on current models.** Missing: any test on current judges (K25 uses 2023 models). Unblock: U8.
- **Effect of external rating storage on the LLM shift.** Missing: any study with a stored first rating (d15). Unblock: U4.

## Order to run

1. **E11.02-U7 (vote margin vs stated confidence, escalation threshold).** Cheapest: it uses logs we already produce, the signal is already established in the literature, and it directly sets thresholds for S0, S6 and S7.
2. **E11.02-U4 (frozen evidence ratings).** It tests the only new guardrail the research supports (A5 in DEEP.md), costs no extra tokens in the frozen arm, and the human effect behind it is large and established.
3. **E11.02-U9 (planted coverage, coherence, uniqueness).** No one has run it; it tells us whether the story-model principles describe LLM choice at all, and its stories feed U1 and U13.
