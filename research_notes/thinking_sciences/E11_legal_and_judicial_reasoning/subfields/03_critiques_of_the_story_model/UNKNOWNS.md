## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Real first-ballot-to-verdict base rates from direct sources (Kalven and Zeisel 1966; Devine et al. 2001). Unblock: open both and check the figures; not needed for the schema.
- Smith 1988 and any replication or failed replication of the story-order effect, including the unverified student-vs-community reversal. Unblock: library access or a working scholarly API.
- Exact numbers for McKenzie, Lee and Chen (2002). Unblock: full text.
- Why 17 of 18 LLM juries hung (persona, temperature, RLHF, stop rule). Unblock: a persona-free, temperature-swept, 5+ model rerun with larger N; this is a panel question, and our design does not use panels.
- Whether the dissenter effect holds when the task is weighing evidence rather than answering factual questions. Unblock: an evidence-weighing bench item set with planted wrong peers (U5 builds it).
- Whether story form matters for humans beyond structure. Unblock: a human experiment with matched non-story structure; out of scope for us.
- Whether results from small open models (7-9B) carry to the models we run. Unblock: rerun the debate and dissent comparisons on our own model.
- Per-cell values for Wynn et al., Zhu et al., and Bessis et al. Unblock: transcribe the tables from full text.

## Order to run

1. **E11.03-U12 (first-answer logging).** Cheapest: needs only our past logs and one added log field; it tells us how much any critique or panel step can move.
2. **E11.03-U2 (evidence-order flip check).** Order sensitivity is established in LLMs, so the only question is whether the flip predicts error and whether a recheck pays; high expected gain, cheap to run.
3. **E11.03-U1 (causal order vs source order vs matched list).** The one human causal result is single-study and no LLM test exists; this answers both the story-order question and the "why story form" critique.
