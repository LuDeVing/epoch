## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human count under varied output cost.** No study varies typing or output cost. Unblock: run a small human or model pilot where list entry is cheap vs costly. We have no human subjects. Only a model proxy is possible.
- **Real expertise.** Mehle's experts were mostly motor-pool staff. Unblock: think-aloud data from practising technicians. None in hand.
- **Rasmussen (1981 to 1986) and Morris and Rouse (1985) hypothesis results.** Primary texts sat behind bot walls. Unblock: library access to the Human Factors review and the Rasmussen chapters. Do not cite either for a count until read.
- **Mechanical and software fault spaces with an omniscient reference.** Our cause sets are synthetic. Unblock: a real incident corpus with root cause labels and a full cause list (Lu RCABench-style).
- **Large-N staged-disclosure data.** Wang has 10 deep cases. Unblock: build 60 staged faults with ground truth.
- **Peer-reviewed LLM results.** Most are 2026 preprints with LLM judges. Unblock: replicate the key tests (Handler, Braitsch) with code-scored items and our own models.
- **Mechanism of LLM anchoring.** The autoregression account (b30) is untested. Unblock: probes on attention or ablate the first-position text; needs open weights.
- **Identity of causes across wordings.** Cause-key dedupe needs a reliable "same cause?" judge. Unblock: a labelled pair set and a blind judge with measured agreement.
- **Soar-like external ledger at equal tokens.** No source tests it. Our U5 and U13 are the first.

## Order to run
1. **E08.03-U1 (forced missing-cause step vs free list vs re-sampling).** It targets the largest measured LLM gap (about 70% false commit) and its result decides whether the S4/S8 NOTA branch is worth its tokens.
2. **E08.03-U2 (breadth curve with permuted facts).** Cheap, uses the same fault set, and sets the draw count and cause-key dedupe that U1, U3 and U4 all need.
3. **E08.03-U5 (staged anchoring: free vs ledger vs re-rank).** Tests the 65.6% stuck rate and the ledger's top-1 cost together, and shows whether memory in the schema keeps or hurts the right answer.
