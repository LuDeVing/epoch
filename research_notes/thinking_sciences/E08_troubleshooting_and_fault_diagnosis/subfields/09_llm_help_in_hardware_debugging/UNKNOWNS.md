## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Real bench photos.** Our bench is text. We cannot measure the false-claim rate per image (gap 2) or test real perception. Unblock: a small set of real breadboard photos with known wiring, scored by pin, with a control set.
- **Does LLM help change real repair time and success?** Needs a controlled human study with a no-LLM arm (the Hu and Ash authors list it as future work). Unblock: a collaborator with a lab course, or wait for their follow-up.
- **Replication of Hu and Ash, and technician populations.** No citing paper found (d16). Unblock: a later citation check, or running a small replication ourselves.
- **Human reliance effects of our agent's output style.** Needs human readers. A simulated reader model is only a weak proxy (U8).
- **Vocational electronics quasi-experiment.** Page returned HTTP 403 (d04). Unblock: a mirror or the authors' copy.
- **VeriPilot number (77.1% vs 85.71%).** Unblock: read the full results table.
- **Calibration of 2026 reasoning models on diagnostic claims.** RLVR overconfidence (d09) was a snippet. Unblock: read the paper, then run U7.
- **Why waveform context helps 8 models and hurts 13.** Needs per-model, per-bug-type breakdown; not in the sources we read.
- **Version-locked benchmarks.** GPT-5.4 and Opus 4.6 numbers in HWE-Bench cannot be reproduced; use them for direction only.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (measurement-before-claim gate).** It is the one direct schema link to the key paper, cheap to build with planted false claims and a scripted sensor, and a pass or fail is clear.
2. **U2 (state register).** The strongest general evidence of multi-turn loss (-39%, unreliability +112%) with no hardware test; the same items as U1 can be reused, so it is nearly free.
3. **U3 (cost-weighted test ordering).** Biggest measured effect among the untested ideas (UoT 43.7% to 67.3%), implemented in code so token cost is small; then U4 and U6 on the same item set.
