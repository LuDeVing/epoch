## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Schaafstal 2000 numbers (N, errors, effect size).** Full text is behind a paywall; three routes gave the abstract only. Unblock: a library copy or a request to the authors. Until then use only "about twice as many malfunctions, in less time".
- **Jonassen and Hung 2006 body and any test of the case-library design.** Paywalled. Unblock: institutional access; search citing papers for an empirical test.
- **Van Gog 2005-2008 numbers (orientation time, worked-example transfer).** Abstracts have no values. Unblock: full text.
- **A fault bench with executable probes and healthy-system items.** Needed for U1 to U4. Unblock: build 40 or more small component-graph items (circuits, service graphs) with scripted probe outputs and 12 or more no-fault items. Public sets (AIOpsLab, NIKA, ITBench) are small, partly closed, or emulation-heavy.
- **Independent judges.** SADE and FaulT-Bench share authors and judge; SIA uses its own rubric. Unblock: a different-family judge plus a human check on a sample before relying on any published effect size.
- **Whether results carry to 2026-era models.** Most numbers come from GPT-4 or GPT-3.5 era agents. Unblock: run our own arms on the current model; do not copy published sizes.
- **Needless repair in LLMs.** No study counts it. Unblock: log every state-changing action against a ground-truth minimal fix in the fault bench.
- **Human numbers for the cycle itself.** Only 8 pairs on one circuit with no outcome link. Unblock: not needed for the schema; an LLM-side test (U1) is the deciding evidence.
- **Whether the six knowledge types matter separately.** No test found. Low value for the schema; skip unless a cheap probe exists.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (cycle vs ReAct vs direct at equal tokens).** It is the missing direct test, and it decides whether S1-S4 should be imposed on fault items at all.
2. **U2 (no-fault exit plus verify gate).** The problem is measured in two benchmarks, the fix is cheap, and it touches G3, S8 and G6.
3. **U3 (code-chosen vs LLM-chosen probes).** Strongest outside evidence (UoT, BED-LLM), simple to build, and it fits G5 (code selects).
