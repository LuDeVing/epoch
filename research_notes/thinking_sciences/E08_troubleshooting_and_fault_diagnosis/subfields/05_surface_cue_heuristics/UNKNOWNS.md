## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Primary Rasmussen and Reason error rates by level. Unblock: obtain the paywalled texts (K32). Until then, cite no rates.
- Real-world base rate of cue-driven failures in live incidents. Unblock: a labelled incident set with known cue and true cause; ITBench and AIOpsLab do not separate them (K27).
- Whether human expert results (Chi-style deep-structure sorting, cognitive forcing in experienced clinicians) have an LLM analog. Unblock: a sorting task on fault cases by deep cause versus surface symptom.
- Token cost of rewrite-then-reason versus best-of-N. Unblock: our own equal-token runs (U1); neither paper reports it.
- Faithfulness of hint use on current frontier models. Unblock: re-run the hint-verbalisation test on our model.
- Whether the null human cognitive-forcing results reflect missing structure or missing expertise. Unblock: no direct route for an agent; use U4 and U11 as proxies.
