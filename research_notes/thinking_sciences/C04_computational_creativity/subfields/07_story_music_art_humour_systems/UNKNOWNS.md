## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Expert raters and a skilled-human baseline.** Every quality claim here depends on them (Chakrabarty vs Gomez-Rodriguez). We have neither. Unblock: recruit 3 writers or comedians for a one-off rating, or use a public set with expert labels.
- **EMI listening test counts.** Only an unquantified Wikipedia line. Unblock: obtain Hofstadter 2001 or Ariza 2009 full text.
- **Human preference for AI art and music at scale.** Only detection studies found. Unblock: a preference dataset with N and rater type.
- **Equal-token comparison of multi-agent writing systems.** Agents' Room, CritiCS, COMIC and StoryWriter all use more tokens than baselines. Unblock: rerun baselines with matched budgets and published prompts.
- **2025 to 2026 frontier models on stories.** Evidence is 2023 to 2024. Unblock: rerun TTCW or turning-point counts on current models.
- **Judge validity vs stability.** High agreement among LLM judges (tau 0.889) does not show agreement with humans. Unblock: a human-labelled held-out set with per-item expert labels.
- **Volume asymmetry in humour comparisons.** ChatGPT made 20 jokes per prompt, humans 1. Unblock: a design with equal pick-best from both sides.
- **Whether creative-task results say anything about reasoning tasks.** None of these is a single-answer task. Unblock: run U1 and U4 on a checkable task and see whether diversity helps pass rate (links to the Retrieval and ensembles and Automated discovery subfields).
- **Unopened sources.** Hofstadter 2001, Turner 1994, Cohen 1979, Ritchie 2007, Meehan 1976 and Binsted 1997 were not read.

## Order to run
1. **C04.07-U1 (diverse-pick selector at equal N).** Cheapest, code-only, and tests G4 and G5 directly on any open-ended item; the result also informs U2 and U12.
2. **C04.07-U3 (plan plus critique vs plain at equal tokens).** The largest unresolved claim in the literature (gains with extra tokens only); settles whether O3 earns its tokens for open tasks under G7.
3. **C04.07-U2 (verbalized distribution vs k independent calls).** Cheap, one call per arm, and the only intervention with measured human-rated diversity gains; the story-quality drop makes the equal-token test necessary before any adoption.
