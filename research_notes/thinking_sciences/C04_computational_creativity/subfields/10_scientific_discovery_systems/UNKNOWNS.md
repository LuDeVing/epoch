## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Independent replication of the ideation and execution studies (Si et al.).** Needs 40 or more experts and 100 hours each, or a cheaper automated executor we trust. Unblock: use the 2026 execution-grounded executor design on code-checkable items only.
- **BACON-era numbers** (law list, noise tolerance, the 4 of 14 human count). Needs the 1987 book and the Qin and Simon 1990 paper. Unblock: borrow both from a library; no bench work needed.
- **Whether the Kosmos computed-versus-interpreted gap is reproduced.** Needs long-running data-analysis agents and expert audit of statements. Unblock: a small audit by us on our own reports (U7).
- **Effect of retrieval grounding on the 24% plagiarism rate.** Needs expert checkers with paper-by-paper access. Unblock: a seeded-known-idea set (U5) as a cheap proxy.
- **Rate of gaming in noisy, non-code evaluators.** No measured rate exists. Unblock: build a leaky-judge item set (U9).
- **Cross-system tally of error types in 2025 to 2026 discovery agents.** No source. Unblock: tally our own failures with the trace log (U4).
- **Open-ended, unlabelled hypothesis tasks.** No benchmark exists without labels. Unblock: a small hand-built set with a scoring script.
- **Whether newer models change any of the 2023 to 2025 numbers** (judge accuracy near 50%, few-shot lowering novelty, diversity wall). Unblock: rerun the three cheapest versions on our current model.
- **Whether model mixing (cheap plus strong) helps in the generator role.** One qualitative note from AlphaEvolve. Unblock: two-model draft arm at equal tokens.

## Order to run

1. **C04.10-U1 (execute, then rank).** It tests the main gap in this field and our closest match to open mode with a running check; bench items and checks mostly exist.
2. **C04.10-U4 (trace log for verify).** Cheapest to build, and every other test is easier to read once the log exists.
3. **C04.10-U3 (loop versus memory on altered laws).** Decides whether the propose-fit-feedback pattern is worth the setup cost beyond famous problems, and it supplies the altered-variant control for later tests.
