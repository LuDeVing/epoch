## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Token and wall-clock share of descriptor labelling, judging and embedding.** No source reports it; Rainbow Teaming counts calls only (judge 4 of 7). Unblock: instrument our harness to log tokens per role and report shares before U1.
- **Archive share of AlphaEvolve and FunSearch.** No open reimplementation with a no-archive, matched-context control and more than 3 seeds. Unblock: a public ablation, or our own small code-evolution control.
- **Failed replications of novelty search on real deceptive tasks.** None found. Unblock: a search of negative-result venues, or a replication on our own deceptive item set (needs items with a known misleading partial-credit signal).
- **PAIR with restarts at the same prompt budget.** Rainbow Teaming's baseline had no restarts. Unblock: a re-run, outside our scope.
- **Mind Evolution with a model-only verifier.** Every task had a programmatic evaluator. Unblock: our bench items with only a model check, if any exist.
- **Pugh et al. 2016 numbers.** Not re-verified (download failed). Unblock: retrieve the PDF and re-read before citing a number.
- **Reasoning-method descriptor labelling agreement.** No study. Unblock: U6.
- **Human-judged QD gain at scale.** QDAIF's human study had one median run per method and co-author raters. Unblock: a larger blinded study, outside our scope.
- **Whether diverse-method gains on ARC (Land 2026) come from diversity or from judging.** The author skipped the ablations. Unblock: U8 with a duplicate-filtered control.

## Order to run

1. **U2 (duplicate filter)**: cheapest, one embedding call per attempt, and established in two settings; also yields the token-share log needed for every later test.
2. **U3 (stagnation counter)**: a counter in code, no archive; tests the only explore-then-exploit rule with measured sensitivity, and checks G1 on our data.
3. **U1 (archive vs best-of-N with dedup control)**: the decisive question (gap 1); run after U2 so the dedup arm exists, and after U6's labeller check so the descriptor is trusted. If U1 fails, the archive stays out and only U2, U4 and U5 survive as the cheap parts.
