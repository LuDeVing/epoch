## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **A verifiable benchmark of real rule changes where the familiar frame provably fails.** Baba Is AI is combinational; OMEGA is template maths; TwistBench and Spark were not retrieved. Unblock: retrieve TwistBench and Spark, or hand-build 60 items with a proof that the familiar tactic fails.
- **A measure of transformation that is not relative to the agent's own history.** Bhushan's level 4 is P-creativity only. Unblock: external reference corpus per task class, plus a code check for "outside the old space".
- **Whether LLM transformation exists at all.** Position papers deny it, no test is direct. Unblock: pass@k widening test (U4) on items that need a rule change.
- **Why T-UoT fails: bad edits or lost consequences.** Only 12 hand-sorted cases. Unblock: code-checked propagation measure on 100+ cases.
- **Hivemind validity under a matched null.** One author group on each side. Unblock: independent replication with a same-prompt null and a human frame label.
- **Wiggins 2006, Ritchie 2006, Toivanen 2018, Boden's books** only second-hand. Unblock: open the originals before citing any detail.
- **Long-run behaviour.** Bhushan used 10 episodes. Unblock: multi-hundred step runs on our own harness.

## Order to run

1. **C04.04-U2** (operator vs plain, "be creative", temperature, best-of-N): it decides whether O4/O8 stay at all, and every other test needs the same controls.
2. **C04.04-U1** (consequence step after a rule edit): the cheapest fix for the one failure mode measured in detail, and it is code-checkable.
3. **C04.04-U5** (impasse-triggered reframe vs schedule): it tests G1 and S6 directly with data we already log, and speaks to the 87.0 vs 6.5 percent refine/abandon gap.
