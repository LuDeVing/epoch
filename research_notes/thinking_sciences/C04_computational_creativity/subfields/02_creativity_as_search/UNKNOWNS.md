## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Transformational creativity: there is no benchmark of space-changing moves. Unblock by building one from ARC-like items where the standard representation provably fails, and checking whether a changed representation solves them.
- Original ideas: Heuresis found zero of 3,222 runs rated Original. Our bench has no long-horizon research task with executable outcome. Unblock with a multi-hour code improvement suite with held-out scoring.
- Ideation-execution gap for our own ideas: needs executors (people or an agent harness) and days per item. Unblock with an execution harness for a small set of well-defined ideas.
- Replication of Creative Beam Search, QDAIF human evaluation, Nair et al. and Bhushan et al.: we have no raters or vision-model setup. Unblock with a small paid rater panel or an LLM-judge validated against it (Spearman 0.732 is the bar seen so far).
- Human-side calibration of creative quality: needs a rater pool; the LLM-judge numbers (r = 0.732 to 0.80) come from small validations (30 to 50 items).
- Which search structure suits which task: needs a systematic grid of greedy, beam, MCTS, evolutionary and QD across many task types; ours is too small. Unblock by reusing the Heuresis task set if released.
- Text-only proxy exploitation rate: all measured rates come from code tasks. Unblock by seeding fabrication traps in text scaffolds and counting.
- Wiggins and Ritchie theory sources were not opened. Unblock by reading them; low expected gain.
