## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Debate vs vote with long-reasoning models: no published test found, and our bench has no multi-agent debate harness. Needs a small debate runner with token accounting.
- Current-model flip rates in each reasoning mode: needs the probes written into our harness (U3, U4).
- Whether RL-trained reasoning models self-correct with a verifier better than older models did: needs the same loop on a 2025 model, which is part of U5.
- An LLM analogue of the human wrong-hub effect: needs a topology test with planted wrong answers (U14).
- Heterogeneous model pools: only possible if we may use more than one model family on the bench.
- Primary texts for Goldman 1999 and Christensen 2007/2009 were not read; the philosophical side rests on SEP and Elga. This does not block any test.
- Aggregation rule under clusters (cluster count vs calibrated weights vs extremizing): shared with the Aggregation and extremizing subfield; needs a joint test (U6, U7, U8).

## Order to run
1. **U2 (effective N pilot).** Cheapest, needs no new harness, and every aggregation rule depends on the answer.
2. **U3 and U4 (flip rates and S1 hygiene).** Cheap to write, tests the largest known harm (10 to 30 point drops) and a rule already planned in S1.
3. **U1 and U5 (vote vs debate, and external check vs critic).** Highest value: settles whether any peer-style loop stays, and what the revise step gets. Run after U2 so effective N is known.
