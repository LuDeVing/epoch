## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human Jeffrey data (Zhao and Osherson 2010; Hadjichristidis et al. 2014): need the full papers (closed access) for N and effect sizes. Unblock: library access or author preprints. Norm use only; it does not change the agent.
- Any LLM study of true soft evidence (Jeffrey-style input): none found; search was blocked (quota, HTTP 429). Unblock: repeat gap searches 1, 4, 8, 9 with search working.
- Intra-model correlation of repeated check errors: no coefficient exists. We must measure it in U3.
- QUITE per-model accuracies for verbal probabilities (a22): need a figure read or the data table.
- Verify the 64% WebPPL figure, the BayesBench effect sizes, the Gemma simple case in Filbry (a16), and the Yu 2021 table alignment before citing.
- Frontier reasoning models: most dynamics papers use 2B-8B models. Unblock: run the same protocols on our target model.
## Order to run
1. D02.04-U1 (code multiplies vs model restates): it decides whether the whole ledger design is worth building, and the arms are cheap.
2. D02.04-U3 (correlation discount): false confirmation from repeated same-model checks is the largest risk to S4, and it feeds U1.
3. D02.04-U5 (change-gate): the cheapest rule to implement, with a clear failure to watch on both sides (wrong flips and under-update).
