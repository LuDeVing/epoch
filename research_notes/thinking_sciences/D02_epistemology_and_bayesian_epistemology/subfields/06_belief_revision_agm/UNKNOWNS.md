## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **AGM-Bench per-postulate numbers.** Only the abstract is public; OpenReview is blocked (HTTP 403). Unblock: author copy or a later arXiv version.
- **Does the human "explanation drives revision" effect replicate outside one lab, and does it predict LLM revision?** Needs a human replication and an LLM study with items varying the number of known exceptions (disablers). Not something our bench can produce.
- **Error location from stack traces on ARC-style tasks.** Our bench has no stack traces for ARC. Unblock: instrument programme-synthesis runs to log which claim caused each failure, then score by hand.
- **Independent reproduction of memory-framework scores** (Kumiho, CUPMem, MOIRA, TOKI). Needs unmodified benchmarks and strict answer-matching, not an LLM judge.
- **Outside replication of dependency propagation.** We can run U4, but until another team does, it stays a bench-local result.
- **Revise versus restart-with-summary at equal tokens on long tasks.** Needs tasks long enough that history cost matters. Our short items may not show it.
- **Entrenchment by "number of dependents".** No benchmark has a ground-truth dependency structure. Unblock: build items with planted dependency graphs.
- **Taint tracking for laundered claims** (a low-tier claim restated by a high-tier source). Needs a multi-source task set.
- **Frontier 2026 models on Belief-R-style items with tools.** Most published numbers are 2024 to 2025 models, zero-shot. Unblock: rerun a small Belief-R subset on our models.

## Order to run

1. **D02.06-U1 (one-sentence outranks rule).** Cheapest, largest published effect (4.2% to 91.4%), and it is the baseline every richer Revise state (U2, U3) must beat.
2. **D02.06-U9 (one gate, both ends).** Tests the core design claim that the discrimination belongs in the scaffold, not in the weights. Both failure directions in one run.
3. **D02.06-U4 (dependency rollback by code).** Largest claimed gain (0.44 to 1.00), but single-author, so it needs our own bench result before it earns a place. Run U5 on the same items at no extra build cost.
