## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Frontier reasoning models in a large matrix.** The 4,575-model leaderboard has none; Krakauer has n = 3-8 per epoch. Unblocks: a model-by-benchmark matrix with 30+ reasoning models, collected from API runs on a common bench.
- **Contamination-free ability measure.** Without fresh items, loadings may reflect shared test material (Gkn tests correlate .98-.99). Unblocks: a held-out, regularly refreshed item pool.
- **Reasoning factor in open-ended tasks.** The only open-ended second axis seen is instruction following. Unblocks: a battery of at least 20 open-ended tasks with automatic checks, run on 25+ models.
- **Test-retest reliability of loadings.** No study repeats a whole factor analysis. Unblocks: two independent runs of the same model pool.
- **Primary Carroll 1993 and Horn numbers (461 data sets, 9-10 broad abilities).** Lending-only (HTTP 401). Unblocks: library or purchased copy; until then use "over 450".
- **Per-item scaffold benefit at equal tokens.** Needs a paired plain/scaffold run with item-level logs; our harness can produce it, but demand annotation for our items is not yet done.
- **Whether the CHC-style profile (Hendrycks) is a valid factor structure.** The mapping is the authors' own, graded manually, two models. Unblocks: factor fit on a larger agent population, not equal-weight scoring.
- **Bias-corrected bifactor vs higher-order comparison for LLM data.** Needs the simulation approach of Murray and Johnson applied to our matrix.

## Order to run

1. **A13.01-U3 (noise floor).** Cheapest, and every other test needs its seed count and minimum detectable effect.
2. **A13.01-U1 with U8 (class-level scaffold gain, split by plain pass rate).** Highest expected value: tells us whether the S0 gate should be driven by class and by foothold, and it reuses the same item runs.
3. **A13.01-U10 then U2 (cheap IRT subset, then demand regression).** The subset makes later runs cheaper; the demand regression is the only route to a per-item gate.
