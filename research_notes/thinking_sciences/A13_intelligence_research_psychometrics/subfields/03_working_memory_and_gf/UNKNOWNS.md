## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Dokic, Koso-Drljevic, Bilalic 2025 (the named key paper): no full text. Would unblock: a copy via a library or the authors' open version; until then no number from it is used.
- Kyllonen and Christal 1990 full text: exact task list and overlap sensitivity analyses. Would unblock: a scan of the paper.
- Au 2014/2015 numbers for active-control splits (g = 0.24 not re-read): needs the paywalled paper or supplementary tables.
- Kovacs and Conway 2016 (process overlap) was not opened; its LLM prediction in this folder is inference. Would unblock: reading the paper and writing a precise test prediction.
- Human WM-Gf causal claims (CDA, pupil, heritability, connectome): not testable on an agent bench.
- Behaviour of current frontier thinking models on n-back and span with lure analysis: needs API budget and a lure-controlled item set.
- Restraint versus constraint attention in LLMs (Kane 2016 wording was not tied to the citation): needs a task pair that separates suppressing distractor rules from holding rules.
- Which model scored about 68% at 500 rules in IFScale (probably gemini-2.5-pro): needs the paper table or the released data.
- Collapse beyond 500 rules: IFScale stops there; needs our own sweep (U3).

## Order to run
1. **A13.03-U1** (written state versus rule count): the only direct test of the "limit on holding many rules" idea for our agent, cheap (text items), and the answer decides whether the state table is core.
2. **A13.03-U2** (checklist re-pass on dense rules): cheapest change to the verify step, with the best existing evidence in the schema's own language (code checks, G5).
3. **A13.03-U4** (checkpointing long procedures): tests whether state passing removes the length collapse at equal tokens; it also settles U13 as a side result.
