## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Novel versus familiar labels.** We have no items with a ground-truth novelty level. Perturbed copies give a proxy. Missing: a set where novelty is set by construction and known to the grader. Unblock: build counterfactual variants of existing items (U4).
- **Contamination size on ARC.** Needs tasks written after model cutoffs. Unblock: generate fresh ARC-style tasks with a script and check the plain pass rate against public ones.
- **Reliability and g loading of LLM Raven scores.** Needs many models and repeated runs; the human norm does not transfer (K23). Unblock: matched human baseline in the same text format, N above 30.
- **Primary Gf sources.** Cattell 1943 and Horn and Cattell 1967 were not read (K15). Unblock: library copy or open-access scan. Low value for the schema.
- **Working-memory limits in LLMs.** The n-back critique and the backward-digit-span paper are leads not opened (d13, d14). Unblock: fetch both.
- **Prompt-only frontier results on ARC-AGI-2 at matched cost.** Only vendor data (Poetiq). Unblock: our own B10 run with a verified cost log.
- **Does the human/model difficulty anti-alignment hold for prompt-only frontier models?** Only fine-tuned code-trained transformers were studied (d16). Unblock: score our own items by human accuracy and by model accuracy.
- **Why the counterfactual gap differs between Webb, Hodel-West and Lewis-Mitchell.** Prompt wording, human pool and code access are confounded. Unblock: one fixed prompt across tasks and models (K05).

## Order to run

1. **A13.02-U2 (program with execution check vs plain chain of thought).** Cheapest and highest prior; it also builds the item set and logging the other tests reuse.
2. **A13.02-U4 (perturbation consistency).** Gives the novel-versus-familiar tag that U1 and U5 need, and tests the contamination question directly.
3. **A13.02-U1 (pilot-attempt novelty gate).** It is the S0 question this subfield exists to answer, and no paper has measured it.
