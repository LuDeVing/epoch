## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Founding numbers.** Bethell-Fox, Lohman and Snow (1984) effect sizes and the switch point. Missing: an open copy. Unblock: library access or a request to the author. Does not block any test above.
- **Accuracy by strategy in humans.** Jarosz et al. (2019) did not give it in what we read. Unblock: full text or the data. It would set the prior for U5.
- **Human baseline for our items.** We have no human sample on our bench items. Unblock: a small paid or volunteer pilot; the NeuroCognition pilot (N=9) shows how weak that is.
- **True strategy labels for agent traces.** No validated coding of build vs eliminate in LLM text. Unblock: hand-code 100 traces with two coders and report agreement before trusting any metric (U4).
- **Mediation by working memory.** Three studies, three answers (K05). Not testable on an LLM; the analogue (context length) is a different construct.
- **Reasoning-model behaviour with options hidden.** Needs the open-weight reasoning models or an API that lets us stage the call; cost is in the extra calls.
- **Calibration source paper.** Mitchum and Kelley (2010) unread (U10).

## Order to run

1. **U3 (leak controls).** Cheapest, nearly certain to pay off, and it guards every other result from credit given to leaked gains.
2. **U1 (build-first vs options-in-view at equal tokens).** The central question for S3/S4; no study answers it for reasoning models, and a positive result changes the schema.
3. **U6 (decomposition and exact tool for arithmetic rules) with U4 trace logging riding on the same runs.** Strongest existing effect (27.1 to 73.6 for GPT-4), and the logging costs nothing extra while it builds the evidence for U2.
