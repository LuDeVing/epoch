## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Woodcock-Johnson Concept Formation and Analysis-Synthesis: no LLM result exists, and the test items are copyrighted. Unblock: build an analogue (instances and non-instances with feedback for rule discovery; a key-based sequential puzzle) from our own generators, and read the technical manuals.
- Original Carpenter, Just and Shell 1990 numbers (sample sizes, fixation statistics, model fit): paywalled. Unblock: a library copy or the DTIC file from another network.
- Vodegel Matzen error-type frequencies: not read. Unblock: library access.
- Memorisation of public Raven items by current models: needs a leak-free held-out item set that no model has seen. Unblock: generate fresh items with a private grammar and keep them unpublished.
- Visual matrices: our bench is text. Perception findings (near-chance multimodal results) cannot be tested until we add image items and a describe-first step.
- Human text-versus-image difficulty: the pilot was 9 people with a borrowed image figure. Unblock: a larger human sample, which we cannot collect.
- Run-to-run variance of format and reasoning effects on matrices: needs at least 5 seeds per model. Budget decision, not a knowledge gap.
- Whether the reasoning-model collapse under stress is due to prompt length or a limit in keeping several outcomes alive: the benchmark authors left it open. Unblock: our U5 arm with short prompts.
- Why auxiliary rule labels help on PGM and hurt on RAVEN: needs training runs, outside our no-training scope.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (predict before options, with options-only control).** Cheapest, highest expected gain, and it decides whether the S0 gate should hide options.
2. **U2 (code check of the stated rule on all rows and columns).** Strongest literature support (K10) and it maps onto G2 and S8 directly. One run confirms or kills it at equal tokens.
3. **U3 (code tool for arithmetic rules at ranges 10, 100, 1000).** Settles whether the arithmetic weakness is fixable by code, which changes how O3 and tool use are specified.
