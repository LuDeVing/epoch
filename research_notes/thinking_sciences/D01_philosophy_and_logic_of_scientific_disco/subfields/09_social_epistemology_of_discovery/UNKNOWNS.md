## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Alexander et al. (2015), Thompson (2014), Zollman (2007), Rosenstock et al. (2017): full texts were blocked. Unblock by fetching PDFs from author pages or a library proxy, then extract the exact parameters where less communication helps.
- Replications of Hofstra et al. and Yang et al. in non-US or non-medical data: need a literature search with working API access.
- Any test of Longino's four norms or of standpoint claims with humans: none exist in our sources, and our bench has no human arm.
- A Vendi threshold at which ensemble gain appears: needs a dataset of ensembles with measured diversity and gain; U6 builds this.
- Heterogeneity at equal tokens with several real models: needs access to two or more models of near-equal quality.
- Whether hidden-profile fixes transfer to one agent gathering scattered evidence: needs an item type with distributed evidence.

## Order to run
1. D01.09-U1 (method-diverse branches vs same-config samples): cheapest, one model, and its answer decides whether most other items matter.
2. D01.09-U5 (forced counter-reason vs free self-critique): one extra step, clear metric (flips), and the only Longino-style rule that applies to one agent.
3. D01.09-U3 (distinct-answer branch selection): reuses the U1 branches, no new setup, and has a concrete 0.79 to 0.91 prior to check at equal tokens.
