## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A direct LLM study that labels agent traces progressive or degenerating in Lakatos's sense. None was found in 2 searches (search tools rate-limited). A fuller 2025-2026 literature search would unblock this; or our own labelling in U1 and U3 produces the first data.
- Meehl (1990) primary text on when to shield a core. The primary site returned HTTP 403. Obtain the paper by another route; extract any numbers; until then K23 stays single-source.
- Hidden-state probes and thought-switch penalties (Wang, Zhang d15) need model internals or decoding control. Unblock by access to an open model with logits and hidden states.
- Rival-programme falsification (Lakatos's rule needs a rival with excess content). Running two independent attempt lines is cheap to build but its benefit over plain sampling has no LLM evidence; G4 and G9 argue against. Needs a task where the two lines differ in kind, not only in seed.
- Open-ended tasks with no verifier (research ideas, essays). There is no external check, so excess-content tests cannot run. Unblock with a held-out human or rubric panel.
- Reliable numbers for DeepSeek-R1 and V3 in the Zhang "Dark Side" tables (rows unreadable in text extraction). Unblock by re-reading the PDF tables.
- Whether the "thinking models are not affected by their own errors" claim (d11) is real. Based on summary text only; read the paper body.
- CRITIC round-by-round numbers (figures not read).

## Order to run

3. D01.03-U1 (verified held-out prediction tag): the only operational Lakatos test, with the highest expected gain; run it right after U2 fixes the baseline cap. Run U3 post hoc on the same logs for free.
