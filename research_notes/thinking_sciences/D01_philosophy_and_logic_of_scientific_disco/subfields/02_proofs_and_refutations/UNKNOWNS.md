## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Lean or formal verifier items.** The restart-beats-repair result comes from Lean with a clean diagnostic. We have no formal prover on the bench. Unblock: a small formal set (Lean or a model finder) or accept the code-with-unit-tests proxy and say so.
- **Informal proof repair with a real hidden lemma.** We lack a corpus of informal proofs with known hidden assumptions. Unblock: build one by dropping lemmas from verified proofs and rewriting the proof as numbered steps.
- **Pease thresholds and the 2017 dialogue game.** The barring minimum, interestingness thresholds and the argumentation-semantics results cannot be tested without the original program and the body of the 2017 paper (blocked). Unblock: read the paper through a different route; do not rebuild HRL.
- **Theorem Modifier with an LLM proposing the restriction.** Needs a prover and a model finder (Otter and MACE or successors). Unblock: a small algebra set with Vampire or Z3-based checks.
- **Lakatos-labelled LLM loop with a matched control.** No published design exists to copy. Unblock: U1 is that design.
- **Judging "interesting" repairs.** Pease's outcome was circular. We have no external measure of whether a restricted claim is worth keeping beyond coverage and correctness. Unblock: a held-out usefulness measure (share of future items the claim helps solve).
- **Natural, non-planted errors for the external-role effect.** The effect was shown on planted errors (30 tasks per cell). Unblock: use our own logged first-pass failures as the draft pool.
- **Whether 2026 preprints replicate.** All are single-group. Unblock: rerun the cheap ones on our items (U3, U11) before trusting sizes.

## Order to run

1. **D01.02-U5 and U6 together (false-refutation rate and code-run counterexample search):** cheapest, uses public item sets, and tells us whether the refute operator can be trusted at all before we build repair on it.
2. **D01.02-U3 (repair-then-restart against pure restart and long repair):** highest expected gain; it decides how many S5 rounds we allow and whether the whole typed-repair idea can win at equal tokens.
3. **D01.02-U1 with U2 (typed repair on seeded false claims, with and without numbered steps):** the main Lakatos question; run only after U3 fixes the budget schedule, so the typed arm competes against the strongest untyped control.

U4 (silent drift) is cheap and should run alongside the first pass, since it only needs logs from the other tests.
