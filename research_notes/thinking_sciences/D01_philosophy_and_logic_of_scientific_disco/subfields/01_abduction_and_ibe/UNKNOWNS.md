## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Outside replication of the human results.** Lombrozo 2007 and Douven and Schupbach 2015 have no direct replication or failed replication in our files. Unblock: a literature search of citing papers (forward citations) and a check for preregistered replications. Affects U8 only.
- **Real-world abduction.** Diagnosis and debugging evidence is one paper (Graph of States) and synthetic sets. Unblock: a bench with real fault-finding items that have a known cause and a runnable check.
- **Modern reasoning models on the Wang and Qiu loops.** The loops were measured on GPT-4. Unblock: rerun a small ARC and ACRE subset on the model we deploy, as plain vs loop, before trusting the sizes.
- **Whether the true cause is in the pool for open tasks with no runnable check.** Without a check, S4 and the gate in U1 and U3 cannot run. Unblock: a proxy check (consistency with all observations) plus a small hand-labelled set.
- **How an interactive "creative leap" could be coded.** Peirce and Hanson give no mechanism. Not testable; only sampling plus testing is available.
- **Real distractor-only data for the bad lot.** Our U3 pools are built by deleting the true rule. A natural source of pools with no true answer is missing. Unblock: collect failed runs from the bench where no candidate was right and label them.
- **Cross-subfield links, not covered here.** Lemma discovery and conjecturing, Proofs and refutations (conjecture refinement), Conflict-driven clause learning (no-goods), Analogical access, Bandits for operator choice. These share the S6 operator and S7 selection questions. Unblock: read their UNKNOWNS.md files and merge duplicate tests.
- **Extended-thinking baseline.** SCHEMA section 7 lists "does one extended-thinking attempt already beat this graph" as the first equal-token test. Several tests above use it as an arm; the answer to that test decides whether any loop here is worth its setup cost (G7).

## Order to run

1. **U12 (S0 routing) with U13 logging.** Cheapest, uses items we already have, and tells us which classes should use the hypothesis loop at all. Every other test only makes sense on classes where the loop helps.
2. **U1 (held-out gate versus fit gate), then U6 (selector comparison) on the same pools.** The largest known loss is the right answer in the pool but not picked; one pool run gives both answers and the U2 virtue test almost for free.
3. **U3 (bad-lot code gate).** Highest-value unknown: models never say "none fits", the philosophy says it is the core flaw of IBE, and no benchmark exists. A pass here adds a code-checked good-enough gate to S7/S8.
