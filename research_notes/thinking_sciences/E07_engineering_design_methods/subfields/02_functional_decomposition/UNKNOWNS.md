## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Kurfman et al. 2001 and 2003: participant counts and agreement percentages. Needs full text from a library. Without them we cannot weigh the only founding test of the method.
- Atilola, Tomko and Linsey 2016 journal paper: only the thesis chapter was read, and its pairwise statistics look inconsistent. Needs the paper.
- Human replication with enforced diagram quality (Booth null: poor use or weak method). Needs human participants and experts, which our bench does not have.
- Whether human fixation findings apply to an LLM that sees its own decomposition. Covered in part by U6, but the human mechanism (commitment, effort) cannot be tested.
- Expert Stone-and-Wood function structures for 10 or more products. Not available; must be built by hand before U8 can run.
- Unread leads: the 2024 Function-Behavior-Structure prompting paper, the MAST per-mode percentages, the small-model Dual-CoT figure (24% vs 78%, second-hand).
- Replication of Atom of Thoughts on reasoning models. Needs the authors' code or a clean reimplementation with matched cost.
- A reward-free stop rule for decomposition. ADaPT's self-judgement overstated WebShop success by more than 30 points; no alternative is known.
- Whether coarse versus fine grain of sub-problems matters (vocabulary level dispute, K24). Needs a grain parameter we can vary and an outcome we can score.

## Order to run
1. **E07.02-U1.** It decides whether O3 should exist as a default step for a strong model; every other decision is conditional on it, and the cheapest set (100 code-checked items) already exists on the bench.
2. **E07.02-U3.** It turns the S0 gate from a guess into a measured threshold, and it also shows whether the 2-step loss appears on current models. Reuses the U1 item set, bucketed by step count.
3. **E07.02-U2.** It tests the one design choice we already built in (failure-triggered O3 at S6) against the retry baseline that the original papers did not match, so it can confirm or remove existing schema content.
