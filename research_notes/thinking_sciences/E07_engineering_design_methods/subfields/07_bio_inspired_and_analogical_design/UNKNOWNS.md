## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **2025 to 2026 models on AnaloBench T2 and StoryAnalogy.** The known results are GPT-4 era (d04). We need to run them ourselves on the models we use; the benchmark data must be downloaded first.
- **Numbers from Fu 2013, Linsey 2012 and Christensen and Schunn 2007.** Full texts were not obtainable (d16, a22, a23). Unblock by library access or author copies. Until then no sweet-spot distance can be cited.
- **Blind expert feasibility rate of LLM bio-inspired concepts.** Only about 6 concepts and 9 raters exist (d13). We need expert raters; the bench has only LLM judges, and G8 says they are weak for novelty.
- **Reranker token cost at scale.** The Cross-Pollination paper does not report tokens (d08). We must measure it in our own runs (U2).
- **Reasoning models on self-recall prompts.** Gwak 2026 covers chat models without CoT (d07). Needs reasoning-model runs.
- **Biology-specific retrieval.** No bank of biological mechanisms with verified function is on our bench. Building one needs AskNature-type data (hand-curated, licence unknown).
- **SAPPhIRE and structured causal models for retrieval.** Chakrabarti's original papers were not opened; Majumder 2024 (c11) uses an LLM judge on three artificial systems. Unblock by reading the originals and running skeleton vs SAPPhIRE on one bank.
- **Human baselines on our items.** We have no human reference for our own analogue bank, so we cannot say how close an agent is to human-level retrieval.

## Order to run

1. **U2 (two-stage retrieval on our bank).** Cheapest, highest prior (three independent sources), and it feeds U3, U4 and U8 with a working bank.
2. **U1 (explicit analogy vs best-of-N).** The key equal-token question that no paper has answered; decides whether O7 is worth any tokens in open mode.
3. **U4 with U7 (source ledger and feasibility critic).** Both address documented failures (source homogeneity, feasibility loss) and reuse the U1 items, so the marginal cost is small.
