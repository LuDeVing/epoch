## Order to run
1. **A18.06-U2** (reminder vs padded CoT on state-to-action). Cheapest, largest measured effect so far (+30 points), and tests an established gap on a current model.
2. **A18.06-U3** (original versus perturbed items). Sets the size of the problem for our model and the threshold for the check we are already adding.
3. **A18.06-U1** (ledger vs SimToM filter). The biggest open gap in the literature, and a code-based method fits our guardrail G5.
