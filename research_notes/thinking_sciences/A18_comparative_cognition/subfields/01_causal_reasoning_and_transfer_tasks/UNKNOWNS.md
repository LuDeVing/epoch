## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Outside replication of the Taylor 2009 series.** Needs the Taylor, Roberts, Hunt, Gray follow-up text, Seed 2006 and Tebbich 2007 in full, and Neilands 2016. Unblock by fetching full papers; arXiv, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar APIs returned HTTP 429 during the search.
- **Animal-AI trap-tube per-task numbers.** Mecattaf gives per-level proportions only. Unblock by running the Animal-AI Testbed ourselves (needs the Unity environment, a finer action set and a newer model), or by writing the authors for per-task data.
- **Child baseline: 52 vs 59.** Voudouris 2022 analyses 52; Mecattaf states 59. Unblock by reading both papers' participant sections against the shared data.
- **Aesop's fable numbers from Ghirlanda and Lind 2017.** Only the abstract was read. Unblock by opening the paper and its Figshare data.
- **CLadder with 2025-2026 models and equal-token baselines.** None found. Unblock by running the benchmark ourselves (U6).
- **Reasoning-model blicket AND values.** Samiei 2026 per-model values sit in garbled tables and figures. Unblock by opening the PDF figures or the data release.
- **Contamination.** We cannot tell whether a model has read trap-tube, Aesop and blicket descriptions. Unblock by using invented entities and post-cutoff items (Kiciman's pair-set approach).
- **Whether a causal reason from an LLM is evidence.** G10 says reasoning text is not evidence; U8 is the only route to test it, and it needs a code-checkable feature list per item.
- **Equal-token self-consistency baseline in the cited papers.** None of them include one; only our own runs can supply it.

## Order to run

1. **U1 + U2 (decoy and naive control, text trap-tube).** One item family serves both; they check whether our own G3 checks are valid, which affects every later result.
2. **U4 (simple-format re-run).** Cheap, uses failures we already have, and tells us whether a failure is interface or knowledge.
3. **U5 (hypothesis sampling on AND rules).** Strongest published effect for a scaffold element, but untested at equal tokens on current reasoning models; a pass or fail changes S4 directly.
