## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Reasoning-model results on the ToM and permuted-alphabet sets (2025-2026).** No published run. Unblock: run the public sets on our current model (U12).
- **A false-accept curve from outside.** None exists for LLMs. Unblock: our own tasks (U1) with known answers so false accepts are countable.
- **Razeghi-style frequency effect on frontier models.** Needs pretraining term counts we do not have. Unblock: a proxy such as web-search counts for number strings, or open models with published corpora.
- **Whether the feedback-loop account or insight explains tool use in birds.** No independent lab has settled it. Needed only for understanding, not for the agent. Unblock: nothing we can do.
- **Heyes's reply to the inanimate control (TiCS note, d02).** Not read. Unblock: open the TiCS exchange to see whether any design resolved it.
- **Whether CoT unfaithfulness holds for long hidden reasoning in the models we use.** Our access to hidden reasoning may be limited. Unblock: use the output-only swapped-question test (U8).
- **Human baselines on our items.** Animal and LLM studies compare to humans (letter strings 0.753, ConceptARC 0.95). We have none. Unblock: a small human sample on a subset.
- **Contamination of our own benchmark sets.** GSM1k-style fresh matched sets exist for math only. Unblock: write 30 fresh matched items per class (U13 and the distinguishability audit).

## Order to run

1. **U2 (ARC control comparison).** Cheapest to run on data we already have, and it decides which G3 check the S4/S8 states use by default. Our own data already showed two ARC programs that fit every example and were wrong.
2. **U1 (variant count).** Reuses the same items and gives the number of variants per class; without it the ladder has no size.
3. **U3 (counterfactual gap under scaffolds).** The only test of the central claim that a scaffold does more than recall; it also tells us whether name and number swaps (no longer informative on current models) can be dropped.
