## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Chappell and Kacelnik 2002 tool-selection numbers and Betty's exact trial counts are unverified. Unblock: open the primary papers; no schema effect expected.
- Whether the Lomekwi recognition inversion holds for frontier reasoning models with extended thinking. Needs the Lomekwi environment or an equivalent build-a-tool game; unblock by building or requesting it.
- Whether library gains hold on very large libraries (above 525 tools) on a modern model. Needs a task pool big enough to fill a library; unblock by generating tasks.
- Tool making across users (shared libraries, culture). Needs the multiplayer layer; see the multiplayer note in memory.
- Whether reflection helps current reasoning models on MacGyver. The public benchmark exists, but human scoring of feasibility is costly; unblock with an automatic feasibility judge validated on a human subset.
- Whether Betty's wire bending was invention. No agent consequence, so not worth testing.

## Order to run
1. A18.04-U1: the maker/user split at equal tokens with making cost charged. It decides whether the whole library idea pays for itself (G7), and uses only a procedural item pool we already have.
2. A18.04-U2: the recurrence gate. It is nearly free (one counter in code) and AllocBench says models over-build without it.
3. A18.04-U3: the forced "tool exists or buildable?" check. One extra prompt line at S0, a large effect in the one study, and it shows whether strong models skip tools.
