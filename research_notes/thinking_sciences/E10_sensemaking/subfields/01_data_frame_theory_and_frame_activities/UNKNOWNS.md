## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Klein 2007 exact rules** (frame limit, anchor count, activity definitions). Blocked by the unread chapter and FOCUS report (403). Unblock: manual download of the chapter; until then treat as design hints.
- **Plausibility model predictions** (abrupt closure, strain before reframing). Need a human or LLM process trace with a labelled strain signal. Unblock: instrument the agent log and see whether strain rises before reframes (U3).
- **Chinn and Brewer original.** Needs the 1993 paper to confirm the seven classes and the credibility/ambiguity factors.
- **Le et al. 2024a vs Kornowicz.** Rival evaluative-AI results. Human-interface question; unblocks only if multiplayer work needs it.
- **Independent fixation benchmark.** Only mARC exists (same group). Unblock: build our own trap set where the familiar pattern gives the wrong answer.
- **Cost of the disconfirming step alone.** SIA bundles many parts and reports no cost. Unblock: U4 with token-matched filler arm.
- **Defining an anomaly for an LLM** (failed check, violated prediction, unexpected tool output) and separating minor from fatal. Unblock: label our logs first.
- **Pontis and Blandford expert years (>5 vs >10).** Minor; needs the PDF re-open.

## Order to run
1. **E10.01-U6 (check with outside run vs text re-check, by checkability).** Cheapest, uses existing items, and sets the base for every other revise or reframe test.
2. **E10.01-U1 (trigger-gated vs always-reframe vs plain).** The biggest decision-relevant gap (gap 1); no one has measured it, and it decides whether O8 and the strain machinery stay.
3. **E10.01-U2 (frame object held by code vs in context vs free text).** Largest expected effect in the literature (d11) and tests the main structural idea of the subfield; U3 and U5 can reuse its harness.
