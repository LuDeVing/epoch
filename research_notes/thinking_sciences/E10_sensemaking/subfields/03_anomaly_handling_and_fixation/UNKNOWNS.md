## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Chinn and Brewer 1998 response frequencies and which predict theory change.** The JRST paper and the 1993 review were blocked (HTTP 403). Unblock: the user supplies the PDFs, or an interlibrary copy. Until then the seven-way log code is a coding scheme, not a model with known base rates.
- **Human thresholds for reframing.** No source gives a count of anomalies tolerated before a frame is questioned. Dunbar 1993 and Trickett 2009 were the likely sources. Unblock: open those PDFs; otherwise tune on our own logs (mark [O]).
- **Rudolph's simulator figures.** About 23 percent correct diagnosis, 28 percent failing to abandon an early diagnosis, 44 percent hopping untested come from a conference snippet. Unblock: find the paper. Do not use now.
- **Aviation fixation rates.** Not searched with a usable source.
- **Human controls on our items.** Several expected effects (noticing high, patching high) come from tiny human samples (3, 6, 11 people). A human pilot on our bench items would show whether the LLM pattern differs. Unblock: a small human panel.
- **Frontier proprietary models in agent loops.** Cheng's drag is large in open-weight reasoning models but small on proprietary models in competition math. Several 2026 papers name models we cannot cross-check. Unblock: rerun the key arms on the models we actually deploy.
- **Token cost of the draft-filtering and re-derive arms.** Cheng reports no token counts. Unblock: measure on our bench (part of U1 and U7).
- **Cost of abstain operators on answerable items with agent tools.** No paper measured it for an operator. Unblock: paired twins (U12).
- **Dunbar-style goal effect for LLMs.** Needs the goal framing test (U16) and the original human result.
- **Knowledge shields in LLM text.** No phrase list from Feltovich was opened. Unblock: the paper, then pattern-matching on our traces; G10 limits how far text can be trusted.
- **Braitsch numbers.** 65.6 percent persistence and 50.8 percent worsening not re-checked (PDF unreadable). Unblock: read the full paper.
- **Equal-token comparison of the full schema against one extended-thinking attempt** (SCHEMA section 7). Applies to every row above.

## Order to run

1. **U7 with U8 (clean restart and withheld commit on staged evidence).** Most solid harm in the subfield (39 percent drop, 24.2 percent MINT loss) with an untested fix and a cheap, code-only arm.
2. **U1 with U2 (delete-and-re-derive and required replacement on a dropped hypothesis).** The only direct measure of whether the schema's drop step must remove text; shares items with U3 and U19, so extra arms are cheap.
3. **U4 (trigger comparison) run together with U10 (authority frame with valid-update cost).** U4 sets when O8 may fire, which G1 requires; U10 measures the trade-off that Shapira says every stand-firm rule pays, using the same planted-anomaly harness.
