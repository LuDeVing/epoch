## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Primary text of Weick 1993 and 1995, Maitlis 2005, Maitlis and Christianson 2014.** Not opened. Needs institutional access or an open PDF. Until then, no claim about case counts, the four sensemaking forms or boundary conditions.
- **Replication of Weick-derived findings.** No failed or successful replication read. Unblock by reading Vogus and Sutcliffe 2007 and the 2012 reconciliation paper.
- **Strain on a trace (Klein 2023).** No operational definition tested, even on people. Needs a coded set of reasoning traces with human strain labels.
- **Generative AI and organisational sensemaking in measured studies.** Only one weak 2026 abstract (41% lower narrative alignment above 60% AI content). Not a bench question.
- **Cross-case cue-ignoring metric** (Mann Gulch, Columbia, Challenger, El Faro). Sources are Wikipedia; unblock by reading CAIB and NTSB reports and coding cue events.
- **Audience effect with a second instance on plausibility drift.** Not tested for self-judgment; d06 covers debate only.
- **Human behaviour claims** (hindsight bias size, identity and cosmology episodes). Our bench cannot test people.
- **Weick's identity, social and ongoing properties** have no agent counterpart we can measure.
