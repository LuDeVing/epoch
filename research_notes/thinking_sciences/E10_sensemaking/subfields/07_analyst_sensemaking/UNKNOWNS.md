## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Analyst-style open-ended tasks with a human answer key.** Our bench items have checks. The Sign of the Crescent, VAST and terror-plot sets have keys but few cases. What would unblock it: build 20 to 30 such cases with keyed facts and hypotheses.
- **Expert-faster-than-novice claim (Figure 6).** No measured data exists. Needs human experiments we cannot run.
- **Long-context regime (64K to 1M tokens).** Our bench rarely reaches it. Unblock with a long-document set in the harness (SCOUT used LOOGLE-V2).
- **Independent replication of the falsification gain.** Same authors ran both tasks. Unblock by running our own rule-discovery item type, which U3 does.
- **Effect of early AI insight on user anchoring.** Position-paper claim only. Needs a human-in-the-loop study.
- **Whether tokens per solved episode stay equal under falsify prompts.** Source reports tokens per turn, not per episode. U3 measures it.
- **Role of model-stated uncertainty in a stop gate.** Conflicts with 4b (verbal confidence). Needs a calibration check on our models before testing any entropy-based stop.
- **Search coverage.** The gap search could not use Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex (rate limits). Papers may exist that we did not see. Re-run when limits clear.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (evidence file vs notes in context).** Biggest single-source gain (+7.7 at equal cost), cheap to build, and it decides whether the memory section needs a separate store.
2. **U3 (falsify step, thinking mode).** Largest claimed lift (0.413 to 0.613) and cheapest (one prompt line), but the token cost and non-thinking harm must be measured.
3. **U2 (forage/sense split).** The core claim of this subfield has no test at all; the answer sets whether the P&C structure belongs in S1-S3 or stays a design frame.
