## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Whether the deep-story effect is real in people at scale: no experiment and no independent replication. Unblock: read Johnson and Seifert, Green and Brock, Walter and Tukachinsky and design a small human or LLM-simulated study.
- Frontier-model numbers for Farm, ClashEval and PoisonedRAG. Unblock: run public benchmark items on our model and arms.
- Whether story form matters for LLMs (storytelling effect in Hackenburg 2025 not read). Unblock: read Fig 3A or run story-framed vs list-framed contexts (K12).
- Multi-turn real deployments where the frame arrives across many sessions. Our bench is mostly single item. Unblock: add a persistent-memory arm.
- Real, not fictional, deep stories on current events. Our items must be neutral or fictional. Unblock: a safe synthetic corpus of frame stories.
- Inter-coder agreement of Prochaska's coding (no kappa seen). Unblock: ask the group or recode a sample.
- Separating frame effects from sycophancy and recency. Unblock: add a control where the frame sits far back in context and is irrelevant to the item.

## Order to run
1. U1 (planted-frame priming): the core claim has no LLM data; a null result lets us drop the S1 frame note and the whole line.
2. U4 (prior-first reconcile): established mechanism, best cost per gain so far, easy to run on existing items.
3. U3 (provenance tags plus subset vote on injected coherent passages): cheap, and tests whether the injection defence carries over to framing; run U2 on the same items right after.
