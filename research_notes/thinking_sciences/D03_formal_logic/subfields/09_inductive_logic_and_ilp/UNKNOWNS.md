## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Muggleton's primary papers (1991, 1995 Progol, 2014/15 MIL) were not opened. Example-efficiency numbers are missing. Unblock: obtain the PDFs through a library route; then check whether "often one example" holds in the reported benchmarks.
- 2026 papers (ALBI, Popper-ly, ADVENT, AutoSpec, SLR) are single-lab and unreplicated. Unblock: wait for a second lab, or re-run one on our bench with a different model.
- No study reports equal-token results. Unblock: our bench harness must log tokens per arm, including feedback and code-run text.
- Do 2026 reasoning models behave like GPT-4 on MiniSCAN, ACRE and List Functions? Unblock: a rerun of those tasks with a current model; the datasets are public.
- LLM-versus-solver on deep predicate chains (CHAIN) with 2026 models. Unblock: build a small CHAIN-like generator and a Popper or Prolog baseline.
- Wrong-rule propagation in a rule store has no study. Unblock: planted-error experiment in U7.
- Open-domain predicate invention and its token cost. Unblock: a domain with executable checks beyond poker.
- Whether human cognitive-window findings (short rules help, complex rules hurt) apply to models reading rules. Unblock: ablate rule length in the store prompt (part of U7).
- Failure-driven constraints from the agent's own history (Popper style) have no direct LLM paper. Unblock: U2.

## Order to run

1. **D03.09-U1** (hypothesis, code, run, repair vs CoT and best-of-N at equal tokens): it decides whether the whole S2/S3 rule recipe pays, and it is the one question every paper here leaves open.
2. **D03.09-U3** (refine, revert if held-out fit drops): cheap, one code check, and it guards against the MiniARC and SIRBench losses.
3. **D03.09-U2** (located directional failure message and forbidden shape): the ILP-derived loop is established for ILP, single-source for LLMs; the test reuses the U1 items and costs little extra.
