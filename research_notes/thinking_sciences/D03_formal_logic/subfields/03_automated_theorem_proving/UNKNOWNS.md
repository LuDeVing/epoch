## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Real Lean or Isabelle kernel.** We have no formal-proof checker in the bench. Unblock: add a Lean REPL on a small set (miniF2F-v2 corrected statements, 30 items) or use code-with-tests as the proxy.
- **Token-matched numbers from the literature.** Nearly all papers report samples (pass@k), not tokens. Unblock: re-run a small prover with token logging, or use our own bench only.
- **Classic ATP counts.** CASC solve counts and LLM-guided premise selection for saturation provers were not found. Unblock: read a CASC summary, or run E/Vampire on a small TPTP slice ourselves.
- **Contamination.** miniF2F and PutnamBench are public, and none of the cited papers rules out overlap. Unblock: build fresh items.
- **Formalisation fidelity at scale.** Human judging of Lean statements needs experts. Unblock: use planted-error sets with known truth (U5).
- **Cost accounting.** Leaderboard costs differ by up to 6x with subscription vs API pricing. Unblock: log our own tokens and dollars per accepted answer.
- **Lean version non-replication.** None found; not searched systematically.
- **Frontier agents on proof tasks (Aleph, Theo, Seed 1.5).** Numbers are from one leaderboard table and not independently verified.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (with U2 on the same items).** Largest expected gain and a direct answer to whether the whole scaffold pays for itself at equal tokens (G7); U2 adds false-accept counts at nearly no extra cost.
2. **U4.** Decides whether decomposition (O3) stays the first operator at an impasse; reuses the U1 items.
3. **U5.** Cheap to build (planted errors), and it protects every later test that uses a self-made formal form.
