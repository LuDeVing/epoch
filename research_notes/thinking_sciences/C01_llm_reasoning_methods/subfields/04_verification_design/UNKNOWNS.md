## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Frontier reasoning models on intrinsic self-correction.** All tests are on GPT-3.5/4, Qwen2.5-Math-7B and 1.5B-8B RL models. Unblock: run T-D (critique vs extra sample) on the strongest model we can call, with right-to-wrong logging.
- **Error correlation between solver and verifier on hard items.** No source measures it where the generator fails. Unblock: a labelled hard-item set with both solver and checker verdicts logged per sample.
- **Verifier gaming at inference time (selection only, no RL).** The hacking evidence is RL-based. Unblock: sweep N for a model-judge selector and plot gold accuracy against N (peak test).
- **Recognition versus familiarity as the cause of self-preference by intervention.** Only observational binning exists. Unblock: swap authorship labels and paraphrase with a third model on matched pairs.
- **Open-ended or non-numeric tasks.** Nearly all numbers are math or code. Unblock: build checkers for our own non-numeric item types and repeat U4.
- **Hidden-state probes.** Signal exists (AUC above 0.9 at 32B) but needs weights. Unblock: only if we get an open-weight run.
- **Zhao's gain split.** How much is tie-breaking on tasks other than MATH. Unblock: U2 on our own tasks.
- **Equal-token crossover curve for verify-then-vote.** No paper has one. U1 produces it.

## Order to run

1. **C01.04-U4 (verifier error rates per task class).** Cheap, only labelled sets. Every other test depends on knowing whether our checker rejects right answers or accepts wrong ones.
2. **C01.04-U2 (pairwise tie-break of the top two voted answers).** Cheapest verifier use; the evidence suggests tie-breaking is where most of the gain sits; it can be adopted at almost no token cost.
3. **C01.04-U1 (k check chains vs vote at equal tokens, by difficulty).** The largest open question and the largest possible gain; run after U4 so the check is trusted, and with U3 and U5 sharing the same item pool.
