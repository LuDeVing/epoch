## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Outside replication of Zhou's flip ratio and the 67.5% abandonment audit. Nothing found in 21 citing papers. Unblocks if another group publishes, or if we log flips on our own runs (U1).
- Matched-difficulty natural experiment that separates length as cause from length as symptom. Needs forced-extension and natural-length arms on the same items, with item difficulty fixed from a prior run.
- Logprob or hidden-state access for DEER-style confidence and probes. Our text-only scaffold lacks it. Unblocks if the model API gives token probabilities.
- Non-math domains with matched controls (code repair, planning, open judgment). Needs bench items with checks in those domains.
- Outside replication of Gema's behaviour shifts across budgets and models. Not a scaffold concern now.
- Deterministic-state-tracking claim (Guo et al. 2026) was read from the abstract page only. Unblocks when the full text is opened.
- Effect of trained stop heads on our agent. Out of scope while we do not train.

## Order to run
1. **C01.10-U1 (budget sweep).** Cheap, needed by everything else; sets the cap and tells us whether our model shows any decline at all.
2. **C01.10-U5 (equal-token: long chain vs k short vs short-m@k vs NoThinking).** Closes the biggest gap in the evidence and decides the default sampling policy.
3. **C01.10-U3 (verify-before-revise gate with harmed/rescued counts).** Highest expected gain from flip evidence, and no one has tested it.
