## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Whether unfaithful chains reduce accuracy or only mislead readers. Needs a way to label which chains are unfaithful on our items; the hint-injection probe gives labels only for planted cues.
- Whether Shojaee's high-complexity collapse is real once output caps are raised. Needs a backbone with a very large output budget and solvable-only instances (no larger-budget rerun found).
- Whether CoT gains hold on a 2026 frontier model across many task types. Needs access to the same backbone with and without internal reasoning, which an API may not offer.
- Prompted invalid-exemplar results on current models. The only recent evidence is fine-tuning (d12). Needs the B9 test run on our backbone.
- Faithfulness over time and with RL (28% and 20% plateau). Needs repeated probing of each new backbone version; we cannot control training.
- CoT-decoding (latent chains). Needs token-level decoding access, which an API backbone lacks.
- Whether size of the original Wei symbolic gains (49.3 to 98.6 and similar) is right. The appendix columns were garbled in extraction; needs a clean read of the PDF table.

## Order to run

1. **C01.01-U4** (trigger on a reasoning backbone). Cheapest, and it decides whether our default prompt carries dead weight.
2. **C01.01-U1** (task-type gate). Biggest expected token saving, and it feeds S0 directly.
3. **C01.01-U2** (short-m@k vs majority vs long chain). Directly tests G11's "several short attempts" at equal tokens, the largest gap in the literature.
