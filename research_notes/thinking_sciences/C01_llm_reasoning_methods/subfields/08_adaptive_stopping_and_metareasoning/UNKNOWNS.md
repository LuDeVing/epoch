## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Free-text and open-mode stopping.** No answer to vote over. Missing: a task family with a cheap grader. Unblock: build a small set with a check that is not a model opinion (G8).
- **Independent replication of DeepConf, DSC, ReASC.** Only the authors ran them. Citation lookups were rate-limited. Unblock: re-run each on our own non-maths items (U5, U13), and repeat the forward-citation search.
- **Total-token cost of stop rules on reasoning models against a fixed small baseline.** Not in the literature. Unblock: log input and output tokens per item in every test.
- **Which signal separates "model stopped too early" from "model already answered".** Needed to reconcile s1 with Zeng and Kang. Unblock: U4 with first-answer correctness labels.
- **Token logprobs.** DeepConf, P(True) and RaM-style scoring need them. Unblock: check whether our API exposes logprobs; otherwise use answer agreement only.
- **Wrong-consensus on overconfident non-maths items.** No data at all. Unblock: U1 on ARC and BRAINTEASER.
- **Whether the stop threshold should depend on model strength for vote rules.** Agreement rules adapt, confidence gates do not. Unblock: U8 on two model sizes.
- **Theory to practice.** No LLM test of a stop rule derived from rational metareasoning against a heuristic. Unblock: U2.

## Order to run

1. **C01.08-U1 (wrong consensus).** It tests the main failure of the best-supported stop rule on our own labelled items, and it decides whether the "add now" vote stop is safe.
2. **C01.08-U3 (difficulty class from 3 to 4 samples).** It decides whether S0 can set budgets cheaply; the potential gain is largest (claimed up to 4x) and the pilot samples are reused as the first attempts.
3. **C01.08-U11 (length cap with restart) together with U9 (early exit at a transition).** Both use traces we already have, cost little, and test the two strongest overthinking results on reasoning models.
