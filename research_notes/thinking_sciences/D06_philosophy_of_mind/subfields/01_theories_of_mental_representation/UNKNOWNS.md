## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Closed frontier models: no internal access, so Othello-style probe and intervention results (K05-K09, K28) cannot be replicated. Unblock: use only behavioural analogues (T7 edit tests).
- Philosophical grounding (selection history, Swampman, disjunction; K27): no operational test exists. Unblock: a fine-tune vs prompt contrast on robustness, which is training and outside the scaffold scope.
- Primary-source claims of Fodor, Dretske, Millikan and Block: only SEP summaries read. Unblock: open the original texts.
- Whether 2025-2026 RL-trained models self-correct better than the 2023 GPT-3.5/GPT-4 figures (K22). Unblock: rerun the Huang and Tyen protocols on current models; cheap but needs a model list.
- Truth-direction contest (K09, K11): Levinstein's full rebuttal not read; only matters if we build probe-based verifiers, which we do not.
- Representational convergence (K15): irrelevant to the scaffold until a use case appears.
- Per-model numbers in figures not extracted (Prakash IIA, Zhu table, Qiu per-model, Slocum percentages). Unblock: read the figures or contact authors' code.

## Order to run
1. **D06.01-U3 (typed slots vs free restatement)**: it decides whether S2 needs structure at all and it is the central unanswered gap; cheap to run on existing items.
2. **D06.01-U4 (slot-to-source check)**: highest chance of a real gain, since models find errors poorly but fix them well once shown; it also feeds G2.
3. **D06.01-U2 (re-derive vs copy-forward)**: long-run error carry-over is large in older studies and the fix costs few tokens.
