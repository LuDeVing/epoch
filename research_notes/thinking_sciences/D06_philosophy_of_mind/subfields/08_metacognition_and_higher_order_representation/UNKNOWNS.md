## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Token-level probabilities (for DeepConf, self-certainty, semantic entropy over tokens): we need an API or open model that returns logprobs. Until then only agreement, length and retry signals can be tested (U4, U5).
- Introspective accuracy tied to task outcome (Lindsey, Binder, Naphade): needs model internals or finetuning access; no prompt-only test. Unblock: an open-weight run of concept injection, or a published result linking self-prediction to task gain.
- Whether reasoning models of 2025-2026 disclose more cues: needs a cue-design matching Chen or Chua and Evans; partly testable (U6) but one design only.
- Original human numbers (Nisbett and Wilson 1977, Koriat 1997, Johansson 2005, Hall 2010, Proust 2013, Rosenthal books, Maniscalco and Lau 2011 and 2012, Rounis 2010, Steyvers 2025): unopened. Unblock: fetch the full text (library access or open copies) and re-run the audit. A failed choice-blindness replication has not been located.
- Whether reflection-step agents such as Reflexion gain only from an external success signal: Reflexion was not opened; unblock by reading its tables and Huang et al. Table 2.
- Self-preference on agent traces rather than dialogue or summaries: needs a human or running-based truth set for traces.
- Whether a trace critic keeps its value when the generator can see it or learn from memory that it exists: needs a multi-session setup.
- Whether introspection or self-prediction improves with scale: needs several model sizes of the same family on one probe set.

## Order to run

1. **U10 (re-score existing logs by difficulty bin and within question).** Costs no new model calls and fixes how every later monitor test is judged.
2. **U2 (one check call vs the same tokens as extra samples) together with U1 (P(True)-weighted vote).** The two cheapest equal-token tests of the main open question (critique gap 1) and they share the same sample pool.
3. **U3 (trace critic on fit-but-wrong answers) and U7 (check-reliability break-even), run together on ARC.** They answer the biggest scaffold risk in our own data: answers that fit all examples and are still wrong, and whether revising on an imperfect check helps.
