## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Frontier-model CFQ, COGS and SCAN-length numbers. Missing: a harness for these public benchmarks. A small wrapper that scores model output on them would unblock U1, U6 and Gap 1.
- Training-based ideas (MLC, Coconut, JEPA, grokking, Csordas setup fixes). Missing: fine-tuning access to a model we control.
- Mechanistic binding results. Missing: open-weight models with activation access, and even then no scaffold lever.
- Human rule-learning numbers (Marcus 1999 sample sizes, animal replications). Missing: the source texts. Low value for the schema.
- Independent replications of least-to-most, self-ask, MLC and Coconut. Missing: published or our own re-runs. U1 and U2 are our own replications.
- Hidden-error rate of tool-using reasoning agents. Missing: step-annotated traces from such agents. U5 gives a small version.
- Two unopened leads: the nine-frontier-LLM two-hop study (arXiv 2411.16353, snippet only) and the founding papers (Fodor and Pylyshyn 1988, Smolensky, Quilty-Dunn). Opening them could add numbers but is unlikely to change the schema.

## Order to run
1. **U3 (code-as-thought vs CoT).** Cheapest, large reported effect, gives a direct routing rule for S0 if it holds.
2. **U8 (rare and irrelevant-clause twins).** Needs no new components and sets the baseline for every other test; shows how much of our bench score is pattern reuse.
3. **U1 (least-to-most vs CoT).** Tests the one operator-level claim with a huge reported effect (99.7 vs 16.2) that rests on a single retired model.
