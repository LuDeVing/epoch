## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Philosophical claim (parity, constitution).** No metric exists for "part of the thinker". Would need a formal criterion from Smart, Clowes and Clark follow-ups or Perrier 2025; critics (Adams and Aizawa, Rupert) were never opened.
- **Independent replication of MemGPT and Cognitive Workspace.** Needs the same tasks run by someone else; our bench lacks their long multi-session sets.
- **Multi-agent transactive memory (Wegner, Hutchins).** Our schema is single-agent. Only MATM (d16) found; test needs a multi-agent harness.
- **Bastani 2025 guardrail effect.** Human classroom study; we cannot run it. Needs the PNAS text for the standard errors.
- **Human offloading (Google effect, brain drain).** Cannot be tested on an agent, and the evidence is weak (pooled d=-0.02). Skip.
- **Equal-token comparisons from independent labs on 2025-2026 models.** Not published for notes plus search; our own T4 is the first source.
- **Token cost of a loop guard.** Not measured anywhere; our logs can supply it.

## Order to run

1. **U12 (scratch or tool for exact sub-steps).** Cheapest to run, with a likely win; confirms the one established finding on a modern model.
2. **U1 plus U3 together (re-verify at read, overwrite on update).** Same stream and injected stale notes; the largest safety gap and the largest agent-specific departure from Clark and Chalmers.
3. **U4 (notes plus search versus full context at equal tokens).** Decides the default memory mode and fills the biggest gap (GAP 1) in the literature.
