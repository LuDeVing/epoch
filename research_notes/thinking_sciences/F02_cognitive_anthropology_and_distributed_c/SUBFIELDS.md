# Cognitive anthropology and distributed cognition: subfields

This family studies thinking as it happens in real settings, across people, tools and time. Hutchins' study of Navy ship navigation, and of how a cockpit remembers its speeds, showed whole systems doing cognitive work no single person holds. Distributed cognition has a theory, a method (cognitive ethnography, often with video) and neighbours (extended cognition, activity theory, situated action). For one agent the group side is mostly irrelevant. The tool and representation side is relevant, because our agent already thinks with code, checks and stored state.

## Dig into next

- Which tool calls reveal information cheaply (run on a probe input, list differences) versus move toward the answer?
- The follow-up literature is mixed: one study found these actions decline with expertise. Does cost matter at our scale?

## Does not transfer

Crew coordination, culture, apprenticeship, communication and group memory are about people. Wikipedia flags some of its distributed-cognition claims as needing verification, especially the mother-fetus model. Team gains are not shown to arise when one model plays several roles, and SCHEMA G9 rejects debate. Extended mind is a philosophical thesis without a testable procedure.
