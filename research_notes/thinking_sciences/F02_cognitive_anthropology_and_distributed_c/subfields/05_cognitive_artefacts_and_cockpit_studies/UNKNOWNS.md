## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Hutchins' direct claim** (a chain of media beats one long context). Needs a long-horizon task suite with a fair no-media control and a way to separate medium from prompt. Unblock: a task set with early constants and late use (U2 is a start).
- **Why checklist effects shrink at scale in medicine.** No study separates real use from paper use. Not testable for agents; only the LLM analogue (U10) is.
- **Degani and Wiener 1993 and Norman 1991/1993 from full text.** Needs the Human Factors paper and the books. Unblock: obtain copies; check for skip rates and error counts.
- **Cognitive Workspace (58.6% reuse vs 0% RAG).** Self-reported preprint. Needs an independent replication before any use.
- **2026 preprints (b19-b22, c20, c22) not re-opened.** Unblock: a second reading pass before they are used for thresholds.
- **Prevalence of compliance without doing in our agent.** U10 measures it on existing logs.
- **Whether the gate effect size holds on real tasks.** Zhang 2026 is synthetic booking-style; tau2 and PM-Bench show it is task-dependent.

## Order to run
1. **U10 (audit executed vs claimed).** Costs almost nothing: reads existing logs. It tells us whether U1 is worth running and for which guardrails.
2. **U1 (gate vs advice).** Highest expected gain; decides whether guardrails need enforcement code.
3. **U5 (map and drop pure self-review checks).** Cheap, uses on/off runs we mostly already have, and Huang and CRITIC say some current checks may be costing accuracy.
