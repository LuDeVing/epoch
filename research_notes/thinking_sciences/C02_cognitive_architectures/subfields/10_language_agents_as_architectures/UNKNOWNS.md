## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- ACT-R activation equation, decay parameter and fit numbers: Anderson 2004 not read. Unblock: read the paper and the ACT-R tutorial equations, then write U6 arms to match them.
- Soar chunking speed-up data: Laird, Rosenbloom, Newell 1986 and Assanie 2022 not read. Unblock: obtain the PDFs; needed to set the expected effect in U4.
- Direct replication of Huang et al. on 2025-26 reasoning models: none found. Unblock: run U3 ourselves, or a fresh literature search once the search quota is back.
- Break-even check accuracy of 60-70%: from a 2023 simulation figure. Unblock: measure on our own checks in U2.
- Bounded content competition with broadcast and uncertainty-driven stopping: no agent measurements; a single-agent scaffold has no competing modules. Unblock: a multi-module design, outside current scope.
- Learned memory control (Memory-R1, AgeMem) and trained self-correction (SCoRe): need training; outside the no-training rule.
- Independent surveys on the same gaps: the 48-author survey (d20) was skimmed only. Unblock: read its memory and reasoning chapters.
- Total-token accounting for experience-gathering phases (ExpeL, Voyager, AWM): not reported. Unblock: our own accounting in U4.

## Order to run
1. C02.10-U2 (check false-positive gate): cheapest, needs only logs of checks we already run, and decides whether any reflection is allowed.
2. C02.10-U1 (typed impasse vs retry vs self-consistency): largest expected gain and the least modern precedent; it tests our S6 slot directly.
3. C02.10-U4 (cache of verified solutions at equal total tokens): turns the established "works where a check exists" result into a payback number for the subgoal library.
