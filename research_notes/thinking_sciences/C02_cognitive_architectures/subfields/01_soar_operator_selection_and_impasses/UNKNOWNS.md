## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Laird 2012 (book) and the 1987 founding paper.** Not read. Claims about the link between impasse type and search method, and the original decision-procedure rationale, rest on Laird 2022 and the Manual. Unblock: a person with library access, or a mirror that is reachable.
- **Replication of CogRec, NL2GenSym and STARS.** All single-lab; only NL2GenSym's citation list could be checked (empty). Unblock: re-run a small version with seeds; check Google Scholar when search quota returns.
- **Real tool-failure impasses.** PALADIN-style typed recovery needs real tool traces and fine-tuning. Our bench has few tool failures. Unblock: add a tool-using item set with injected failures, or use logs from agent runs.
- **Whether a classifier matches a typed taxonomy.** The only source is a search snippet (c26, 63.6% vs 43% on 16,163 real events). Unblock: open the paper.
- **Human impasse data.** Brown and VanLehn 1980 repair theory and Ohlsson-style impasse studies were not opened. Unblock: read the originals; the parallel representational-change subfield covers Ohlsson.
- **Constraint-failure exact wording.** The Manual detail was read but the Laird 2022 footnote conflicts with it. Unblock: check the Manual chapter 3 and the Soar mailing list for the intended claim.
- **Expensive-chunk effect for text libraries.** Only abstract-level evidence from Soar. Unblock: measure retrieval cost in U8 and compare to token savings.
- **Long-horizon agent runs.** THREAD, Context-Folding and AWM use multi-step environments (ALFWorld, WebArena) that our bench lacks. Unblock: a small multi-step environment for U2 and U9.
- **Equal-token comparison of tree search with impasse typing.** Needs both U1 and U7 results first.

## Order to run

1. **C02.01-U4 (base rates).** Log-only, no new arms, one pass over existing items. It tells us whether typing can matter at all and feeds U1, U3 and U6.
2. **C02.01-U3 (trigger false alarms).** Cheap hand-labelling on the same logs. A bad trigger poisons every later test, and no source has published precision or recall.
3. **C02.01-U2 (isolation).** The cleanest and cheapest ablation. Every nearby paper points the same way but none isolates the factor; the result also decides how U1 and U5 are built.

Then U1 (typed vs generic) and U5 (repair vs best-of-N) once the base rates show enough impasses to measure.
