## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Whether CLARION's human-data fits replicate and with what fit statistics. Needs independent groups' reports or raw data; search was rate-limited (d19).
- Whether "LLM = implicit level" is true. Needs a measured comparison of LLM errors to human implicit-learning signatures, beyond the one CoT-hurts paper (d01-d03).
- Sigma or Standard Model plus an LLM. No quantitative paper exists in the sources opened. Needs a public Sigma build with an LLM interface.
- Whether Schooler 1993 insight narration replicates in humans at larger N. Needs a registered replication.
- Whether reasoning models keep the CoT penalty on pattern tasks. Reasoning cannot be switched off in o1-style models, so a fair direct arm needs a non-reasoning sibling.
- Whether the dual-process split helps if the fast module is not fine-tuned. SwiftSage's fast module was trained on oracle trajectories; our setting forbids training.
- Whether an explicit impasse substate (Soar/Sigma style) beats a program-controlled retry. Needs an implementation.
- Newell and Shanks (2014) on awareness measures, and Reber/Shanks critiques, were not read. Reading them would clarify how much of the human implicit evidence is real.

## Order to run

1. C02.08-U1 (fast-default vs always-slow at equal tokens): the biggest potential saving, four papers point the same way but none controls tokens strictly.
2. C02.08-U3 (code veto vs LLM veto on reasoning models): cheap, and decides whether the strongest established guardrail still holds on current models.
3. C02.08-U2 (forced rule-finding vs direct on pattern items): cheap, model dependent, and decides whether S0 needs a direct-answer route.
