## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Non-grid tasks: no compression solver exists for text puzzles or maths. Unblock by building a text analogue (a rule-length score over candidate rules) and testing it through U1.
- ARC-AGI-2-style hard items: CompressARC scores about 4% (d05), so we cannot learn from it there. Unblock by adding some ARC-AGI-2 public items to the bench so we do not overfit to ARC-1.
- Log-probability access: closed-API models may not return token probabilities. Unblock by checking what our API returns, or by running a local open model for U9.
- Weight-updating arms (TTT, LoRA per task): not possible with our closed API. Unblock only by a local open model with fine-tuning; separate project.
- Seed variance of CompressARC: no published number (a07, d03). Needs U7.
- Where the 4% ARC-AGI-2 figure comes from: the prize page gives a summary only (c14, d05). Needs the source run.
- Prompt-level compression versus free-form at truly equal tokens in a coding-agent loop: Rodionov lacks a textual-plus-simplification arm (d06). Needs our T2 test.
- Whether bits per puzzle predict correctness across puzzles (c05 shows cases only). Needs per-puzzle KL logs from U7.

## Order to run
1. C11.09-U10 (cost log): free, applies to every later test, and is required by G7.
2. C11.09-U1 (shortest-rule tie-break vs vote): cheapest real test of the compression idea on our stack, and it uses outputs we already produce.
3. C11.09-U4 (view-vote selection with a closed-API model): the one established mechanism (view-based selection) that could work without weights, with the largest expected gain.
