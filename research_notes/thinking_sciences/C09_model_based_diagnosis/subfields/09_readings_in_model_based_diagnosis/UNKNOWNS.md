## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- The 1992 Readings chapters on probe selection, abstraction and fault models were not read. We need the book or chapter PDFs.
- No bench item type with a formal system description exists for text tasks. Needed: a small set of circuit and logic-network items with exact diagnoses (generated by code) plus a text-analogue set.
- Real incident data (ITBench or OpenRCA style) for the RCA failure-mode tests. Needed: access to those suites or an in-house incident set.
- Dependency graphs for EoG-style tests. Needed: items that come with a graph, or a way to build one from text.
- The Sztyber-Betley result on non-OpenAI frontier models. Needed: their circuits (not yet released to our knowledge) or our own regenerated set.
- Component failure priors for entropy probe choice. Needed: a defensible source of priors, or a uniform-prior variant to test first.
- Absence findings (no LLM-in-hitting-set paper, no GDE probe test, no fault-model-choice study) rest on rate-limited searches. Needed: a cleaner literature pass.
- Intrinsic self-review of 2025-26 reasoning models on diagnosis. Needed: a diagnosis task with a flip-rate log.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (conflict check plus solver vs free-form).** It is the cleanest, cheapest, and no published test exists; it decides whether the MBD core is worth importing.
2. **U3 (existence and double-fault check).** A pure code check, tiny token cost, and it targets the largest documented small-model failure.
3. **U2 and U4 together (re-derive, then entropy-ranked probe).** One circuit set with probe sequences tests both; they cover the nonmonotone-candidate and next-test questions that the schema's S4 to S7 depend on.
