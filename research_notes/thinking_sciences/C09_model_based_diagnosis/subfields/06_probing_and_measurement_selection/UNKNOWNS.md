## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Real noisy, open-ended probing.** All LLM results use a simulated answerer and closed lists. Need a bench with real software or hardware faults where the answer is an actual run, not an LLM. Unblock: build 30 to 50 debugging items with executable probes and a known fault.
- **Probing inside an agent that also repairs.** No study separates probing from the repair. Unblock: log probes separately in the harness, then compare runs with and without the S7 step.
- **Original GDE lookahead numbers (de Kleer 1987, 1992, 2006).** Paywalled, scholarly APIs were rate-limited. Unblock: get the PDFs by hand and extract one-step vs deeper measurement counts.
- **Independent replication of Uncertainty of Thoughts.** None found. Unblock: rerun UoT on a small subset of our bench and compare with the reported +10 points.
- **Why UoT open-set gains hold while Number Game open-set stalls.** Likely the proposer. Unblock: run the same tasks with both proposer types.
- **Reasoning models with BED-style scoring.** Only Kriz tests it, with their own reimplementation. Unblock: a clean run on an o-series or thinking Claude model with token counts (U1).
- **Exact reading of UoT appendix lookahead curves and QuestBench per-model cells.** Extraction interleaved the text. Unblock: read the original figure images.
- **Which part of UoT drives the gain: reward or tree lookahead.** Unblock: UoT with simple split reward and no tree vs full tree at equal tokens (partly U1, U11).
- **BED-LLM 37.4 vs recount 40.4.** Unblock: ask the authors or recompute from released data.
- **A probe cost model for our agent.** Tokens, wall-clock, irreversibility have no agreed price. Unblock: measure real costs per probe type on the bench, then fix a default price table.

## Order to run
1. **C09.06-U1 (equal-token head-to-head).** It decides whether the whole S7 scoring step is worth its cost against plain thinking; every other test depends on its answer.
2. **C09.06-U3 (form rules).** Costs no tokens, may deliver most of the gain, and sets the honest baseline for all later arms.
3. **C09.06-U4 (answer flips).** Real probes are noisy, and no paper has tested this; if scored probing breaks at 10 to 20% flips, the design needs a contradiction check before anything else.
