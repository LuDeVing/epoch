## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A real conflict finder inside the loop. Our bench has no sound prover for free-text tasks, so we cannot check whether an LLM contradiction is real or minimal outside generated logic and circuit items. Unblock: build solver-checked item generators first (needed for U1, U2, U3, U10).
- Compounded errors. The only LLM pipeline paper fed gold output to each step (DX 2025). A full conflict, then hitting set, then probe chain with no gold inputs is untested. Unblock: end-to-end items with solver scoring at each step.
- Newer models. The LLM pipeline data are o1 and o3-mini from early 2025. Unblock: run U3 on current models before trusting the "falls toward 165 diagnoses" shape.
- Outside replication of DynamicHS and RBF-HS. All numbers come from one lab on ontologies. Unblock: U5 is our own replication in token terms, not a replication of runtime.
- Multi-fault real-world traces. RCA benchmarks are single-fault. Unblock: synthetic multi-fault items (U4) with known gold sets.
- SafeMin's 42.1% claim and the TU Delft thesis are snippet-only. Unblock: open both papers before citing.
- Counterfactual drop-one-and-rerun for agents is unstable (d18). Unblock: use fixed recorded traces, not live replay.
- Cost of conflict finding vs hitting-set work. Solver benchmarks ignore it, and it dominates in practice. Unblock: log tokens per state on U1 to U3.

## Order to run
1. **C09.04-U2** (non-minimal contradiction rate): cheapest, and it tells us whether the Greiner failure matters for our agent at all. It also gates U1.
2. **C09.04-U1** (reopen pruned suspects on a smaller contradiction): the direct test of the one idea unique to this subfield, with no LLM measurement anywhere in the literature.
3. **C09.04-U3** (code tool vs in-context hitting set): the highest expected gain, and it settles the tool-offload question the literature leaves open; it reuses the item generators built for U1 and U2.
