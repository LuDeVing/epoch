## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Replication of the human spreadsheet result with practising users: needs human participants and a set of real sheets with natural faults. Unblock by recruiting or using public EUSES-style corpora with known fixes.
- Real-world faults at volume: our bench mainly has planted faults. Needs a set of real failures created after the model cut-off. Unblock by collecting our own failed runs and labelling the cause.
- LLM running a full hitting-set loop: no study; needs a conflict-extraction harness (QuickXPlain over our checks) built first.
- Multi-agent log attribution with unambiguous labels: Who&When annotators were uncertain on 15-30% of items; needs cleaner labels before gains can be trusted.
- Error-value spreadsheet cases: needs multi-sheet workbooks at SpreadsheetBench 2 scale (about 600 edited cells per task); our bench lacks them.
- Cost of gate options in tokens: needs a token-accounted harness that logs each gate separately.

## Order to run
1. U2 (regression-first gate): cheapest, high expected gain, and the strongest adjacent evidence (1.3% vs 11.8% regressions).
2. U1 (code-computed suspect list vs free-form): directly tests the core MBD idea for S5 and shares items with U2.
3. U3 then U4 (information-gain probe choice, then oracle noise): the one place where the field has large effects (over 50% fewer queries) but no real-fault LLM test, and noise is the main risk to the method.
