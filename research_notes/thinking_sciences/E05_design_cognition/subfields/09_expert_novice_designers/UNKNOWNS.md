## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Lawson (1979) group sizes, scores and significance. Needs the paywalled paper, or Lawson's later block-task follow-ups with professionals. Nothing in open sources.
- Kruger and Cross (2006) effect sizes. Needs the full paper (blocked in this run); N is only 9, so even then it is a hint.
- Human expert-novice samples of 30 or more per group on breadth-first scoping. None found; would need a human study, or a new dataset.
- Frontier reasoning models (2025-26) on design fixation and on co-evolution. Needs an agreed fixation task and a frontier-model run; we should do this as part of U3/U4.
- Whether education, specific experience or personal idiosyncrasy sets the human style. Needs a longitudinal human study; not testable on an agent.
- A reliable design-quality ground truth. Rubric raters or experts are needed; LLM judges are only moderately aligned.
- Whether human fixation and attachment rates predict LLM rates. Unknown; the two may be unrelated mechanisms.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (candidate-first plus falsify vs constraints-first):** the direct Lawson test on a model, cheap with an oracle, and the one question every other item leans on.
2. **U3 (persist-then-restart with carry-over):** the best-supported practical gain across studies (Ege, TraceML, Fail-Fast), and cheap with existing pass checks.
3. **U2 (order by problem definedness):** settles the contested solution-first versus problem-first claim and tells S0 how to choose; reuse the U1 items and add the brief sets.
