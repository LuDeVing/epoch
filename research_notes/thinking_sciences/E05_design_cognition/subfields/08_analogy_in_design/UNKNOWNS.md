## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Expert designers or finished-design outcomes with LLM analogy support. Missing: a design bench with real downstream evaluation. Unblock: a physical or simulated design task with a measurable function score.
- Human-style novelty judged by people. Our bench scores by code. Unblock: a small blind rater panel on a subset, only to calibrate code-based novelty.
- Whether far analogies help when the criterion is correctness rather than novelty. Missing: tasks with both an executable check and real structural cousins. Unblock: build the cousin bank from solved bench items.
- The primary sources behind K07 (Gentner, Rattermann and Forbus 1993; Gick and Holyoak 1980/83; Linsey WordTree; Goel). Unblock: open them and re-extract the numbers.
- Whether a typed SME-style mapping beats free-text mapping. Missing: reliable predicate extraction from messy text. Unblock: a small hand-typed set to measure the ceiling.
- Whether harm from analogies (Ding: up to 25%) occurs on code-checked tasks. Unblock: count newly-broken items in U1.
- Expertise-mediation of far analogies (does a model with more domain knowledge profit more from far sources?). Unblock: compare a weaker and a stronger model on the U1 far arm.
- 2025-2026 LLM analogy literature, which the search quota left thin. Unblock: a fresh search pass once quota returns.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (analogue step vs same-token resampling)**: it is the missing measurement behind the whole subfield and decides whether O7 stays a default step or becomes impasse-only.
2. **U2 (retrieval key)**: cheap to run on the same items and bank as U1, and it gates every memory item that uses retrieval.
3. **U4 then U3 (far-analogy failure on the current model, then structure-first step)**: U4 is a short zero-shot run that tells us whether U3 is even needed.
