## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Exact Jansson-Smith 1991 and Chrysikou-Weisberg copy counts, and Sio 2015 effect sizes. Need the paywalled papers. Until then, size nothing from human effect sizes; U4 and U7 must set their own thresholds.
- Human judgement of idea novelty. Our bench has checks, not human raters. Embedding distance is a proxy (best embedding agreed with human coders 59%). Need a small human-rated sample to calibrate.
- Share of agent errors due to mental set. No paper reports it; our bench would need error labels by mechanism (see U3 logging).
- Whether context truncation behaves like human incubation. Needs a way to separate fixation relief from simply fewer give-ups (U5 logs both).
- Whether results hold on non-product, non-NLP tasks. Most diversity numbers are fitness products, student products or NLP ideas.

## Order to run

1. **U2** (list per fresh context vs single-idea contexts at equal tokens): it tests the one rule most likely to change S3, and it is cheap.
2. **U1** (persona or constraint per context): reuses the same items and harness as U2, so the cost is one extra arm.
3. **U3** (switch trigger on repeated same-mechanism failure): the largest measured agent gain (+19.6 points on Claude Sonnet 4.6) and it needs only logging plus one rule.
