## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Lawson 1979 and Dorst and Cross 2001 primary numbers. Missing: paywalled full text. Unblock: library access or author copies.
- Sio, Kotovsky and Cagan 2015 effect sizes and moderators for when examples help or hurt. Missing: paywalled. Unblock: full text; it would set how strongly to apply U8.
- Independent replication or critique of Cross's five aspects. Missing: APIs returned 429, so the search was partial. Unblock: a clean search of 2015-2026 design-research reviews.
- Whether the human findings (fixation with images, persona effects) carry to a text-only agent. Missing: a text-only control with the same measures. Unblock: use U8 and U5 results.
- Non-verbal media (sketches) for an agent. Missing: any sketch-like tool in our harness. Unblock: a diagram or typed-graph scratch tool.
- Tasks with no check (wicked problems). Missing: a stop rule from outside. Unblock: a budget-based rule and a blinded rating panel.
- Whether results depend on the model generation (studies used GPT-3, GPT-4o, Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.4). Unblock: run each test on at least two model sizes.

## Order to run

1. **U2 (one-line constraint vs method scripts):** cheapest, single-prompt arms, and its result decides whether any design-method content enters the scaffold at all.
2. **U8 (overlap check on all earlier attempts):** pure code, extends an add-now item, and fixation is the best-replicated failure mode here.
3. **U1 (monitor on plateau at equal tokens):** largest reported effect (d=1.85) but never tested at equal tokens, and it must be reconciled with G1 and G9.
