## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Human FBS-coded design protocols with reliability numbers and outcome ratings. We have none, and the original human numbers (Dorst and Cross 2001, Chen 2023, Jiang, Gero and Yen 2014) were never opened. Unblock: obtain those papers, or a small set of human design transcripts, and code them with the same coder as U3.
- Faithfulness on open-ended design and ill-structured tasks. All opened tests use multiple choice. Unblock: build an open-brief hint probe with a checkable hidden influence (for example a hidden constraint in the system prompt).
- A chance-corrected reliability for FBS codes by an LLM coder. Only raw percent exists (Zhang 79.5%). Unblock: two trained human coders on 100 design-talk segments.
- Whether linkography or entropy predicts design outcome at all. The one opened original shows the opposite sign for whole-session entropy. Unblock: not worth a bench slot until U2 shows any process share works.
- Whether LLM-LLM coder agreement above LLM-human reflects shared bias. Unblock: a human-coded set plus coders from 3 families (U3, U10).
- Replication of Li's guidance gain outside the authors' trace pool. Unblock: U5 on our items.
- Explanation versus plain think-aloud in models with hidden reasoning (the visible trace is a summary, as with Claude 4.5 Haiku in Walden). Unblock: access to raw traces, or a comparison on open models only.

## Order to run
1. E05.06-U1 (trace requirement at equal tokens): cheapest test, and it decides whether any scaffold that asks for visible reasoning pays for itself under G7.
2. E05.06-U3 (per-code coder reliability on 100 hand-coded traces): every later trace test depends on a coder we can trust, and it is cheap once the traces exist.
3. E05.06-U5 (structure guidance on ill-structured items): the largest reported gain (+9.7 points) with a single-source base; one replication either confirms it or removes it.
