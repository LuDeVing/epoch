## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Expert-judged design briefs: we have no blind-expert panel. Unblock with a simulator-scored task (EngDesign-style) or a recruited panel of 3 raters per item, and a rule for rater agreement.
- Effect sizes in Sio et al. (43 studies) and sample sizes in Goel and Pirolli and Dorst and Cross: papers are paywalled or scanned. Unblock by access to the full texts.
- Rittel and Webber properties: no testable form on a bench with known answers. Unblock by turning one property (no stopping rule) into a measurable budget-stop test.
- Shojaee collapse results: the critiques about token limits and unsolvable instances were not opened. Unblock by reading them before using K29.
- Shin (N=280), EngDesign, EngiBench and GraphicBench were not independently re-opened by the checker. Unblock by re-reading the tables.
- Real tokens of 2025-2026 reasoning models with thinking on: costs differ by provider. Unblock by logging thinking tokens in the harness.
- Human vs LLM fixation under the controlled Jansson and Smith design: needs a flawed-example task set we do not have. Unblock by building one (U6).

## Order to run
1. E05.01-U1 (tree vs own long CoT vs verified best-of-N): it decides whether S6's tree is worth its tokens at all, and the old 74% vs 70% gap says it may not be.
2. E05.01-U12 (obfuscated twins): cheap, and it protects every other result from a recall confound.
3. E05.01-U4 (difficulty gate): the best-established mechanism (K26) and the largest expected saving; run it on the same item sets as U1.
