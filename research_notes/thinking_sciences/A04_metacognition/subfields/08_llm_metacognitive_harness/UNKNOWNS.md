# LLM metacognitive harness: what we don't know and should test
*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: arms at equal total tokens (include FOK, JOL, probe, aggregator and setup tokens), at least 3 seeds, paired bootstrap on per-item differences. "Plan number" for any gain is +1.5 to +4 points, not the +8.6 headline.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Independent replication of Cao.** Code, prompts and data are not released (arXiv v1 only). Unblock: the release, or our own rebuild from the paper (U1 to U3 are that rebuild).
- **Why the harness candidate pool is better than four independent samples** (oracle 62.2 vs 60.5). Needs the full prompts and per-item logs to see whether compact feedback or FOK in the solve prompt causes it.
- **Anchor size needed before the controller stops overfitting.** Needs a labelled pool of at least 1,000 items and a sweep of anchor sizes 50, 100, 300, 1,000. We do not have the labelled pool for every class yet.
- **Long-horizon agent control by confidence.** No published test of a confidence-gated stop on an agent with external feedback. Needs an agent suite with irreversible steps and a paired act/abstain design like AgentAbstain.
- **Full 33-model atlas (domain dependence of monitoring, shape change across Anthropic generations).** Only the abstract was read. Unblock: read the full paper (arXiv 2605.06673).
- **Snippet-only papers** (Judge, Retrieve, or Abstain; Learning When to Escalate; Answer instability). Unblock: open the PDFs before using their designs.
- **Cost of the cheap difficulty estimate** (Snell does not count it). Unblock: the token log from U9.
- **Whether results from open 1.5B to 30B models carry to Claude.** Needs U4, U5 on Claude; until then treat d11 to d15 as direction only.
- **Monitoring-versus-control link in Claude** (Cacioli r = .17, n = 20, underpowered). Needs a battery with at least 100 items per task and test-retest.

## Order to run

1. **U4 (signal audit, with U5 on the same data).** Cheapest and a precondition: no gate is worth building if AUROC and ECE fail on our model and classes, and the same logs tell us which stop signal wins.
2. **U1 with U2 (headline at equal tokens, directed vs random retry).** Decides whether the whole confidence-gated loop earns its tokens against best-of-K plus vote; reuses the U4 logs and the existing harness arms.
3. **U7 (Huang protocol on our reasoning model).** Cheap, and it decides whether G1 and G3 stay as written or get narrowed; it also frames how the S5 revise step may be used.
