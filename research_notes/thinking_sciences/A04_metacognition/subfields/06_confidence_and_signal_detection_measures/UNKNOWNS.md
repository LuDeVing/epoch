## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Original Koriat effect sizes and N.** The 2012, 2008 and 2011 papers are paywalled. Unblock: obtain full text or supplementary tables S2-S6 of the 2025 review. Needed to judge how strong the human consistency-confidence link is.
- **Failed replications of the human consistency effect.** None found, none actively sought. Unblock: a targeted literature search.
- **Whether LLMs use anything like Koriat's mechanism.** Only an analogy (Steyvers and Peters). Unblock: interpretability work, outside our bench.
- **Frontier-scale metacognitive efficiency.** Cacioli says unknown above 8-9B and cannot score API models without logprobs. Unblock: logprob access, or the Dai verbal method with a many-level scale.
- **Agreement among partial traces for API models without logprobs.** Untested. Unblock: access to streaming partial output and a rule for comparing unfinished answers.
- **Prefix-cache cost of the P(True) self-check on our API.** Depends on our serving. Unblock: measure billed input tokens with and without caching.
- **Large-scale human panel for reader effects.** B8-type tests need recruited readers. Unblock: a panel budget, or a proxy with a held-out grader model (which has its own bias, G5).
- **Original Cash 2025 and Griot 2025 results.** Seen second-hand only. Unblock: read them.
- **Transfer of grader error.** Residual 18.4% scorer error varies 2.4-fold by model (Cacioli). Unblock: a human-labelled sample of 100+ per task family for our own graders.

## Order to run

1. **A04.06-U1** (agreement vs verbal vs token). It is the base for every other test and the only one that can overturn the core design; it also yields the data reused by U7, U12, U13.
2. **A04.06-U2** (high-agreement-wrong rate and breaker). The main known failure of agreement-based confidence; its result sets how far G4 and S8 must lean on tests and second framings.
3. **A04.06-U3** (adaptive stop). Cheapest and largest likely token saving; reuses the U1 item set and needs no extra labels.
