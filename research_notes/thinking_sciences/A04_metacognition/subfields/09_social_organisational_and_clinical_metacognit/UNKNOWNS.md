## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Altered ToM items validated against humans: humans fail half of perturbed items (c08). We need a human-checked item set, or we cannot tell brittleness from a bad item.
- Multi-agent social tasks (hidden profile, negotiation): the 2026 studies (b18, b19, b24) were seen at abstract level. Unblock: open the papers and rebuild one task.
- Independent MCT evidence: only a protocol exists for the independent tMCT-vs-CBT trial (a25, d12). Unblock: wait for results. This does not change the schema, since MCT does not transfer.
- Exact Bahrami 2010 numbers and Mahmoodi 2015: paywalled. Unblock: open full text or a preprint.
- Reasoning-model results on Hi-ToM order 3+ and a 2025-26 self-correction replication: not found with a limited search budget (APIs rate-limited). Unblock: rerun the search when budgets recover.
- Wells' S-REF and MCQ primary sources were not opened (a26, a27). Low value for the agent; open only if MCT is revisited.

## Order to run
1. A04.09-U2 (knowing-doing gap and reminder): cheapest (a system-prompt line), and the gap could be large on other-party tasks.
2. A04.09-U1 (perspective filter with twins): also builds the true-belief and altered twin item set that every other social test needs.
3. A04.09-U4 (confidence-weighted merge of unequal runs): no LLM evidence yet, and a positive result would change S7; it reuses runs we already have.
