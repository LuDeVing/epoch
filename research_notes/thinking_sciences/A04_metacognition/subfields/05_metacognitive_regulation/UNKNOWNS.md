## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Swanson (1990) compensation: the original paper is unread (APA 403). Unblock: a library copy or author PDF. Even then a "weak vs strong backbone under the same scaffold" test is a different question.
- Human training effect sizes (Dignath, Theobald, de Boer): only a secondary summary was opened. Unblock: open the original meta-analyses.
- Whether the chosen backbone's API exposes logprobs or hidden states needed for DEER, DeepConf and probes. Unblock: check what the API returns; otherwise only prompt and trace triggers are possible.
- Whether SCoRe-style trained self-correction can be had off the shelf. Unblock: a fine-tuned model; outside the scaffold.
- Tasks with no check at all (open-ended). No studied gate exists; needs a design, for example independent-sample agreement.
- Independent replication of Meta-R1 and MetaRAG on other benchmarks. Unblock: wait for replications or reproduce on our items.
- Human online-vs-offline gap decomposition (measure vs task, I2 up to 99.9%). Unblock: Muncer raw data or a moderator re-analysis.
- 2025-26 frontier closed models (reasoning and non-reasoning) with a clean intrinsic-review arm. Unblock: budget to run U2 and U3.

## Order to run
1. A04.05-U2 (intrinsic review vs extra sample on the current backbone): cheapest, and it decides whether G1/G2 stay as written.
2. A04.05-U4 (two-sided stop/hold rule with trace triggers): likely the biggest token saving, and it builds the trace-trigger arm U1 needs.
3. A04.05-U1 with the length-matched arm U13 (self-question vs trace trigger vs fixed budget): the headline gap of this subfield and the only one with no data at all.
