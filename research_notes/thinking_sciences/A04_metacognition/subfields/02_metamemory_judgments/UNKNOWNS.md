## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Human FOK and EOL accuracy numbers.** Needs the original papers (Hart 1965, Nelson and Narens 1980, Underwood 1966, Leonesio and Nelson 1990 full text) and the original Rhodes and Tauber subgroup values (g=.70 and g=.03 unverified). Our bench measures LLMs only; this is background.
- **Hidden-state probes and SAE entity latents.** Need white-box access to a model. Unblock: run an open-weight model on the bench.
- **Binder-style privileged self-access.** Needs fine-tuning on our own agent's behaviour. We do not train. Unblock: a small open-weight copy.
- **Fluency effects on human users reading our output.** Needs a human rater panel.
- **Whether Koriat's consensuality effect replicates outside his lab.** Needs human experiments; low value for the schema.
- **Frontier 2025-2026 model calibration with AUROC.** Needs API budget for large item sets with graded labels; the critique found none published.
- **Equal-token gate comparisons with long reasoning models.** Needs token accounting that includes hidden reasoning tokens.

## Order to run
1. **A04.02-U1 (draft vs prompt-only gate).** The central open question for S0; no head-to-head exists, and the answer decides whether S0 is a lookup or a mini-attempt.
2. **A04.02-U2 (class base rate vs own rating).** Cheapest to run from existing logs, near-zero model cost, and human data say it should win.
3. **A04.02-U4 (5-sample agreement vs verbal confidence).** Largest known effect (AUROC 54.8 to 92.7), but it needs re-checking at equal tokens on current models before it becomes the confidence rule.
