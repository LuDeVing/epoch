## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Independent replication of Cao 2026 and the Cacioli battery.** Needs other labs or open models. Our U1 and U4 runs are the replication; until then both are hypotheses (K35).
- **White-box monitoring** (activation reports, probes, P(IK) heads). Needs weights or logits. Unblocked by an open model with hidden-state access; out of scope for a scaffold-only agent (K22, K23).
- **Training-based monitors** (Self-RAG tokens, fine-tuned probes). Needs a training budget; treat as an upper bound only.
- **Why introspection helps some reasoning models and hurts Claude 3.7 Sonnet.** Needs a per-model sweep; add to U2 if our model is in the set.
- **Whether a bigger model is a better monitor.** No scaling law in the data (K11). Needs several sizes of one family; we have few.
- **Whether LLMs have a counterpart to the human learning-state effect** (FOK collapses after overlearning). Needs controlled exposure counts; hard with API models.
- **Human-style long-term retention monitoring.** Our agent has no retention interval; only the memory component (lessons across runs) could test it, after multiplayer data exists.

## Order to run
1. **U1 (gate vs best-of-N vs Self-Refine vs S0 gate):** the one claim that decides whether "code monitors, model acts" earns its place under G7; everything else assumes it.
2. **U2 (monitor choice per domain):** cheap to run on the same answers as U1, and U1 only works if the monitor has resolution.
3. **U8 (stopping rules) with U4 (health switch) in the same run:** both reuse the U1 logs, save tokens directly, and U4 protects the gate from a bad monitor.
