## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Token probabilities and hidden states.** DeepConf, CISC with P(True) and semantic entropy probes need them. Unblock: confirm which models in our harness expose top-k logprobs; if none, drop those arms.
- **Closed frontier model results for thinking-versus-non-thinking calibration.** Only open 32B models were measured (Yoon d02, Zeng d03). Unblock: U8 on our own model.
- **Full text of Rhodes and Tauber, Kruger and Dunning, Koriat 1997 and Thompson 2011.** Moderator tables and per-experiment numbers were not read. Unblock: obtain full texts; low priority because human numbers will not set LLM thresholds.
- **Koriat sign-flip statistic.** Within-person gamma .24 for consensually wrong items against an aggregate negative was not reconciled. Unblock: read the figure panels.
- **Cause of the LLM hard-easy pattern.** Regression to the mean is asserted, not tested (d04). Unblock: a representative item design plus a simulated-noise control.
- **Pre-work versus post-work confidence.** CALIBER (d14) says two different questions; Kaddour (d07) found no significant difference at N=100. Unblock: larger N, separate budget-prediction and answer-check questions.
- **Training-based gains (finetuned confidence heads, trajectory calibrators).** Outside the no-training rule. Unblock: only if we later relax the rule.
- **Equal-token comparison on tasks with no single exact answer (open mode).** Agreement and cluster counts need a meaning-equivalence check. Unblock: a code-side clustering method for open outputs.
- **Whether metacognitive skills are one faculty.** Single-question and pairwise confidence did not transfer (c22). Unblock: run U1, U2, U7 on both formats.

## Order to run

1. **A04.03-U2 (agreement of k short samples versus one long trace, equal tokens).** It decides which confidence signal everything else uses, and its logs also supply the seed items for U3.
2. **A04.03-U1 (blind re-solve versus answer-visible check).** Biggest human effect size in this subfield (g = 0.93), cheap to build, and untested on LLMs.
3. **A04.03-U3 (catching consistent-and-wrong items).** Sets the hard floor of what agreement can do (5.5% to 28% of items) and decides whether S8 needs a different-method re-solve.
