## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Frontier-model PaperBench and CORE-Bench numbers for 2026: needs the benchmark harness and compute budget. Would show if early finish and anchoring still occur.
- A measured false-positive rate for two-prompt LLM-judge A/B tests: needs our own A/A runs (U6); no published value found.
- Effect of an explicit abstention reward in an agent loop: needs impossible-item sets built on our bench (U5).
- Whether Hu and Tang results (small Qwen models) hold at frontier scale: needs the same protocol on our model.
- How the 36-62% human replication rates map to agent-gain survival: needs several batches of retested gains (U9).
- Simmons per-practice rates, Button median power, Franco 40/60-point figures: need the full texts; not decisive for the schema.
- Arce (2026) audit of 41 self-improving agent papers: not opened; would give a base rate of evaluation traps.

## Order to run

1. **E15.07-U2** (selected-vs-fresh gap by N and n): cheapest, uses data we already generate, and its size decides how much of U1 and U6 is needed.
2. **E15.07-U3** (frozen control arm): one extra arm per batch; tells us the noise floor for every later test.
3. **E15.07-U6** (A/A false-positive rate of the judged comparison): fixes the minimum item and seed count before we trust any other result on this list.
