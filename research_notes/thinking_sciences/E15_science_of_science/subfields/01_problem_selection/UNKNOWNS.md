## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Questions outside NLP and AI research (debugging, engineering, business). No study exists. We need a task set with numeric outcomes for these.
- The 100-hour execution gap. We have no way to execute research ideas at that scale. Unblock: use tasks with outcomes in minutes and test only the probe effect.
- Expert gold labels. Pairwise agreement is about 56%, so 3 to 5 raters per item are needed. Unblock: use automatic numeric outcomes.
- Pivot-penalty cause (lost knowledge vs reputation). Needs a manipulation of context availability, which U7 starts.
- Replication of Wen et al. on newer cutoffs. No replication found. Needs a fresh outcome-pair set built after our model cutoff.
- BoLeBench full paper not read. Needs the full text before U8 is worth running.
- Optimal risky share under a token budget. No model has numbers. U6 gives only an empirical estimate for our tasks.
- Whether 2026 model generations change the 2024-era results (saturation, novelty ratings). Needs a re-run of Si-style generation with current models.

## Order to run

1. U1 (probe before commit): the biggest measured selection gain (65% to 78.5%) at the lowest cost, and it fits G8 directly.
2. U3 (overlap list vs scalar novelty, retrieval depth): retrieval is the one established fix for false novelty, and the depth curve is missing.
3. U2 (precedent retrieval judge): the only way to get the trained-judge gain without training; also decides what memory stores.
