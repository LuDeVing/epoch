## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Whether Wu 2019 survives removal of zero-reference entries. Needs the Wu data and CD index code, not an LLM bench. Unblocked by a data request or a rerun of the public CD index.
- Exact 17% per decade. Needs the Wuchty 2007 body text. Low value for the agent.
- Whether the remote-team penalty and the Hao gains are causal. Needs a natural experiment or randomisation; human-only.
- Whether Kim's 0.45 ceiling and the Ringelmann ceiling replicate on frontier models by an independent group. Needs a sweep of one frontier model across accuracy levels and a full team curve.
- Whether MAST shares reconcile (44.2/32.3/23.5 vs 41.8/36.9/21.3). Needs the authors' trace set.
- A disruption-like novelty metric for LLM outputs that is not a model judge (G8). Without it, U2, U6 and U7 rest on distinct-answer counts only.
- Exact scores of Smit 2024 and Wang 2024 (debate). Low value; G9 already holds.

## Order to run
1. E15.04-U1 (spec verification step): cheapest, largest measured gain in the literature (+15.6%), and fits our existing ARC and BRAINTEASER harness.
2. E15.04-U4 (MAST modes in our traces): uses logs we already have, and tells us which stop and repeat rules are worth coding.
3. E15.04-U2 (diverse restarts vs deep chain on novelty): closes the one gap no study has measured, and touches our proven open-mode result (3.18 to 9.63).
