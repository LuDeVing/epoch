## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Chinn and Brewer-style anomaly categories (discard, patch, revise) in LLMs.** Needs a labelled set of planted anomalies and a labeller that is not the model under test; existing labels came from Opus 4.7 grading Opus 4.7 (d10) and LLM-assisted pipelines (d09). Unblock: human or code labels on a small set.
- **Failed replications or large-sample human 2-4-6 studies.** None found; searches were rate-limited. Unblock: a proper literature pass (Wetherick, Wason 1962 exchange, later work).
- **Klahr, Fay and Dunbar 1993 rates (83 / 65 / 53 / 33%).** Second-hand only. Unblock: a legal open copy.
- **Klahr and Nigam 2004 and the Alfieri 2011 meta-analysis.** Not retrieved. Unblock: open copies; then build the LLM analogue (U16).
- **SHELDON outside the authors' tasks.** Needs code or a faithful reimplementation; task counts are inconsistent. Unblock: release or reimplementation.
- **Independent replication of any 2026 preprint** (FalsifyBench, Failing to Falsify, DiscoverPhysics, EvoSCM, Rios-Garcia). Our own runs are the replication.
- **Mechanism of thinking-mode gains.** Needs reasoning traces scored step by step; written reasoning is not evidence of the real cause (G10).
- **Real noise and cost of measurement.** Most testbeds use clean simulators and count queries, not tokens (d18). Unblock: a noisy simulator with a cost per measurement.
- **Real-science anomaly handling and the communication space** (Dunbar's labs, Klahr). Needs multi-agent or human-in-loop settings outside this bench.
- **Where in DiscoveryWorld the chain breaks.** Needs a transition-level breakdown from the benchmark authors or our own rerun.
- **Wason 1960 and BigTrak's remaining tables.** Not opened. Unblock: legal open copies.
