## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Does atypicality predict success at all (Lin 2022 vs Li 2025)?** Needs matched definitions on the same human data. Not a bench question. Until it is resolved, atypicality stays out of any selection score (K30).
- **Functional equivalence as a mechanism.** Needs an outside-lab intervention study with a topic model that is not the authors' own. Our bench can test the operator split (U2) but not the mechanism.
- **Human inverted-U for source distance (Fu et al., Chan and Schunn).** Not opened; links failed. Unblock: fetch the papers and read the numbers, then reuse them as priors for U3.
- **Whether idea-stage to execution gaps hold outside NLP.** Needs tasks that can be executed cheaply, with a ground-truth outcome. Our bench has code and ARC only; math novelty has no formal check in the literature.
- **Hao et al. 2026 and Doshi and Hauser 2024 numbers.** Both unopened (3x papers, 4.8x citations, -4.63% range; 10.7% lower diversity). Unblock: read the papers. Relevant to the multiplayer plan, not to the single agent.
- **Whether the LLM theory-versus-method labels in Li et al. are valid.** No human check in the text read. Unblock: contact or reproduce on a small labelled set.
- **Delayed payoff for models.** Human delay is years; for a model the analogue is untested. U6 is the nearest test, but it only shows a revision effect, not the same mechanism.
- **Novelty judged by experts on our own outputs.** Expert originality ratings have test-retest ICC about 0.6 (d01), so a human rating study needs 2+ raters and repeated waves. Unblock: budget for raters, or keep to verifier-only outcomes.
- **Disruption-style outcomes for agent answers.** There are no later citers. No test is possible; use head-to-head against the named incumbent instead (U2, U7).

## Order to run
1. **E15.02-U9 (diversity form: regeneration, verbalized-k, forced far seed vs plain resampling).** Cheapest, only needs prompts and a dedup count, and its result sets the candidate pool that every other test draws from.
2. **E15.02-U1 (mix versus all-distant versus all-conventional).** The largest open gap in the subfield and the direct test of the founding result for an agent; reuses the pool from U9.
3. **E15.02-U2 (recombine versus displace operators on ARC rule-inversion and new-frame items).** Decides whether the schema gets a second novelty operator, which is the main design consequence of the key paper.
