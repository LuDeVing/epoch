## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Frontier-model baseline.** Every LLM transfer study found uses 2020-2024 models or mid-tier 2026 models. We need our own frontier-model numbers; no published number substitutes.
- **Barnett and Ceci tables.** The nine-dimension tables need the full paper (closed access). Unblock with a library copy; then map our lesson distances onto their content and context dimensions.
- **Cross-class vs near-duplicate in published agent memory.** ExpeL, ReasoningBank and Voyager were not re-bucketed by distance. Unblock with the authors' released task lists, or by running our own distance-labelled benchmark.
- **Compare-two-cases for LLMs.** No published test. Needs our own paired-instance bench with a third member as target (U2).
- **Stronger baselines for the false-match cost.** Only one study splits harm on solved vs failed items. Needs our own solved/failed split (U6).
- **Einstellung from a stored lesson.** No LLM measurement. Overlaps the Einstellung subfield; needs a bench item where a stored lesson gives a plausible but wrong approach and a plain attempt succeeds.
- **Lewis and Mitchell table order.** Needs the rendered table image to confirm which model scored 0.488, 0.350 and 0.452; and the model confidence intervals.
- **Hodel and West.** Not opened; arXiv id unverified. A direct read would settle the Webb dispute.
- **Hint rate in current models.** The 1-of-15 / 15-of-15 result is one paper, one problem pair, GPT-4, self-reported use. Needs a rate across many pairs on current models (U5).
- **Detterman 1993 and Perkins and Salomon 1992** primary texts were not read; claims about bridging and hugging rest on summaries.

## Order to run

1. **U1 (cross-class lessons vs extra attempts, equal tokens).** The main question: if it fails for a frontier model, the lesson store is not worth building beyond near-class reuse, and everything else is lower priority.
2. **U3 (typed code-checked mapping vs model judge vs embedding).** Cheapest way to cut the false-match cost, and it is the one design change our schema can make that no published study has tested.
3. **U2 (two-case lesson vs one-case lesson).** The strongest human effect (45% vs 21%; 40% vs 22%) with no LLM test; costs only one extra solved instance per lesson. Run U5 (impasse cue) in the same batch if capacity allows.
