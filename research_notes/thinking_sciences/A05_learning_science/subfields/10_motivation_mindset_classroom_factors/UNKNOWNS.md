## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- True size of the human effect (0.02, 0.05 or 0.09 SD): needs new independent, pre-registered trials by neutral teams. We cannot run children.
- Blackwell 2007 Study 2 slopes, Li and Bates 2019 JEP:General numbers, Bastani 2025 and Kim 2024 effect sizes: need paywalled full text. Unblock with library access.
- Teacher-mindset moderation outside the Yeager data (Chile +0.02 SD; a reverse-direction trial seen only as a snippet): needs the Psychological Science paper opened.
- Whether any LLM has a measurable growth-mindset trait: no validated instrument exists; would need a behavioural battery first (U12).
- Inference-time curiosity or learning-progress selectors in the literature (d16 unopened): needs a full read of arXiv 2504.03380 and a literature scan.
- The Wuji framing needs the original scenario set and prompts to be shared by the author for a faithful replication.

## Order to run
1. **U2 (encouragement x verifier 2x2).** Cheapest, uses existing retry loops, and its answer decides U1, U7 and U8 and whether any encouragement line stays out of the schema.
2. **U11 (mean versus all-correct reporting).** Costs only a metric change in every run, and protects every later test from false wins.
3. **U3 (trust versus fear framing).** Largest reported effect (d=2.28) but weakest evidence; a 50-scenario replication either confirms a real G1/S6 addition or removes it.
