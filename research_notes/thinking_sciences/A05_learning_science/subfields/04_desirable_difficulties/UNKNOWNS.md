# Desirable difficulties: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: arms at equal tokens (setup, lesson-writing and retrieval counted); a learning stream plus a held-out set no arm sees until the end; a gap of N unrelated tasks before the held-out test; at least 5 reruns per arm; paired per-item analysis; "beats the floor" means the paired mean delta exceeds 2 times the run-to-run SD measured in U7. Report the immediate pass rate beside the delayed one and count rank reversals.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Long-horizon decay across sessions.** Human spacing works over days to months. Our bench runs are short. Unblock: a multi-session replay harness that carries the lesson store across days, or an accelerated version with many unrelated tasks between revisits.
- **Large preregistered replication of interleaving or testing on LLMs.** No such study exists. Unblock: a public benchmark of lesson-order effects with 5+ seeds per cell, or our own U1 results released.
- **Per-lesson memory evaluation on public agents.** SeqMem-Eval and Hu et al. (d23, d24) could not be read. Unblock: read them via another route; they may supply backward-transfer and forgetting numbers.
- **Weight-level effects.** SRT, LFR and Chen concern gradient training. Our agent does not train. Unblock: only if the project moves to fine-tuning.
- **Whether a human-style fluency illusion exists inside an LLM tuning loop.** We see noise and selection effects (c08), not a measured illusion. Unblock: U9 plus the rank-reversal counts from U1.
- **Effect of the Bastani design critique and correction notice.** Not read. Unblock: read the PNAS correction and critique before using the -0.054 figure as a quantitative anchor.
- **Engagement-driven nulls.** Online testing-effect nulls come with heavy attrition (b03). An LLM has no attrition, so their meaning for us is unclear. Unblock: no test needed; treat as human-only.
- **Hard-vs-complex.** Complexity of the task and difficulty of the practice condition are confounded in the literature (a10, a22). Unblock: a bench with matched items at two complexity levels.

## Order to run

1. **A05.04-U7, the floor.** Every other test needs it to say whether a delta is real; cheap and already supported by independent sources.
2. **A05.04-U2, per-lesson immediate vs held-out gain.** Uses logging we can add now; it tells us whether the immediate pass rate can be trusted at all, which is the whole caution of this subfield.
3. **A05.04-U1, harder vs easier lesson practice on a delayed held-out set.** The central untested claim; U3 (attempt-first) and U5 (order by class) can run as arms of the same stream.
