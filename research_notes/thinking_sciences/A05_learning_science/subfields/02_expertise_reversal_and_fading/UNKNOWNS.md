## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- **Within-class reversal at scale.** Detecting 5 pp needs about 560 to 1570 unpaired trials per arm (141 to 392 for 10 pp); existing agent studies (30 to 87 tasks) detect only 8 to 17 pp. Needs a large class set and several seeds.
- **Human primary numbers.** The 1998, 2000 and 2001 founding papers were never opened; the Lee et al. 2006 row is internally inconsistent. Unblock with library access to the papers.
- **Chen et al. 2017, Atkinson et al. 2003, Salden 2009 and 2010 full text.** Needed for exact fading and element-interactivity effect sizes.
- **Unpublished studies.** The meta-analysis searched only PsycINFO and ERIC; a file-drawer check is not possible.
- **Item-level complexity labels.** We have no annotated step counts. Unblock by deriving them from verified solution traces.
- **Cross-session memory.** A true delayed test needs an agent with persistent lessons; our harness resets between runs.
- **Load-rating analogue.** Kalyuga's efficiency indicator uses rated load; an LLM has no clean analogue (tokens or revisions are proxies).
- **Lesson load and follow rates.** Needs trajectory logging beyond pass or fail.
- **Seed variance.** Single-seed studies show sign flips (Cho and Park); measure our own seed variance first.

## Order to run
1. **A05.02-U5 (probe size).** Cheap, needs no lessons, and every other test depends on a trustworthy band assignment.
2. **A05.02-U1 (band x lesson on/off).** The core question: it decides whether S0 gets a lesson gate at all and where its threshold sits.
3. **A05.02-U11 (verified vs model-written lessons).** The best-supported harm in the agent literature, cheap to run, and it fixes what goes into memory before the gate test (U2) is run on top.
