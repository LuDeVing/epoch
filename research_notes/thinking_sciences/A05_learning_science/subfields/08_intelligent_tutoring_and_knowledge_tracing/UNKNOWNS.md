## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- A delayed, unaided outcome for the agent itself. Our bench scores immediate answers; U7 needs a later no-helper run on matched items, which has to be built.
- Schwonke et al. (2009) exact numbers, Pane (2014) per-level effect sizes and VanLehn (2011) answer-based d. The papers are paywalled. Unblock by getting the full texts through an institution or author copies.
- Independent replication of NTKT (AUC 90.32) and CIKT. We have no Eedi access or training budget, and training is out of scope for the project.
- The cost of LLM label noise in dialogue tracing (K23). Needs a tracer trained on noisy versus clean labels.
- Whether a better student model causes better learning in people. Not testable on a bench with no human learners.
- The mastery threshold when the true learning curve is not two-state. Needs a misspecified simulator, not only our well-specified one (d09).
- Whether "reasoning hurts concrete prediction" (c05) is general. Needs a second dataset and reasoning-on versus off arms.

## Order to run
1. **U2 (self-estimate vs outcome):** cheap, needs only logs, and tells us at once whether the model's own sense of competence can be trusted.
2. **U1 (count-based tracker for routing and spend):** the main open gap (K25); it uses the same logs as U2 and costs no extra model calls.
3. **U3 (margin stop rule and threshold sweep):** a direct, cheap change to the stop condition; the threshold sensitivity is the one place where this field's own model is known to mislead.
