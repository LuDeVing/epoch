## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Exact human numbers: Wood, Bruner and Ross 1976 (30 children, 21 blocks), Klahr and Nigam 77/23, Smit Bayes factors, Hmelo-Silver 2007, Lazonder and Harmsen. Needs full-text access (publisher PDFs were blocked).
- A stronger hint-writing model: nearly all inference-time hint results use one. We have none. Unblocked by testing same-model hints (U6) or buying one comparison run with a larger model.
- Long-run skill growth: a frozen model does not learn from help. Only memory-write tests (U10) approach it.
- Whether the Scaf-GRPO or SEELE schedule (late, targeted, incremental) helps a prompt-only agent: needs U6 and U12 first.
- Whether LLM-tutor findings with humans (Kestin, Bastani) say anything about an agent: they measure human learners. Not testable on our bench.
- Guided play and early-education effects: not testable on an LLM.
- Token cost of verification and of the hint loop: no source gave it; needs our own measurement in U8.

## Order to run
1. U1 (decompose vs plain CoT on current models): cheapest, highest expected gain, and closes the oldest open gap (all numbers are from a 2022 model).
2. U2 (scaffold on/off by model class): decides whether every other test is worth running for the model we use, and can reuse the U1 items.
3. U10 (failing-step memory vs answer-only memory): cheap on an existing stream, and tests the only memory-side finding with a large human effect (-17% crutch effect).
