## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Exact Chi 1989 and 1994 numbers and conditions: the primary texts were blocked (Wiley 403, ASU login). Unblock: library access or an author copy. Until then, do not cite the numbers as verified.
- Effect sizes in McEldoon 2013, Matthews and Rittle-Johnson 2009, Schworm and Renkl 2006: only abstracts read. Unblock: full-text access.
- Does a long-run effect exist (delayed test, as in Lawson and Mayer)? Our bench is one-session. Unblock: a multi-session memory benchmark where lessons from day 1 are used on day N.
- The human mechanism (explain every example step) on an LLM-as-learner: no prior study. Unblock: our U7/U8 tests are the first; needs items with examples whose steps code can verify.
- Free text vs menu outside calculus: no source. Unblock: U6.
- Faithfulness of the line to the real cause: cannot be measured on our bench without activation access. Unblock: planted-hint tests (Turpin style) on our own models.
- Current-generation reasoning models: all measured LLM gains are from 2022-2023 models except a few 2026 tutor studies. Unblock: rerun U1 and U5 on whichever models the bench uses; treat older results as priors only.

## Order to run
1. **A05.07-U1** (line vs extra sample at equal tokens): the decisive test; every other candidate depends on whether the line earns its tokens (G7).
2. **A05.07-U3** (external-check gate): cheapest, highest expected gain, and most of the older evidence says unguided revision hurts.
3. **A05.07-U2** (content of the line, with wrong-but-plausible and filler arms): reuses U1 items and tells us whether to keep the naming rule or reduce it to a focus cue.
