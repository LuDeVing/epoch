## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Founding numbers.** Cooper and Sweller 1987, Paas 1992, Quilici and Mayer 1996, Nievelstein 2013, Atkinson 2003 and Chen, Kalyuga and Sweller 2015 were read only as abstracts or snippets. We cannot size "transfer", "completion equals full" or "three examples no better than one" from primary data. Unblock: obtain the full PDFs through a library route and read the tables.
- **Independent replication of the 1985 effect with a verifier-style control.** Only one lab, small cells. Unblock: a direct human replication is outside our bench; use A05.01-U13 as the machine analogue.
- **Frontier-model behaviour.** Most clean LLM tests use old or small open models (code-davinci-002, text-davinci-002, Qwen3 up to 8B). Cheng 2025/26 and SkillsBench are the exceptions. Unblock: our own runs on the current model in U1 and U2.
- **A cheap element-interactivity classifier for agents.** No published method. Unblock: use solution-step count as a proxy (U12), then check against human labels.
- **Dose response for wrong entries by percent wrong.** No paper reports it; U4 is the first.
- **Whether copying the case is the main failure mode on our bench.** Needs per-run logs of whether outputs reuse the case's values; add to the run log first.
- **Human-style self-explanation effect on a model.** Only an N=8 judge pilot exists. Unblock: U10 is the only practical route.
- **Productive failure versus worked examples for a model.** Puech 2025 gives no learning comparison. Unblock: U3 is the closest test (attempt first, case after).
- **Ill-structured domains with numbers.** Law and science results are abstract-level or small N. Unblock: U14 on our own open set.
- **Pooled expertise-reversal size by assistance type.** d08 was seen only in snippets and has no worked-example split. Unblock: read the full paper.

## Order to run

1. **A05.01-U1 and U2 together (format control plus zero-shot gate).** They are cheap (one item pool, four arms), decide whether the library is worth building at all, and set the threshold every later test needs.
2. **A05.01-U3 (case after failure versus prefix).** Cheap, uses the existing S5 hook, and answers the biggest risk: copying a mismatched case at the first step.
3. **A05.01-U4 (wrong-entry dose response).** It sets the verification threshold and the break-even wrong share before any library is filled, and every other entry-form test depends on a clean store.
