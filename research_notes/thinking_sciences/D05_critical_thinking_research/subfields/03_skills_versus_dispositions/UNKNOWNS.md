## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet
- Original Stanovich and West AOT and Argument Evaluation Test effects after ability control (primary papers paywalled and never opened). Unblock: obtain the full text through a library route or open copy.
- CCTDI-CCTST correlations, factor structure and Halpern's transfer data (not opened). Unblock: read the CCTDI manual or an open review.
- Whether a persistent policy changes a human disposition more than a reminder (no controlled study found). No agent analogue until U12 runs.
- Frontier reasoning models on hard single-answer tasks under challenge (d01 to d03 use multiple-choice or true/false). Unblock: a hard held-out set with a check.
- A validated behavioural disposition score tied to human scales (none exists). Unblock: run the same probes on human participants, which we cannot do now.
- Frozen-ground-truth versions of MillStone-style open-mindedness tests (opinion issues have no truth).
- Model-name mapping of Zhou 2025 appendix scores. Unblock: read the paper's released data.
- Our own 92% vs 75% n and CI: find the original log or rerun (U1 covers it).

## Order to run
1. **U1 (code-fired vs prompt-fired doubt, with n and CI):** it either confirms G1 on numbers or shows the guardrail is over-claimed; the cheapest high-value test, and it fixes the one open [O] gap.
2. **U3 (trigger miss rate on natural errors):** the whole scaffold depends on triggers firing; if recall is low, G1 is safe but weak and we need detectors.
3. **U5 with U4 scoring (challenge resistance from input hygiene):** sycophancy is the best-replicated failure here; a two-sided score keeps us from trading flips for lost fixes.
