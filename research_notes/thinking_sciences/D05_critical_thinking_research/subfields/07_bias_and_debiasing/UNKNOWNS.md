# Bias and debiasing: what we don't know and should test

*Candidates for the schema that are not established. Nothing here goes into SCHEMA.md until its test passes. Source: DEEP.md, claims.jsonl, critique.md in this folder.*

All tests: arms at equal total tokens (setup and rewrite calls counted; the plain arm spends the saved tokens on extra samples chosen by code). Items come in pairs, cue present and cue absent, with mutated wording. At least 5 samples per item. Metric "bias delta" = accuracy (or target-option rate) on the cue-free twin minus the cued item, signed.

## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Biases that arise inside the model, not in the prompt.** Almost all LLM debiasing evidence plants the cue. We need items whose bias is natural (no author edit) and a way to measure it. Unblock: build item pairs from real data where the bias is defined by a paired neutral version.
- **Frontier 2026 reasoning models on classic bias batteries.** No paper compares their bias size with Many Labs 2. Unblock: run our own battery on our model and state size as d, to compare with median d = 0.15.
- **RLHF and reasoning RL effects.** Both Itzhak papers cover 7-11B and supervised tuning. Unblock: a base vs chat vs reasoning variant of one model family (needs access).
- **Independent replication of the human training results.** Sellier 2019, serious games and Sedlmeier 2001 have no independent-lab replication that we found. Unblock: an external lab; or a fresh literature search with full text of Lord 1984, Sanna 2002, Fischhoff 1982, Sedlmeier 2001 and Kahneman and Tversky 1996 (all unopened).
- **Origin of Sellier's "29%".** The raw cells give a 19% relative drop. Unblock: author data or a note from the authors; low priority.
- **Weights-level fixes.** Activation steering (26-32% bias cut, with capability loss on Qwen) and fine-tuning on balanced pairs cannot be tested in a scaffold-only agent. Unblock: model with open weights and a training budget.
- **Cross-family auditing.** Chain-of-Models gains need a second model family. Unblock: a decision from the project owner to allow two models.
- **Tool outputs and the agent's own history as bias sources.** Only user turns were tested. Unblock: a harness that logs history and tool text and lets us prune them.
- **Judge cost at equal tokens for swap-and-aggregate.** Unblock: the U7 run.
- **Equal-token debiasing study of any kind.** None exists. Our G7 test would be the first.

## Order to run

1. **D05.07-U3 (generic reminder backfire sweep).** Cheapest (one extra line, no setup cost), settles the strongest 4b claim on our model, and every later test needs its signed per-bias baseline.
2. **D05.07-U1 (neutral restatement on natural versus planted cues).** Highest expected gain and directly tests the one remedy with real evidence (S1 input hygiene) on the case that evidence does not cover.
3. **D05.07-U7 (judge flip rate and swap-and-aggregate vs one-call rubric).** Any ranking or critic step in the schema depends on a judge; flip rates of 11-56% in 2026 judges could cap the whole design, and the test is cheap.

Then U2 (rules first), U8 (context hygiene) and U4 (code-chosen disconfirming test), in that order.
