## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- Official Watson-Glaser, Cornell, CCTST, HCTA items: proprietary, no LLM study found. Unblock: a licence or a published item-sample set; until then use open proxies (LSAT family, LogiQA, ReClor, LogiEval).
- 2026 frontier-model numbers on AR-LSAT, LogiQA 2.0, ReClor HARD: none found. Unblock: run our own harness on current models; do not rely on aggregator pages (inconsistent, some wrong entries).
- New LSAT or Bebras items after the model cutoff: none located. Unblock: write fresh items with a generator, log the item source, or license newly released exams.
- Human reference for item difficulty: LogiEval human figures are historical passing rates, not same-sitting data. Unblock: a small human panel on the gate items.
- Verified human effect sizes (Huber and Kuncel per-year d; Abrami tables): only abstracts opened. Unblock: open the full papers to give a reference scale for scaffold gains.
- Dispositional measures (CCTDI, Need for Cognition) on LLMs: no study; LLM self-report is unstable (d17). Unblock: design with wording and option-order controls.
- LLM score on staged-evidence tasks: none. Unblock: build the probe (U6).
- Judge reliability on Ennis-Weir or CLA+ against two human raters: not found. Unblock: collect 100 human-coded open items.

## Order to run

1. **U5 (noise floor).** Cheap, needs only the plain arm, and sets the thresholds every other test depends on.
2. **U1 (scaffold vs plain on LogiEval-Hard at about 1,000 paired items).** The question the whole subfield feeds: does the schema pay for itself (G7) on an unsaturated set.
3. **U2 (solver vs CoT on constraint items).** The best-supported operator in this subfield; one clean test shows whether it survives on reasoning models.
