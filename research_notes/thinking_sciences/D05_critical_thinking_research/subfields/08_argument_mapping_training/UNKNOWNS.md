## Open questions we cannot test on our bench yet

- **Randomised, actively controlled human trial of mapping on a standardised test** (K26). Missing. We cannot run humans. It would only change how much weight the human literature gets, not the agent design.
- **Pooled effect of the 2025 and 2026 reviews** (K08). The numbers could not be read; the 2026 chapter is by a method developer. Unblock: read the full texts, remove developer and conference studies.
- **Harrell 2008 numbers** (d04). Paper blocked. Unblock: library copy.
- **Whether the human feedback effect (0.45 vs 0.22) is causal** (K06). Needs a randomised comparison; only the LLM analogue (U4) can be tested.
- **Deployed agent loops using Rationale, Carneades or Argdown** with measured benefit. None found; search was rate limited. Unblock: a second search pass with full API access.
- **Whether LLM-built maps keep their structure across seeds** (K17: large run-to-run variation, one seed per model). Needs repeated runs with a code-validated format; part of U1.
- **Effect of model size and reasoning-mode models on the map benefit.** Evidence is on small and medium models (d09). Unblock: run U1 on at least three model sizes.
- **Whether a human-facing map is worth its cost.** Needs human study; our agent does not currently output maps to people.

## Order to run

1. **D05.08-U1 (map vs plain CoT vs free outline).** It is the direct experiment no one has published, it decides whether S2 gets a map at all, and every other test depends on its answer.
2. **D05.08-U2 (gate).** Cheap to add to U1 as a third arm, and it decides whether the map is worth its tokens on easy items (G1, G7, G12).
3. **D05.08-U3 (map before vs after the answer).** Cheap, uses the same maps as U1, and tests the G10 risk that a map is only a justification written after the fact.
