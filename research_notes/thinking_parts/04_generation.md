# 04. Generation: operators, units, conditioning, sampling

Prepared 2026-10-13 for the HTSA nano loop. The layer after briefs 01-12 and 00_SYNTHESIS: how candidates are *produced* (operators, edit grammar, unit, conditioning, sampling), not ranking. Grades: **M** peer-reviewed or at least 3 seeds; **M-** controlled ablation, seeds unstated or one task; **L** single run, no ablation, self-report, or abstract only (abs). Nothing reaches H. The web-search quota ran out mid-session; later checks were direct fetches of primary papers (long pages: first ~100k characters). [n] = Sources.

## Bottom line

1. **Operators and inputs beat the controller.** With AIDE's operators, MCTS and evolution added nothing; better operators lifted MLE-bench Lite medals from ~40% to 45.5% (greedy) and 47.7% (MCTS), 20 seeds [8] (M). An i.i.d. baseline and a "3 random past successes" baseline matched ShinkaEvolve on 4/9 and 6/9 maths bounds [10] (M-); rankings flip between 1 and 40 seeds [11] (M).
2. **No idea-level operator has a controlled ablation.** Code-level sets are ablated on one task each; the co-scientist's Evolution agent gained +4.5 points, CI [-2, +11] [22] (M).
3. **Scripts should choose inputs.** GEAR's agent chose the same crossover pair in 28 of 29 crossovers (14% became elites); script-chosen pairs promoted 71-72% [15] (L). Injected instruction pools: +9.5 pass@100 on MBPP, injected roles +0.5 [33] (M-). Observation subsets turned into plans: pass@200 55.6 to 77.0 [32] (M).
4. **The unit moves up; the formulation matters most.** Intent or plan first, code second (K-Search, Arbor, PlanSearch, IFG). Reformulating the problem beat every pipeline change; no system does it unprompted [10] (M-).
5. **Feedback must assign credit, briefly.** No component-level reflection: -28.6% [28] (M); flag-to-directive text 47.3% vs 26.8% generic [29] (L); extra per-group detail never helped [27] (L).
6. **Decoding-level diversity is unreachable** (llm.py sends max_tokens and thinking only) and unproven for ideas.
7. **Local data (one real run, 5 direct builds, 2026-10-11, before the sketch stage):** 4 of 5 proposed QK-norm, 3 of 5 QK-norm plus zero-init projections; all 5 used under 0.1% of the allowed +10% parameters and at most ~20% of the 2x step-time allowance (runs\nano-fable-1). No sketch-stage run exists; every effect below is a literature analogue.

## 1. What the loop does today (read from code)

The builder returns a complete train.py (max_out 12,000; real candidates are 14-18 KB). Revise issues only fix and specialize; "recurse" is never issued. compose.py returns None on any shared-symbol conflict; the "combine build" its docstring promises is not coded. The program card sends k calls differing only in "Variation i". deepen.py offshoots are untyped, carry no TARGET/SCOPE/PART, and are filed under the source part even when "for OTHER parts".

## 2. Operators and grammars, 2023-2026

Read-across. (a) The vocabulary recurs: improve, re-parameterise, simplify, common-core and far crossover, structural leap, debug with ancestry. (b) Scoped context belongs to the operator: siblings only for new ideas, ancestry for repairs [8]. (c) At nano's scale (tens of builds per run) a per-run bandit cannot learn; keep operator counts in the ledger across runs. (d) Many 2026 reports lack seeds or component ablations [3][5][12][21][15][23].

## 3. Diff or rewrite, and the output grammar

AlphaEvolve marks EVOLVE-BLOCK regions, asks for SEARCH/REPLACE hunks, lets the model emit the whole block when better; removing any of five components, including full-file evolution, hurt (figure only) [1] (M-). Shinka and SMCEvolve mix both, LEVI rewrites by default, an autoresearch setup uses Search/Replace plus a preflight compile [3][4][6][18]; I found no 2026 diff-versus-rewrite ablation. Older: unified diffs lifted GPT-4 Turbo 20% to 61% on an 89-task set built to provoke "lazy" elision (GPT-4 family only) [38] (M-). Diff-XYZ: no format wins; search-replace suits large models for generation, not small ones or analysis [39] (M, abs). A lint guard on edits was worth +3.0 points (18.0 vs 15.0) [40] (M). JSON or XML output restrictions cost reasoning [41] (M, abs; indirect for labelled lines): keep reasoning free, structure last. Diffs buy output cost and attribution, not accuracy.

## 4. Units of generation

- **Intent before code.** K-Search searches natural-language "algorithmic intents" with LLM-edited priorities, then samples code per intent until 5-7 attempts in a row fail; kernel score 56.1 vs 26.7 for OpenEvolve, no ablation of planner or split [12] (L). Arbor: hypothesis tree, executors may not change the hypothesis; any-medal 81.8% full, 63.6% without tree, 54.5% without insight feedback (22 tasks) [13] (M-). PlanSearch: 3-6 observations, all pairs, plans (plus a second plan written after assuming the first wrong), then code; pass@1 falls and tokens per completion rise ~6x [32] (M). IFG samples a short intent hot, the answer cold [34] (M, abs).
- **Hypothesis from failures.** HypoGeniC pools examples its top hypotheses get wrong and asks for new ones; +0.7 to +8.1 points over no updating, some not significant [30] (M).
- **Experiment as unit.** MLE-STAR has the LLM write an ablation script and refines only the block that mattered; targeting is never ablated [23] (L). Kosmos: data-analysis statements 85.5% accurate, synthesis 57.9%; no world-model ablation [21] (M-).
- **Structure by LLM, constants by optimiser.** LLM-SR: dropping the skeleton-plus-optimiser split gave out-of-domain NMSE 3.8e-1 against 3.8e-5 in its main table (ablation baseline only in a figure); one problem, GPT-3.5 [31] (L).
- **Failure typing.** DeepScientist: ~1,100 ideas run, 21 beat baseline, ~60% of failed trials were implementation errors; its selector never validated [20] (L).
- **Formulation.** Reformulating the uncertainty inequality helped every method more than any pipeline change [10] (M-). AlphaEvolve asked for a search heuristic beat a construction; a human-imposed move set hurt [2] (L).

## 5. Conditioning on measurements (diagnosis to idea)

Eureka feeds per-component trajectories, not the score: removing it cost 28.6% of normalised score, and 32 first-attempt samples lost to two evolved iterations (5 runs) [28] (M). SignalClaw turns binary flags (queue above its historical 75th percentile) into fixed directive sentences: 47.3% vs 26.8% with "optimize" and 21.5% with random directions; one scenario, one run [29] (L). Against that, LLaMEA's per-group feedback never helped [27]. Reading: feedback helps when it names the component and direction against a reference; more detail does not.

## 6. Constraint-guided and counterfactual generation

I found no controlled LLM study of counterfactual or constraint prompts. Related: hints about achievable targets steered AlphaEvolve, an imposed move set hurt [2] (L); injected text helps in an inverted U, unrelated or verbatim-repeated text does nothing, task-aligned ideas peak [33] (M-); PlanSearch's assume-wrong, criticise, re-plan step is not ablated alone [32]. What demonstrably works is output-side: lint guard [40], preflight compile [18], boundary diff (have).

## 7. Sampling beyond temperature

- **Decoding level, unreachable.** Diverse Beam Search (abs) [36]; contrastive decoding (two models' logits; text quality and reasoning, not idea diversity) [37]; min-p at T=2 left 61.2% of pairs above 0.8 similarity [42]. No 2026 agent read uses them; TTT-Discover changes weights, "a few hundred dollars per problem", self-reported [24] (L).
- **Prompt level.** Verbalized sampling: 1.6-2.1x creative diversity, human poem diversity 1.90 to 2.39, quality loss on small models, probabilities not shown calibrated [35] (M); our 5-8-sketch list is its beaten "Sequence" baseline (09 #5 covers the variant). TurboEvolve asks K = 1-7 programs per call, +2 after a stagnant window; no ablation [5] (L). Instruction pools add to temperature; majority voting loses the gain [33] (M-). Hot and cold prompt pools with swaps: circle packing 2.5461 vs 2.4986 for one pool, diversity 0.78 vs 0.61 [7] (M-).
- **Self-consistency over ideas** is in the loop: n_angles is the largest positive weight in cluster_score. Voting favours the modal answer and costs diversity [33]; 79% of same-prompt pairs exceed 0.8 similarity (brief 09). Treat it as a buildability prior; test it on the ledger.

## Improvement ideas for this part

Small = hours, medium = a day. Effects are directional, not predictions; kill rules where a threshold is clear.

**G1. Observation-pair contexts (swap, do not add).** *Mechanism:* a script turns diagnose.py's statements and ledger facts into 8-10 observations, each against a reference (the long baseline run) with one hand-written directive; it gives the least-covered pair to a sketch context ("a mechanism that moves both"). Replace half of the "seeded" (random noun) and "low_prior" (comments stripped) contexts; keep the rest as control. *Effect:* input diversity by construction; TARGET assigned, not self-reported. *Cost:* small; hold the call count fixed (budget estimate ~28k tokens per web-enabled sketch call). *Measure:* new clusters per call and rung-3 survival vs control; fidelity hit rate. Kill: under 1.2x clusters or 0.5x survival. *Status:* partial (diagnosis card, TARGET, fidelity exist; pairs and directives new). Basis [32][33][29][28].

**G2. Typed offshoots (deepen.py).** *Mechanism:* replace "3 to 6 NEW IDEAS" with slots: DROP (one per ASSUMES line), STRENGTHEN, SPECIALISE (layers, token class, phase), PORT (to a named part), COMBINE (with a named checked candidate); add PART, TARGET, SCOPE and file each offshoot under its own part; one no-web REDRAFT call per interrogated idea ("assume the top pre-mortem objection is true; write the variant that survives"). *Effect:* offshoots differ by construction; objections become variants; correct boundary for other-part ideas. *Cost:* small. *Measure:* cluster novelty and rung-3 survival per slot vs untyped; rung-0 failures of other-part offshoots. *Status:* partial (slots echo brief 06's weak-graded curriculum; missing fields and mis-filing are defects). Basis [32][22][25][26].

**G3. Constraint-slack card.** *Mechanism:* a measured headroom table (params 21.24M of 23.37M, step time vs the 2x cap, VRAM vs 5.6 GB) and "propose changes that spend named headroom; say which and how much"; optional untested variant "what if C3 allowed +50%; now fit it in +10%". *Effect:* targets the slack the real run left unused. *Cost:* tiny. *Measure:* share of builds spending headroom; their learn_z vs others. Kill: survival below the formal card. *Status:* new. Basis: local data; [2][10] (L).

**G4. Premise probes (the experiment as generated unit).** *Mechanism:* for the top-k clusters one cheap call turns TEST/TARGET into probe requests from a menu (diagnose.py's CELLS plus parameters, e.g. attention mass beyond a window); one instrumented 100-step baseline run answers all of them; results join knowledge_for(); a falsified premise demotes the cluster (10% audited). Interrogation may emit PROBE lines for model-specific questions now answered "unknown". *Effect:* facts replace guesses; false premises die before the ladder. *Cost:* medium. *Measure:* share of open questions answered by measurement; falsification rate; rung-3 survival of probed vs unprobed matched clusters. *Status:* new (the fidelity probe is post-build). Basis [23][9][21] (M-/L).

**G5. Patch-grammar builder with duplicate instantiation.** *Mechanism:* the builder returns per-symbol SEARCH/REPLACE hunks (small applier, whitespace-normalised match, whole-symbol fallback, one retry echoing the error, edit and original snippet [40]); changed symbols known by construction; revise and compose work on hunks; top-lane sketches get two independent builds (both fail: idea failure; one fails: implementation failure). *Effect:* ~5x fewer output tokens (inferred from 14-18 KB files), fewer silent out-of-boundary edits, hunk-level composition; no accuracy gain claimed. *Cost:* medium. *Measure:* tokens per candidate, rung-0/1 pass rate, apply failures, paired learn_z on 10 sketches built both ways. *Status:* partial (recommended in brief 04, not built). Basis [1][3][4][38][39][40][20][12].

**G6. Typed post-build operators.** *Mechanism:* FIX (ancestry), SPECIALISE, RETUNE (script sweeps the diff's own new constants, at most 6 rung-3 runs, then a fresh-seed verdict), SIMPLIFY (smallest patch that keeps the gain), CROSS-CORE/CROSS-FAR (E2/E1 on two checked parents of one part), COMBINE (the merge compose.py cannot do, given both diffs and gains), SHIFT (strong model sees one winner per cluster, must be structurally new). Script picks partners by complementarity; counts go to the ledger; fixed mix until ~20 trials per operator, then Beta weights. *Effect:* credit per operator; composites beyond disjoint symbols. *Cost:* medium (RETUNE costs GPU). *Measure:* P(child beats parent beyond noise) per operator, tokens per improvement. *Status:* partial (fix, specialise, compose exist; combine promised, recurse unused). Basis [8][25][26][4][15][31] (M-/L).

**G7. Program search rebuilt (program card).** *Mechanism:* one call returns K = 4 distinct versions with stated probabilities instead of 4 i.i.d. "Variation i" calls; symbol-only output; context = 3 random earlier successes with z, not the top 2; the LLM writes structure with named free parameters and a script fits them; a random-search arm on the same family. *Effect:* distinct versions, cheaper calls, structure and constants credited apart. *Cost:* small-medium. *Measure:* effective clusters among the 4; best z per GPU-minute vs random. *Status:* partial (arm exists). Basis [35][5][10][31][2].

**G8. Coverage-driven control.** *Mechanism:* per part, a Good-Turing unseen-mass estimate (singleton clusters over sketches) and exp(H) effective clusters [14] from discovery.json; next contexts go where unseen mass is highest, a part stops below ~10%; sketches per call adapt (+2 after a stagnant window [5]); after 3 builds in one edit family force another [16][17][18] (a generated tabu mechanism helped at n = 3 [19]); log n_angles against survival, cap its weight if it predicts nothing. *Effect:* stops buying copies of the mode. *Cost:* small. *Measure:* new clusters per 10k tokens; n_angles vs survival at ~100 builds. *Status:* partial (discovery curve, retire_cards exist). The estimator is classical, unvalidated for LLM ideas (inference); counts depend on the clusterer (brief 09, S7).

**Already in the loop (have):** sketch-then-build (the intent-then-code split of [12][32][34]), independent per-part contexts, port-and-adapt card, typed analogy mapping, contradiction card, staged and exclusion arms, immediate fix, mechanical splice, novelty gate with audit.
