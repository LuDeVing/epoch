# 06. Elaboration and reasoning: working an idea through

*2026-10-14. Brief for part 6 of 00_DECOMPOSITION.md. Sources 2022–2026, newest first within each section. Grades: **strong** = replicated by independent groups or a meta-analysis; **medium** = one controlled study (peer-reviewed, or several models and benchmarks) or a public leaderboard; **weak** = one preprint or workshop paper, position paper, vendor or press claim, small n. Most papers were read at abstract level, so figures are headline numbers. The search quota ran out mid-session; I then fetched primary pages directly.*

## 1. What the loop does today

`nano/deepen.py`: per selected idea a root call asks "at least 6" self-chosen questions with web access and returns KNOWN, ASSUMES, NEW IDEAS, OPEN; up to two child calls follow one build-relevant open question each; a pre-mortem call (by default fed the interrogation's own facts) adds objections. The builder gets at most 4,000 characters of facts and Q&A plus the objections (`knowledge_for`), not the measured diagnostics table, which only the root call sees. It writes the complete file in one call (up to 12,000 tokens) with no plan, no self-check and no effort control. Rungs 0–3 verify. A rung 0/1 failure gets one repair whose prompt holds the parent script, a one-line hypothesis and a JSON failure record, not the facts. Predictions are regexed after the build (`predict.py`) and the TARGET cell probed.

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 Reasoning models and test-time compute
- **Step-level checks pay where an oracle checks the step.** 2026 work gains when symbolic or algorithmic oracles give turn-level reward, bounded by oracle quality [S1 weak]; outcome-only signals grow uninformative as horizons lengthen [S2 weak]. R1's authors dropped process reward models (undefinable steps, unreliable labels, reward hacking) and tree search [S3 medium]; Monte-Carlo step labels are noisy [S4 medium]; step supervision beat outcome supervision on MATH with 800k human labels [S5 medium]. Our rung ladder is the oracle kind.
- **RL on checkable rewards sharpened sampling more than it added capability.** R1-Zero AIME pass@1 15.6% to 71.0% [S3 strong]; at large k base models match or beat RL models, only distillation added new patterns [S6 medium; contested, S7 weak]; "aha" behaviour pre-exists in base models and GRPO inflates wrong-answer length [S8 medium].
- **Thinking helps unevenly.** Same model with and without thinking on Aider's code-editing set: +4.5 (Claude 3.7), +4.9 (Sonnet 4), +1.3 (Opus 4), GPT-5 low to high +6.7, at 1.0–7.5x cost across all rows [S9 medium]; a vendor reports medium effort matching its predecessor's best SWE-bench score with 76% fewer output tokens [S10 weak]. Against: longer reasoning lowers accuracy with distractors [S11 medium]; reasoning models run on when a premise is missing [S12 medium]; explicit step-by-step prompts add little for 20–80% more time [S13 medium]; CoT gains concentrate in math and symbolic work [S14 strong]; compute is best set by difficulty [S15 medium].
- **Reasoning text is an unreliable trace.** Hints that decided an answer were verbalised in 25% (Claude 3.7) and 39% (R1) of cases [S16 medium]. o3 hallucinated on 33% of PersonQA against 16% for o1 [S17 weak, press report]; the training recipe matters [S18 weak]. The "illusion of thinking" collapse [S19 medium] is disputed as mostly token limits and unsolvable instances [S20 weak].

### 2.2 Mechanism modelling, calculation, prediction
- An interpreter doing the arithmetic beat CoT by 15 points on GSM8K [S21 medium]; CoT's gain is mostly symbolic execution and a solver beats it [S14 strong].
- Models do not execute code in their heads: best CRUXEval output prediction was 81% on 3–13 line functions [S22 medium]; a code world model trained on execution traces reports only early results [S23 weak].
- Direction is easier than size. An LLM predicted the signs of experimental effects, not their magnitudes, and simulation told it what questions could not [S24 weak]. Stated 99% intervals on Fermi questions covered the truth 65% of the time; conformal widening fixed it [S25 weak].
- Ranking ideas from text needs outcome data: off-the-shelf frontier models were at chance on pairs of research ideas, a model tuned on 6,000 pairs reached 77% [S26 medium]; tuned 8B models beat GPT-5, 77.1% vs 61.1% [S27 weak]. Sequential falsification of measurable sub-claims, with e-values, controlled false positives and matched scientists about 10x faster [S28 medium].

### 2.3 Self-questioning
- Follow-up questions help multi-hop QA and the compositionality gap does not close with scale [S29 medium]; recursive Socratic questioning beat CoT and tree-of-thought on MMLU, MATH, LogiQA [S30 medium]; a principle question first ("step-back") lifted PaLM-2L physics and chemistry by 7 and 11 points [S31 medium]; choosing questions by expected information gain raised success 38.1% on average [S32 medium].
- Models pick the wrong question: 40–50% on logic and planning clarification [S33 medium]. A model attending to its own output repeats its errors; verification questions answered independently of the draft cut hallucination on lists, QA and long-form text (Llama-65B era) [S34 medium].
- Procedure can hurt where context helps: TDD instructions raised regressions from 6.08% to 9.94%, a test-dependency map cut them to 1.82% [S35 weak].

### 2.4 Decomposing builds, verifier in the loop
- Plan-then-implement, test-based flows and execution feedback helped older models: +25.4% relative Pass@1 [S36], 19% to 44% pass@5 [S37], up to 12% [S38]; Agentless solved 32.0% of SWE-bench Lite [S39]; AIDE's tree search over executed solutions led MLE-bench [S40] (medium each).
- Repair is limited by feedback quality [S41 medium]. Builder-written tests changed process and cost, not outcomes (six models) [S42 medium]; model-written tests used to select among candidates helped (57.4% SWE-bench Verified) [S43 medium].
- Edit format: unified diffs cut "lazy" elision from 12 to 4 of 89 tasks (score 20% to 61%, GPT-4 Turbo, 2023) [S44 medium]. An Architect/Editor split lifted o1-preview 79.7 to 85.0 in 2024 [S45 medium], but o3-high with an editor scored 78.2 against 81.3 alone in 2025 [S9 data]. Viewer/editor separation: +2.1 points, −17.9% cost [S46 weak].
- Research-code agents: 21.0% replication on PaperBench [S47 medium], 0.5% complete experiments on EXP-Bench [S48 medium]; agents declare success despite failures [S49 weak]; fidelity fell when the spec arrived gradually, single-shot winning on 16 of 20 and 14 of 20 papers [S50 weak].

### 2.5 Limits
- Without outside feedback self-correction fails or hurts [S51, S52 strong]; finding the error is the bottleneck, correcting at a given location works [S53 medium]; showing the same wrong claim as external raised explicit correction by 23–93 points [S54 weak]; LLMs generate, external critics verify [S55 weak, position].
- Chains degrade: models err more when their earlier errors are in context [S56 medium]; multi-turn runs lose 39% on average through early assumptions [S57 medium].
- Citations are not support: 74.5% of citations supported their sentence, 51.5% of sentences were fully supported (2023 engines) [S58 medium]; benchmarks reward guessing [S59 medium].

## 3. Reading for this loop

**Have (keep):** fresh-context critique; a one-shot spec to the builder; the ladder as process reward; facts framed as context; "unknown" allowed; no ranking from elaboration [S26]. **Weak:** elaboration yields text nothing checks; model-stated numbers and URLs are trusted; repair drops the spec; the whole file is re-emitted; whether interrogation helps builds is unmeasured, and the evidence on stepwise prompting predicts a small effect [S13, S14, S35, S42].

## 4. Improvement ideas for this part

**1. Mechanism chain with falsifiers, not a free question list** (`DEEPEN_FORMAT`, `root_prompt`, `parse`). First 3–6 links `cause -> effect | quantity | baseline value (from the measured table, else "unmeasured") | direction and ordinal band | what would break it`; link 1 is the premise, the last the loss effect. Then at most 8 questions aimed at the weakest link. Builder and pre-mortem receive the chain; objections cite link ids. Directions and bands only, no point estimates [S24, S25]. Why: S28–S32, S35. Effect: checkable elaboration (feeds 2, 7); no gain alone. Cost: no extra call, about 60 lines. Measure: parse rate; sign and band hit rate and 80%-band coverage per card via `predict.py`, widened conformally. **Partial** (TARGET and PREDICTION exist; no links, no falsifiers).

**2. Check premises and links by measurement** (`_call_node`, `diagnose.py` cell readers). A script copies each link's baseline cell value into the chain; for a premise outside the panel one cheap call writes a probe of at most 40 lines on the same hooks and the launcher runs it 100 steps. A refuted premise demotes the idea to the long-shot lane; a confirmed one reaches the builder as a computed fact. Why: models miss false premises [S12], cannot run code mentally [S22], know more than they say [S24]; offload computation [S21, S14]. Effect: fewer builds on false premises. Cost: at most one cheap call and 1–2 GPU minutes per idea. Measure: share refuted; rung 3 pass by premise status, then probe vs no probe at equal builds; known winners must stay "confirmed". **Partial** (the table reaches only the root call; the fidelity probe is post-build).

**3. Typed provenance and a fresh-context fact check** (`parse`, `URL_RE` rule). Tag KNOWN lines `[script]`, `[computed]`, `[web URL]`, `[belief]`. For ideas chosen to build, one cheap call sees only up to 6 numeric `[web]` claims and the fetched page and returns SUPPORTED, NOT SUPPORTED or UNCLEAR with a quote. Builder and child prompts get verified facts; the rest travels as beliefs. Why: S58, S34, S54, S56–S57, S51–S52. Effect: unsupported numbers stop setting hyperparameters (size unknown). Cost: 5–10k tokens per built idea, about 6 per round. Measure: NOT SUPPORTED rate; rung 1–2 failures and prediction checks, verified-only vs all facts (idea 9). **Partial** (a number passes if any URL is present).

**4. Fork test, question budget, stop rule** (`follow`, prompts). Each question names the two build choices its answer decides between and is skipped if both give the same code; `follow` ranks by weakest link, then fork size; fan-out 2 only for independent forks; stop when a child adds no verified or computed fact or changes no link status. Why: S32, S33, S15; extra reasoning rarely pays [S11–S13]; chains drift [S56, S57]. Effect: 20–40% fewer interrogation tokens at equal rung-pass (estimate from S13). Cost: negative, about 40 lines. Measure: tokens per idea; fact yield (identifiers from KNOWN or computed lines present in the diff); rung-pass and `learn_z`, depth-2 vs adaptive, paired by idea. **Partial** (build=yes flag; fixed depth and fan-out).

**5. Plan plus symbol patches instead of the whole file** (`OUTPUT_FORMAT`, `run_card`; `boundary.symbols`, `compose`). The builder writes PLAN (symbols touched, new parameters and expected delta, init rule, expected step ratio, chain link per symbol), then full replacement text per changed or new symbol, `<module>` and Config included. A splicer on the `compose` AST code applies it to the baseline; apply failure falls back to the whole file. Why: S44, S46, S36; one call, one model (the two-model split stopped paying, S9, S45); keep formats natural [S60]. Effect: protected-symbol violations impossible by construction; 50–70% fewer builder output tokens (estimate); PLAN deltas become free `predict.py` checks. Cost: about 120 lines, no extra call. Measure: same 12 specs, whole file vs patch: rung 0/1 failures, output tokens, PLAN-vs-diff agreement, apply failures, `learn_z`. **New**; run as an arm, the evidence is mixed.

**6. Repairs restate the whole spec** (`spec_prompt("revise")`, `_immediate_fix`). Add chain, verified facts, objections and the targeted cell; replace the 3,000-character JSON dump by failing check, first traceback lines, the offending symbol and the link it should implement; one fresh single-shot call. Why: S50, S57, S41, S53, S35. Effect: higher fix rate, fewer repeat failures. Cost: no extra call, about 30 lines. Measure: fix success per rung, tokens per fix, repeat-failure rate. **Partial**.

**7. Link-to-lines audit before the GPU** (new `_audit`). Scripts first: each new Parameter read in forward and reached by a gradient, identity at init when gated, PLAN vs diff symbols and parameter delta (cf. synthesis 3). Then a cheap call sees only the chain and the unified diff and answers per link IMPLEMENTED at symbol:lines, MISSING or CONTRADICTED, plus UNEXPLAINED HUNKS; MISSING goes back to the builder once. Why: locating is the hard part [S53]; outside framing [S54]; feedback quality [S41]; external critics [S55]; drift [S49, S50]; builder tests do not move outcomes [S42]. Effect: unknown; catches no-op or partial builds before rung 3. Cost: 4–8k tokens per build, about 60k per round. Measure: recall on seeded defects (path dropped, gate zeroed, wrong init, unused parameter), false flags on good builds, agreement with fidelity failures. **New**.

**8. Set effort per role** (`harness/llm.py _call_cli`). Add an `effort` argument that sets `CLAUDE_CODE_EFFORT_LEVEL` or `--effort` [S61]: high or xhigh for implement, revise, audit; low or medium for interrogation answers, clustering, research; 1–3 sentence answers; no "think step by step" in builder prompts. Why: S9, S10; stepwise text is redundant and can hurt [S11, S13, S14]; S15. Effect: higher rung-pass on hard specs at 1.5–3x builder cost (estimate). Cost: about 6–10 builds per round. Measure: same specs at low, medium, high, xhigh: rung 0/1 failures, `learn_z`, tokens; pick the knee. **New** (`thinking_budget` exists for the API backend and is never passed).

**9. Pay-for-itself ablation** (`run_card`, flag `--ablate-knowledge p`). For a third of selected clusters also build from the plain brief (no KNOWN, chain or objections), deduplicated by source hash; compare pairs on rung reached, `learn_z`, prediction checks, tokens, over three replicates. Why: untested here; S13, S14, S35, S42 predict a small effect. Effect: shows whether 4 interrogation calls per idea beat the same tokens spent on more builds (synthesis C4, C8). Cost: one extra build (about 15k tokens, 2 GPU minutes) per ablated cluster. Measure: paired difference with CI over 24+ pairs. **New**.

**Not worth doing:** LLM plausibility scores [S26, S27]; builder-written test suites [S42]; TDD or stepwise-thinking instructions [S35, S13]; a two-model Architect/Editor chain [S9]; multi-turn stepwise builds [S50, S57]; same-context self-review [S51–S54]. **Hand-offs:** the pre-mortem pack defaults to the interrogation's own facts (S34, S56) → part 7; confidence tags → part 11; which questions led to wins → part 10.
