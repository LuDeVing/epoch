| STA-04 | Wakeful drift from criticality, reset by sleep | Distance to criticality grows with time awake and sleep restores it. | STA-05 | What fills the offline interval matters: quiet rest vs demanding task | 15 minutes of eyes-closed rest after encoding improved declarative and procedural memory compared with a distractor task, in some studies for a week or more; internally focused autobiographical thinking blocked the benefit. | STA-06 | Global relaxation of priors flattens state-transition barriers | Under LSD (n=15) and psilocybin (n=9), the control energy needed to move between every pair of brain states fell, and 5-HT2A-weighted inputs did this most efficiently. | STA-07 | Affective valence shapes processing mode | Higher positive mood predicted more problems solved, and more solved by insight (N=79), mediated by anterior cingulate activity during preparation; positive mood also strengthened Aha ratings. (2) Failure-heavy context acts like negative affect, so the fresh generator spawned by /incubate gets no failed ideas, impasse text or negative evaluator comments, only the restructured problem.md and serendipity candidates (extends P8). | STA-08 | Breathing phase alternates fast-lenient and accurate modes | In N=41, responses were about 19-21 ms faster during inspiration, which lowered decision boundaries and biased faces toward 'happy'; expiration gave about 2 points more accuracy. | STA-09 | Weak theme cue during a low-constraint offline phase | Playing a theme cue ('tree') at sleep onset (N=49) raised creativity and semantic distance compared with wake. | STA-10 | Quiet wake beats both action and deep sleep | A temporal renormalization-group analysis of 13 mouse V1 recordings placed quiet wakefulness closest to criticality (d2 about 0.02, vs 0.10 in NREM sleep). | STA-11 | Executive control modulates retrieval entropy | Entropy modulation theory: retrieval candidates compete to reach a threshold, and executive control that raises both the strength and the variance of activation increases retrieval entropy. | STA-12 | Offline generative-model optimisation: widen, compress, corrupt | When stimuli are weak or absent, spontaneous activity during rest and dreaming may optimise generative models by maximising the entropy of explanations and minimising model complexity (Pezzulo et al. 2021). | STA-13 | Brief offline phase aids hidden-rule insight, stage disputed | In Lacaux 2021 (N=103), at least 15 s of N1 during a 20-minute break raised hidden-rule discovery to 83%, vs about 30% for those who stayed awake and 14% for those who reached N2; hypnagogic imagery content did not predict insight. Run a short, problem-decoupled /wander (constraint level 0, high seed distance, capped at 5-8 steps), then have a fresh subagent with clear logic inspect the outputs for a usable rule, the 'semilucid' pattern of watching the wandering while keeping the logic to recognise a spark. Because imagery content did not predict insight, do not assume serendipity candidates are the active channel. For unsolved problems, also schedule a delayed retry: after /consolidate and /incubate run while other tasks proceed, a fresh agent re-attempts with only problem.md and semantic memory. | STA-14 | Near-critical, edge-of-chaos operating point | The awake cortex sits slightly below criticality rather than at it (effective coupling about 0.88 in human fMRI, N=136; spiking branching ratio 0.97-0.99). A meta-analysis of 140 avalanche datasets argues the functional benefits hold near criticality, while sitting exactly at criticality is unstable. In humans, rats and mice, transfer of slow thalamocortical context into fast gamma content peaked at a largest Lyapunov exponent of about 0, and both chaos (propofol) and periodicity (seizures) reduced it. | contested | harness; wander | Calibrate the default looseness with a two-sided failure check. Track per run the periodic failure, mean pairwise embedding similarity in ideas.jsonl (near-duplicates), and the chaotic failure, integration-failure rate in evaluations.jsonl (ideas that ignore the constraints). The harness sweeps /wander and /forage constraint levels 0-3, plots rubric score and both failure metrics, and picks the level that minimises their sum. Expect the peak one step short of maximum looseness, e.g. level 1 for normal generation with 0 kept for short incubation bursts. Write the calibrated default into state.json. |
| STA-15 | Dopamine-like exploration shows inverted-U for divergence | Of 29 dopamine studies, 6 on divergent tasks found better performance with higher dopamine, 1 worse and 3 null. Eye-blink rate predicted flexibility in an inverted-U (N=117), and a high blink rate impaired convergent thinking. The link may reflect motivation rather than creativity. | contested | wander | Treat /wander constraint level and /forage seed distance as an exploration knob with an expected inverted-U, not something to maximise. Separately check that high-exploration settings lower convergent (puzzle) accuracy, the predicted trade-off. |
| STA-16 | Broadened states raise originality, not accuracy | Psilocybin acutely raised spontaneous creative insights but lowered deliberate creativity (Mason 2021). | STA-17 | Perceived volatility sets the belief revision rate | Higher estimated volatility raises the precision weight on prediction errors and speeds belief updating. Sulpiride did so in N=76 men, depending on genotype. | STA-18 | ANTI: approach motivation reliably narrows attention | In a registered conceptual replication of Gable & Harmon-Jones 2008, participants rated dessert images as more positive and more motivating than rocks, yet attentional breadth did not differ by image type or by participants' valence or intensity ratings. | contested | evaluate | Do not build a 'focus mode' that adds desire or reward framing to prompts to narrow convergent phases. ### Sources and caveats

- **STA-01**: Key sources: PLOS ONE 2025 walking meta-analysis, https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0347878; Oppezzo & Schwartz 2014, https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/xlm-a0036577.pdf. Caveats: Samples were mostly students, and certainty is moderate. The mechanism (arousal, loosened control) is not established. The step from walking to prompt constraint level is pure analogy. Verdict upstream: PLAUSIBLE. Merged from: Low-control activity boosts divergent, not convergent [modulators_and_practical_levers].
- **STA-02**: Key sources: NBR 2025 stress meta-analysis, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0149763425001137; Beversdorf 2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12466433/; Cognitive Research 2022, https://cognitiveresearchjournal.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s41235-022-00447-w. Caveats: The effect is small, and the moderator results are paywalled. The noradrenaline evidence comes mostly from convergent tasks (only 2 divergent studies). The valence x arousal interaction rests on one correlational study. Whether LLMs have anything like arousal is speculative, so this is a cheap hypothesis to test, not a known effect. Merged from: Stress and arousal impair creative solving [modulators_and_practical_levers]; Positive affect broadens only under low arousal [emotion_body_and_curiosity].
- **STA-03**: Key sources: Nieuwenstein et al. 2015, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/on-making-the-right-choice-a-metaanalysis-and-largescale-replication-attempt-of-the-unconscious-thought-advantage/A31405A97BC221C10153E9CF78A94DB6; Vadillo, Kostopoulou & Shanks 2015, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4436803/; Dijksterhuis et al. 2006, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1121629. Caveats: The null result concerns multi-attribute choice, not insight problems; the notes explain incubation by forgetting of fixation instead. Proponents' replies were not retrieved. Arguably a control finding about incubation design, kept here because it was tagged state. Merged from: ANTI: Unconscious thought advantage for decisions [consciousness_and_global_workspace].
- **STA-04**: Key sources: Xu et al. 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-023-01536-9; Meisel et al., https://cris.iucc.ac.il/en/publications/fading-signatures-of-critical-brain-dynamics-during-sustained-wak/; Scarpetta et al. 2023, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10520337/. Caveats: Evidence comes from rat V1 and human EEG. Equating LLM context accumulation with synaptic drift is untested. A fresh context resets everything, while sleep restores selectively. Merged from: Wakeful drift from criticality, reset by sleep [brain_dynamics_and_neuromodulation].
- **STA-05**: Key sources: Wamsley 2019, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7024394; Siegel et al. 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07624-5; Mediano et al. 2024, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10853937. Caveats: The rest finding is a neural memory-consolidation mechanism (arguably memory-tagged), and LLMs lack it. The autobiographical-interference result has a narrow evidence base. The drug-state studies have n=6 and n=17 and measure brain state, not idea quality. Two related findings were merged because both concern what fills the interval. Merged from: Quiet rest consolidates within minutes [sleep_dreams_and_replay]; External task demand re-stabilizes a loosened state [brain_dynamics_and_neuromodulation].
- **STA-06**: Key sources: Singleton et al. 2022, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-33578-1; Ruffini et al. 2023, https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010811; Siegel et al. 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07624-5; Carhart-Harris & Friston 2019, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6588209/. Caveats: Samples are small, and model choice matters: Ruffini models LSD as moving away from criticality, Toker as moving toward it. The replicated part is the neural state change. The creative relevance (REBUS) is untested theory, and its authors flag it as speculative. Annealing is already standard in search, so any gain may be generic rather than brain-specific. Merged from: Serotonergic agonism lowers all state-transition barriers [brain_dynamics_and_neuromodulation]; Relaxed high-level priors enable belief revision [brain_dynamics_and_neuromodulation].
- **STA-07**: Key sources: Subramaniam 2009, https://researchdiscovery.drexel.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/A-Brain-Mechanism-for-Facilitation-of/991014878237204721; Shen 2019, https://openaccess.leidenuniv.nl/bitstream/handle/1887/77956/Shen_et_al_2019_T.pdf?sequence=1; Huntsinger 2014, https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037669; Rosa 2023, https://doi.org/10.21500/20112084.6497. Caveats: The key study dates from 2009, with no 2024-2026 peer-reviewed mood-induction study, and samples are small.
