## measurement

| ID | Mechanism | Claim | Evidence | Maps to | Agent translation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MSR-01 | ANTI: Divergent-thinking or raw semantic-distance scores as headline metric | Divergent thinking shares only about 3% of variance with real creative achievement (70 studies, N=14,901). A 2026 second-order meta-analysis gives an effect of .14 for divergent thinking against .27 for creative-process measures. Four common DT tasks do not measure the same construct, and DT tests have predicted creativity no better than intelligence measures. LLMs also saturate distance metrics: with greedy decoding GPT-4 beat 96% of humans on the Divergent Association Task. Raw distance is therefore necessary but not sufficient for creative thought. | meta-analysis | harness | The harness's primary outcomes are verified solve rate on MacGyver and BRAINTEASER, plus blinded rubric scores on the 30 open-ended design problems, with checks that ideas actually work (simulation, counterexample search). Embedding novelty is only a secondary diagnostic. Report it only for answers that pass the rubric (the 'conditional DAT' approach), and report its correlation with solve rate across runs to see whether novelty tracks success for this system at all. An ablation that raises distance without raising rubric score or solve rate counts as a failure. |
| MSR-02 | ANTI: Trusting unadjusted, weakly controlled or single-run gains | Creativity training (k=169, 844 effect sizes) shows 0.53 SD unadjusted, which falls to about 0.29-0.32 SD after correcting for strong publication bias, and method flaws are widespread. In organizations the effect drops from g=0.86 immediately to g=0.40 delayed, and on-the-job transfer is g=0.34 (not significant). Effects also shrink as design quality rises in chess, music and working-memory training. Predicting individual creativity from brain data plateaus near r=0.3-0.4: an EEG CPM result (r=0.36) survived leave-one-out but not 5- or 10-fold cross-validation. Corcoran, Hohwy & Friston propose comparing rival models by Bayesian accumulation of evidence instead of one-shot falsification. | meta-analysis | harness | Assume HTSA gains are small and inflated until shown otherwise. (1) Before any tuning, preregister conditions, metrics and the keep/drop rule for skills in harness/plan.md. (2) Keep a held-out problem set that is used once, only after switch budget and distance thresholds are tuned, plus a transfer set from a different problem family. (3) Use active controls: the same token budget and the same number of plain-prompt subagents with their outputs merged, not just single-pass chain-of-thought. (4) Run 5-10 or more seeds per problem, compare paired against the baseline on the same problems, bootstrap confidence intervals, and report every run, failures included. (5) Treat baseline, full HTSA and each ablation as competing models. Fit a beta-binomial model for solve rate and a hierarchical normal model for rubric scores, report the posterior probability that each skill helps, and accumulate evidence across benchmark runs instead of deciding keep/drop from a single run. |
| MSR-03 | LLM help raises quality, shrinks collective diversity | Generative AI raises individual novelty and quality (+8% novel, +9% useful with five GPT-4 ideas, and the least creative writers gain most), but outputs become more alike: similarity between stories rose 10.7%. A meta-analysis of 19 experiments confirms the homogenization, which is strongest in constrained tasks. | meta-analysis | harness | The harness reports two separate numbers, computed by a python script from runs/*/ideas.jsonl: per-answer quality (rubric score) and population diversity (mean pairwise embedding distance across 10 runs and across conditions). A skill change counts as an improvement only if quality rises and diversity does not fall. Also flag idea sets in which many ideas share key concept tokens, as in the 'Build-a-Breeze Castle' case. See MSR-14 for measuring diversity with several metrics. |
| MSR-04 | ANTI: Confidence, self-rating or visible effort as evidence of quality | In an LSD microdosing RCT (N=80, 14 doses), participants felt more creative, but AUT, RAT, CAT and EPSQ scores did not change. Controlled microdosing studies broadly show null objective effects alongside substantial expectancy effects. Across 13 studies, deliberative reasoners were rated better than intuitive ones at equal accuracy (eta_p^2=0.59), and ChatGPT 3.5/4 reproduced the bias strongly (d=1.44-4.77). In 2,806 radiology decisions, confidence was calibrated on easy cases but more than 40% overconfident on hard ones, and longer deliberation predicted more errors. | replicated | harness | Never score novelty or quality with the generating agent's own judgment, or with any agent that knows which condition produced an idea. In /evaluate, a fresh subagent judges shuffled ideas without condition labels. It sees only the final idea or answer, normalised to a fixed length, never the trajectory or reasoning trace. Report score per token. As a control check, the same answer with a long and a short justification must get the same score. Log self-reported and evaluator confidence only to study the gap between confidence and correctness: report calibration per difficulty bin (bins set by baseline solve rate) next to insight.log. /think treats long runs (many switches used) as a sign of inflated confidence and sends their candidates to /insight-check with stricter verification. |
| MSR-05 | Human advantage lies in the upper tail | GPT-4-class models match or beat average humans on AUT, Consequences and the DAT (Hubert 2024, N=151). Humans, however, show greater variance and a higher ceiling, and the top humans outscore every model (Huang 2025, about 10k humans; Bellemare-Pepin, about 100k humans). | replicated | harness | The harness reports the tail as well as the mean. Run baseline and HTSA k times per problem and compare best-of-k, the 90th-percentile judge score, and the share of runs that clear a fixed high bar. HTSA aims to push the tail. A higher mean with an unchanged maximum points to polishing, not novelty. |
| MSR-06 | Semantic distance along a thought trajectory indexes exploration | Forward flow is the mean distance of each unit in a chain of associations to all earlier units. It predicted divergent thinking (beta=.48) independently of intelligence (beta=.36), Study 2 replicated this, and later samples gave smaller correlations with AUT (r=.26 and .37). Across 482,760 Wikipedia readers it correlated rho=0.79 with information diversity and -0.05 with network tightness. DAT distance (N=8,914) correlated with AUT about as strongly as DT tasks correlate with each other. In the standard pipeline, consecutive cosine distance between embedded thought units tracked self-rated freedom of thought (r=0.13) and was higher off-task than on-task (0.730 vs 0.696). Jump profiles classify search as persistent or flexible: humans use both, while LLMs lock into one pathway per task, and the more flexible LLMs score higher. | replicated | harness; wander; forage | Build harness/trajectory_metrics.py and run it on every /wander and /forage output (trajectory.md or ideas.jsonl). It splits the text into units, embeds them in generation order, and outputs: mean consecutive distance, forward flow, jump rate (consecutive similarity below a threshold or percentile cut), mean cluster dwell, and post-jump concreteness. Report these against a shuffled or random-walk baseline and log them in state.json next to solve rate and rubric score. For /forage, check that its subagents show both persistent and flexible profiles; if all converge to one, the mode instructions are not working. Use these metrics to calibrate the /wander constraint knob and to let /think reseed a forager with low forward flow. They stay diagnostics, never targets to maximise (see MSR-01), until they correlate with rubric originality. |
| MSR-07 | Correct final answers usually already intuitive | Two-response studies across logic, moral dilemmas, economic games, remote associates and Moses illusions show that most correct final answers were already given at the fast stage. Over 70% of post-deliberation utilitarian moral answers were given initially. Fluid-intelligence matrices are the exception, where deliberation corrects substantially. | replicated | harness | For each problem, the harness records two responses: a first answer under a deadline (small token budget, no tools) and the final HTSA answer. Report the transition matrix (wrong to right, right to wrong, unchanged) per benchmark and per ablation. If HTSA rarely turns wrong answers into right ones on a benchmark, the loop is mostly confirming answers there and its cost is not justified. |
| MSR-08 | ANTI: A single insight flag or score as proxy for restructuring | Restructuring and the Aha experience often coincide, but not always (Wiley & Danek 2024), and an Aha can accompany wrong solutions (Danek: 70 participants, 37 magic tricks). The dimensions of the Aha also dissociate. Surprise tracks metacognitive prediction error. Positive emotion uniquely predicts nucleus accumbens activity (N=32). Certainty most strongly predicts anterior hippocampus (t=6.77) and amygdala activity (N=31). In eye tracking, only non-linear models and change-point analysis captured abrupt restructuring that ANOVA missed, though restructuring is not always sudden. | replicated | insight-check; harness | insight.log keeps separate fields: representational change (embedding distance between problem.md versions, or a /restructure event), surprise (prediction error against a prediction stored in state.json) and certainty (evaluator confidence). Pleasure has no LLM analogue and is left out. In the harness, embed every trajectory.md step and every problem.md version, run change-point detection on that series (python, e.g. ruptures) to find restructuring events, and report whether solved runs show a change point before the solution. Do not average across steps, and analyse hinted and unhinted runs separately. Report how often the fields co-occur and test which one predicts verified correctness and later reuse from memory. An insight flag never counts as evidence of reframing, and reframing never counts as evidence of insight. |
| MSR-09 | Temporal irreversibility marks engaged wakeful dynamics | Classifiers told forward from time-reversed ECoG epochs during wakefulness (AUC above 0.75 for all 81 models). Performance degraded in deep sleep and fell to chance for phase-based models under ketamine. Rat LFP and human fMRI show that irreversibility is highest in wakefulness and higher during tasks than at rest. | replicated | harness | A harness metric for whether a thought trajectory is directed or a random walk. Embed each trajectory.md step or each ordered idea list, train a simple python classifier across runs to tell forward from reversed sequences, and report AUC. An AUC near 0.5 means order carries no information (aimless drift). Calibrate against a null of baseline outputs, since text can be trivially irreversible from discourse cues, and track AUC next to solve rate to see whether more directed trajectories predict better outcomes. |
| MSR-10 | Fluency and originality dissociate; report creativity dispositions separately | Direct cortical stimulation of DMN nodes in 9 epilepsy patients lowered AUT originality measured as semantic distance (median 0.97 to 0.94, P<0.001) and left fluency and flexibility unchanged. Earlier work by the group reports that lateral DMN stimulation cut idea count and medial stimulation cut originality. Hills & Bird define creativity without value as linked dispositions: fertility, originality, imagination (representational manipulation rather than copying) and motivation (carrying ideas through), with value judged separately. | single-study | harness | For every run and ablation, the harness reports four separate scores beside solve rate. Fertility: count of distinct ideas after embedding dedupe (cosine above a threshold counts as a duplicate). Originality: mean embedding distance from default-schema or baseline answers. Imagination, read as non-copying: maximum similarity to web-search or retrieved-memory text, with near-copies flagged. Motivation: share of shortlisted ideas taken to a testable specification. /evaluate scores value separately. A skill whose ablation changes idea count but not distance has not shown an originality effect. |
| MSR-11 | ANTI: Crediting memory or transfer gains on the run or distribution that produced them | Sound cues for abstraction problems replayed during REM sleep (not SWS) improved later performance (N=27), but the improvement became significant only at a retest one week later, which fits gradual gist extraction. Meta-learning for compositionality (MLC) reached near-zero error on SCAN add-jump and COGS lexical splits but 100% error on SCAN length and COGS structural splits. It cannot generalize beyond its meta-training distribution. | single-study | harness | Judge /consolidate and the offline abstraction jobs by performance on later related problems, not on the run that wrote the memory. Build problem families, interleave them across runs, and score run N+k on related items after k consolidation cycles, against a version with memory frozen. Include out-of-distribution splits whose problems need longer or structurally deeper solutions than any memory entry or example, and report them separately. Retrieval gains count as transfer only if they hold on these splits. |
| MSR-12 | ANTI: assuming stacked interventions add up | In a randomized sham-controlled study (N=110), tDCS raised verbal DT originality and tRNS raised figural DT fluency, but combining them did not improve divergent thinking. All active conditions improved convergent thinking. | single-study | harness | Do not assume two skills that each help will help together. Once skills pass single-skill ablation, the harness runs pairwise combination tests (e.g. wander+analogize, incubate+restructure). A combination stays only if it beats each component alone on the primary metric. |
| MSR-13 | ANTI: Serial-order effect useless per run | In the Alternate Uses Task, the finding that later ideas are more original is robust at the group level but explains at most 3.5% of variance and shows no stable individual differences (ICCs < 0.31). | single-study | harness | Do not use within-run serial position (e.g. 'the last ideas in a list are more original') as a selection rule or a per-run quality signal in /evaluate or the harness. At most, treat it as weak structure in averages. The /forage rule that bars the default schema early is a design choice to test by ablation, not something serial-order data justifies. |
| MSR-14 | ANTI: One entropy metric as diversity proxy | In 28 participants, 7 of 12 fMRI brain-entropy metrics showed no psilocybin effect, and the authors conclude 'brain entropy' is not a single construct. Lempel-Ziv complexity was the weakest marker of DMT experience, and context alone changes entropy independently of conscious level. | preprint | harness | Never report a single 'diversity' or 'entropy' number for agent outputs. Report several named measures side by side: mean pairwise embedding distance, number of clusters, distinct n-gram ratio, and distance from baseline answers. Check whether they agree across conditions. A condition counts as more diverse only when several measures move together and beat a shuffled null. This feeds the diversity side of MSR-03. |
| MSR-15 | LLM ignition index: internal phase transitions | A single-author preprint fit sigmoids to per-layer probe accuracy against signal strength across 11 models. Feedforward transformers were 89% steeper than Mamba state-space models (d=0.52, p<1e-13). The author does not link ignition to reasoning ability. | preprint | harness | Not transferable to HTSA. It needs per-layer activation probes, and skills that call Claude through Claude Code cannot see activations. At most it could be studied on open-weight models outside HTSA. It is neither a harness metric nor a design driver. |
| MSR-16 | Higher-order novelty and the adjacent possible | A Polya urn with triggering, in which one novelty makes others more likely, reproduces Heaps' law across Wikipedia, tags, text and music. Higher-order novelties (first co-occurrences of two or more elements) can differ between processes that share the same standard Heaps exponent. | theory | harness | The harness extracts concepts from all ideas across runs and computes, as the idea count grows, the rate of first occurrences of single concepts and of concept pairs (first co-occurrences). Fit a Heaps exponent for each and compare baseline with HTSA. Real recombination should raise the pair exponent, not just the single-concept one. Optionally, /think seeds the next round from concepts that appeared for the first time. |
| MSR-17 | ANTI: Reading switching or scaling signatures without null models | A plain similarity-driven random walk over rich embeddings already reproduces MVT foraging signatures, so conformity to MVT is weak evidence of strategic control; the diagnostic signal is systematic deviation (fast switches, slow clusters). In resting fMRI (N=136), PCA-eigenspectrum and renormalization signatures were reproduced by a null model with zero coupling, from autocorrelation and short recordings alone, and only time-shift randomization separated real collective dynamics. Noise-driven multistability gives exponential dwell-time distributions, while delay-coupled metastable dynamics give gamma-distributed ones. Observing switching alone is therefore not diagnostic. | contested | harness; forage | Compare every dynamical or distributional statistic of agent output (MVT fit, idea-cluster sizes, jump lengths, dwell times, power laws) against two nulls: the same items in shuffled order (time-shift randomization) and independent samples that share no context. Report only differences that exceed both nulls. For /forage, fit an MVT baseline to the embedding-distance sequence, report early switches and extended dwells, and correlate those deviations with rubric score. 'Forages like a human' is not evidence that a skill works. For dwell times, cut a topic segment wherever consecutive cosine distance passes a threshold, fit exponential vs gamma by AIC (gamma means structured clumping), and compare with the shuffled null. Never treat a long tail as evidence of a critical or 'creative' regime. |
| MSR-18 | ANTI: Justifying components by unresolved neuroscience theory (untyped surprise, single dream-function theory) | Prediction-error signals can look alike yet reflect different computations, the evidence is inconsistent, and critics argue predictive processing can fit any phenomenon, so any surprise step must specify what is being predicted. Likewise, no 2023-2026 study distinguishes the overfitted-brain, NEXTUP, threat/social-simulation and protoconsciousness theories of dreaming. Critics call NEXTUP untestable, TDLM detection of replay at rest is fragile, and serial-awakening designs reveal only correlates of dream recall. | contested | insight-check; incubate; consolidate; harness | Every brain-inspired signal gets an operational definition. In /insight-check, 'surprise' or 'suddenness' means the embedding distance of an idea from the preceding ideas in its cluster, or a mismatch with a prediction written to the workspace before the idea was seen. Do not build a single theory of offline processing into the architecture. Each offline operation (episode blending, perturbation, gist extraction, structure search, delayed retry, cued reactivation) is its own flag in /incubate and /consolidate. The harness ablates each signal and flag, and only those with a measurable effect stay. Fidelity to brain theory never justifies a component on its own. |

### Sources and caveats

- **MSR-01**: Sources: DT-creative achievement meta-analysis, https://www.researchgate.net/publication/361681918_Divergent_thinking_and_creative_achievement-How_strong_is_the_link_An_updated_meta-analysis; Second-order meta-analysis 2026, https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10400419.2026.2644245; PMC8920217 2022, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8920217/; Chen & Ding 2023, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.11158v1. Caveats: The validity evidence comes from human trait-level psychometrics, partly from older reviews. The LLM saturation evidence is an arXiv preprint, and DAT scores depend on the embedding model (the 2026 review tests this). For agents, the gap between novelty and usefulness may be smaller or larger and has to be measured directly. Merged from: ANTI: divergent-thinking scores as headline metric [brain_networks_of_creativity]; Divergent-test scores weakly predict real creativity [modulators_and_practical_levers]; ANTI: Raw semantic distance as success metric [thought_dynamics_and_mind_wandering].
- **MSR-02**: Sources: Sio & Lortie-Forgues 2024, https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000432; McKay 2024, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/caim.12605; Chhade et al. 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06461-6; Corcoran, Hohwy & Friston 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.08.027. Caveats: These are human training and neuroimaging literatures, so they carry over only as methodological warnings about weak baselines and tuning on the test set. The Bayesian framing is a theoretical proposal, and its results depend on the chosen priors. The generic-training finding also implies a generation-side lesson that belongs under generation or control: change what the agent sees (seeds, evidence, records) rather than adding 'be creative' instructions. Merged from: Creativity gains shrink under bias correction, transfer weakly [modulators_and_practical_levers]; ANTI: Generic creativity training barely transfers [development_language_and_culture]; Small effects; results depend on validation scheme [brain_networks_of_creativity]; Compare architecture variants by accumulated evidence [predictive_processing_and_active_inference].
- **MSR-03**: Sources: de Rooij & Biskjaer 2026, https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0144929X.2026.2726451; Doshi & Hauser 2024, https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290; Meincke, Nave & Terwiesch 2025, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02173-x. Caveats: The studies measure human+AI pairs, not AI-only pipelines. Individual-level and group-level effects are separate questions (Lee & Chung reply). A 2026 study found that diverse AI personas reduce homogenization, which conflicts with the architecture's no-persona rule (P10). Merged from: LLM help raises quality, shrinks collective diversity [modulators_and_practical_levers].
- **MSR-04**: Sources: Murphy et al. 2024, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00213-024-06680-z; Microdosing controlled studies review 2024, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2451902224000156; De Neys & Raoelison 2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12480527/; Bercovich & Ackerman 2026, https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.252834. Caveats: The expectancy evidence is from human studies, and the transfer to LLM self-assessment is a plausible analogy. The deliberation bias was measured with human raters and older GPT models, and current models may differ. The overconfidence result is a single radiology study. The judge-blinding and overconfidence-routing parts are arguably evaluation and control rather than measurement. Merged from: ANTI: self-rated creativity as an outcome measure [modulators_and_practical_levers]; ANTI: Judging answers by visible deliberation [reasoning_metacognition_and_working_memory]; Overconfidence concentrates on hard problems [reasoning_metacognition_and_working_memory].
- **MSR-05**: Sources: Hubert, Awa & Zabelina 2024, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10858891/; Bellemare-Pepin et al. 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13012; Huang et al. 2025, https://www.shanghaitech.edu.cn/2025/0606/c14749a1111628/page.htm. Caveats: These are divergent-thinking tests, not real problem solving, and some numbers come from summaries. Treating one model's run-to-run variance like human population variance is an analogy, not an established equivalence. Merged from: Human advantage lies in the upper tail [memory_semantic_networks_and_combination].
- **MSR-06**: Sources: Beaty, Zeitlen, Baker & Kenett 2021, https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/forward-flow-and-creative-thought-assessing-associative-cognition/; Zhou 2024, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11506172/; Mallett et al. 2025, https://philosophymindscience.org/index.php/phimisci/article/download/10307/11817?inline=1; Nath, Dayan & Stevenson 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00899. Caveats: The correlations are small to modest (r about .13-.37) and depend on sample and task. They were validated on humans with static English embeddings. Human agreement on boundaries is kappa=0.41, so automated jump metrics are noisy. LLMs saturate distance scores, and an agent told to wander may inflate the metric without producing useful ideas. The jump-profile result is a threshold-dependent preprint on 2024-era models, and the 2024 forward-flow paper was read at abstract level only. Caveats: Mostly from one lab (De Neys). The matrices exception comes from a preprint. Whether an LLM's first pass and its extended reasoning show the same pattern is untested.
