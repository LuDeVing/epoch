| OTH-01 | ANTI: covert bodily simulation shapes meaning and mind | An 18-lab preregistered test found no action-sentence compatibility effect (meta-estimate about 0). | OTH-02 | ANTI: dedicated creativity module or prompt | An ALE meta-analysis (787 experiments, 17,228 adults) found that creativity tasks recruit the same networks as semantic cognition, controlled episodic retrieval and executive function, with only small creativity-preferential clusters. Dietrich 2024 calls the search for a creativity faculty a fallacy. | contested | think | Do not add a special 'be creative' skill or instruction. Any gain must come from orchestrating general capacities: what gets retrieved (encode, seeds, memory), how control is applied (evaluation gates, switch budget) and how these are scheduled. Ablate each v0.1 skill against a generic 'retrieve more broadly' instruction under the same token budget. |

### Sources and caveats

- **OTH-01**: Key sources: Morey 2022, https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-021-01927-8; Solana 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2024.108832; Elkjaer 2022, https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691620919358. Caveats: The nulls concern language-motor and posture effects. The Slepian 2012 and Leung 2012 embodied-metaphor creativity results have unknown replication status; they are not refuted. This is arguably a representation/generation finding (what not to put in the encoding or seeding prompt), but it is kept under 'other' as a cross-cutting negative design constraint. Merged from: ANTI: covert bodily simulation shapes meaning and mind [emotion_body_and_curiosity].
- **OTH-02**: Key sources: Chan, Lambon Ralph & Robinson 2025, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.19.639155v1.full; Dietrich 2024, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1373299/full; Chen et al. 2025, https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-07470-9. Caveats: The meta-analysis is a preprint, and about 76% of its tasks are verbal. Countered by Chen 2025 (network switching predicts creativity but not intelligence) and by Liu 2024 (gradient alignment), which is why it is graded contested. It is an architecture-level principle that spans generation, control and memory, so 'other' fits. Merged from: ANTI: dedicated creativity module or prompt [brain_networks_of_creativity].
