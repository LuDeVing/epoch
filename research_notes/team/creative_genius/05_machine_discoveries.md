# 05. Machine discoveries 2020-2026: what produced the new step, and the hit rates

Compiled 2026-10-13 for the ambitious lane of the nano loop. Citations are [n] (Sources). Numbers come from primary papers, repositories or the authors' own tables; the Navier-Stokes row rests on news and one preprint (weak). Interpretations are marked "(my reading)". The shared web-search quota ran out mid-task; later checks were direct fetches. Not read: OpenAI's own Navier-Stokes post (HTTP 403), X posts, the GNoME critique (publisher 403). Islands, MAP-elites, Elo, Rehearse and the CMU specialists are in 04 and 09 [30] and not repeated.

## Bottom line
1. Every documented win was sampling plus a cheap exact check, inside a slot a human defined. The model supplies variance with a code or literature prior. Humans supplied the framing, the skeleton, the seed method and, usually, the reading of the result.
2. Realistic hit rates for ambitious candidates: 0.5-3% per run; 20-26% of targets per campaign of thousands of samples; about 1 in 3 after an expert shortlist. On DeepMind's own scale (0 negligible to 4 landmark) autonomous results top out at Level 2, "publishable" [16].
3. Claims shrink under audit. Exact verifiers (Lean, symbolic engines, integer certificates, simulator replay) produced the wins; LLM graders, Elo and novelty judges without search inflated.
4. For a quota: quota well-specified attempts, expect 97-99% of them to fail, and score the lane over 100+ builds, not per night.

## Hit rates and what survived checking
- **Per candidate:** FunSearch 2.9% of runs on the hard target; Virtual Lab 2.2%; Aletheia 0.6% of 700 problems novel; MatterGen 1 per 32,768 raw; autoresearch about 3% kept (Karpathy's self-report via [30]), with late runs under 0.0001 val_bpb each [29].
- **Formulation matters:** FunSearch found a full-size I(12,7) set in 60% of runs on the indirect admissible-set formulation, against 2.9% for direct n=8 construction [6].
- **Rediscoveries and beaten records:** Erdos-397 matched a 2012 Chinese team-selection problem [16]; #1026 matched Steele 1980 [18]; #707 matched Hall [19]; Aletheia's "autonomous" count fell from 9 to 4 during literature checks [15]. The rational 48 came within a month [4] and the kissing record fell within months [5].
- **The Erdos funnel is the cleanest count:** of 200 graded answers, 31.5% were technically right, but 50 of those 63 answered a different statement than intended; 6.5% were meaningful, 2% novel after literature checks [15]. The loop analogue is a gain from a bug, a leak or a retuned knob.
- **Fuzzy judges:** a novelty judge scored known ideas 6.14/10 without search and 2.38 with it [11].

## What the generative step was
- **Variance under a prior, filtered exactly.** Tao's gloss: the LLM plays the random-number generator of an evolutionary algorithm [3]. The 4x4 record was fifteen mutations to an optimiser script, each a familiar move (my reading) [1].
- **Humans define and read.** FunSearch's skeleton and symmetry, AlphaEvolve's search mode, Cordoba-Martinez-Zoroa's forcing, the isotropy that made 48 rational. AlphaEvolve "will always simply try to squeeze the most out of the advice it was given" [2].
- **Instance, structure, rule.** Knuth's solution appeared when annealing output showed dependence on one coordinate [21]; 34% of EinsteinArena's kissing-number talk was structure decoding [5].
- **Analogy and retrieval.** A-Lab, AlphaGeometry constructions, co-scientist, First Proof P2, Steele 1980 behind Erdos #1026 ("not particularly novel") [18].
- **Compute as curriculum.** AlphaProof's variants, AlphaGeometry's synthetic proofs, the Navier-Stokes swarm.
- **Exceptions.** Erdos-1051 (not directly inspired by a human argument; classical tools), First Proof P5, AlphaProof P6. Nothing autonomous above Level 2 [16].

## Design rules for an ambitious lane
1. **Fix the slot, free the content.** Each ambitious idea rewrites one named, checkable slot (schedule function, per-layer multiplier rule, init, loss term, routing score); everything else frozen. Let the agent write a search procedure over the slot that runs on a cheap proxy, so one LLM call buys many evaluations. Evidence: FunSearch's skeleton, and 60% vs 2.9% of runs for indirect vs direct formulations; search mode gave "much better results" than direct construction; Borg's heuristic is correct by construction because it only ranks feasible machines [1][2][6].
2. **Plan for 1-3% and judge the lane per 100 builds.** These are analog priors, not nano measurements. A 15-20% lane of 20-30 nightly builds is 3-6 shots, so expect 0.03-0.2 verified wins a night. Buy breadth in cheap rungs; read the lane over weeks.
3. **Verifier tiers, hardened as agents push.** Exact where possible; else fresh seeds, hidden slice, neutralised patch with logged knob values, scale transfer. Add a causal-leak test (perturb future tokens; earlier-position loss must not move): the AI Scientist's own limitations section lists future-token leakage giving "deceptively impressive perplexity" [13]. AlphaEvolve is "extremely good at locating exploits" [3]; EinsteinArena moved to 80-digit precision [5]; a correct proof can answer the wrong statement (Erdos-75) [15], so check the build does what the idea says.
4. **Dense scores for near-misses.** The matmul fitness was best rank plus the fraction of seeds reaching it; continuous losses beat counting; an agent's Taylor-linearised least-squares score drove the final kissing violation to zero [1][2][5]. Report loss-vs-token area and multi-scale loss so a failed idea yields a gradient.
5. **Probe, then read structure.** Log every exploration in a progress file (Knuth's instruction); run tiny-width probes with diagnostics; propose from observed structure. FunSearch's step came from reading the program [6]; AlphaEvolve generalised better when shown only small cases ("less is more") [2].
6. **Tag provenance; test novelty with retrieval.** Keyword search called micro-batching novel [14]. Count only "novel after retrieval" ideas toward ambition. Ports that work (backout, smear) are main-lane wins, not ambition.
7. **Humans supply the leap; the lane supplies the long tail.** Navier-Stokes ran on a human construction [22]; no autonomous Level 3 [16]. Give each ambitious agent a different expert-written seed mechanism per round and set the quota on instantiated, stress-tested variants.
8. **Mix models.** A cheap model across many runs beat a literature bound for a few dollars; hard constructions needed the strong model; a weaker model still adds variance [2]. Mostly cheap sketches, a minority of strong long shots.
9. **Abstain, grade, keep partials.** Aletheia declined 4 of 10 First Proof problems, and its pipeline cut 700 to 13 [15][16]. Let "no ambitious idea this round" cost less than a junk one; grade claims by level; archive near-misses with measured deltas. 593 -> 604 came from public partials, verifier fixes and idea-borrowing [5]; show partials late (anchoring risk in 09).
10. **Turn near-misses into a curriculum.** AlphaProof's test-time RL on variants of the target added 15 absolute points over a 12-TPU-hour search, at hundreds of TPU-days [10]. After a near-miss, run a family (move layer, change width, relax, strengthen) as one batch.

Gaps: AlphaEvolve's per-sample success rate is unpublished; the rational-48 derivation by isotropy is from a search snippet; I found no independent co-scientist replication.
