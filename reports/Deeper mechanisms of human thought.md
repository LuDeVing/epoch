# Minds Build Ideas by Editing Structured Models

Across neuroscience, psychology and philosophy, the 2023–2026 literature converges on a picture in which human thought is a resource-bounded sampler running over a structured generative model, and a genuinely new idea is an edit to that model's *structure* (a new latent factor, a merged or pruned component, a dropped constraint) rather than a new belief inside a fixed model. Candidates come to mind a few at a time, drawn from a prior that weights likelihood and value (which is why first ideas are conventional), are composed from a library of reusable program-like abstractions, and must pass through a conscious workspace that carries roughly **10 bits per second** and holds about **three chunks**, so complex combinations are built serially, chunked, or pushed onto paper. How far the sampler reaches is set by a single precision/gain axis: phasic noradrenaline and 5-HT2A agonism flatten the brain's attractor landscape and raise transition rates, acetylcholine and external task demand deepen and restabilize it, and the quiescent, post-sleep brain sits closest to the near-critical regime in which slow and fast signals communicate best. Offline, replay does not replay: human MEG shows it assembling compound hypotheses in order from predictable to uncertain elements and propagating value along never-traversed paths, REM-targeted cueing improves rule abstraction a week later, and a model in which REM mixes unrelated memories with noise learns semantic representations markedly faster than one that replays single episodes. Deliberation, meanwhile, mostly confirms what intuition already produced; the real levers are metacognitive (low confidence triggers re-sampling, high confidence gates out disconfirming evidence), yet people's judgments of their own ideas correlate only **0.1–0.3** with expert ratings and reliably under-rank their best idea. Development is a cooling schedule (random choice temperature settles by about age 8, directed exploration by 9, generalization by 13), language is a tool for installing and transmitting abstractions rather than the medium of thought, and at population scale novelty requires diverse inputs, a selective filter and external records. For an agent architecture this licenses a concrete loop: sample a handful of candidates from a deliberately shifted prior, compose them from a named abstraction library, test them with partial counterfactual simulation, allocate computation by its expected value, broadcast only compressed pointers through a narrow workspace, consolidate offline by expand-then-merge, and drive curiosity with learning progress and empowerment rather than information gain alone. The field's main limits are that the thought-level claims of active inference still rest on simulations, no study yet links criticality or entropy measures to measured creative output, dream-function theories remain undiscriminated, and most "embodied" effects on thought have failed large preregistered replications.

## Thinking is counterfactual rollout, and a new idea is a structural edit to the model

The earlier report concluded that creativity is a control policy over memory search. The predictive-processing literature supplies the formal object that policy operates on. In the canonical 2024 statement, sentient behaviour "depends upon our brains' implicit use of internal models to predict, infer, and direct action," with the decisive developments being sequential models for planning and hierarchical, temporally deep generative models ([Pezzulo, Parr & Friston 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsycho.2023.108741)). Thinking, on this account, is planning as inference: the model rolls out counterfactual policies, scores each by expected free energy (expected information gain plus expected value, where information gain is "the KL divergence between predictive posteriors with, and without, the consequences of action"), and uses Bayesian model reduction to compare candidate model structures quickly ([Friston et al. 2025, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21129)). "Sophisticated" inference extends this to beliefs about beliefs, which "effectively implements a deep tree search over actions and outcomes in the future" over sequences of belief states rather than states ([Friston et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco_a_01351)). Offline thought is the same machinery run without input: spontaneous activity at rest and in dreaming "optimize[s] the generative models for future interactions by maximizing the entropy of explanations and minimizing model complexity" ([Pezzulo, Zorzi & Corbetta 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2021.05.007)), and even mental effort has been formalized as covert action that changes beliefs without touching the world ([Parr et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2023.108562)).

The human evidence for this picture is indirect but specific. Imagined and perceived signals share one axis, with reality judgments depending on whether the mixed signal crosses a threshold, so strong imagery "become[s] subjectively indistinguishable from reality" ([Dijkstra & Fleming 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37322-1)). Frontoparietal cortex predicts "higher-level, longer-range and more contextual representations than temporal cortices" during language ([Caucheteux et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01516-2)). Most importantly for idea generation, MEG replay during a constructive inference task "form[s] constructive hypotheses about possible scene configurations" and plays them out "in an optimal order for relational inference, progressing from predictable to uncertain scene elements" ([Schwartenbeck et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1101/2021.06.06.447249)); the accompanying theory holds that replay "is able to implement a form of compositional computation where entities are assembled into relationally bound structures to derive qualitatively new knowledge" ([Kurth-Nelson et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2022.12.028)). A biologically constrained simulation in which hippocampal replay trains neocortical generative models reproduces semantic memory, imagination, episodic future thinking and schema-based distortions in one architecture ([Spens & Burgess 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01799-z)). This is the neural counterpart of the earlier report's finding that hippocampal ripples transiently loosen prefrontal schemas during ideation: in active-inference terms, ripples are the moments when model structure, not just model parameters, is open to revision.

### Expand online, reduce offline: the only procedure-ready account of a new idea

The framework's operational definition of a new idea is a change in model structure, produced by two complementary moves. Model *expansion* adds latent states when existing ones cannot explain the data: a generative model with spare "slots" switched on by novel inputs adds new concepts "with relatively few observations" and shows one-shot generalization ([Smith et al. 2020](https://frontiersin.org/journals/computational-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncom.2020.00041/full)). Model *reduction* prunes or merges afterwards, "a special case of structure learning that typifies learning in the absence of new evidence" ([Neacsu et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0277199)), which Friston's group has long linked to sleep and to "all the hallmarks of 'aha' moments" ([Friston et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco_a_00999)) and which the earlier report met as Laukkonen's Eureka heuristic. The first quantitative demonstration at scale is the AXIOM agent: new mixture components are added online "when no existing component explains new data well enough," and every 500 frames Bayesian model reduction samples up to 2,000 components and greedily tests merges, keeping one "only if it lowers the expected free energy." On the Gameworld 10k benchmark AXIOM scored **190 ± 18 on Gold against −26 ± 12 for BBF and −21 ± 10 for DreamerV3**, with 0.3–1.6 M parameters against 420 M for DreamerV3; ablating reduction dropped Gold from 190 to 45 and Hunt from 206 to 48, but reduction *hurt* on the Cross game "because early merging reduces the information-gain term and discourages exploration" ([Heins et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.24784v1)). That last result is a design constraint, not a footnote: consolidation must be scheduled after exploration has plateaued. The human-validated algorithmic layer underneath is few-sample hypothesis generation, in which "hypotheses are generated stochastically from a sampling process, such that the sampled hypotheses form a Monte Carlo approximation of the posterior" with only a handful of samples, reproducing anchoring, subadditivity, the "crowd within" and the self-generation effect ([Dasgupta, Schulz & Gershman 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2017.05.001)).

### Curiosity is not information gain alone, and the epistemic term is unstable

Under active inference, curiosity is the information-gain term of expected free energy, and small-N human work gives it some support: in a contextual bandit, active inference "better explains human decision-making under novelty and variability" than reinforcement learning, with expected free energy "encoded in the frontal pole and middle frontal gyrus" in 25 participants ([Zhang et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.92892)), and about half of participants in four two-step datasets showed information-gain sensitivity ([Gijsen et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-21766-4)). But the most careful 2025 test found the opposite: in a task with a highly stochastic, reward-free subregion, participants "persistently explore the stochastic subregion, and their decisions are best explained by a novelty-driven exploration strategy, compared to alternatives driven by information gain or surprise" ([Modirshanechi et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2502193122)). Learning progress fits free task choice better than competence alone ([Ten et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-26196-w)), and in the open-ended crafting game Little Alchemy 2, **29,493 players** showed "an intrinsic motivation to create objects that empower them to create even more objects," a drive that "is eliminated when playing a game variant that lacks recognizable semantics" ([Brändle et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01661-2)). The Brändle result is the single most useful curiosity finding for an idea-generating agent: humans prefer moves that expand the space of future moves, and they do so using world knowledge about what combinations could enable. The framework's own theorists concede that "exploration does not directly follow from free energy minimization into the future" ([Millidge, Tschantz & Buckley 2021](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco_a_01354)), and a deep EFE-minimizing agent "almost always picks the same action, which makes it unable to solve the dSprites environment" ([Champion et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco_a_01697)). Information gain is therefore one heuristic among several, not a normative guarantee, and a faithful curiosity module should combine a count-based novelty bonus, a learning-progress signal and an empowerment term.

The neuromodulatory precision story that would connect this to brain chemistry is, in humans, confined to learning tasks. A D2/D3 antagonist "increases the volatility of beliefs, which leads to higher precision weights on prediction errors" in 76 men, driven by those with genetically higher dopamine availability ([Mikus et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-39823-5)); high trait anxiety "undermined learning through an overestimation of volatility, leading to faster belief updating, more stochastic decisions," with gamma signatures of precision-weighted errors in ACC, dmPFC and OFC ([Hein et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-023-04628-1)); and post-feedback pupil dilation tracks information gain, with a context-dependent sign ([Colizoli et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.105287)). No 2023–2026 human study measured dopaminergic, noradrenergic or cholinergic signals during insight, divergent thinking or hypothesis generation, and a preregistered study failed to replicate a canonical volatility-overweighting finding in autism ([Plank et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13229-026-00730-3)). The broader critiques remain live: predictive processing "fails to deliver general, simple, homogeneous, and systematic explanations" and its models "are frequently offered as mere just-so stories" ([Litwin & Miłkowski 2020](https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.12867)); a 2026 Annual Review finds that "varied definitions and inconsistent empirical evidence have raised questions about its validity and explanatory scope" and that prediction-error signals "may appear similar in their responses yet reflect fundamentally different underlying computations" ([Furutachi & Hofer 2026](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-neuro-102124-031410)). The practical corollary is that any "prediction error" in an architecture must say what is being predicted, a sensory feature or hypothesis-level model evidence, which is exactly the surprise-versus-metacognitive-error distinction the earlier report drew for the Aha. Finally, the framework's authors argue that biological generative models "must capture and control the sensory consequences of action," letting agents "intervene upon their worlds in ways that constantly put their best models to the test," unlike "the passive models learned by generative AI systems" ([Pezzulo et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2023.10.002)); LLM-wrapped active inference so far uses the framework only as a control policy over prompting and search ([Prakki 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10425v1)), which is precisely the layer where an idea-generation procedure would live.

## Concepts are programs in a library, and structure transfers as cognitive maps

If a new idea is a structural edit, the next question is what the structure is made of. The language-of-thought hypothesis was relaunched in 2023 as an empirical "working hypothesis" defined by six clustered properties (discrete constituents, role-filler independence, predicate–argument structure, logical operators, inferential promiscuity and abstract content) that "cluster together throughout cognitive science," while explicitly granting that "the mind may harbor many formats and architectures, including iconic and associative structures as well as deep-neural-network-like architectures" ([Quilty-Dunn, Porot & Mandelbaum 2023](https://philosophersannual.org/42articles/quiltydunnetal-thebest.pdf)). The composition mechanism they endorse is the probabilistic language of thought: "a set of primitives with basic operations for their combination (such as the lambda calculus)," so that primitives "are recursively combined to form concepts of arbitrary complexity," with a noisy likelihood and simplicity prior ([same source](https://philosophersannual.org/42articles/quiltydunnetal-thebest.pdf)). Thirty commentaries pressed the "footprints or the bear" objection, that passing the six-property battery does not settle whether the implementation is classical ([commentary listing](https://core-prod.cambridgecore.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/volume/901042062D203290E441000007F45322?pageNum=15)), and a 2025 hardware-level analysis concludes "Classical LOT (Fodor 1975) is ruled out" in favour of a nonclassical LoT encoded nondigitally ([Piccinini 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10251)). The format question matters less for an architect than the operations, and here the 2024 program-induction work is unusually concrete.

Rule and colleagues tested 392 participants on 250 list functions and found that only models searching over *metaprograms*, programs that revise programs using metaprimitives such as MemorizeAll, Recurse and AntiUnify, matched human performance; the MetaProgram Learner's posterior at **5,000 search steps exceeded a conventional LoT sampler's at 5 million**, and at the median human response time of 14.7 s people could afford between roughly 20 and 6,000 steps depending on per-step cost, a range the model fits ([Rule et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11316799/)). The lesson for hypothesis generation is that new hypotheses are *edits of old ones guided by the structure of the evidence*, not blind enumeration. Library learning formalizes how solved problems become reusable primitives: DreamCoder alternates waking search, "abstraction sleep" that refactors shared sub-programs into a growing library, and "dream sleep" that trains a recognition network on imagined problems, rediscovering "concepts from functional programming, vector algebra, and classical physics, including Newton's and Coulomb's laws" and solving "creative tasks like drawing pictures and building scenes" ([Ellis et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381)). Its successor LILO adds an auto-documentation step that names each abstraction in natural language, and naming measurably improves later reuse ([Grand et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19791)). Humans behave like compression-optimal library builders: the part concepts people name for ~2,000 procedurally generated objects reflect "a fundamental information-theoretic tradeoff" between concise descriptions and a compact lexicon ([Wong et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05666)), and when generating novel figures people "went beyond the model in structured ways," completing sets and "reconfiguring existing parts in highly novel ways," which forced the addition of a compositional recombination mechanism to a Bayesian program model ([Zhou, Feinman & Lake 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19374)). Language can serve as the front end: LLMs translate sentences into probabilistic programs over which inference, physics simulation and planning run ([Wong et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12672v2)).

Compositional generalization itself is systematic but biased. In an instruction-learning task, humans produced the algebraically correct output **80.7%** of the time and 72.5% correctly produced outputs longer than any seen, with errors following one-to-one, iconic-concatenation and mutual-exclusivity biases; a 1.4 M-parameter transformer meta-trained on 100,000 compositional episodes matched both the accuracy (82.4%) and the item-level error profile (r = 0.788) while a standard seq2seq scored 0%, yet the same model fails length and structural splits outright and has "no mechanism to emit new symbols" ([Lake & Baroni 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10620072)). Smolensky's answer to the resulting "Central Paradox of Cognition," that the head is "simultaneously a neural computer and a compositional-structure computer," is tensor-product role-filler binding that honours both continuity and compositionality ([Smolensky et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.01128)). The remaining philosophical hole is the primitive base: composition explains productivity but not new primitives. Carey's Quinian bootstrapping fills it with a mechanism an architect can copy: a placeholder symbol embedded in an external structured system (a count list, a notation) is mapped by analogy onto existing representations and the mapping is generalized, yielding concepts that go beyond anything innate; a 2025 information-theoretic analysis of Fodor's puzzle concludes "there is an important sense in which people do indeed learn new concepts" ([Rule & Piantadosi 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18277)).

### Maps give zero-shot transfer; simulation and construal decide what to compute

The hippocampal–entorhinal system supplies the other half of structure: a factorized code for relational form that can be rebound to new content. In the Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine, medial entorhinal units path-integrate abstract location, hippocampal units conjoin that structure with sensory input, and the trained system makes first-presentation inferences in transitive-inference and social-hierarchy tasks ([Whittington et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7707106/)); nonnegativity and energy constraints provably produce the disentangled, cell-type-like representations such transfer requires ([Whittington et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01768)). Human data now follow: after four days learning four graphs, right entorhinal representations generalized across hexagonal tasks differing in size and stimuli (t(27) = 4.2), "the only shared feature being the rules governing statistical structure," though no such generalization appeared for clustered community structure ([Mark et al. 2024/2026](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/101134)). Which map is used is itself inferred online: a Gaussian-process model combining spatial and successor-representation similarity best explained reward generalization (model frequency 0.681), with hippocampal signals tracking trial-wise reweighting and medial OFC tracking evidence for which map applied ([Garvert et al. 2023](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.10.22.465012v1.full)). In mice, medial frontal "goal-progress" cells form task-structured memory buffers that let animals apply an abstract ABCD schema to new goal layouts "from the first trial" ([El-Gaby et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08145-x)). And in 17 epilepsy patients with 2,694 recorded neurons, the capacity for one-shot inference was accompanied by hippocampal reorganization into an abstract, disentangled geometry (shattering dimensionality **0.57 → 0.62**), present only after learning, only on correct trials, and reachable by "trial-and-error learning and verbal instruction" alike ([Courellis et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07799-x)). Flexibility that transfers thus depends on first building a disentangled latent code, and instruction can install it.

Mental simulation is the engine that tests candidates. The counterfactual simulation model needs "a generative mental model of the world, the ability to perform interventions on that model, and the capacity to simulate the consequences"; causal and counterfactual judgments about the same collisions align at **r = 0.96**, and people asked causal questions spontaneously make "counterfactual saccades" along the path a ball would have taken ([Gerstenberg 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2024.04.012)). The simulator is, however, partial: people show a conjunction fallacy in physical predictions, miss changes in a tower after it collapses, and judge arrival order below chance in two-second videos ([Ludwin-Peery et al. 2021](https://www.gureckislab.org/papers/ref/ludwin2021limits); [Vicovaro 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10607174/)). The field's synthesis is partial simulation, which "moves forward in time only parts of the world deemed relevant" and explains the conjunction fallacy quantitatively ([Bass et al. 2021](https://nips.cc/virtual/2021/34418)), while a noisy-physics engine still generalizes to unseen scenes in human-like ways where learned dynamics models do not ([Wang et al. 2024](https://www.mit.edu/~k2smith/publication/physion_sim)). Gerstenberg's own open question, "what determines what counterfactuals come to mind," is the generative step and remains unsolved ([Gerstenberg 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2024.04.012)).

What gets simulated is a cost-weighted choice. People "construct simplified mental representations to plan," including only obstacles whose relevance outweighs the cost of representing them (likelihood-ratio **χ²(1) = 2297** on awareness probes in 161 participants), and the effect survives controls for perception and execution ([Ho et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06948)); they decompose tasks into subgoals "to reduce the overall cost of planning while maintaining task performance," matching betweenness centrality across 806 participants ([Correa et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03890)); they plan deeper with expertise in a four-in-a-row game whose model passed a Turing test ([van Opheusden et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06124-2)); and they adapt planning strategy to where variance sits in a decision tree ([Callaway et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01332-8)). The one mechanistic model of how long to think is a meta-RL agent that at each step either acts or performs an imagined rollout costing 120 ms, "learns to plan when planning is beneficial," correlates with human thinking time (r = 0.186), and loses reward from 7.54 to 6.54 per episode when rollouts are disabled ([Jensen, Hennequin & Mattar 2024](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.01.16.523429v2.full)). Replay in the same framework is prioritized by gain × need ([Mattar & Daw 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6203620)), and when the agent's own model is forgetful the optimal policy replays sub-optimal transitions, which explains human MEG "pessimized" replay and raised optimal choice by 3.36% per individual fit ([Antonov et al. 2021/2022](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.04.27.441454v1.full-text)). The umbrella is expected value of control: dorsal ACC selects the control signal that maximizes payoff minus cost, so control is "just strong enough" ([Shenhav, Botvinick & Cohen 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3767969/)). The implementable meta-level loop is therefore: state = current beliefs; actions = {compute X at cost c, act}; choose the computation with the highest value of computation; stop when none is positive, with construal and subgoal choice treated as computations in the same loop.

## Philosophy relocates creativity from faculty to process type

The philosophical literature matters here because it has already decomposed the creativity concept into components an architecture can own. The Stanford entry (first published 2023) distinguishes person, process and product senses, Boden's P-creative from H-creative, and four candidate process conditions: surprise, originality, spontaneity and agency ([SEP, Creativity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/)). The most useful decomposition drops value: creativity is "the disposition or set of linked dispositions of an individual: to have many ideas (fertility); which are novel (originality) and generated through use of the imagination (imagination); and to carry through these ideas to completion (motivation)," with value handled downstream by taste, since "it is not psychologically realistic" to posit two dispositions in Tesla, one for valuable and one for worthless ideas ([Hills & Bird 2018](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/ws/files/118143340/Creativity_Without_Value.pdf)). That maps cleanly onto a generate (imagination × fertility) then select (taste) architecture, and the SEP notes that people good at evaluating ideas also tend to be good at generating them, that the "productivity theory" holds good ideas come from generating many and discarding most, and that externalization is key for complex tasks because working memory is limited ([SEP, Creativity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/)).

Boden's framework remains the only philosophical account that is directly computational. A conceptual space is "the particular set of generative principles" of a style; exploratory creativity navigates it, transformational creativity changes it, and the transformation repertoire is small and enumerable: "dropping a constraint" (non-Euclidean geometry, atonal music), "consider the negative" (Kekulé's rings) and "vary the variable," applied most powerfully "at a relatively deep level of the generative system" ([Boden 1998](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/Boden.html)). Boden also predicted the asymmetry every later section of this report confirms: "AI will have less difficulty in modelling the generation of new ideas than in automating their evaluation" ([same source](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/Boden.html)). The standing criticism, that her "exact definition of conceptual spaces remains elusive" ([Wiggins](https://axon.cs.byu.edu/~dan/673/papers/wiggins.pdf)), is answered in practice by the program-library view above: a conceptual space is a DSL plus constraints, and Boden's operators are edits to the DSL. The "paradox" that creativity must be both novel and caused dissolves once novelty is located in the absence of certain causes (copying, routine) rather than in indeterminism ([Kronfeldner 2009](https://pub.uni-bielefeld.de/record/1590727)), and Gaut's distinction between algorithms (which preclude creativity) and heuristics (which do not) explains why a rational process can yield what could not be derived ([SEP, Creativity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/)). Simonton's blind-variation account survives only with the concession that "expertise, remote associates, and strategy play substantial though subsidiary roles" ([Simonton summary](https://scottbarrykaufman.com/study-alert-creative-thought-as-blind-variation-and-selective-retention-why-creativity-is-inversely-related-to-sightedness/)), which Gabora argues is inconsistent with Darwinism ([Gabora](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.2210)).

### Imagination under constraints, frames, and the logic of abduction

The imagination literature has converged on the "puzzle of imaginative use," that imagining is both free and world-tracking, and solved it with constraints: Kind's reality constraint (initialize from the world as it is) and change constraint (roll forward only consequences that follow from the stipulated change), against structure-based constraints built into the architecture ([SEP, Imagination](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/imagination/); [Kind](https://scholarship.claremont.edu/cmc_fac_pub/532)). That is a counterfactual simulation with controlled interventions, which is why the objection that constrained imagining "collapses into trivial hypothetical reasoning" ([Levy & Kinberg 2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-021-03156-x)) matters: imagination's distinctive contribution must lie in implicit, imagistic or embodied rollout dynamics rather than explicit inference. Nichols and Stich's single-code hypothesis, that pretend inputs run through the same inference engine as beliefs, is the architectural reason suppositions yield non-obvious consequences ([SEP, Imagination](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/imagination/)). Most philosophers now hold that creative imagination is a *use* of ordinary imagining rather than a separate type, one that "intentionally manipulates imagined contents, guided by subjective value" ([same source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/imagination/)), and the empirical baseline is sobering: imagined novel creatures retain bilateral symmetry and sense organs, so default imaginings are near neighbours in concept space and transformation requires explicit constraint-dropping ([SEP, Creativity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/)). Camp adds a non-propositional format: a "frame" or "characterization" is a weighting of prominence and centrality over features that "suggest[s] an intuitive way of thinking about their subjects" and is double-edged, "blinding us to known features that don't fit" ([Camp 2019](https://philarchive.org/rec/CAMPAF-7)); changing the frame by metaphor reconfigures the whole structure at once, which is a concept-level version of Boden's transformation.

On reasoning, Mercier and Sperber's interactionist account relocates reason as one more intuitive metarepresentational module that "takes several representations as inputs—the premises and conclusion of an argument—and delivers as output an intuitive metarepresentational judgment of the quality of the support relation," evolved "to find justifications and arguments, and to evaluate the justifications and arguments offered by others." Its biases are features: "System 2 suffers from a massive myside bias," it "is lazy when it comes to evaluating our own reasons," and "by and large, solitary reasoning doesn't correct mistaken intuitions" ([Mercier & Sperber 2020](https://www.dan.sperber.fr/wp-content/uploads/2020_mercier_sperber_bounded-rationality-in-a-social-world.pdf)). The prediction for an agent is that idea quality improves under adversarial dialogue, so a solitary generator should simulate interlocutors or externalize critique. Philosophy of science supplies the generation operators. Peirce's abduction describes "the process of generating hypotheses in order to explain surprising facts" ([Peirce 150 years later](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.08054)), its schema runs backwards from a surprising fact to a hypothesis-type that would make it a matter of course, and its permissiveness demands a pursuitworthiness filter ([SEP, Scientific Discovery](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/scientific-discovery/)). Van Fraassen's "bad lot" objection, that inference to the best explanation may yield "the best of a bad lot," is "essentially about generation: whether the best explanation is among the hypotheses considered" ([SEP, Abduction](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/abduction/)); it formalizes why no amount of evaluation recovers a hypothesis never generated. The three concrete generation operators in this literature are schema instantiation from a library of mechanism types (Darden), analogical transfer with near domains for repair and distant domains for new concepts (Dunbar's in-vivo studies), and simulative or visual model-building (Nersessian) ([SEP, Scientific Discovery](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/scientific-discovery/)).

Finally, 4E cognition explains why externalization is generative rather than merely mnemonic. Kirsh lists seven ways external representations add cognitive power beyond saving memory, including creating persistent referents, serving as a shareable object of thought and enabling reformulation ([Kirsh 2010](https://philarchive.org/rec/KIRTWE)); Menary argues new notations acquire non-derived content and redeploy older neural circuits ([extended mathematical cognition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7437646/)); Malafouris's "thinging" holds that "withness and throughness takes precedence over aboutness" and that "the affordances of clay bring forth the potter's intentions" ([Malafouris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambros_Malafouris)). The empirical record supports the functional claim regardless of the constitution debate: Picasso's 61 Guernica sketches, Darwin's notebooks, and experiments in which sketching helped people discover and solve more problems ([SEP, Creativity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/)). Idea generation on this view is an iterate–externalize–re-perceive loop, and a new notation is a conceptual space one can enter.

## Conscious access is a serial ten-bit channel that broadcasts pointers, not content

Global Neuronal Workspace Theory holds that a content becomes conscious when it triggers "ignition," a "sudden, coherent, and exclusive activation" of long-range workspace neurons about 200–300 ms after onset, and that the function of access is to make one selected content available to otherwise encapsulated processors for maintenance, amplification, binding, mental operations and the "successive mental steps required to solve a task" ([Mashour, Roelfsema, Changeux & Dehaene 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8770991/)). The claim most relevant to idea combination is that chaining requires consciousness: single arithmetic operations could be partly performed on masked digits, but applying two operations in sequence occurred only for consciously perceived digits ([Sackur & Dehaene 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2009.01.003)), which underwrites the "human Turing machine" picture in which each conscious state selects one operation whose result becomes the next input ([Zylberberg et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2011.05.007)). The 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration (N = 256; fMRI, MEG and intracranial EEG; preregistered) sharpened rather than overturned this. Posterior face-versus-object decoding exceeded **95%** for the stimulus duration while prefrontal decoding reached about **70% only 0.2–0.4 s after onset**; orientation was absent from prefrontal cortex; none of 655 prefrontal electrodes showed the predicted offset ignition; and adding prefrontal signals to posterior decoders sometimes reduced accuracy, leading the authors to conclude the data "substantially challenge key tenets of both" GNWT and Integrated Information Theory ([Cogitate Consortium 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12137136)). GNWT proponents reply that offset ignition was never a core prediction, that mixed selectivity makes prefrontal decoding hard, and that transient onset ignition in the predicted window was validated ([Naccache et al. 2025](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2025/1/niaf037/8280147)). For thinking, the defensible reading is that the workspace broadcasts a compressed, categorical pointer rather than a high-fidelity copy, and detailed content is re-read from posterior stores; Cogitate tested passive viewing and is silent on what consciousness does for reasoning, and the INTREPID and ETHOS collaborations have reported no results ([Corcoran et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00555); [Templeton ETHOS](https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/blog/how-ethos-empirical-tests-of-higher-order-theories-of-consciousness-is-accelerating-research-on-consciousness)).

Higher-order theories add the second ingredient. On Lau's perceptual reality monitoring, a content is conscious when a prefrontal mechanism tags it as reflecting current reality rather than noise, memory or imagination ([Lau 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198856771.001.0001)); on Fleming's higher-order state space, awareness is an inference that a first-order content is present, tying access to confidence ([Fleming 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niz020)). Shea and Frith argue the workspace "needs metacognition," since representations in it must carry confidence and source tags to support flexible control ([Shea & Frith 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2019.04.007)), and GNWT's own taxonomy separates C1 (global availability) from C2 (self-monitoring) ([Dehaene, Lau & Kouider 2017](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan8871)). Attention Schema Theory supplies a mechanism for the monitoring itself: a simplified model of one's own attention, which in reinforcement-learning agents improved attention control ([Wilterson & Graziano 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2102421118)) and in multi-agent transformers improved prediction of other agents ([Farrell, Ziman & Graziano 2024/2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00983)). For conscious ideation, the implication is that metacognitive signals (confidence that an idea is promising; discrimination of "remembered" from "generated") are constitutive rather than add-ons.

### What the unconscious provably cannot do

The claim that unconscious thought outperforms deliberation on complex decisions has not survived. The published literature contained predominantly non-significant effects, prior positive results were confined to underpowered studies, and a large preregistered replication found "no reliable support for the claim that a momentary diversion of thought leads to better decision making than a period of deliberation" ([Nieuwenstein et al. 2015](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/on-making-the-right-choice-a-metaanalysis-and-largescale-replication-attempt-of-the-unconscious-thought-advantage/A31405A97BC221C10153E9CF78A94DB6)); a medical-decision meta-analysis called the effect "unreliable" ([Vadillo, Kostopoulou & Shanks 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4436803/)). Two highly powered preregistered experiments found that "the current evidence does not support the claim that arithmetic operations can be flexibly initiated unconsciously," concluding that consciousness "may indeed be necessary for tasks that require integrating different kinds of information in a complex and flexible manner" ([Tal & Mudrik 2024](https://people.socsci.tau.ac.il/mu/mudriklab/files/2024/09/TalMudrik_2024.pdf)); multi-word meaning ([Rabagliati et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000348)) and object–scene integration ([Biderman & Mudrik 2018](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617735745)) likewise failed to replicate without awareness, while subliminal solution-relevant hints did raise insight solution rates ([Hattori, Sloman & Orita 2013](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-013-0389-0)). The consistent pattern is that unconscious processing can bias which elements become available (spreading activation, hints, accumulating activation on a solution) but cannot select a novel rule, chain steps or bind two items into a new relation. This revises the folk reading of incubation that the earlier report already qualified: the break works by forgetting fixation and renewed conscious search, not by productive unconscious deliberation.

### Three chunks, ten bits per second, and why the bottleneck is a feature

Multiple lines converge on a low-throughput serial channel. The embedded-processes model puts the focus of attention at about 3–5 unassociated chunks, with fits of recognition data concluding "about three chunks could be retained, no matter whether they were unrelated words or multiword phrases" ([Cowan et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12143923/)); Oberauer's concentric model holds that one item occupies the focus at a time ([Oberauer 2002](https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.28.3.411)); the psychological refractory period and attentional blink share a late frontoparietal bottleneck ([Marti, Sigman & Dehaene 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2011.09.063)); and Zheng and Meister compute human behavioural throughput at **about 10 bits per second against roughly 10⁹ bits per second** of sensory input, posing "why can we only think about one thing at a time?" as unexplained ([Zheng & Meister 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10234)). Chunking is the escape: retrieving a chunk from long-term memory as a compact representation frees capacity for other items ([Thalmann, Souza & Oberauer 2019](https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000578)), and the number of alternatives people generate in a likelihood task scales with operation-span capacity ([Dougherty & Hunter 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0001-6918(03)00033-7)). Hence the architecture of combination: at most a few candidate elements are co-active, one is operated on at a time, and rich combinations depend on compressing sub-combinations into single tokens and offloading intermediates.

The machine-learning evidence that this bottleneck is functional is the strongest in the consciousness literature. Specialists that communicate only through a limited-slot shared workspace generalize better, with performance peaking at **5 slots (ARI 0.915) and collapsing at 2 (0.154) or 10 (0.351)** ([Goyal et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01197)); a vision–language global workspace aligns modalities with "4 to 7 times less" paired data ([Devillers, Maytié & VanRullen 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15711)); an embodied workspace agent "performs better and more robustly at smaller working memory sizes" than a recurrent baseline ([Dossa et al. 2024](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11211627/)); and a controller routing information through a workspace to chain arithmetic operations "outperforms LSTMs and Transformers when tested on unseen addition operations" with fewer parameters ([Chateau-Laurent & VanRullen 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01906)). A multi-author assessment derived indicator properties (parallel specialist modules, a capacity-limited workspace, global broadcast, state-dependent querying) and found no current system satisfies them all ([Butlin et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)). Working-memory capacity itself is a weak predictor of creativity, **r = .083** across 28 studies ([Gong et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9861316/)) and more related to convergent than divergent thinking ([Gerver et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-023-02303-4); [Lloyd-Cox et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/2qnj4_v1)), because working memory buys persistence and evaluation rather than generative breadth ([De Dreu et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167211435795)). The design lesson is an intermediate bottleneck with chunking and externalization, not a wide one.

## Deliberation mostly confirms intuition; confidence picks what to sample and when to stop

Dual-process theory has been rebuilt from the inside. De Neys argues there is "no solid ground" for the exclusivity assumption that intuition and deliberation each generate responses the other cannot, and that deliberation is triggered when the uncertainty between competing intuitions exceeds a threshold ([De Neys 2022/2023](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/identifier/S0140525X2200142X/type/journal_article)); commentators insist the switch needs explicit monitoring and stopping rules, since on the bare model "under substantial uncertainty people are doomed to think forever" ([Ackerman & Morsanyi 2023](https://ackerman.net.technion.ac.il/files/2023/06/Ackerman_Morsanyi_inpress_commentary.pdf)). The two-response paradigm shows the modal pattern across domains: in more than 70% of cases where people chose the utilitarian option after deliberation it was already their initial answer ([Bago & De Neys 2019](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000533)); in compound remote associates, "correct final answers were usually preceded by correct intuitive answers, so extended deliberation was often unnecessary" ([Beucler & De Neys 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2025.106422)); even on fluid-intelligence matrices over 20% of sound reasoners were consistently correct intuitively ([Charbit, Boissin & De Neys 2026](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/wmc49_v2)); and a meta-analysis of 2,428 participants found that thinking longer did not substantially improve error correction among the most biased ([Beucler et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/ze2ku_v2)). The feeling of rightness predicts not only whether but in which direction answers change ([Thompson & Markovits 2025](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-025-02662-0)), and debiasing works best by training intuitions, with animated-video training improving intuitive responses for two months ([Franiatte et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2025.105131)). This matches the earlier report's finding that insight solutions are computed pre-consciously and are more accurate than analytic ones; the new point is that deliberation's distinctive functions are "response control, response generation, response justification, and regulation" ([De Neys 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2023.08.014)), of which generation is the one creativity research needs.

Confidence is the control variable. Fleming's synthesis treats it as a second-order inference that "depends on the observer's models of the world and of their own cognitive system," supported by a domain-general medial and lateral prefrontal, precuneus and insula network ([Fleming 2024](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-022423-032425); [Vaccaro & Fleming 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6238228/)), with local confidence signals integrated by anterior prefrontal cortex into global self-beliefs ([Fleming 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-026-01081-x)). Confidence does not merely read out quality; it steers search. High confidence amplified post-decision processing of confirmatory evidence and suppressed disconfirmatory evidence in MEG ([Rollwage et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7250867/)), and frontopolar cortex tracks alternative options while anterior lateral prefrontal cortex compares simulated futures ([Miyamoto, Rushworth & Shea 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2023.01.005)). Applied to ideas, monitoring is poor. In two studies (N = 425 and a preregistered 317), self-assessment–performance correlations on the alternate-uses task were **about 0.1–0.3**, and the Dunning–Kruger pattern largely reduced to regression to the mean ([Lebuda et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11126607/)); people systematically underestimate the originality of their own ideas ([Sidi et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2019.103002)); the idea they rank second tends to be their best after development, and forecasting at a more abstract construal level helps ([Berg 2019](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/when-silver-gold-forecasting-potential-creativity-initial-ideas-0)); creators forecast others' ideas better than their own, an advantage that comes from doing both generation and evaluation ([Berg 2016](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/balancing-creative-high-wire-forecasting-success-novel-ideas)); and greater psychological distance raised the originality of selected ideas via construal level ([Zhang et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11837695/)). Yet accurate monitoring was a *necessary* condition for creative performance in a necessary-condition analysis of 385 people ([Urban & Urban 2023](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/9d34r)), and idea selection engages the brain's valuation system and dorsal ACC as a value comparison ([Moreno-Rodriguez et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.06.710072)). The practical levers are to evaluate at a distance, to evaluate as if the ideas were someone else's, and to expose evaluators to the task first ([van Broekhoven et al. 2022](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/creative-idea-forecasting-the-effect-of-task-exposure-on-idea-eva)).

### Effort, stopping rules and the likelihood-times-value prior on what comes to mind

Mental effort is robustly aversive, with a strong positive association between effort and negative affect across 170 studies and 29 countries ([David, Vassena & Bijleveld 2024](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000443)), though effortful activities are rated more meaningful ([Campbell et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12286852/)). Switching between performance goals incurs control-adjustment costs because people undershoot the target control level, growing with target distance and switch frequency ([Grahek et al. 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13023295/)), which favours phased generate-then-evaluate over rapid alternation. People stop when confidence meets a criterion that declines with time spent ([Ackerman 2014](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0035098)), and a 2026 three-stopping-rule model adds an opt-out criterion whose confidence level is stable across incentives and tasks ([Ackerman 2026](https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0001640)); in sequential search people scale thresholds to outcome variance but lower them for longer horizons "less strongly than would be optimal" ([Baumann, Schlegelmilch & von Helversen 2023](https://media.suub.uni-bremen.de/bitstream/elib/8128/1/Baumann_Schlegelmilch_von-Helversen_Adaptive%20behavior%20in%20optimal%20sequential%20search_2023_accepted-version.pdf)). Because the serial-order effect in idea generation explains at most 3.5% of variance with no stable individual slopes ([Barbot et al. 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13300940/)), the stopping problem in ideation is dominated by effort and opportunity cost, not by large expected gains from one more idea, unless early ideas are stereotyped.

They usually are, and the reason is now well characterized. In the HyGene model, hypotheses are retrieved from long-term memory into a small working-memory "set of leading contenders" that then drives judgment and testing ([Thomas et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295x.115.1.155)); divided attention at encoding reduces the number generated ([Sprenger et al. 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3120978/)), and the first hypothesis generated guides visual attention ([Buttaccio et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-014-0463-5)). The options that come to mind by default are "samples from a distribution combining likelihood and desirability" ([Bear et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2019.104057)); adults treat immoral and irrational events as impossible by default ([Phillips & Cushman 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5422784/)); category members in particular regions of feature space come to mind "largely without conscious control" ([Mills & Phillips 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2023.105549)); and across nine experiments with 3,972 participants people use cached value estimates from past contexts to propose a few candidate actions quickly, after which deliberation evaluates only that small set ([Morris et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1177/09567976211005702)). Sequences generated "as they come to mind" deviate from randomness in nearly the same way as deliberate random generation, supporting a local-sampling account ([Castillo et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2026.106554)). This prior is efficient for everyday action and is exactly what suppresses novel, low-probability or norm-violating ideas; creative generation requires shifting the sampling distribution, not sampling longer. The interventions that work do precisely that: a "think in opposites" prompt nearly doubled success on Wason's 2-4-6 rule-discovery task by reducing repetition of the same hypothesis rather than by more testing ([Branchini, Bianchi & Burro 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12402026/)); verbalizing evidence against a first diagnosis lowered confidence and changed diagnoses ([Kojima et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11863399/)); counterfactual explanations improved decision accuracy more than causal ones because they convey more possibilities ([Celar & Byrne 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10520145/)); and judging whether an agent could have done otherwise recruits a 14-cluster prefrontal–parietal–striatal network beyond judging what happened ([Bernhard et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0001676)). Confirmation bias has a common individual-difference factor across paradigms ([Berthet et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11561113/)) and is better described as a confidence-gated search policy than a reasoning error.

## Offline brains compose hypotheses: replay recombines and REM links distant structure

Human replay is inference over learned structure, not a tape. During rest after learning an ordering rule, MEG replay "followed instead a sequence implied by learned abstract knowledge" rather than visual experience, with a factorized position × identity code preceding the sensory code by about 50 ms ([Liu et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6657653)). At reward receipt in a task where value could propagate to untraversed paths, backward replay at a 160 ms lag represented non-local paths more than local ones, was stronger for high-utility paths (p = 0.003), and trials with it showed higher non-local learning rates (0.70 vs 0.61) ([Liu et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7610948)). In the 2023 constructive-inference study, within-trial replay content moved from early unspecific sequences, through sequences involving only candidate "present" blocks, to late sequences involving only the correct ones, "progressing from predictable to uncertain elements and gradually converging on the correct configuration," though the authors found no replay–performance correlation and present generative replay as a candidate mechanism ([Schwartenbeck et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10914680)). Awake rest does selection on the scale of minutes: in 11 intracranial patients, the cortical trace that was later retrieved was the one reactivated more strongly during a 30-minute rest, reactivation peaks coincided with hippocampal ripples (p < 0.001), and hippocampal–default-mode interactions *preceded* cortical reactivation ([Duan et al. 2025](https://faculty.wcas.northwestern.edu/~paller/Progress%20in%20Neurobiology%202025.pdf)), while awake replay prioritizes weak memories and converts into durable gains mainly after sleep ([Schapiro et al. 2018](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/natcom/v9y2018i1d10.1038_s41467-018-06213-1.html)). Together with the earlier report's ripple findings, the picture is of a default-mode context that selects which traces are reactivated, and of reactivation that assembles rather than copies.

Sleep-dependent restructuring is real but stage-contested. The 2004 result that sleep more than doubled insight into a hidden rule has been followed by nulls, and a preregistered 90-person nap study found that **85.7% of the N2 group gained insight against 63.6% for N1 and 55.5% for wake**, with N2 the only significant predictor and the aperiodic 1/f slope predicting insight beyond stage ([Löwe et al. 2024](https://2024.ccneuro.org/pdf/29_Paper_authored_CCN_2024.pdf)); this contradicts the N1 "creative sweet spot" the earlier report flagged as unreplicated, and the reconciliation is that a regime of reduced constraint reachable in N1 or N2 matters, not the stage label. The strongest causal datum for REM is that cueing abstraction problems during REM but not slow-wave sleep improved later performance, significant only at the one-week retest ([Pereira et al. 2023](https://nottingham-repository.worktribe.com/output/19010035/rule-abstraction-is-facilitated-by-auditory-cuing-in-rem-sleep)). The dominant framework assigns NREM to hippocampus-driven gist abstraction and REM to forming "novel associations between distant knowledge," in which "only connections with some structural overlap are presumably retained, so REM acts as an unconscious search for shared structure" ([Lewis, Knoblich & Poe 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7543772/)). Two models say why the alternation is needed: when neocortex cannot replay its own knowledge in REM, "the information there gets overwritten" ([Singh, Norman & Schapiro 2022](https://neurosciencenews.com/memory-sleep-hippocampus-neocortex-21719)), and in a GAN-inspired cortex, REM "adversarial dreaming" that linearly combines multiple random episodic memories with spontaneous activity was required for linearly separable semantic representations (about **59% CIFAR-10 and 79% SVHN** linear readout), with removal of REM leaving separability "significantly below" the full model even after many epochs, while NREM perturbed replay bought robustness to occlusion ([Deperrois et al. 2022](https://elifesciences.org/articles/76384)). That specific bet, that REM input is a mixture of unrelated episodes plus noise, matches dream phenomenology: about 49% of dreams combine multiple memory sources, REM dreams incorporate recent lab material more than NREM dreams (46.9% vs 28.7%), and recent sources decline across the night independent of stage ([Picard-Deland et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10091095)).

What dreams are *for* remains unresolved: the field's own 2024 exchange concedes progress needs "risky, clearly formulated, and empirically testable" mutually exclusive predictions that have mostly not been run ([Revonsuo, Valli & Tuominen reply](https://research.utu.fi/converis/portal/detail/Publication/3069456?lang=en_US)), and Hoel's overfitted-brain hypothesis, that dreams supply corrupted out-of-distribution input "for increasing generalizability," has no completed deprivation-versus-generalization test ([Hoel 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8134940)). The first controlled REM dream-engineering study nonetheless moves incubation beyond the Dormio N1 work the earlier report covered. In 20 frequent lucid dreamers over 39 lab nights, cueing unsolved puzzles' soundtracks during REM reliably inserted them into dreams (β = 1.84, p < .001); conceptual incorporation predicted next-morning solving (β = 1.24, p = .037) while mere sensory incorporation did not; cueing alone had no main effect; and solving after incorporation was lower in lucid dreams (11%) than non-lucid ones (46%) ([Konkoly et al. 2026](https://faculty.wcas.northwestern.edu/~paller/NeuroCSC2026.pdf)). The content, not the cue, carries the benefit, and deliberate control during the recombination phase appears to re-impose the very constraints offline processing relaxes. For an architecture, the offline phase should alternate veridical-plus-perturbed replay (robustness, gist) with generative mixing of unrelated episodes and noise (semantic abstraction), run reduction after exploration has plateaued, order constructive replay from predictable to uncertain elements, and keep top-down control low while recombining.

## The brain idles near criticality, and noradrenaline, acetylcholine and tasks tune the landscape

The dynamical-systems consensus has shifted from "the brain is critical" to "distance-to-criticality is a regulated state variable with a quiescent-wake optimum." Resting fMRI from 136 participants gave a pooled effective coupling of about **0.88** (1.0 = critical) once autocorrelation and sampling artefacts were controlled, with the lead author arguing near-critical operation "can retain many of the proposed computational benefits" without the instability of sitting at the point ([Calvo et al. 2026, via Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-03-human-brain-critical.html)); a meta-analysis of 140 avalanche distributions argues criticality is a homeostatic set-point but concedes the functional-benefit evidence is "correlational or indirect" ([Hengen & Shew 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12374783/)). Distance moves with state: over 10–14 days of continuous recording in rats, extended waking increased distance to criticality (P = 2.4e−16) and sleep restored it, with distance predicting future sleep better than time of day or slow-wave activity ([Xu et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11272063)); in mice, quiet wake was closest to criticality, NREM farther, and within wake distance rose with body movement ([Sooter et al. 2024](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.05.29.596499v1.full)); and the behaviour-coupled, near-critical part of awake cortical dynamics lives in a subspace of rarely more than three dimensions ([Fontenele et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11051676/)). The most concrete mechanism by which "near-critical" cashes out is information transfer: across humans, rats and mice, slow thalamocortical rhythms were encoded in the other region's high gamma during waking, propofol pushed dynamics toward chaos and seizures toward hyperstable periodicity, both cutting transfer, and a mean-field model showed transfer peaks when the largest Lyapunov exponent is about zero ([Toker et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10805384/)). Maximal capacity to combine slow contextual with fast content signals is a plausible prerequisite for combinatorial thought, but no 2023–2026 human study has manipulated distance to criticality and measured flexibility or idea generation, and critics show scale-free statistics can arise from resource-coupled non-critical dynamics ([Sipling, Zhang & Di Ventra 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21071)).

Metastability is the better vocabulary for how thought moves between states. The 2025 consensus review insists that observing switching is not sufficient to infer metastability, that "metastable cycling can occur in the absence of noise" via delays, heterogeneous coupling, saddle cycles and ghost attractors, that metastability is maximal at an intermediate global coupling, that removing hub nodes *increases* it, and that clinical signatures follow an inverted U (lower in ageing, Alzheimer's and anaesthesia, higher in psychosis), so "there may be a 'sweet spot' for metastability in healthy brain functioning" ([Hancock et al. 2025](https://ccs.fau.edu/hbblab/pdfs/2024_Hancock_Kelso_NRN.pdf)). The two-knob answer to what controls dwell time comes from the Shine lab. In 7T fMRI, phasic locus coeruleus bursts *lowered* the energy landscape so unlikely transitions became more probable and were followed by an anterior-to-posterior travelling wave and increased frontoparietal integration peaking 6–9 s later, while basal-forebrain bursts *raised* it; in meditators, LC activity peaked about 4 s before the button press signalling awareness of mind-wandering ([Munn et al. 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-26268-x)). Causally, inactivating nucleus basalis in two macaques flattened the landscape and lowered transfer entropy among cholinergic targets, so acetylcholine "stabilizes macroscale brain states by deepening attractor wells" ([Taylor et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11255396/)). Serotonergic psychedelics act as a global version of the noradrenergic knob: LSD and psilocybin lowered the control energy for every pair of brain-state transitions, most efficiently along the 5-HT2A receptor map ([Singleton et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-33578-1)). The best-controlled psychedelic dataset, precision functional mapping with about 18 scans per person, found 25 mg psilocybin produced a whole-brain connectivity change of **3.52 units of day-to-day variability against 1.10 for an arousal-matched stimulant**, driven by desynchronization in association cortex that scaled with 5-HT2A density and tracked mystical-experience scores (r² = 0.81); critically, engaging in a perceptual task suppressed both the connectivity change and the desynchronization, and anterior hippocampus–default-mode coupling stayed reduced for about three weeks ([Siegel et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11291293)). Under LSD, Lempel–Ziv complexity rose but external video decoupled it from experience, with no correlation exceeding |r| = 0.1 ([Mediano et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10853937)). Two cautions follow for anyone tempted to equate novelty with entropy: 7 of 12 fMRI entropy metrics showed no psilocybin effect in a multi-metric study ([McCulloch et al. 2023/2026](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.07.03.23292164v2.full.pdf+html)), and psychosis shows *higher* metastability with worse function ([Hancock et al. 2025](https://ccs.fau.edu/hbblab/pdfs/2024_Hancock_Kelso_NRN.pdf)). Entropy indexes a state's susceptibility, not productive thought.

This resolves into one axis that unifies the earlier report's separate levers. Noradrenaline and 5-HT2A agonism lower the effective precision of dominant top-down states in association cortex, flattening barriers and raising transition rates; acetylcholine and task engagement raise precision and restabilize. The locus coeruleus bursts that the earlier report found at event boundaries and before spontaneous thought transitions are, in this vocabulary, landscape-flattening resets, and "release from control" is lowered top-down precision, which the REBUS account of psychedelics states explicitly as relaxing "the precision of high-level priors or beliefs" ([Carhart-Harris & Friston 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6588209/)). Oscillatory evidence adds timing. In 93 intracranial patients, theta and alpha travelling waves propagated posterior-to-anterior during successful encoding and anterior-to-posterior during recall, and participants with bidirectional propagation encoded **5.8%** better, so the ability to switch wave regime, not either regime alone, predicted success ([Mohan et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-01838-3)); wave shape (planar, rotational, concentric) distinguished tasks and, in 13 of 26 clusters, individual letters held in memory ([Das et al. 2024](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.01.26.577456v2.full)); and theta–gamma phase–amplitude coupling, not raw power, decoded correct from incorrect retention in 12 of 16 patients, with gamma bursts "consistently restricted to specific phase ranges of the theta oscillations across participants" ([Borderie et al. 2024](https://lead.ube.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Borderie_et_al._2024.pdf)). These are the best current mechanistic story for "phase gates what gets combined," but no study has yet shown that manipulating phase changes which items get bound, and sceptics hold that waves merely reflect synaptic activity ([Quanta 2026](https://www.quantamagazine.org/surprisingly-complex-waves-reveal-the-brains-inner-workings-20260930/)). For an agent, the translation is a global gain parameter coupled inversely to external task demand, scheduled so that generation happens in a low-precision, high-transition regime (quiet wake, post-sleep) and evaluation in a high-precision one, with phasic resets used to trigger topic jumps.

## Curiosity tracks learning progress, and affect validates whatever mode is running

The quantity human curiosity best tracks is improvement rate. Among 382 adults choosing freely among learnable and unlearnable activities, a model over competence plus learning progress fit best, learning-progress-driven participants mastered at least two activities **90.5% of the time against 70.6%** for competence-driven ones, and the sensitivity appeared without instruction ([Ten et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8514490/)). Four-year-olds prefer options with higher novelty and higher learning progress, and progress sensitivity predicts learning ([Poli et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.14158)); eight-month-olds whose attention tracked information gain more strongly had higher IQ at 3.5 years ([de Boer et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.70090)). Curiosity is also path-dependent: across 5,831 participants, satisfying answers increased subsequent curiosity only when the new information was semantically related to prior learning, supporting a reinforcement-learning over an arousal account ([Abir et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2529176123)), and the number of candidate answers a person has in mind predicts curiosity and five-day memory ([Recht & Yeung 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2026.106626)). The construct is nonetheless an umbrella: across 820 participants, nine information-demand tasks shared one axis contrasting directed and random exploration, and the best trait predictors were need for cognition, thrill seeking and stress tolerance rather than openness or joyous exploration ([Jach et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2415236121)). The memory boost is focal rather than a general open window: unrelated facts presented near a high-curiosity answer were encoded *worse* ([Keller et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00234-w)), and children's curiosity benefit did not pass through the preregistered hippocampus or accumbens ([Ziv et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2026.101687)). At scale, among 482,760 Wikipedia readers in 50 countries, a "dancer" forward-flow dimension (semantic leap distance) correlated 0.79 with information diversity and only −0.05 with the tight-versus-loose "hunter/busybody" axis, so creativity-relevant exploration is orthogonal to network density, and readers showed lower forward flow than random walks ([Zhou et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11506172/)). The proposal that curiosity and creativity share one novelty-seeking mechanism ([Ivancovsky, Baror & Bar 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0140525x23002807)) drew the objection that novelty is neither necessary nor sufficient ([Holm & Schrater 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0140525x23003321)), and no causal study shows that inducing curiosity improves subsequent divergent thinking; the curiosity–creativity links located are correlational.

Affect's role has moved from "positive broadens" to validation of whatever processing mode is dominant: positive affect "confers value on whatever cognitive orientation is currently accessible," so it can narrow as well as broaden ([Huntsinger, Isbell & Clore 2014](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037669)). Naturally occurring positive affect predicted conceptual breadth only under low arousal and reversed under high arousal ([Cognitive Research 2022](https://cognitiveresearchjournal.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s41235-022-00447-w)); a registered replication of the motivational-intensity narrowing effect found no attentional-breadth difference at all ([Goodhew et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1080/02699931.2025.2549318)); and in 210 employees sampled three times daily, evening idea generation was highest when negative mood *declined* over the day and midday self-motivation was high ([Bledow et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/jopy.12913)). Shenhav's affective-gradient hypothesis makes affect the only form of value that drives action, with motivated behaviour as movement along how imagined future states would feel ([Shenhav 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11620945/)), and Barrett's programme now has 7T support for a large-scale allostatic–interoceptive system whose connectivity corroborated more than 96% of tract-tracing connections ([Zhang et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-02087-x)). Interoceptive rhythms behave as gain controls: responses were about 19–21 ms faster during inspiration, and drift-diffusion modelling showed inspiration lowered decision boundaries and biased faces toward "happy" ([Brændholt et al. 2025](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013086)); near-threshold visual sensitivity was higher during inspiration and people entrained breathing to predictable stimuli ([Chalas et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71604-8)); and rTMS over right anterior insula altered both heartbeat counting and judgments on the emotional and social dimensions of abstract concepts ([Mancano & Papagno 2026](https://doi.org/10.1523/jneurosci.0238-25.2025)), the first causal link between interoception and abstract content. No study has tested cardiac or respiratory phase against creativity.

The embodiment literature has been pruned by replication. An 18-lab preregistered test found no action–sentence compatibility effect, with a meta-analytic estimate near zero ([Morey et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-021-01927-8)); motor-cortex rTMS gave BF01 > 30 against a role in action-sentence meaning ([Solana et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2024.108832)); power posing lost its hormonal effects after bias correction ([Elkjær et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691620919358)); and handedness does not predict creativity ([Morgan & Casasanto 2025](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-025-02717-2)). What survives is the body doing visible representational work. Encouraged iconic gestures predicted alternate-uses fluency (b = 0.24), originality and elaboration in 80 adults, whereas spontaneous iconic gestures *negatively* predicted flexibility, as if locking in one image category ([Hyusein & Göksun 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11142943/)); encouraging gesture raised children's creative ideas ([Kirk & Lewis 2017](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797616679183)); and the walking advantage disappeared when movement was restricted while unrestricted sitting also helped, implicating widened attention rather than locomotion ([Murali & Händel 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-021-01636-w)). Flow fits the same "expertise then release" pattern: in 32 jazz guitarists, high-flow improvisations showed transient hypofrontality in all players, but only experienced players showed reduced posterior default-mode activity and enhanced auditory–somatosensory activity, favouring optimized domain-specific processing over default-mode ideation ([Rosen et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2024.108824)), and in 400 participants the smooth-performance component of flow raised idea count but lowered originality while absorption raised originality and semantic distance ([Koivisto et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2026.104060)). The design lessons are a learning-progress-plus-empowerment curiosity signal, affect as a mode-validation rather than a breadth parameter, externalized partial representations in place of literal motor simulation, and generation by trained domain modules with control dialled down rather than by a generic default-mode generator.

## Children search hot, adults cool, and culture compounds what individuals cannot

Development quantifies the temperature knob. In a spatially correlated bandit with 281 participants aged 5–55, reward rose with age (r = 0.51) and unique options sampled fell; in a Gaussian-process model, **random temperature decreased with a changepoint at 7.7 years, directed (uncertainty-seeking) exploration at 9.1 years, and generalization increased with a changepoint at 12.7 years**, with the human trajectory best matched by stochastic hill-climbing with fast cooling ([Giron et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10663152/)). Preschoolers generate more diverse, less sequentially dependent guesses about a novel causal relation than adults ([Bonawitz et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qpj6n_v2)), find unusual conjunctive rules that adults miss ([Lucas et al. 2014](https://news.berkeley.edu/?p=39876)), and the advantage is caused by the evidence exploration generates: adults given child-like evidence learned like children ([Liquin & Gopnik 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2021.104940)). Children take on unnecessary costs in play while behaving efficiently in instrumental contexts ([Chu & Schulz 2023](https://doi.org/10.1162/opmi_a_00076)), and infants learn more about objects that violate expectations, test hypotheses matched to the violation type ([Stahl & Feigenson 2015](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa3799)) and stop exploring once the violation is explained ([Perez & Feigenson 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2021.104942)). The same cooling appears across species: younger killer whales produced more variable behaviours on an innovate task ([Hill et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10071-022-01635-3)), and across 203 birds from 15 species, novel motor solutions to extractive problems predicted wild innovation rates and brain size while associative learning, reversal learning and self-control did not ([Audet et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-024-02342-7)). Generating novelty is about producing varied actions, not inhibitory control. Expertise raises domain-specific quality without general gains: mathematics experts had higher mathematical but not general creativity ([Meier et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-39309-w)), only experts could predict a creator's creativity from a product ([Mutter & Hübner 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-64494-7)), and creativity training shrinks to about 0.3 SD after bias correction ([Sio & Lortie-Forgues 2024](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000432)).

Language's role has been settled in a direction that matters for architecture. Language is "primarily a tool for communication rather than thought": the language network is largely silent during reasoning, severe aphasics solve mathematics, play chess and plan, and languages look optimized for transmission ([Fedorenko, Piantadosi & Gibson 2024](https://news.mit.edu/2024/what-is-language-for-0703)). A 2026 follow-up found the language network unengaged during formal logic, aphasic patients normal on logic tasks, inductive reasoning in the multiple-demand network and deductive reasoning in regions distinct from both ([Kean et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2520095123)). The counter-position does not dispute this but holds that language "provides compact representations for abstract concepts and carries culturally accumulated abstractions" ([Lupyan, Gentry & Zettersten 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916251398539)), that it makes perception more categorical ([Lupyan et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2020.08.005)), and that hearing a shared label makes people sort novel shapes more categorically and aligns their representations ([Suffill, van Paridon & Lupyan 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/opmi_a_00153)). Inner speech fits the tool view: experience sampling puts it at about a quarter of waking moments with wide individual variation ([Heavey & Hurlburt 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2007.12.006)); adults with little inner speech were worse at verbal working memory and rhyme judgments but not task switching or categorical perception ([Nedergaard & Lupyan 2024, via PsyPost](https://www.psypost.org/anendophasia-scientists-examine-the-cognitive-impact-of-life-without-an-inner-voice)); inner speech and executive function doubly dissociate in 59 aphasic adults ([Fama et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2024.108997)); and dialogic inner speech recruits theory-of-mind regions ([Alderson-Day et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4692319)), which makes self-talk the natural vehicle for the simulated interlocutor Mercier and Sperber's account requires. Language's contribution to idea generation is upstream (installing categories and abstractions, as LILO's named abstractions do) and downstream (compressing, stabilizing, transmitting), not online.

At population scale, new ideas come from recombination under conditions that preserve diversity and allow selective learning. In a 12-generation transmission experiment with 3,450 participants, complex high-performing sorting algorithms persisted only when learners could choose whom to learn from; otherwise populations converged on simpler, more transmissible ones ([Thompson et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abn0915)). Larger populations improved cumulative culture only when participants could use an external record supporting payoff comparison and recombination ([Walker & Fay 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-40973-x)), which is the earlier report's homogenization finding read in reverse: external records lower the cost of comparison just as shared LLM suggestions raise the cost of divergence. Surprising advances tend to arise when scientists from one field publish for an audience in a distant one ([Shi & Evans 2023](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC10039062)); the highest-impact work rests on conventional combinations plus an intrusion of unusual ones ([Uzzi et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1240474)); and remote teams do more codified late-stage work and less conceptual work where knowledge is tacit ([Lin, Frey & Wu 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06767-1)). The claim that 45 million papers and 3.9 million patents are becoming less disruptive, linked to a narrowing in the use of prior knowledge ([Park, Leahey & Funk 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05543-x)), was challenged as an artefact of zero-reference entries ([Holst et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14583)) but survived re-analysis on the critics' own data, with the authors noting the decline has been documented in roughly 100 independent studies ([Park et al. 2025/2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00184)). In large fields, citations ossify and new papers rarely disrupt, plausibly because of limited absorptive capacity for novel ideas ([Chu & Evans 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021636118)). The population-level story mirrors the within-person one: as the knowledge base grows, search becomes more conventional unless something deliberately preserves variance. The chimpanzee data isolate what humans add: 14 naive chimpanzees learned a puzzle box from trained conspecifics within weeks after failing to innovate it during three months with all materials ([van Leeuwen et al. 2024](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC11132989)), so individual innovation exists widely but only connectivity plus faithful transmission makes it compound. Measured "thinking skills" are themselves partly cultural: unschooled Namibian children differed qualitatively from schooled UK children on standard executive tasks ([Kroupin et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2407955122)).

## The evidence licenses one generate–test–consolidate loop

No single paper combines these mechanisms, but each step below has at least one human-validated model or a quantitative machine demonstration behind it. The table orders them as a processing loop and grades the evidence so the architecture can weight them.

| Step | Procedure the evidence licenses | Strongest human evidence | Machine demonstration | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Propose | Draw a *few* candidates (3–5) from a likelihood × value prior; deliberately shift the prior with opposites, sparse cues or counterfactual framing before sampling more | Bear et al. 2020; Morris et al. 2021 (N = 3,972); Branchini et al. 2025 (success nearly doubled) | Dasgupta et al. 2017 sampling model | Human behavioural, replicated |
| 2. Compose | Build candidates from a named abstraction library; generate by revision operators (memorize, recurse, anti-unify) on existing programs rather than from scratch | Rule et al. 2024 (392 participants); Wong et al. 2022 part-naming | DreamCoder; LILO (naming improves reuse) | Human-calibrated model plus machine |
| 3. Transfer | Reuse structural forms across domains by rebinding a factorized structure code to new content | Mark et al. 2024 (entorhinal generalization); Courellis et al. 2024 (0.57 → 0.62) | TEM; El-Gaby 2024 mouse schema | Human neural, single tasks |
| 4. Transform | Edit the generative grammar itself: drop a constraint, negate it, promote a constant to a variable; frame-shift by metaphor | Ward/Karmiloff-Smith structured imagination (default = near neighbours) | Boden's analysis of AARON, EURISKO | Philosophical with case support |
| 5. Test | Partial counterfactual simulation of only the relevant elements; score "whether" and "how" separately | Gerstenberg 2024 (r = 0.96); Ho et al. 2022 construal (χ² = 2297) | Bass et al. 2021 partial simulation | Human, strong |

| 7. | 8. Evaluate | Attach confidence tags; evaluate at psychological distance or as a simulated interlocutor; use the "second-ranked" heuristic with caution | Lebuda et al. 2024 (r ≈ 0.1–0.3); Berg 2016/2019; Zhang et al. 2025; Mercier & Sperber | Shea & Frith 2019 (theory) | Human behavioural |

| 10. Tune gain | One precision/temperature parameter: flatten (NE/5-HT2A-like) for generation, deepen (ACh/task-like) for evaluation; phased rather than rapid alternation; phasic resets trigger topic jumps | Munn et al. 2021; Siegel et al. 2024 (task suppresses); Grahek et al. 2026 switching costs | Singleton et al. 2022 control-energy model | Correlational human, causal in macaque |

What the evidence does *not* license is equally important. It does not license unconscious deliberation as a generator (steps 1–5 are conscious or workspace-dependent operations; the unconscious supplies availability). It does not license entropy or complexity maximization as an objective, since entropy indexes susceptibility and decouples from experience under external input. It does not license information gain as the sole curiosity term, a wide workspace, rapid generate–evaluate alternation, or metaphoric body states as content generators. And it does not yet license a specific claim about which sleep stage or oscillatory phase an offline module should imitate; the defensible target is the regime (reduced top-down precision, recombinative mixing, delayed pruning), not the label.

## Conclusion

The deeper mechanisms converge on something more specific than the earlier report's "control policy over memory search." The thing being searched is a structured generative model with a program-like library and a factorized relational code; the search proposes few candidates by editing existing structures under a conventional prior; the proposals are tested by partial simulation and graded by a confidence signal that is also the stopping rule; everything must pass through a channel narrow enough to force compression, serial composition and externalization; and the single parameter that governs how far proposals stray is the precision of top-down states, set by noradrenergic and serotonergic flattening against cholinergic and task-driven stabilization. Seen this way, the earlier report's levers (walking, incubation, hypnagogia, loosened frontal control, hippocampal ripples, locus coeruleus resets) are all manipulations of that one parameter or of the offline consolidation schedule, and the known failure modes of machine ideation (homogeneous outputs, coherence collapse at high temperature, strong generation but weak evaluation) are the predictable consequences of lacking a learning-progress drive, a disentangled structure code and a calibrated second-order evaluator.

Three implications follow for anyone building an agent. First, the bottleneck is the design, not the bug: capacity-limited broadcast forces the specialization and compositionality that make ideas transferable, and the machine results put the optimum at a handful of slots. Second, the generation problem cannot be solved downstream; the bad-lot objection, Boden's asymmetry and the metacognition data all say that evaluation only ranks what was proposed, so effort belongs in shifting the proposal distribution and in the offline expand-then-merge loop. Third, the field's most important open experiments are the ones an agent could run on itself: whether a learning-progress-plus-empowerment drive yields more structural edits than information gain, whether delayed merging preserves exploration as AXIOM's Cross result suggests, and whether a simulated interlocutor closes the gap between self-rated and expert-rated idea quality. The human literature cannot yet answer these because no study has manipulated distance to criticality, oscillatory phase or neuromodulatory gain while measuring idea generation; an architecture that exposes these parameters becomes a test bed for the theory it implements.
