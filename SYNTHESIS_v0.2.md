A default number with no source is marked "placeholder" (or "design cap" where it bounds cost by choice) and is tuned on the dev-tune split. Labels Pr1-Pr19 refer to the design principles in ARCHITECTURE.md section 1; they are numbered apart from the priority labels P0, P1 and P2.

Three decisions from the review shape every section and are stated once here:

1. The control loop is code. `harness/think.py` makes every decision (allocation, admission, stop, reset, verification order) from the run's jsonl files, and `harness/run_card.py` renders fixed prompt templates and calls the model once per step with a fresh session, a per-call sandbox directory holding only the routed inputs, and a per-role tool allowlist. LLM calls are leaves. The `/think` skill only launches the driver and summarizes `answer.json`. Without this, every "structural" row below would be a prompt.
2. Verification types are split. External types (execution, solver, demo_match, sourced_fact) are the only ones that make a candidate "verified". A same-model falsifier that finds no counterexample is `llm_falsifier`, a premise agreed by three fresh contexts is `llm_consensus`; both are "checked", never "verified", and are reported in their own calibration stratum; they enter the stop rule only behind `flags.llm_falsifier_validated`, on their own track with threshold T_checked (EVL-02). The primary outcome is ground-truth correctness on benchmarks with an exact or executable key. Answer keys and held-out test outputs never enter the loop: they are used only by the harness scorer after a run, so no arm can select with the label (EVL-15).
3. Cost is defined. B is a multiple of the measured plain chain-of-thought token count per item (m x CoT, m in {5, 10, 20, 40}, 20 primary), counting input and output tokens of every call including system-prompt overhead, cached input at full price, encode tokens, and offline tokens amortized per item. The budget is checked before every spawn and every verification, against a counter that adds the usage of every call as it returns: encode before the loop, then generators, the converter, the judge, the agreement probe (run once per problem and cached), every verify attempt and restructure. est_cost and est_verify are defined in `harness/plan.md`. Hard caps: m_verify = 3 candidates verified per round, k_max = 3 falsifier attempts, at most 2 revise spawns per round, R_max default 3.

## Unifying picture

What the two research rounds say the mind does when it makes a new idea, stated as claims the architecture acts on. The grade in brackets is the grade for the claim as used, after review.

1. There is no creativity faculty. New ideas come from controlled retrieval and construction over ordinary semantic memory, executive control and episodic recombination. The design orchestrates general capacities and never adds a creativity module or prompt. Whether executing a procedure in isolated contexts beats describing it in one prompt is a project hypothesis that no catalog entry supports or refutes; it is tested by the described-strategies ladder. [contested for the no-module claim (preprint ALE, mostly verbal tasks, countered by Chen 2025 and Liu 2024); hypothesis for structure over instruction] OTH-02, MEM-01, EVL-17.

2. Generation and selection are different control regimes. Low-control states help divergent output (d about 0.93) and leave convergent accuracy undetermined (d 0.16, CI -0.31 to 0.63; the CI is taken from `research_notes/How the brain generates new ideas/modulators_and_practical_levers.md` line 81, since the catalog row gives only d=0.16, null); tight control helps selection. The asymmetry is inferred across meta-analyses, not tested directly. Each regime gets its own fresh context and its own template, and roles change only by spawning. The engineering reason is blind judging without self-preference. [inferred; indirect meta-analytic and correlational] CTL-01, STA-01, GEN-09, EVL-03, CTL-17.

3. What comes to mind first is a sample from a prior shaped by likelihood and normality. People propose a few cached candidates and evaluate only those. That moving away from defaults raises creativity is an inference with preprint support only. A new idea therefore needs a changed input (different facts first, withheld domain cues, a transformed constraint, a remote source), and each such input is a lever with a prior of no effect until its ablation says otherwise. [replicated for default sampling; inferred for the design move] GEN-03, REP-02, GEN-10, GEN-06, REP-08, SOC-02, SOC-07, GEN-11.

4. Structure codes that generalize across content accompany zero-shot transfer in brains and models (correlational, plus models). Analogy works in LLMs mainly when the model can re-encode the problem in executable form (Webb 2025); explicit role-mapping tables are an engineering check, not an evidence-backed one. Executable notation lets a tool, not prose, do the reasoning; this is an engineering reason (it gives the verifier something to run), not a replicated finding about notation. [correlational for structure codes; single-study for code re-encoding; theory for notation changing thinkable operations] REP-01, REP-03, REP-15, GEN-08, GEN-15.

5. Felt rightness is not evidence. False insights are inducible, warnings do not remove them, confidence suppresses disconfirming evidence, imagined simulation is incomplete, people have little insight into the quality of their own ideas (self-assessment vs rated performance r about 0.1 to 0.3 on the Alternate Uses task, Lebuda et al. 2024, N=425 and preregistered N=317; the task is named in `research_notes/Deeper mechanisms of human thought/reasoning_metacognition_and_working_memory.md` line 82, not in the catalog row), and judging others' ideas is better but often near chance at selection. Confidence may only decide verification order and effort before verification; acceptance depends on verification type and agreement, never on the judge's confidence. [replicated] EVL-01, EVL-02, EVL-03, EVL-04, EVL-16, REP-10, MSR-04.

6. People control the conditions of thought: when to stop, when to opt out, where to spend effort, and which signal (agreement, prediction error, surprise) triggers deliberation. In humans with strong bias, thinking longer rarely corrects a fixated answer; on matrix-style problems deliberation does correct. Neither pattern is tested in LLMs, so the "switch, do not try harder" rule is a default with an ablation. [replicated for stopping and opt-out; preprint for the fixation claim; untested for LLMs] CTL-05, CTL-06, CTL-07, CTL-08, CTL-04, CTL-14.

7. Fixation is released by changing the representation or the context, not by effort. Incubation helps (d 0.39, publication bias possible, filler moderator unsettled), and which component does the work is contested. For an agent, the cheapest testable candidate is a fresh context that never saw the failed attempts, plus operators that transform the problem. [meta-analysis for incubation; contested for the active ingredient; single-study for operators] CTL-02, CTL-11, CTL-18, GEN-12, REP-09, REP-05, STA-04.

8. Good ideas are built on, not just picked. Far ideas pay off as stepping stones through revision with feedback; selection by developed versions beats selection of raw ideas in one paper on creative products. [observational plus single-study] GEN-14, EVL-04, SOC-04, SOC-05, REP-07.

9. Memory contributes mainly through which procedures, first moves and traps it carries forward, and only verified outcomes should write it. Generative abstraction drifts toward schemas in models and can narrow later search (an analogy from bibliometrics), so memory is numerical and script-derived at P0 and P1, capped, audited, and tested on later problems only, against frozen snapshots. [meta-analysis for the memory-creativity link (r 0.19, correlational); replicated within one lab for first-move retraining; models only for drift] MEM-01, MEM-05, MEM-08, MEM-11, MEM-14, MEM-16, MEM-17, MSR-11.

10. Groups accumulate what individuals cannot: generate independently then pool (for LLMs the reason is anchoring, not production blocking), keep a scored external record, let payoff-biased selection choose which lineage to extend, and keep connectivity partial. The population mechanics are untested below Stage 4 and run there. [meta-analysis for nominal groups (human mechanism not transferable); single-study and observational for the rest] SOC-01, SOC-03, SOC-04, SOC-05.

11. Human judgment of idea quality is weak, divergent-thinking and distance scores barely track real achievement, LLMs saturate them, and reported gains shrink under good controls. So the primary outcome is ground-truth correctness at matched compute against the stronger of two best-of-N nulls, decomposed into coverage and selection, with abstention scored separately and every keep/drop decision preregistered. [meta-analysis] MSR-01, MSR-02, MSR-04, MSR-05, MSR-07, MSR-14, MSR-17.

12. State and offline effects (walking, sleep stages, mood, criticality) are real in humans; the catalog rates their LLM analogues as speculative or loose, and none is attributable at this scale. Each is at most an ablation flag with a prior of no effect, never a justification. [contested] STA-03, STA-05, STA-13, STA-14, CTL-18, MSR-18.

Design notes. Representation in v0.2 does three jobs. First, it makes the problem checkable: a validated, hashed, read-only formal model or a merged constraint checklist written before any generation is what /verify runs and what the absolute bar refers to (REP-03, EVL-11, EVL-15). The review's blocker here was that the formal card edited the same file the verifier ran; the file is now split, hashed and validated, and the formalizable subset is fixed per benchmark in advance. Second, it changes the input each generator conditions on: shuffled fact order (REP-08), withheld domain cues (REP-02), neutral relabelling (REP-09), and transformed constraints (GEN-12). These are levers with a prior of no effect, not "the only real diversity lever"; each is measured against the permutation null, and views built by LLM rewriters are schema-linted because priors can enter at that stage (measured by the per-item harm rate). Third, at P1 it produces the signature (REP-01) that keys strategy memory; at P0 the key is the family label, because an LLM-written free-text signature would split classes until posteriors never fill. Encode overhead is logged as `encode_tokens`, capped at 2x one CoT call (P1 outputs are skipped when the cap is hit), and charged to every arm that uses the outputs, including seeded best-of-N. Nothing in this section asks the model to introspect: dropped elements, fixating cues and property tables are script-computed or schema-linted.

Design notes. Generation is a portfolio of strategy cards, each a versioned YAML procedure against `memory/strategies/schema.json` (steps[{id, template, inputs[], model, max_tokens, n, parser}]) that `harness/run_card.py` executes: it renders the fixed templates in `memory/strategies/templates/<card>.<step>.txt` with the routed file contents, copies only those files into `runs/<id>/calls/<call-id>/in/`, and calls the model once per step with a fresh session, cwd set to the sandbox, no file tools for generators (Bash only for formal and abduce-probe), and a logged rendered prompt. The orchestrator never writes a generator prompt. This answers the review's blocker that an LLM orchestrator reading YAML and composing prompts is instructional one level up, and the second blocker that subagents with Read and Glob over `runs/` can see defaults, judgments and each other. P0 cards: seeded, low_prior, transform (3-call), formal, revise (1-parent operators). P1 cards: analogy, relabel, compose, revise 2-parent operators; abduce-probe at Stage 4. P2: sparse. The thesis contrast is built into the arms: seeded best-of-N isolates diversity, best-of-N with the same verifier isolates verification, and the go/no-go uses the stronger of the two per benchmark. Tool use is tested within one card by the exec_feedback flag, not by comparing cards that differ in procedure.

Design notes. Evaluation is split into a blind judge and a verifier, and the judge is never the final word when a verifier exists. The review's main internal objection was that the design note said p_cal had two uses while the orchestrator used it in the stop rule and the choice among verified candidates; v0.2 removes judge confidence from everything after a pass. Its arms at matched token spend are: plain prompting; best-of-N with majority vote; best-of-N with the same /verify for selection; seeded best-of-N with the same /verify (random seeds and permutations, no cards); instruction-only (seeded best-of-N plus one fixed broadening line); the scaffold; the described-strategies ladder (L0 one context with the menu, L1 N contexts with the menu, L2 N contexts with assigned card text and no routed views; L3 is the scaffold); and from P1 the memory-snapshot arms. The go/no-go statistic is the scaffold minus the stronger best-of-N null, because unseeded best-of-N without temperature control is a weak null. Cost is defined in plan.md as input plus output tokens of every model call, orchestrator overhead included, cached input at full price, plus amortized offline tokens; it is read per call from the CLI's JSON usage into `results/<batch>/cost.jsonl`, and best-of-N arms stop at the scaffold's realized spend per item. The total run count is bounded: budget sweep for the scaffold and the primary null only; R_max sweep for the scaffold at the largest budget only; dev subset of about 150 items stratified by difficulty; 3 replicates on dev, 5-10 on test; the ladder at 1x only. The first-answer transition matrix uses the scaffold's own round-0 candidate against a churn null. Calibration is reported per difficulty bin with defined bins and minimum cell sizes. The MacGyver blocker is handled by making it secondary with a human-labelled subset; whether BRAINTEASER and ARC-1 have headroom is checked by a P0 pilot before the item set is fixed (see Open questions).

## What we will not build

Every ANTI finding is listed with the component it rules out and the guard that enforces the rule. Guards are design conventions unless a row above gives evidence; none is justified by brain fidelity.

- GEN-06 (serial order): no agent asked for 30 ideas by default; the count is a dev-tuned parameter (sweep 4/8/16) and extra budget buys fresh contexts.
- GEN-07 (unconscious integration): no blended-vector or priming-only combination. Every call with more than one candidate artifact in its routed inputs is a logged revise or recombine call; embeddings only retrieve, rank and dedup.

## Open questions

1. Does verification or procedure control carry the gain? The go/no-go compares the scaffold with the stronger of best-of-N with the same verifier and seeded best-of-N with the same verifier. If the scaffold does not beat that at matched cost, the thesis fails even when it beats plain prompting. Run: Stage 3, keyed benchmarks, 5-10 replicates on test, cluster bootstrap over items.

2. Does structural execution beat instruction? The ladder answers it in parts: L1 - L0 is isolation, L2 - L1 is assignment, L3 - L2 is input routing plus feedback. Only an equivalence result within the preregistered margin may be read as "the machinery is not earning its cost".

3. Do the gate benchmarks have headroom? BRAINTEASER is 4-way multiple choice from 2023 and ARC-1 items have circulated; current models may sit near ceiling or have seen them. The P0 pilot runs the plain arm on dev and keeps only items with accuracy between 20% and 80%, substituting reconstruction variants, fresh paraphrases or ARC-AGI-2 public eval items. Until the pilot runs, the benchmark set is provisional and the power calculation cannot be done.

4. Does strategy memory transfer? Unknown whether per-class posteriors fill within the ledger volume one benchmark run supplies. Run: snapshot curves at 0/100/300/600 train rows on fixed held-out items; if the share of held-out items whose class has N_min rows is below about 30%, the family key is too coarse or the cards too similar, and the memory test is declared uninformative.

5. Is judge confidence worth anything beyond agreement? p_raw enters the calibration model only when dev-tune has 300 labelled pairs and it improves held-out Brier. Run: agreement-only vs p_raw-only vs both, ECE per defined difficulty bin with CIs; fall back to agreement if ECE drift exceeds 0.1.

6. What is incubation's active ingredient for an agent? The CTL-11 reset family (continue same context, fresh plus failures, fresh without failures, no reset) decides whether anything beyond a context wipe is worth tokens; the with-vs-without-failures arm runs at P1.

7. Does any fixation-breaking card beat resampling on BRAINTEASER insight items? Run: transform (3-call DAG) and low_prior leave-one-out on the lateral subset; if transform's pass rate against ground truth (harness-scored, since the key never enters the loop) is zero on the first dev batch it drops to P1.

8. Do generators with deterministic tool feedback outperform tool-free ones? Answered within one card by the exec_feedback flag (none, final-only, iterative) and by the same-card with-vs-without-shell ablation on ARC dev (STA-10, a P1 arm run post-gate), not by comparing cards that differ in procedure.

9. Does the coverage vs selection decomposition show the bottleneck? If coverage is high and selection low, build effort moves to the verifier and the counterexample check (SOC-09); if coverage is low, to cards and seeds. Computed on keyed benchmarks only.

10. Do islands with migration beat full pooling in population search for diffs? Untested below Stage 4. Run on the nano-scale rediscovery ladder: isolated vs islands vs pooled on records rediscovered per GPU-hour.

11. What is the right abstention policy on forced-choice benchmarks? Both scorings are reported; the opt-out level is set on dev-tune where the best candidate's precision falls below the plain arm's accuracy.

12. Does memory narrow search as the ledger grows? The paired diversity and coverage delta (snapshot vs empty memory) and the memory-blind generator's wins per call against its 1/n expectation answer it; a negative CI triggers a cap reduction and suppression trials.

13. Can MacGyver be scored at all? Only on a human-labelled subset (100-150 items, second rater on a subset, kappa reported). Until that labelling is done, every MacGyver metric is unmeasured, and the benchmark is secondary.

14. Does the realized per-problem cost fit the budget? Caps bound it (m_verify 3, k_max 3, 2 revise spawns per round, R_max 3, encode cap 2x CoT), but the realized spend of a scaffold run including subagent system-prompt overhead is unknown until measured; plan.md states the cap and the harness reports realized spend beside it.

15. Does a wander-style spontaneous trajectory ever pay off for an LLM? No evidence either way. It stays a flagged arm on the open-ended design set after the P0 gate.

## Appendix: Verification, objections and resolutions

The pre-review draft of this synthesis and of ARCHITECTURE v0.2 were reviewed section by section under two lenses, evidence fidelity (does the row say what the notes say, and does the agent rule follow from it) and LLM reality and implementability (can the mechanism be built with Claude Code primitives, is it structural in fact, can its metric be computed, and is its cost bounded). 288 objections were raised: 19 blockers, 160 majors and 109 minors by the reviewers' own labels. Every blocker and major was resolved by changing content; minors were resolved where the fix was cheap, which was nearly all of them. Six matters remain open and are listed at the end.

Cross-cutting changes (applied in every section):
- The orchestrator is code (`harness/think.py`) and cards are executed by `harness/run_card.py` from fixed templates with per-call sandboxes and tool allowlists; rendered prompts are logged and linted. This answered the blockers that an LLM orchestrator composing prompts is instructional one level up, that subagents could read the shared `runs/` tree, and that the decision log could not be checked independently.
- Verification types are split into external and adversarial-LLM; only external makes "verified"; the stop rule and the choice among verified candidates no longer see judge confidence. This answered the blocker that a same-model falsifier was counted as an external artifact and the major that p_cal was used after a pass.
- Cost is defined in CoT units with per-spawn budget checks and hard caps on verification, revise spawns and rounds; encode tokens are charged to every arm that uses the outputs. This answered the four cost blockers.
- The primary outcome is ground-truth correctness on keyed benchmarks; MacGyver is secondary with a human-labelled subset. This answered the blockers on MacGyver ground truth in Evaluation and Measurement.
- Memory transfer is measured against frozen snapshots of increasing size on fixed held-out items. This answered the three blockers that "live vs frozen on held-out families" was zero by construction.
- Every unsourced numeric default is marked placeholder and swept on dev-tune; every grade that covered a side claim is split or lowered in the row; human-only findings used for LLM rules are labelled analogy with a prior of no effect.
