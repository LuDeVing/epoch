# Pilot: a second check-in desk (March 2025)

We opened a second desk from 8:00 to 10:00 for three weeks, staffed by a nurse borrowed from the back office.

Result: the average morning wait went from 24 to 19 minutes. Not enough to justify it, and the back office could
not spare the nurse after the three weeks. The pilot ended on 28 March 2025.

What we learned: the second desk was often idle while the first had a queue, because patients stayed in the one
line they knew. Signs did not help. The nurse spent half her time on the printer and the phone, not on check-in.
