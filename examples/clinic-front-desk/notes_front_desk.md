# Front desk, how it works today

Two receptionists, Maria and Tom, from 7:30 to 16:00. Both do check-in, phone calls and the printer.

Check-in: the patient hands over the form (or fills it at the desk), the receptionist types it into the system,
checks the insurance card, prints a label and points the patient to the waiting area. The system part takes about
90 seconds per patient; a patient who fills the form at the desk takes three to four minutes more.

About a third of patients fill the form at the desk. The form has 23 fields; 9 of them are the same every visit.

The queue is one line for both desks. Patients with an appointment and walk-ins stand in the same line.

The phone rings most between 8:00 and 9:30. A call takes a receptionist away from the desk for one to three minutes.

Mornings are the problem: 60 percent of the day's arrivals come before 10:00, and the average wait in that window
is 25 minutes. After 11:00 nobody waits more than five minutes.
