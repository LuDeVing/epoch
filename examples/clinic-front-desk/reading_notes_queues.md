# Reading notes: queues and check-in

- Single-line, multi-server queues beat separate lines for the same number of servers; the gain is largest when
  service times vary a lot (our desk times vary from 35 seconds to over four minutes).
- Pre-registration (the form filled before arrival, at home or at a kiosk) is the most common lever in clinics; the
  reported wait reductions range widely, from 10 to 40 percent, depending on how many patients use it.
- Interruptions (phone calls) hurt more than their duration suggests, because each one restarts the current check-in.
- Staggered appointment slots in the first hour reduce the opening-time bulge; the risk is late arrivals bunching anyway.
- A visible estimate of the wait reduces complaints even when the wait itself does not change.

(sources: general queueing texts and clinic operations guides; no specific paper checked yet)
